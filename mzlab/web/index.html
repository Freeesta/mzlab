What is {APP}

An interactive platform for LC-MS/MS triple-quadrupole data analysis: it opens spectrometer files, displays chromatograms and spectra, and assists in identifying transformation products. It runs in the browser, requires no installation, and your files stay on your computer.

Don't have mzML files?

  1. Waters, Shimadzu and Bruker files are not read directly: convert them to mzML with MSConvert. Download ProteoWizard (it includes MSConvert), free, for Windows: proteowizard.sourceforge.io.
  2. Open MSConvert, drag your files (.raw, .wiff, .d…) into the list and press Add (a .wiff must sit next to its .wiff.scan).
  3. Output format: mzML.
  4. Press Start: the .mzML files appear in the output folder.
  5. Load the .mzML files here.

You don't need to convert .raw files: drop them in the box above and the app converts them.

    Select a molecule

    Draw a line across the bonds to break (two bonds of a ring too), or select them and right-click. Formula and mass appear under each piece: candidates to judge, not the answer.
    Cleavage

    Draw with curved arrows where the electrons go. The check only says whether each arrow starts at a lone pair, a bond or a radical and ends at an atom or a bond: it does not say whether the mechanism is right.
