## Note for the pressure-trace chat (PRESSIONE-RAW)
The message "Unit corrected: the file declares {unit} but the values are 10 times too high" is `analog.unit.tenfold`, raised by `analog.rs:31` `pressure_to_bar`: it matches the unit text against `TO_BAR` (bar, psi, pascal...), with no match the factor is 1, and when the largest value is above `BAR_MAX = 2000` bar every value is divided by 10 and the flag is set. A Thermo analog trace takes its unit from `t.unit` of `opentfraw` (`rawlettore.rs` `cromatogramma_di`, kind from `analogiche`); the `"number of detector counts"` text is the one the reader writes for the **TIC** (`rawlettore.rs`, end of `cromatogramma_di`). The chromatogram `index` does not take part (see N4).
