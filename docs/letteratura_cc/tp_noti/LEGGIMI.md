# Database of Known Transformation Products (Known TPs) in the Literature

This folder holds the reference database of transformation products (TPs) already
documented and identified in the peer-reviewed scientific literature for 15 model
compounds of environmental and pharmaceutical relevance.

---

## 1. Database Description and the 15 Compounds Covered

The `tp_noti.csv` database lists 112 transformation products generated by:
- **Direct UV and vacuum-UV photolysis** (VUV at 185 nm and UVC at 254 nm);
- **Heterogeneous photocatalysis** on semiconductors (mainly TiO2 Degussa P25 or modified);
- **Advanced Oxidation Processes (AOPs)** driven by the hydroxyl radical (•OH): UV/H2O2,
  Fenton reagent and solar/UV photo-Fenton, ozonation (O3).

## 2. Field Structure of `tp_noti.csv`

The `tp_noti.csv` file has exactly the following 13 columns:

1. `parent`: name of the precursor compound (one of the 15 compounds).
2. `tp_label_letteratura`: label or chemical/analytical name of the TP reported in the original
   article (e.g. `6-hydroxybentazone`, `desethylatrazine`, `1-(3,4-dichlorophenyl)-3-methylurea`,
   `carbamazepine-10,11-epoxide`, `5-hydroxydiclofenac`, `PD1`).
3. `neutral_formula`: neutral chemical formula written strictly in conventional Hill
   order (carbon C, hydrogen H, followed by the other elements in alphabetical order:
   e.g. C15H12N2O2).
4. `mz_pos`: theoretical monoisotopic mass-to-charge ratio of the protonated adduct [M+H]+ to 4
   decimals, calculated with IUPAC atomic weights (AME2020) and subtraction of the electron mass.
5. `mz_neg`: theoretical monoisotopic mass-to-charge ratio of the deprotonated adduct [M-H]- to 4
   decimals (or `N/A` for species without acidic hydrogens that do not ionize in negative ESI, such as
   hydrocarbons or neutral quinones).
6. `nominal_pos`: integer nominal mass of [M+H]+, rounded to the nearest integer (`round(mz_pos)`).
7. `nominal_neg`: integer nominal mass of [M-H]-, rounded to the nearest integer (`round(mz_neg)`),
   or `N/A`.
8. `reaction`: concise classification of the transformation in English (e.g. `hydroxylation`,
   `dihydroxylation`, `N-dealkylation`, `dechlorination`, `desulfonation`, `cleavage`, `oxidation`,
   `epoxidation`, `deamination`, `hydrolysis`).
9. `net_change`: net stoichiometric change relative to the parent formula (e.g. `+O`, `+2O`,
   `-C2H4`, `-Cl +OH`, `-H2 +O`, `+H2O`, `-CH2`, `-SO2 +H2`).
10. `condition`: reaction condition and AOP process used in the original study (`UV`, `TiO2`,
    `UV/H2O2`, `Fenton`, `O3`).
11. `doi`: unique Digital Object Identifier (DOI) of the peer-reviewed article,
    100% verified via the Crossref REST API.
12. `evidence`: level of analytical evidence demonstrated in the paper:
    - `authentic standard`: TP confirmed by comparative injection of a pure authentic chemical standard;
    - `HRMS`: high-resolution mass spectrometry (Orbitrap, Q-ToF) with accuracy < 5 ppm
      and consistent MS/MS fragmentation;
    - `unit mass`: low-resolution mass spectrometry (GC-MS, quadrupole LC-MS) with NIST library
      match/fragmentation spectrum.
13. `note`: explicit annotation of the origin of the mass value (`calculated from neutral
    formula; ...`) and relevant structural and stereochemical details.

## 3. Inclusion Criteria and Analytical Evidence

1. **Peer-reviewed origin**: no data were generated synthetically or hypothesized. Every
   single TP listed was extracted from studies published in international indexed journals
   (including *Water Research*, *Environmental Science & Technology*, *Applied Catalysis B:
   Environmental*, *Chemosphere*, *Journal of Hazardous Materials*, *Chemical Engineering Journal*,
   *Microchemical Journal*, *Rapid Communications in Mass Spectrometry*).
2. **Automatic DOI verification**: every DOI was checked through an HTTP query to the official
   Crossref API (`https://api.crossref.org/works/<doi>`), confirming the unique match of
   paper title, publication year and journal. Inactive or unresponsive DOIs were
   discarded.
3. **High-precision monoisotopic calculation**: in line with mass spectrometry standards, the
   theoretical exact masses were calculated from the Hill neutral formula with the
   scientific library `pyteomics.mass` and validated with the internal chemistry
   infrastructure of `mzLab` (`mzlab.chem.elements`).

## 4. Compounds with Few TPs Documented in the UV/AOP Literature and the Chemical Rationale

Among the 15 model compounds analyzed, two molecules stand out for a limited number of
identifiable stable primary TPs: **bentazone** and **timololo**. The environmental photochemistry
and AOP degradation literature highlights the following determining physico-chemical factors:

### 4.1 Bentazone (C10H12N2O3S)
- **Instability of the benzothiadiazinone heterocycle**: the bentazone structure contains a
  dihydro-benzothiadiazin-4-one 2,2-dioxide ring. Radical attack by •OH or direct photolysis
  induces rapid destabilization of the S–N sulfonamide bond and of the conjugated carbonyl
  group at C4.
- **Rapid mineralization without accumulation of complex intermediates**: unlike stable
  polycyclic or aromatic substrates (such as carbamazepine or propranolol), opening of the
  thiadiazine ring quickly leads to the release of polar low-molecular-weight fragments and
  inorganic species (SO4^2-, NO3-, NH4+, CO2). The only aromatic intermediates with a lifetime
  sufficient for analytical detection are the C6 and C8 mono-hydroxylated products and the sulfamic
  acid from ring hydrolysis, making the fraction of isolable TPs very small compared with other
  pesticides.
- **Contrast with fate in soil**: whereas in soil and plant tissues enzymatic biodegradation
  produces stable conjugated metabolites and persistent anthranilic acids, in an aqueous environment
  under UV irradiation and TiO2 photocatalysis bentazone degrades with almost instantaneous kinetics
  toward complete mineralization.

### 4.2 Timolol (C13H24N4O3S)
- **Photolytic inertia in the solar UV spectrum (lambda > 290 nm)**: timolol has an
  essentially null molar absorption coefficient in the UV-A and visible region, with a weak
  absorption centered at about 295 nm. Therefore, direct solar photolysis in natural waters is almost
  negligible (half-life of several weeks).
- **Competitive fragmentation of the morpholine ring and of the N-alkyl chain**: in advanced
  oxidation processes driven by UVC or ozone, timolol is susceptible to oxidation of the
  morpholine nitrogen and of the 1,2,5-thiadiazole core. These reactions trigger rapid loss of the
  amino-alcohol chain (tert-butylaminopropoxy) and oxidative opening of the morpholine. Few
  intermediates keep the basic structure intact enough to be quantified as macroscopic
  TPs, reducing the list of stable TPs to a few species identified only recently
  with very high-sensitivity HRMS techniques (PD1–PD5).

## 5. Instructions for Verification and Recalculation

All supporting scripts, Crossref extraction and analytical calculation are located strictly
**outside** the `mzLab` repository in the folder `~/gemini_cc/script/`,
fully preserving the integrity of the project source code.

### Recalculating the Database
To regenerate the `tp_noti.csv` file and recalculate all exact masses:
```bash
~/gemini_cc/conda_env/bin/python ~/gemini_cc/script/build_tp_csv.py
```

### Automatic DOI Verification
To query the Crossref API and verify the validity in real time of all 32 DOIs:
```bash
~/gemini_cc/conda_env/bin/python ~/gemini_cc/script/verify_all_dois.py
```

### Repository Compliance Checks
```bash
python3 tools/indice.py --controlla
python3 tools/controlla.py
python3 -m pytest -q tests/test_esempi_anonimi.py
```
