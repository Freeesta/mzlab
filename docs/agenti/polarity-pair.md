# `table pair` and `features.pair@1`: the [M+H]+ and [M-H]- features of two polarity methods, paired

- **What it does**: takes the feature table of a positive method and that of a negative method and pairs every positive row with every negative row whose **neutral mass** agrees within `mass_tol` and whose RT agrees within `rt_tol`: the species that appear in both polarities (candidate acids, amphoteric compounds). It does not identify anything and removes nothing; a row without a partner is listed as unmatched.
- **Code**: `crates/mzlab-core/src/polarity_pair/` (`params.rs` parameters and adducts, `table.rs` the CSV reader, `pairs.rs` the matching, `report.rs` the `pair/1` document and the CSV); `crates/mzlab-feat/src/pair.rs` (`pair_text`, reads the `.mzfeat` of a Flow plan through `series_csv`) and `steps/pair.rs` (descriptor); CLI `table_pair_cmd.rs` and `flow_pair.rs`; site `flow_pair` of the `flowtab` `.wasm` (`run_tab.rs`), op `flow_pair` of `feat-worker.js`, glue `flow/lazy/flow-web-pair.js`.
- **Rule** (logged in `choices` of every answer): M = (mz · |z| − shift) / k of the adduct of the side (`adduct_table.rs`); pair when |M(neg) − M(pos)| ≤ `mass_tol` and |RT(neg) − RT(pos)| ≤ `rt_tol`. Defaults: positive `[M+H]+`, negative `[M-H]-`, `rt_tol` 0.1 min, `mass_tol` 5 ppm (`mass_unit` ppm or da). Not the polarity parameter of `features.union` (`quant-targets.md`) and not `polpair/1` (`mzfeat-tp.md`, a proof of the same TP across polarities).
- **Adduct lists** (`pos_adducts`, `neg_adducts`, comma separated, user-editable): catalog names of the right sign (`[M+Na]+`, `[M+FA-H]-`, ...) or `LABEL:SHIFT` for an ion that is not in the catalog (k = 1, z = 1). With several adducts or several close rows one row can match more than one partner: each match is a line, the nearest partner of both rows is marked `mutual`; nothing is dropped for having a second partner.
- **Answer** (`pair/1`, `docs/schemi/pair-1.json`): `pairs` (ids, rule, `neutral_mass`, `err_da`, `err_ppm`, `rt_pos`, `rt_neg`, `drt`, `mutual`), `unmatched` (`pos`, `neg`), `counts`, `choices`, `csv`. Same bytes from `mzlab-cli table pair POS.csv NEG.csv [--set NAME=VALUE...] [--json] [--out PATH]` (the parameters are `--set` values: `rt_tol`, `mass_tol`, `mass_unit`, `pos_adducts`, `neg_adducts`), from `flow run` and from the browser. Tables: CSV (id/name, mz, rt) or `.mzfeat`.
- **Exit codes**: 0 done (also with no pair), 2 usage (also a bad parameter value), 3 a table that cannot be used, 4 a file missing or unreadable, 6 `--out` cannot be written.
- **Tests**: `crates/mzlab-core/src/polarity_pair/tests.rs`, `crates/mzlab-feat/tests/pair_step.rs` (golden `tests/data/pair/`), `flow_pair_tests.rs`, `table_pair_cmd_tests.rs`, parity `tests/test_flowtab_pair_wasm.py`.
