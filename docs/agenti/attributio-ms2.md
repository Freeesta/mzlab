# Attributio: MS2 engine, in silico fragmentation and plugins (MS packages)

All in Rust (`crates/mzlab-feat/src/attributio/ms2_engine/`, feature `attributio`), no Java, no network at runtime. The outputs are PROOFS shown alongside the cascade: they do not make identity, they assign no levels (2b stays with the chemist). Off by default (`[annotation] use_ms2_engine = false`), visible only with `uipAdminOn()`.

## MS-00: MS2 engine
- **Compiler** (`compile.rs`): reads GNPS JSON/JSON-lines and MSP libraries with the `mzlab-lib` parsers (`parse_all`), keeps polarity, precursor, adduct, peaks; spectra without polarity, peaks or id are discarded. `clean_library_name` removes from the name the file names (`.mzML`…), the `CollisionEnergy…` tag and the energy suffixes (` - 30.0 eV`), then `names::clean_raw`.
- **Matcher** (`matcher.rs`): `Ms2Library::new` cleans and sorts by precursor (one list per polarity); `search` cleans the query like `ms_entropy` (m/z 10-1000, float32, noise 1 %, 0.05 Da), searches in the ppm window (10) and scores with the `mzlab-lib` entropy at 0.02 Da. Default threshold 0.6, at least 3 shared ions (`min_shared`: new gate, the original did not have it).
- **Output** (`hits.rs`): `engine_<pos|neg>.csv` (`_pol,_id,engine_name,engine_score,engine_precursor_mz,engine_id`); reading without writing into the campaign.
- **Not ported**: the MS2DeepScore search (neural network; the score is not an identity).
- **Parity**: `cargo test -p mzlab-feat --features attributio --test attributio_ms2_engine`; golden data `tests/data/attributio/ms2_engine/golden.json` from `tools/ms2_engine_reference.py` (requires `ms_entropy`): same result sets and scores within 1e-6.

## MS-01: proofs in the cascade
- `readers`: `ms2_engine::load_engine_hits` reads `<data>/engine/engine_<pos|neg>.csv` into `PolSources::engine` (file absent = no rows; the "engine hits" count appears only if there are rows).
- `annotate/join.rs`: with `[annotation] use_ms2_engine = true` the best result of every feature goes to `Feature::engine` (`EngineEvidence`: name, score, precursor, library id); without the option the files are ignored. No gate, no level, no identity changes (proven: equal levels with and without).
- `Feature::engine_agrees()` compares the engine name with the identity or displayed name (letters and digits, case-insensitive): `Some(false)` is a disagreement to show the chemist, not a conflict that moves the level. The name enters the conflict only as shown evidence; the levels table has the columns `engine_name`, `engine_score` and `engine_agrees` (empty without a result; in the "MS2 engine" page, hidden by default, and a row in the card of the open row); the 2b Bench does not show them.
- Proof: `attributio_ms2_engine::engine_hits_are_evidence_not_levels`.

## MS-02: in silico fragmentation like MetFrag
- `ms2_engine/insilico.rs`: `read_candidates` reads a local list (PubChemLite-type CSV: `Identifier`, `CompoundName`, `SMILES`, `MolecularFormula`, `MonoisotopicMass`; if the mass is missing → formula → SMILES); `rank` keeps the candidates within 5 ppm of the neutral mass `precursor ∓ proton`, breaks them bond by bond with `review::fragmenter` (10 ppm, up to 200 bonds) and sorts them by `fragmenter_score` = Σ `mz^0.421·int^0.1307` of the explained peaks / Σ of the weights of all peaks (the MetFrag peak weight). They are hypotheses with proofs, never identities.
- **Intentional differences**: no bond energy term nor the MetFrag `0.005 Da + 10 ppm` tolerance; no PubChem search (the list lives in the libraries folder).
- **Parity**: the fragmenter does not use RDKit; the explained peaks are equal to those of RDKit in 39 structures out of 39 (declared threshold ≥ 0.90, like `attributio_spiegazioni`); the score is equal to 1e-12 against the pure Python reference `tools/insilico_reference.py` (data `tests/data/attributio/ms2_engine/insilico.json`). The full MetFrag round (jar) cannot be run here: the MetFrag score is not compared.

## MS-03: fragment attribution with hydrogen transfers
- `ms2_engine/rearrange.rs`: `attribute` assigns to each peak the nearest sub-formula of the candidate ion (like `review::explain`); if none falls within the tolerance, it retries with the peak shifted by k = +1, −1, +2, −2 H atoms and the fragment formula takes k more hydrogens (transfer between the two sides of a break). For each peak: formula, shift, ppm, neutral loss (the H counts can be negative). Score = Σ intensity·(1 − 0.25·|k|) / Σ intensity: whoever requires shifts ranks below whoever explains the spectrum without them.
- **Grid beyond the limit** (`too_many_subformulas`): in `formula.ms2@1` the candidate stays, without fragments, with `"warning":"warn.flow.formula.subformulas"` (text in `lang/{it,en}/errori.js`), in `subformula` as in `h_rearrangement`; below the limit the bytes do not change. Proof: `flow_formula_web_tests::a_grid_over_the_limit_is_named_on_the_candidate`.
- They are proofs of a FORMULA (isomers share it), never identities. The score and the penalty are our choices, not those of MS-FINDER (which is not run: Mono and Windows).
- **Parity**: `tools/rearrange_reference.py` (requires numpy and pandas, `ATTRIBUTIO_SRC`) builds the reference on the Attributio sub-formula table; `attributio_ms2_engine::rearrangement_attribution_equals_the_reference`: adduct, formulas, shifts, losses equal and score at 1e-9 on 10 formulas (8 peaks with transfer).
- **Grid limit** (`max_subformulas` of `formula.ms2@1`, from 1 to 2·10⁷, default 3·10⁶; it also applies to the review): above the limit the ion's sub-formula table is not built and the candidate explains no fragment; memory grows with the limit.

## MS-04: RT prediction and chimeric intruders
- `ms2_engine/rtpredict.rs`: `morgan` (Morgan-type fingerprint, radius 2, 2048 bits, on the `chem::graph` graph; own hash: they are not the RDKit bits) and `RtModel::train(righe, seed)` / `predict(smiles)`: forest of 100 regression trees (bootstrap, all bits tried at every split, seed 42), at least 10 structures that can be read. Trained on the campaign standards, it follows its column and its gradient; it supports or questions a candidate, it does not make identity.
- **Parity (declared threshold, no equality)**: `tools/rtpredict_reference.py` (RDKit and scikit-learn) on 39 structures with invented RTs (30 for training, 9 for testing); `rt_forest_follows_scikit_learn`: mean error on the 9 ≤ 125 % of that of scikit-learn + 0.1 min and not above 110 % of the constant predictor (measurement: 1.171 against 1.117 and 1.360).
- `ms2_engine/chimeric.rs`: `intruders` lists the features of the same polarity within half the isolation window (`default_window`: 3.0 for a Fusion, 1.5 otherwise) and within `rt_tol` (0.1 min) in RT, from the nearest in m/z; values rounded to 4 and 2 decimals. Hand proof (`window_rt_polarity_and_order`): the rule is a filter, the Python reference adds no information.

## MS-05: `.wasm` and panel
- `.wasm` `attributio-ms2` (crate `mzlab-attributio-wasm`, feature `ms2`, bridge `mzfeat-attributio-ms2/1`, function `ms2_to`): a JSON request `{op: engine|insilico|rearrange|rt|chimeric, ...}` on the campaign folder; libraries and candidate lists arrive as extra files under `__ms2/`. In the site it lives in the merged module `attributio-all` (feature `all`; the `attributio_ms2` manifest entry points to that file). Ceilings: `docs/agenti/wasm-tetti.md`.
- The worker exposes `attributioMs2(files, root, request)` (`TP_Mine/files/feat-worker.js`, `feat-desktop-pool.js`); in the desktop app the route is `feat/attributio/ms2`.
- Panel `TP_Mine/js/54_attributio_ms2.js` (`ATTRIBUTIO_MS2.mount`), in the Flow campaign view and only with `uipAdminOn()`: "Run MS2 engine" downloads `engine_pos.csv`/`engine_neg.csv` (writes nothing into the campaign), then ranks candidates, attributes fragments with H shifts and shows the co-isolated features and the RT prediction, all in tables. They are proofs for the chemist: no level changes. Proof: `tests_e2e/e2e_attributio_ms2.py`.
