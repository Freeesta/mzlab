# Substitution position (classes compatible with the MS2)

Code: `crates/mzlab-feat/src/chem/positions.rs` and `posizioni/` (feature `chem`, on top of `grafo::Mol`); tests: `crates/mzlab-feat/tests/positions.rs` and `positions_prior.rs`. The "Substitution position" panel of Tabui (administrator) is in `TP_Mine/js/44_posizioni.js`; test: `tests_e2e/e2e_posizioni.py`.

## Purpose
- Given a parent (SMILES), a transformation and the observed MS2 of the TP, it lists the atoms where the modification can sit, groups them and says which groups the spectrum supports, excludes or cannot tell apart. It never chooses a position and assigns no levels: `proposal.sublevel` is a 3a/3b proposal for the chemist (no field is called `level`).
- A fragment with the modification says in which PIECE it sits; inside a whole ring the positions stay indistinguishable (level 3a/3b, multicentre of the Drawing).

## Rust API
- `sites(mol, transform) -> Result<Vec<Site>>`: `+O` (`aromatic_oh`: aromatic C-H; `aliphatic_oh`: sp3 C-H; `n_oxide`: neutral non-acylated tertiary N or aromatic N with two bonds; `s_oxide`: sulfide), `-CH2` (`demethyl`: methyl on N, O or S), `+O-2H` (`carbonyl`: sp3 CH2), `+Cl-H` (`aromatic_cl`), `+Br-H` (`aromatic_br`: aromatic C-H) (and `-2H`, below). Spaces, `−` and `+O-H2` accepted. Errors: `error.posiz.transform`, `error.posiz.noSites`, `error.posiz.formula` (`check_formulas`: the products must all have the same formula).
- `+2O` (`dioxid`), `+2O-2H` (`carbonyl_oh`) and `+3O` (`trioxid`): the site is a SET of atoms (`Site.atom`, `partner`, `third`; `+2O`/`+3O`: atoms of the `+O` sites; `+2O-2H`: a CH2 and an atom of `+O`, never the same); the class is the set itself (equivalence by symmetry is given only by the same set of peaks). A fragment carries the modification of every atom it contains (`siti::atom_deltas`); `is_multi(kind)` recognises them; beyond 14 oxidisable atoms `+3O` gives `error.posiz.noSites`. The sites JSON adds `partner` (and `third`) only for these.
- `-C2H4` (`deethyl`): ethyl (CH2 on N, O or S with its terminal CH3) lost whole; `atom` is the CH2, `partner` the CH3; the N-CH2 and CH2-CH3 bonds are not cut and the loss sits on the heteroatom side.
- `-Cl+OH` (`dechloro_oh`), `NH2>OH` (`amino_oh`) and `CH3>COOH` (`methyl_cooh`): group substitutions, single-atom site (Cl on an aromatic ring; N of an NH2 with only one heavy neighbour; C of a CH3 with only C neighbours). `CH3>COOH` has the same net formula as `+2O-2H`: the `+2O-2H` transformation offers the `carbonyl_oh` and `methyl_cooh` sites together. Unlike the multiple deltas, a single site of these can reach `site`.
- `-2H` (`dehydro`): sp3 C-C single bond with at least one H on each carbon. The site is the bond: `Site.atom` and `Site.partner`, and the class joins those of the two carbons; the bond is not cut in the expected fragments.
- `symmetry_classes(mol) -> Vec<u32>`: iterative refinement of (element, degree, H, aromaticity, charge) with the neighbours (bond order, class). Limit: on rare regular graphs it can merge non-equivalent atoms; the effect is one candidate fewer, never a proof removed.
- `expected_fragments(mol, site, pol) -> Vec<Frag>`: every break of `Mol::cleavages()` (closed rings, hydrolysis with OH on the other side), the two sides with formula and m/z of [F+H]+ ([F−H]− in negative); the side with the site takes the modification. With `-CH2` the bonds of the lost methyl are skipped. Cl and Br: most abundant isotope.
- `match_spectrum(mol, sites, peaks, tol, pol) -> Result<Report>`; `Tol::Da` (LR, typical 0.5) or `Tol::Ppm` (HR).
- `positions_json(smiles, transform, peaks_json, tol, ppm, positive) -> Result<String>`: peaks `[[mz,int],..]` or `[{"mz","i"},..]`; `ppm` false = Da. `error.posiz.peaks`, `error.posiz.tol`.
- `positions_prior_json(..., reatt_json)`: the same with the parent's `reatt/1` (`mzlab-chem`; empty string = none) and the extra `prior` field (`prior.rs`, `prior_of(sites, n, reatt)`); `error.posiz.prior` with `reason` `json`, `format` or `atoms` (a heavy atom without f(−) or a `heavy_index` outside the parent).
- `positions_record_json(rec, parent, transform, peaks_json, tol, ppm, positive, reatt_json)` (feature `record`, in no `.wasm`): the same report starting from a `mol/1` record (`mzlab_mol::Mol`) instead of the SMILES; the graph is `grafo::Mol::from_record(rec, true)` (aromaticity perceived from the Kekulé form), `parent` is only the label of the `parent` field. Parity test with the SMILES path: `tests/posizioni_record.rs`.

## `posiz/1` contract
`{format:"posiz", version:1, parent, transform, tp_formula, tp_mz, pol:"+|-", tol, tol_unit:"da|ppm", sites:[{atom, symmetry, kind}], classes:[{id, atoms, kind, status, support:[{mz, frag_formula, side_atoms, iso_mz?}], against:[..]}], ox_type:{h2o_loss, minus_o}|null, unexplained:[mz], suggest:[..], proposal:{sublevel:"3a|3b", why}}`.
- `atom` is the index in SMILES order; `symmetry` the symmetry class; the `kind` of a class is `mixed` if it joins different types. `iso_mz`: +2 peak (³⁷Cl, ⁸¹Br) found for a fragment with a halogen. `why`: `none_excluded`, `excluded_few_left`, `many_classes_left`.
- `ox_type` only for `+O` (otherwise `null`): H2O loss from the precursor and [M+H−O]+ observed or not; they are only evidence shown, they do not filter. Ramanathan et al. 2000 (doi:10.1021/ac9911692): in ESI aliphatic OH loses water more than phenol; [M+H−O]+ is typical of N-oxides, especially in APCI.
- `prior` (only with the `reatt/1`): `[{atom, value, rank, quantity:"f_minus", method, note:"Prev.", caveat?}]`, one per site in `sites` order; `value` = f(−) of the atom (4 decimals), `method` = `method` of the `reatt/1` plus "implicit water" or "gas phase". `rank` dense among the symmetry classes of the sites, sorted by decreasing mean f(−); tie if the gap from the first of the group is < 0.005 (`TIE`).
- `prior`, transformations: for `+O` without `caveat`; for `+Cl-H` and `+Br-H` `caveat` "electrophilic substitution, not validated here"; for `-CH2` and `+O-2H` `prior:[]`. Classes, statuses, `proposal` and the rest of the JSON stay identical.
- `suggest`: `hdx` if C-OH and N-/S-oxide candidates remain together (Cajka et al. 2026, doi:10.3390/analytica7030057); `ms3` if a fragment with the modification is common to several classes (Murakami et al. 2018, doi:10.1007/s11419-018-0410-4); `standard` always.

## Comparison rules
- Peaks used: the 30 most intense above 1% of the spectrum's base peak, precursor removed; "intense" = at least 10% of the base peak.
- A candidate (sites with the same symmetry class and the same type) explains a peak WITH the modification (fragment that carries it) or WITHOUT (fragment that does not carry it).
- Class = candidates with the same set of peaks explained with the modification and the same exclusion: the spectrum does not tell them apart.
- `excluded`: an intense peak is explained with the modification by other candidates and the candidate does not explain it in any way (neither with nor without). `supported`: a peak explained with the modification belongs, among the non-excluded candidates, exactly to the members of the class. Otherwise `undistinguished`.
- `proposal.sublevel` = 3a only if at least one class is excluded and at most 3 remain; otherwise 3b. `unexplained`: peaks that no candidate explains (nor +2 peak of a halogenated fragment).

## "Substitution position" panel (Tabui, only `uipAdminOn()`)
- Engine: `positions(smiles, transform, peaks_json, tol, ppm, positive)` and `positions_prior(..., reatt_json)` of the `grafo` `.wasm` (`crates/mzlab-feat-grafo-wasm`, `version()` = `mzfeat-grafo/10`; same JSON as `positions_json` and `positions_prior_json`, test `tests/positions.rs`); the values come in already computed, the `grafo` `.wasm` does not carry `mzlab-chem`.
- App: the `feat/grafo` route with `kind: "positions"` (`smiles`, `transform`, `peaks` as JSON text, `tol`, `ppm`, `positive`, `reatt` optional as text; `feat_graph.rs`, `positions()` of the `feat-desktop-pool.js` pool). The `.wasm` weighs 158 151 B before and 234 654 B after (`wasm-opt -Oz`), under the common cap of 512 000 B.
- Page: `TPPOS.mount(host, model)` adds at the bottom of a row's card a box with the button; `TPPOS.eligible(model)` is true only with administrator mode, parent SMILES, MS2 with at least one peak and row Δ among `+O`, `-CH2`, `+O-2H` (also `+O-H2`), `+Cl-H`, `+Br-H` (spaces and "−" accepted); otherwise no button.
- Hook points: LR in `show()` of `20_tpmine.js` (peaks = `[mz, rel]` fragments of `d.ms2` matched by `ms2_links/1`, tolerance 0.5 Da); HR in `fill()` of `21_tpmine_hr.js` (peaks of the candidate MS2 `d.ms2.candidate`, 10 ppm). The SMILES is that of the `#tp-smi` field, the polarity comes from the chosen adduct.
- Column "Prev. f(−) (mean, rank)", always present and last: mean f(−) of the class atoms and rank ("rank 1", "ranks 2–3"), "computing…" until the parent's `reatt/1` arrives, "failed" if the computation fails, "–" without `prior`. Above the table the fixed note "Prev. f(−): Prediction (GFN2-xTB, implicit water), not evidence; does not reorder or exclude classes." (plus the `caveat` for `+Cl-H`/`+Br-H`).
- The column's `reatt/1` arrives with the `tp-reatt` event (`{smiles, reatt}` or `{smiles, state}`, sent by `TPREATT.mount` and `TPREATT.state`) or with `TPPOS.setReatt(smiles, r)`; the table already drawn stays, only the column changes.
- Content: proposal ("Proposal: Schymanski 3a|3b — N position classes remain; the chemist decides", no button saves a level); class table (id, atoms as symbol + 1-based SMILES index, type, status written in words with ✓ ✗ ≈, supporting and opposing fragments with m/z and formula, "unexplained" row); bar MS2 spectrum with a marker and per-class hatching (Okabe-Ito colours from CSS variables), `TDATI` table, zoom with buttons and keys (+ − ← → Home, no dragging); "Oxidation type" and "Suggested checks" boxes with DOI.

## Limits
- No ring opening, no stereochemistry; at unit resolution an m/z coincides by chance more often. Fragment convention = that of `Mol::cleavages()` (each side takes an H).
- The reactivity estimate (`prior`) is called "Prev.": it does not reorder, does not remove classes and does not enter `proposal`.

## Rules
- **Evidence and levels**: the evidence for the position is the MS2 (a fragment that carries the shift locates the group in that piece). The program shows the position classes that the spectrum does not tell apart (3a, or 3b if the spectrum excludes nothing) as a proposal and does not choose one; nothing computational rises to 2a or 2b. Positions equivalent by symmetry are a single candidate; a fragment that no break explains is "unexplained", neither against nor for. Reactivity is only "Prev." next to the classes: it does not reorder them and does not remove candidates.
- **Boundaries**: only Tabui and Attributio behind `uipAdminOn()`, nothing in the students' LR world (Data, Drawing, report). No xtb, DFT or CREST in the browser, no predicted RT or CCS to choose the isomer, no ML of the sites, no P450 site estimate. No lab test planned: `hdx` and `ms3` of `posiz/1` stay checks suggested by the literature.
