# Spectral matching against libraries

State of the spectral matching engine (`crates/mzlab-lib`) and where it is used. Every choice carries its why.

## 1. What exists
- **`.mzlib` format** (`crates/mzlab-lib`, no `unsafe`; v1 and v2, `MIN_READER` 1 so a v2 reader reads v1): 32 B spectrum records (precursor, polarity, `u8` license, optional `f32` RT, and in v2 the retention index with `FLAG_HAS_RI`), m/z peaks quantized to 1e-5 Da (`u32`) and `u16` intensities in blocks of 512 zstd-compressed spectra, metadata (10 fields: name, adduct, CE, instrument, formula, InChIKey, SMILES, accession, authors, license) in blocks of 128, **Flash indexes on fragments and neutral losses** (10 B entries in 64 KB pages, sorted by m/z).
  v2 adds `SEC_LIBINFO` (`libinfo.rs`: `kind` experimental/in_silico, `tech` lc_msms/gc_ei/msn, analyzer and polarity counters, `source`, `version`, `sha256`) and `inchikey14` (a `u64` hash of the first 14 characters per spectrum, to group without reading the metadata). *Why*: presets and badges need no reimport.
  Sliced reading with an LRU cache (64 MB, 32 MB on phone/iPad: `read.rs`); the page tells the worker the device in the `config` message (`libreria-corpo.js`, rule of `rust-budget.js` `device()`), because in the worker an iPad with the Mac user agent has no `maxTouchPoints`. Memory-limited construction (`build.rs`, scratch on disk). Flash cleaning (`clean.rs`: precursor − 1.6 Da, noise < 1 %, merge 0.05 Da, ≤ 500 peaks).
- **Parsers** (`parse.rs`): MSP, MGF, MassBank `.txt`, JSON MoNA/GNPS/mzMine, recognized from the content (`detect`). Detection written in `SEC_LIBINFO`: polarity from `Ion_mode`/`IONMODE`/adduct; technique `gc_ei` if the precursor is missing and `Spectrum_type`/`IONIZATION` = EI or there is `RI`; `in_silico` from known names/comments (LipidBlast, "in-silico", `PREDICTED`) or from the catalog; analyzer from `Instrument_type` keywords.
- **Scores** (`scores.rs`, `prep.rs`): spectral entropy (Li 2021), cosine (square root of intensities), forward/reverse, explained intensity, common peaks, common neutral losses, modified cosine (32 peaks), GNPS cosine; per-peak contributions (`contrib.rs`, `score_terms`). Tolerance in Da or ppm (`frag_tol.rs`). Stein weighted cosine (`stein_cosine`, weights I^a·(m/z)^b, defaults a = 0.53, b = 1.3 after Kim et al. 2012, all in `SteinOpts`), the Gaussian retention-index factor `ri_factor` (σ 10 RI units) and `hybrid_entropy` (`stein.rs`); the GC-EI scores have no interface.
- **Searches** (`search.rs`): `identity` (precursor window, every candidate scored), `flash_scores` (open / neutral losses / hybrid on the index, flat accumulators `FlashAcc` in `flash.rs`), `analogs` (hybrid in the ±200 Da window, then modified cosine ≥ 4 peaks). Target-decoy FDR (`decoy.rs`).
- **Multi-library engine** (`mzlab-lib`): `libset.rs` (up to 16 libraries, one `u16` mask per library, shared accumulators, merge of the tops by score, sidecar `MZLS` of duplicate groups: key `(inchikey14, pol, round(prec, 0.001), hash of the 6 highest peaks)`, duplicates get a common group id (`group`); *why*: MassBank, MoNA and GNPS share many spectra and repeating them takes room from other compounds), `matchres.rs` (`libmatch/1`, `docs/schemi/libmatch-1.json`), `preset.rs` (`data/preset-1.json`).
  Only the `mzlab-lib` tests and `examples/libset_bench.rs` call `libset` and `matchres`; neither the Flow nor the panel does.
- **wasm** `crates/mzlab-lib-wasm` (`static/wasm-lib/`, cap in `tools/moduli/librerie.toml`, loaded only when the libraries are opened; worker `libreria-worker.js`): `Importer`, `Lib.open/identity/best/analogs/verify`.
- **Library folder** (`libreria-cartella.js`): desktop app = native memory-mapped folder; Chrome/Edge = `showDirectoryPicker`; elsewhere OPFS `librerie/`. User sources never modified, index in `.mzlab/<name>.mzlib` + `.idx.json` (size, date, SHA-256), states ready/to index/update/damaged; a changed SHA-256 of the source gives "To index", a catalog package with a new `versione` "Update available".
- **Flow**: `spectra.match@1` (algorithms `spectral_entropy` default, `cosine`, `modified_cosine` with `delta_formula_mda`, `tree` for MSⁿ; filters name/adduct/instrument/CE/RT, `decoys`, `matched_peaks`, `score_terms`) and `table.match@1` (the MS² of every row of the `.mzfeat` against one library; output `candidates/1`, query = row id); `annot.level@1` (Schymanski cascade, `livelli-1.json`); `rt.predict` (Crippen/forest, RT direction); `polpair/1` (same neutral mass in the other polarity, evidence and never merging; pos and neg tables stay separate).
- **Page**: "What is it?" (HR, `libreria-finestra.js`: master/detail with Candidates and Analogs tabs, groups by first InChIKey block, 2D structure, mirror with solid/dashed/dotted lines and mouse hover, level from `LIVELLI.assess`); in LR "Search in the libraries…" without levels.
- **MSⁿ side panel**: "Identification with the libraries" (`ident-pannello.js`, tab of the info bar): MS² spectrum of the active cell, loaded libraries with automatic selection of the compatible ones, "Load file…" / "Load folder…", catalog menu (`ident-catalogo.js`), candidates table; the search goes through `LIB.call("search", {ids})` in the worker. MS³+ libraries (MSnLib, `msn-lib/1`) go through `spectra.match` `tree`. LR (Principle 1): `confidence` is empty and no badge suggests an identity; HR uses the instrument profile tolerances (precursor ppm, peaks 10 ppm, minimum 0.005 Da).
- **Reusable chemistry**: `COMMON_DELTAS` (42 modifications) and `delta_formulas` (`mzlab-feat/src/attributio/review/matching.rs`), `TP_Mine/data/regole_tp_3.json` (photocatalysis rules with `delta_formula`, `direzione_rt`), `isoscore.rs` (fine isotope pattern), `kendrick.rs` (KMD), `mzlab-mol/src/descr.rs` (TPSA, Crippen logP), RDBE in `msngraph/candidates.rs`.

## 2. Algorithms per technique
| technique | default | why |
|---|---|---|
| LC-MS/MS identity | spectral entropy (Li 2021), Flash cleaning | more robust than cosine on noisy spectra |
| analogs / TP | hybrid entropy for the candidates, then modified cosine to sort | the hybrid search is in the index; the modified cosine reproduces GNPS and gives the shifted peaks (Δ = prec_query − prec_lib) that the mirror draws dashed |
| GNPS reproduction | GNPS cosine (`gnps_cosine`) | comparison with GNPS/FBMN results |
| GC-EI | Stein weighted cosine with forward and reverse, ΔRI factor | NIST standard; no precursor: open search on fragments |
| MSⁿ | `tree` (`msngraph/search.rs`) | trees, not single spectra |
- Left out: Spec2Vec and MS2DeepScore (models of tens of MB with ONNX/TensorFlow inference: heavy dependencies and no simple parity).

## 3. Engine and index measurements
Synthetic library of `identity_bench.rs` (precursors 100–900, 5–40 peaks from a pool of 400 fragments), whole library in memory, wasm in Node (`wasm32-unknown-unknown`, `opt-level 3`, LTO); numbers in `measurements-rust.md`.
- `identity` stays under 5 ms per spectrum on 1 million spectra even in wasm; `analogs` and the open hybrid search take tens to a hundred ms on 1 million unit-resolution spectra.
- The flat accumulators give the same scores bit for bit as the pairs version and are ×2–5 faster. The synthetic pool is pathological (every fragment in thousands of spectra); real libraries visit far fewer entries.
- Rules: the hybrid is limited by the `analog_max_delta` window (open searches on the whole index only on request); in a multi-library search each library is searched in turn with the same accumulators and the tops merged by score (no global index, since libraries are updated one at a time).

## 4. `libmatch/1` result (`matchres.rs`)
- Per query: `query` (row or scan id), `params` (tolerances, algorithm, preset), `compounds[]` sorted, at most `top_compounds` (10). A compound = first InChIKey block (or normalized name if missing): `inchikey14`, `name`, `formula`, `smiles`, `badge` (exact | analog | in_silico), `best`, `spectra[]` (all the spectra of the compound with score), `libraries` (mask and names).
- Per spectrum: `library`, `accession`, `score` and `score_kind`, `reverse`, `explained`, `matched` n and `of` N (query peaks), `direct` and `shifted` pairs, precursor `delta_ppm`, `delta_mz`, `delta_formula[]`, `adduct`, `ce`, `instrument`, `licence`, `pairs[]` (query_mz, library_mz, kind direct|shifted|loss; the mirror draws from here, in the `matched_peaks` format).
- `confidence` is a level computed by rules (Schymanski/MSI), never decided; `matchres` leaves it null. In the Flow `annot.level` gives it (Rust cascade, `livelli-1.json`); in the panel the JS computes it (`LIVELLI.assess` in `mzlab/web/libreria.js`, same rules, `livelli-1.js`). Experimental exact HR gives 2a; analog 3b; in silico never 2a; LR empty.

## 5. Catalog and presets
- **Catalog** `libraries/2` (`catalogo.json`, generated by `tools/librerie_pacchetti.py` plus the hand-written list `tools/librerie_catalogo.json`, schema checked by `--check-catalog`, details in `libraries.md`; `librerie/1` still accepted): per entry `id`, `nome`, `url` (page), `download` (file), `licenza` (SPDX), `formato`, `dimensione`, `sha256` or null, `kind`, `tech`, `pol`, `doi`, `verificato` (date of the check with a real fetch), `modo` (`site` = 4 MB pieces with SHA-256 on the site, only licenses that allow redistribution;
  `external` = desktop app only, with the dedicated switch; `link` = link to the site only).
- The page downloads only from the site itself (CSP `connect-src 'self'`). Menu: base group (MassBank, MS-DIAL, GNPS/MoNA) and a closed "Specialist / in silico" group; `link` entries for non-redistributable libraries; commercial NIST, mzCloud and paid METLIN are excluded.
- **Presets** (`mzlab-lib/data/preset-1.json`, `preset.rs`): a filtered view, not a copy: `experimental`, `environmental` (MassBank, DIMSpec PFAS, MultiMS2, MS-DIAL), `metabolomics` (MS-DIAL, MoNA, GNPS, LipidBlast), `natural_products` (GNPS, ISDB if present), `forensic` (SWGDRUG if present); the preset translates into a `u16` mask over the open libraries.

## 6. Two spectra against each other
`spectrum compare FILE [FILE_B]` (`spectrum-compare/1`, `mzlab-lib/src/speccmp.rs`) scores one sample MS2 against one standard MS2 without a library.
- Both are the nominal-mass bins of `/api/spectrum?rebin=nominal`, matched with `scores::match_peaks` (`--set tol=DA`, default 0 = the same bin) and scored with `scores::cosine_from_pairs`.
- `--a-mz-offset` / `--b-mz-offset` (`DA` or `auto:MZ[@RT]`, as `spectrum get --bin nominal --mz-offset`) go to the route as `mzoff=`: each side's offset is taken off before its bins, so a sample and a standard of different calibrations share bins. Each side block of the document carries `mz_offset_da` and `mz_offset` (the route's evidence, `auto` included); stderr logs them per side.
- The document lists the matched ions with both intensities, the ions each side lacks and the collision energy of each (a warning when they differ by more than `ce_tol`, default 0.5 eV).
- The CLI, the app (`speccompare` op) and the site (`spectrum_compare` in `librerie`) call the same function on the two route answers.
- **Site card** (`mzlab/web/spettro-confronto.js`, lazy module; menu entry "Compare with another MS2 spectrum…" of a non-HR MS2 spectrum, registered in `libreria.js`). Test: `tests_e2e/e2e_spettro_confronto.py`.
  - Side A is the panel's selection, side B the same in another file of the tab. A side is a file, a precursor (those `ms2_exps` lists) and a time window in minutes; `tol` and `ce_tol` are optional (empty = the engine default, logged in the document).
  - JS fetches `api/spectrum?...&rebin=nominal` per side and hands the two answers to the engine: the `compare` command of `libreria-worker.js` (`spectrum_compare` of the `librerie` .wasm) on the site, the `speccompare` rpc in the app. It only draws the document.
  - The collision energies of a side come from the file's `ms2_exps` for that precursor (the CLI reads the scans of the window): a precursor measured at several energies in the file gives `warn.spectrum.ceMixed` on the site even when the window holds one.
