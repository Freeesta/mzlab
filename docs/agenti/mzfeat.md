# Mass traces in Rust (`crates/mzlab-feat`)
The crate runs detection (level L1 of the pipeline): from the MS1 peaks of a `.mzidx` (one polarity) it produces the mass-trace table of a file. The frozen answers of the parity tests are in `tests/golden/feat/detect.json`. Centroid LC-HRMS only, MS1 only; separate polarities (`Params.polarity`).

## Modules
- `trace.rs`: `Params` (in `trace_params.rs`; `detect` defaults: 4 ppm channels, noise threshold 2e4, minimum height 2e5, minimum points 5, gaps ≤ 2 scans, background traces > 400 scans, `budget` in bytes) and `detect(&mut Index<R>, &Params) -> Result<FileFeatures>`. Peaks are read with `idxread::Index::each_peak` in chunks of 64 K, only those above the threshold are kept and m/z slabs of about `budget / 64` peaks are formed (at most 1.5 M).
- `detect_route.rs`: path of a file: `Peak::Adap` to `untargeted::flow::detect`, the rest to `trace::detect_with` (rejects ADAP: `error.badRecipe`).
- `channels.rs`: `segments` ((channel, scan) cells summed, segments that skip at most `max_gap` scans between two points (0 = consecutive scans; internal distance `max_gap + 1`, written so in the `Debug` of `trace::Params` so the fingerprint of the `.mzfeatf` stays the same), channels with maximum < minimum height / 4 dropped at once) and `stitch` (stitching the pieces of an ion across nearby channels: connected components numbered by first member, like `np.unique` on the Python labels).
- `post.rs`: `merge_grids` (same ion seen on the two shifted grids, by apex or edge containment and, with `merge_overlap` > 0 (default 0, off), also by scan overlap of at least that share of the shorter trace: the one with more points stays), `fourier_artefacts` (FT lobes), `finish` (stable m/z order and final columns).
- `table.rs`: `FileFeatures` (`mz f64`, `rt/left/right f32` in minutes, `apex/scan_lo/scan_hi u32` as MS1 positions, `area f64` in counts × seconds, `height f32`, `npts u16`, `flags u8`, `iso_group u32`, `charge i8`, `dt` = median interval between scans in seconds) and `to_bytes()` (fixed layout, basis of the SHA-256). Quality columns: `shape f32`, `snr f32`, `fwhm f32` (scans), `asym f32` (0 = undefined), `gaps u16`, `qreason u8`; MS2 columns: `ms2_n u16`, `purity f32` (the highest, −1 = no MS2).
  `flags` bits: 0 background, 1 FT lobe, 2 resolved, 3 isotope, 4 good quality, 5 touches the file edge, 6 possible mixture, 7 purity estimated with the default window; `trace::detect` writes 0 and 1, the others are written by the modules below (2 and 6: `resolve`).
- `pipeline.rs`: `analyse(index, &Params, &Steps) -> (FileFeatures, Ms2Links)` = `detect`, then `resolve::assess_and_resolve` (quality and maximum splitting in one pass; with `Steps::split = false` only `quality::assess`), `isotopes::group`, `ms2::link`, `resolve::mark_mixtures`.
- `resolve.rs`: a feature with a long trace (> `long_trace` scans) or shape < 0.9 is searched for maxima with `peakq::detect_prominence` on the trace at ±6 ppm (first a cheap check: a valley ≥ 10 % below both sides).
- Splitting in `resolve.rs`: two close maxima are two ions if the valley is below 50 % of the lower one and the apexes are ≥ 0.1 min apart: the row becomes one piece per ion, `FLAG_RESOLVED` (bit 2), with area, height, edges and trace quality of the piece (shared valley); otherwise it stays one feature and `mark_mixtures` sets `FLAG_MIXTURE` (bit 6) if the MS2 spectra linked before and after the valley have cosine (roots, 0.05 m/z bin) < 0.7. Rows by m/z; isotopes and MS2 links after the split.
- `sweep.rs`: `windows` walks the peak table once, in slabs, and gives each feature (sorted by m/z) the peaks within ±`ppm`; a slab reads only the table stretches touched by its windows (`slab_parts`; stretches separated by ≤ `GAP` = 64 peaks are read as one), at most `max_peaks` peaks, and always contains the whole window of the first feature, so the result does not depend on the slab (`sweep/tests.rs`: the same peaks as the filter on the whole table).
- `sweep.rs` in parallel: `windows_by_slab` gives the windows per slab in bulk; `chunked.rs::chunked` runs blocks on free cores (`spare::lease`, never in the browser) and returns the outcomes in order; each thread has its own cursor (`Slab::cursor`, `add_trace_cur`, after `prepare`). `quality::assess_with` measures rows in blocks of 64 (pure `row_quality`, `RowQ::apply` in order) and `resolve` collects pieces and multiple maxima in the same order.
- `ByScan` (slab grouped by scan, basis of the bisection) is filled in blocks of scans on free cores; `Slab::of_scan` gives the peaks of a scan in a window (the MS2 purity of `ms2.rs`). Identical tables with any number of threads.
- `quality.rs`: trace of each feature = sum of the peaks within ±6 ppm per scan, with 15 scans on each side; baseline and noise from the flanks (median, 1.4826 MAD, excluding points above half apex, never below a third of the intensity threshold); `area_win` = area of the trace within ±0.05 min of the apex; shape, symmetry from `peakq::quality`, interpolated FWHM, gaps. Good = shape ≥ 0.5, S/N ≥ 3, ≥ 5 points (`QParams`); `qreason` has the bits `Q_SHAPE`, `Q_SNR`, `Q_POINTS`.
- **Area of a row, `area_from` of `features.detect`**: `channel` (default of the unit profile) = Σy·dt of the points of the 4 ppm channel segment that found the row (`trace.rs`, dt = median interval); `window` (default at HR, `Recipe::default()`; `QParams::area_window`) = Σy·dt of the `quality.rs` trace between `scan_lo` and `scan_hi` (formula of the `resolve.rs` pieces).
  - `area_from` = `xic` (`QParams::area_xic`) = trapezoid of the same trace on the true RTs of the scans (`quality::trapezoid`), like the chromatogram XIC. In the `Debug` only if on; valid for every algorithm; measurements in `mzfeat-tests.md`.
- **Stitching of the pieces, `stitch` of `features.detect`**: `strict` (default of the unit profile) joins the segments of nearby channels if they overlap by half of the shorter one or if they touch and the lower exceeds 2e6 (`TOUCH_HEIGHT`); `wide` (default at HR; `Params::stitch_wide`, in the `Debug` only if on) joins those that touch at any height and keeps the segments from `min_height`/4 (rows stay ≥ `min_height`). At ±3 ppm (`flow_area_tests.rs`): `strict` splits 2 ions out of 6 (up to 0.94 log2), `wide` gives one row per ion within 0.045; at ±4 ppm `wide` splits too.
- `align.rs`: `align(&[&FileFeatures], &AlignParams) -> Aligned` ("per ion" mode, port of `features.align`): k-way merge of the tables (sorted by m/z) in file order = stable sort of the Python
- Slabs of `align`: chain of features with consecutive m/z jumps ≤ `ppm` (5), cut where it exceeds `max_ppm_span` (10 ppm) at the largest jump (at most 32 steps), sorted by RT and split where the RT jump exceeds `rt_gap` (0.1 min); optional `max_rt_span`; FT lobes (`FLAG_FT_LOBE`) are dropped (`drop_artefacts`). For each (group, file): maximum area and height, apex and width (right − left) of the largest feature; m/z and RT of the group weighted with the area of those. One slab at a time is kept.
- `table::Aligned`: `n_files`, `mz`/`rt` per row, and per cell (`row * n_files + file`) `area f64`, `height`/`apex_rt`/`width f32` (NaN where the file has no peak), `state u8` (`CELL_DETECTED`, `CELL_TARGETED` reserved, `CELL_FILLED`, `CELL_ABSENT`, `CELL_NA` = not measured, area NaN: `no_peak(state)` = absent or NA; presence is read from `state`, never from `area > 0`).
- `famalign.rs`: `align_families(&[&FileFeatures], &FamAlignParams) -> Aligned` ("per family", requires `family_id`, `role`, `charge`): (1) groups per ion as `align`; (2) RT map from those groups; (3) groups again with the corrected RT (`RtMap::reference`; `use_rtmap`)
- Merges of `align_families`: a group that follows a family root (same file, RT within `link_rt` 0.2 min, same charge) joins the group of the root it follows most often (one link per group); a group that is mostly the base of a family joins only if it was dependent in at least `min_dependent` (1) files; the merge is refused if the set exceeds `max_span` (0.5 min) of RT. Advanced parameters of `features.align`: `link_rt`, `max_span`, `min_dependent`.
- Rows of `align_families`: one per set of groups; the reference ion (first the groups that are mostly base, then more FILES in which it is root or lone ion (one vote per file), total area, index) gives the cells (`CELL_DETECTED` only with a peak); the other ions are the members (`member_start`, `member_mz`, `member_rt`, `member_role` = most frequent role; the first member is the reference). `Aligned::rows_near(mz, ppm)` searches among references and members; `Aligned::to_bytes()` is the basis of the SHA-256 for comparing two runs.
- `gapfill.rs`: `fill_file(&mut Index, &mut Aligned, file, Option<&RtMap>, &Params, &FillParams)`: the `CELL_ABSENT` cells of a file are integrated again at the intensity threshold (`floor` 2e4) over the m/z window (± 5 ppm) of the reference ion of the row, in slabs (`sweep::windows`),
- RT window of the filling: the apex must fall inside (with the map: median reference RT of the files that see the row, brought into the file, ± `rt_tol` 0.1 + 2 × rms of the file; without map: median apex ± half median width + `rt_tol`); same rule as `detect` (gaps ≤ 2, ≥ 5 points, traces > 400 scans dropped). The filled cell has `CELL_FILLED`, area > 0 and apex NaN: it never becomes `CELL_DETECTED`.
- Acquired RT: if the expected apex falls outside the RT of the file (`FileFacts::rt_range`) the cell is `CELL_NA`, not zero. `features.detect` `rt_range` `[min,max]` (`Recipe::rt_range`, for all files) keeps the rows with RT inside (`retain_rt`) before the filling.
- `recipe.rs`: `Recipe` (detection parameters, steps, families, `Mode::Ion|Family` mode, alignment, RT map, filling) with `file_text`/`file_hash` (16 hex digits: versions of the crate and of the rules + per-file parameters; the slab size does not count and alignment does not change the file fingerprint) and `table_text`/`table_hash` (32 digits: two-lane FNV-1a).
- `store.rs`: container `MZFEAT01` (magic, meta length u64, JSON meta, little-endian arrays at multiples of 64 bytes). The meta has format, versions, recipe, fingerprint, `inputs` (name and SHA-256 of the files), `times` (empty for identical bytes), shape and directory of the arrays; `evid` is reserved. `write_file`/`read_file` = `<sha256>.<fingerprint>.mzfeatf` (`FileFeatures`); `write_table`/`read_table` = `<series>.mzfeat` (`Aligned`). A damaged file gives `error.badMzfeat`. The `.mzfeat` is `version` 2 (`mzfeat-table.md`).
- `series.rs`: `analyse_file` (detection, quality, isotopes, MS2, families of a file), `align_and_fill` (alignment in the recipe mode, RT map, filling file by file with one index open at a time), `source_sha256`.
- `slabs::align_and_fill_progress(.., progress)`: like `align_and_fill_with` and calls `progress(done, total)` (first with done = 0): one unit per slice (one if in memory), per weak-tier file and per filled file (`prog.rs`); `run::align_to_par_progress` forwards it to the native callers. Identical tables with any callback.
- `weakfile.rs`: the weak candidates of a file (`series::weak_candidates`, at `WEAK_RATIO` of the minimum height) saved next to the `.mzfeatf` as `.mzfeatf.weak` by the step that detects the file (`run::detect_weak`, in parallel across files; on the site the `feat-worker.js` pool keeps them from `detect_weak_file_to` and passes them to `align_series_to`);
  - Alignment and filling read them instead of detecting again. The key `Recipe::weak_hash` (versions, detection, quality and isotope parameters) makes them "not stored" if the recipe changes or the file is missing: detection runs as before, same result.
- Cache of `flow run` (`flow_cache.rs`): a detection is taken from the cache only if its `.mzfeatf.weak` is there and readable (`weakfile::stored_key` and `from_bytes`), otherwise it is detected again (same bytes); a forgotten entry also removes its `.weak`; `index.json` does not change.
- `hypo.rs` (+ `hypo/rules.rs`, `hypo/sources.rs`): hypotheses of the targeted pass. `generate(&Rules, &Inputs, &HypoParams) -> Registry`: ions to search for, each with the source label (`SRC_*` bits): (a) parent × rules in 1-2 steps (one hypothesis per net formula change, fewer steps and then first label; class rules apply only if the parent has the group), cleavages given as formulas (`fragments`: no fragments are derived from the elements alone);
- Sources of `hypo`, cleavage fragments: with 2 steps, one rule reaction on each cleavage fragment (`from_fragment_products`: label `rottura+regola`, 2 steps, only rules without groups, because the known groups are those of the parent).
- Sources of `hypo`, suspects index: (b) suspects index: entries with a reaction in Transformations in PubChem and a formula derivable from the parent (no extra element, O up to +3, H up to +4), the most cited up to `max_suspects` (500, the rest is counted in `suspects_cut`);
- Sources of `hypo`, families and lists: (c) expected members of the families found (`FamilyBase`: M+1, M+2, [M+NH4]+, [M+Na]+, [M+K]+, in negative [M+Cl]-/[M+HCOO]-, and with `other_polarity` the molecule in the other polarity) searched at the RT of the base (`rt_ref`); (d) imported lists (`Imported`: name and formula; a SMILES is converted by the caller).
- Order and ids: by m/z, adduct, formula, label; two hypotheses with the same formula and adduct are merged (sources in OR, other labels in `alternatives`). `Registry::to_json()` (`ipotesi/1`: tried, found, cut, per hypothesis id, sources, label, formula, mass, m/z, adduct, steps, level, `rt_ref`, outcome) is the basis of the SHA-256 between two runs; `Outcome` (`NotSearched`, `NotFound`, `Found(rows)`) is filled by `target`. A single run, never a loop until convergence.
- `target.rs` (+ `target/extract.rs`, `target/null.rs`): targeted pass. `run(&mut [Index], &[&FileFeatures], &mut Registry, &TargetParams) -> TargetResult`: for each hypothesis, in all files, XIC at ±5 ppm with S/N ≥ 3 in place of the minimum height, shape ≥ 0.5, ≥ 5 points, in the RT window of the rule (`window`: more polar = before the parent, less polar = after, family member = RT of the base ±0.1; without `parent_rt` the whole run).
- Rows of `target`: an already detected peak stays `CELL_DETECTED` ("predicted by" proof via `TargetResult::evidence`), otherwise the best accepted one is `CELL_TARGETED` and receives its MS2 (`ms2::link`). The `Aligned` and `table.rs` tables do not change: `to_aligned()` gives the rows found.
- Cautions of `target`: `TargetRow::level_cap()` = `Some(4)` if no cell is detected and no MS2 (read by E1); `Registry.outcomes` (`NotFound`/`Found(n)`) and `tried`/`found` say "searched N hypotheses, found K"; `coincidence` = probability that random ions (`null`: 64 decoys at masses ±0.0236 Da from the hypotheses, rate per decoy per minute per file) give ≥ n hits in the window (Poisson per file, exact Poisson-binomial tail across files).
- Determinism of `target` (like the rest of the crate: `libm`, no HashMap, order by mass then rule id): `to_bytes()` identical at every round. Test: `tests/target.rs` (series from `tools/feat_mirato.py`, skipped without python/numpy).
- Measurements of the targeted pass (V1, `cargo run --release -p mzlab-feat --example targeted -- FILE.mzidx... --truth verita.json --esche [--oracle] [--extra N]`; scaled products with `tools/feat_mirato.py --scala`): with the rules alone the decoys with a TARGETED row are 0.5 %, with row and MS2 0 %; weak products (peak < 2e5) go from 9.3 % to 14.0 % (scale 0.01); with the true masses as imported (`--oracle`, extraction sensitivity) 81.4 % of the weak ones (+33.2 % of the products). 5528 hypotheses on 13 files: ~2.8 s native.
- Rules: `TP_Mine/data/regole_tp_2.json` (`regole_tp/2`, profiles and groups, hand-written: format in `mzfeat-tp.md`; the only rules file, also for Dig, network and mzFinder; a `/1` is still read); unknown format or version = `error.hypo.rulesFormat`/`error.hypo.rulesVersion`; the RT direction is provisional (chemist's curation).
- `rtmap.rs`, `rtwarp.rs` (`warp_map`), `quality::keep_row`/`reason_text`: `docs/agenti/mzfeat-broad.md`.
- `isotopes.rs`: in the file, before alignment, a peak is an isotopologue of a lighter one at +1.003355/z (13C) or +2/z (13C2, 34S, 37Cl, 81Br), charge 1-3, ±5 ppm, apex within 0.05 min, `area_win` ratio plausible for an averagine of the same mass (`isoscore::fine_pattern`: M+1/M between 0.25 and 2.5 times the expected, M+2/M between 0.2 times the expected and 1.2). `iso_group` = position of the lightest member + 1 (0 = alone), `FLAG_ISOTOPE` on the isotopologues; a group has a single charge. Never drops.
- `ms2.rs`: `link` gives `Ms2Links` (CSR on the rows: `start`, `scan` = positions in `Index::scans`, `purity f32`). An MS2 belongs to the peak if its source MS1 (`Scan::parent`) lies between the peak edges widened by the trigger margin (`margin_s`, see below) and the m/z of the peak is in the isolation window (also as co-isolated); purity = intensity of the peak (±6 ppm) in that MS1 / total in the window. Below 0.5 the MS2 proof is weak (not dropped). Without a window in the index: ±0.6 around the precursor and `FLAG_PURITY_EST`.
- `ms2.rs`, trigger margin: `margin_s` of `features.ms2_link` (seconds) widens on both sides the RT interval of the peak for matching the MS2; 0 = one MS1 cycle of the file (median distance between MS1), no margin with fewer than two MS1.
- `family.rs` (+ `family/`): ion families per file, before alignment. `analyse_families(index, &mut f, &Ms2Links, &Params, &FamParams) -> Families` = MS2 spectra (`spectra`, ≤ 40 strong peaks per scan, scan of maximum purity), candidate pairs (`pairs`: steps of the `rel` tables plus dimer `[2M+H]+`/`[2M-H]-` plus fragments read in the MS2 of the heavier one; co-elution within max(0.05, 0.5 × smaller FWHM) min), Pearson correlation of the traces (`traces`, r ≥ 0.9, ≥ 5 points), proofs (`evidence`, port of `edge_evidence`), roles (`roles`) and `Families::apply`.
- `famrecord.rs`: `record(file, &FileFeatures, &Families) -> String` gives `famiglia/1` (per family: `size`, members with m/z, RT, role and parent row, M first; per edge delta m/z and RT, correlation, readings of the step and proofs with value, threshold, points and sign). Only numbers and words, same bytes for the same input.
- `FamParams::orient` (`family::Orient`): `Evidence` (default) lets the proof orient the definitive edges by itself (adduct with exact step, co-elution and correlation; ISF found in the MS2 of the parent), while an edge that two readings explain keeps the factor; `Factor` requires the dependent of an edge to be ≥ `parent_factor` times weaker than the parent (example `families --orient factor`). The `Debug` of `FamParams` always prints `orient`, so the recipe fingerprints depend on the choice.
- Pair = light ion X, heavy Y: Y adduct (Na, K, NH4, ACN; in negative FA, Cl, Na-2H) or dimer of X, or X in-source fragment of Y (neutral loss of `LOSSES`, 23 names, or any loss if X appears in the MS2 of Y). +17.0265 (NH4 / −NH3) and +46.0055 (formate / −HCOOH) stay open: `ALT_*` bits of `alt` and `Families::alternatives`. The dependent must be ≥ `parent_factor` (2.0) times weaker than the parent, otherwise the edge stays in `arcs` but gives no roles.
- An ISF without a ratio between samples (`Ratio::Unavailable`) is "undetermined"; with a stable `Ratio::Over{n,cv}` (cv ≤ 0.30, ≥ 3 samples) it becomes ISF.
- Columns of `FileFeatures`: `family_id` (row of M + 1; 0 = none), `role` (0 none, 1 M, 2 isotope, 3 adduct, 4 ISF, 5 dimer, 6 undetermined), `alt`; `FAMILY_VERSION` and `PROOF_VERSION` enter `Families`. Cost O(n log n) by binary search on the table sorted by m/z.
- Proofs and identity graph (`evid.rs`, `evid/`): `docs/agenti/mzfeat-tests.md`.
- Kinetics as proof (`kinetic.rs`, `kinetic/`, `kinetic::stability`, `parent_kinetics`, `kinetic_report`, `POINTS_MAX`, `points_if_weighed`): `docs/agenti/mzfeat-broad.md`.

## Determinism and WASM
- A slab ends only where two consecutive peaks above the threshold are ≥ 50 ppm apart (no trace crosses that jump: channels are 4 ppm), so the result does not depend on the slab size. The rows of the two grids are collected per grid and not per slab, so the input order too is independent of the slabs.
- `ln` and `log1p` from `libm` (pure Rust); `f64` sums in fixed order; no `HashMap`; stable sorts. Memory is reserved with `try_reserve` and the error is `error.memoryBudget`, without panic. Working tables proportional to the data are born with `trace_base::filled(n, v)` (`try_reserve` + fill) or `trace_base::reserve`; `vec![0; n]` and `unwrap_or_default` only at fixed size, with its comment; `align`, `align_grouped`, `align_families`, `isotopes::group`, `family::build_families` and `tp::explain_by_arcs` return `Result`.
- Dependencies: `mzlab-core` and `libm`. `cargo check --target wasm32-unknown-unknown -p mzlab-feat` and the graph check (no `memmap2`, `zstd-sys`, `libz-sys`, `cc`) are in `rust.yml`.

## Example `examples/feat.rs`
`cargo run --release -p mzlab-feat --example feat -- FILE.mzidx... [--neg] [--budget-mb N] [--analyse] [--tsv DIR]` prints per file time, memory peak (`VmHWM`, one process per file), number of features and SHA-256 of the table (same SHA with different memory budgets). The measurements are in `measurements.md`.
`--analyse` runs the whole pipeline and prints times per step and counts (`--no-split` skips the maximum splitting), `--tsv DIR` writes the table; `python3 tools/valida/picchi.py DIR_VERITA DIR_TSV` compares it with the truth of the synthetics of `sintetici_identita.py`.

## Example `examples/perf_batch8.rs`
`cargo run --release -p mzlab-feat --example perf_batch8 [N_FEATURE] [N_FILE]` measures the best time and the SHA-256 of the output of alignment, normalization, isotopes, RT drift (LOESS and RANSAC) and deconvolution; the same SHA before and after a change proves the output did not change.

## Pitfalls
- The `.mzidx` of a high-resolution MS1 keeps m/z and intensity in `f32` (`hr1`): `each_peak` widens them to `f64`, as Python does with `astype(float64)`; the thresholds (2e4, 2e5) are exact in `f32`, so the comparisons coincide.
- The sum of a cell or segment is sequential; `np.add.reduceat` sums pairwise above 8 elements: the difference is below 1e-15 relative and areas are compared at 1e-4.
- The channel of a peak is `floor(ln(m/z) / ln(1 + 4 ppm) + offset)`: `libm` and `numpy` may differ in the last bit and move a peak to the edge of a channel; in the data tested it never happened.
- `tools/controlla.py` rejects files over 500 lines and functions over 80: this is why the crate has three modules and `stitch` is split into `link` and `join`.
- **CentWave** (`centwave.rs`, `Params::peak`, `centwave` algorithm of `features.detect`): each merged trace is redone as a per-scan series in the `ppm` window and cut into peaks with Ricker wavelets on `peak_width_s` (default 5-30 s); two apexes are two peaks if the valley is below 90 % of the lower one; it stays if height above the base ≥ `snr` × noise (never below `floor`). The default does not appear in the recipe; `algorithm` travels in the `layers`.
- **`algorithm` slot of `features.detect`**: `min_points`, `max_gap`, `long_trace` and `merge_overlap` are common parameters of the step (valid for every algorithm, `--set det.min_points=…` also with `centwave`); the browser recipe text (`job_layers_text`, `recipeOf` of `flow-web-run.js`) writes `algorithm` and the preset values in the layer as `flow run` does. Test: `flow_detect_algorithm_tests.rs` (default byte for byte) and `e2e_flow_detect_algorithm.py` (browser = native with `centwave`).
- **`max_gap` of `features.detect@2`**: scans a trace may skip between two points (0 = consecutive, default 1), like `features.gapfill` (default 2). A plan or preset `features.detect@1` (which counted the distance: 1 = consecutive) is converted on opening (`flow::migrate`, `from_only`): N → N − 1, at least 0; without a value the default stays (same traces). A `--set` applies after the conversion, so in the sense of `@2`. Tests: `migrate_tests.rs`, `flow_max_gap_tests.rs` (same bytes in `flow run`, desktop route and browser layer; route `flow/line` = command line).
- **Smeared peaks** (`centwave.rs`, `untargeted/resolve.rs`): `CentWave::width_s` (`peak_width_s`, default 5-30 s) and resolver duration `MinParams::duration` (`max_duration`, default 0-1 min) are configurable; at default the output is identical.
- **Detection timings** (`prof.rs`): with `MZLAB_PHASES=1` the plan prints on stderr, together with the `tp.link` phases, the seconds (summed over files and threads) of "detect: traces", "quality + resolve", "isotopes", "ms2 links", "mixtures", "families" and "weak candidates"; no clock in the `.wasm`.
- `broad.rs`, `broad_gt.rs`: broad bands + G(t), off: `mzfeat-broad.md`.
