# Tests and goldens of the `mol/1` record (`crates/mzlab-mol`, `mzlab-feat` `chem/`)

Moved from `mol.md`: what each test file compares with RDKit, the golden data, the intended limits and the OpenChemLib trap. The record, its functions and the rules are in `mol.md`; read this file only to change a test or regenerate a golden file.

## Tests (`tests/record.rs`, `tests/data/golden.json`)
- Golden data from RDKit: `python3 tools/struttura_dato/confronto.py --golden crates/mzlab-mol/tests/data/golden.json` (needs `pip install rdkit` only to regenerate them). For the 20 molecules of the study: record with atoms in written order and in RDKit canonical order, CXSMILES, InChIKey, SHA-256 of the core computed in Python.
- The test rereads each record, rewrites it (same JSON), compares the hash with Python's (20/20 × 2 orders). Traps tested: unknown stereo ≠ no stereo (different hashes), S ≠ R ≠ unknown ≠ racemate, layers outside the core do not change the hash, broken records rejected.
- SMILES reader (`tests/smiles.rs`): the 20 molecules and 80 more cases (aromatics, radicals, charges, isotopes, `@` in every position, `/` `\` also in rings, groups `a`/`&`/`o`) compared with RDKit: same atoms, same bonds (in aromatic systems the Kekulé choice may differ: sums per atom are compared), same `defined` stereo elements, same groups.
- `composti.csv`: the 625 rows (atoms, number of bonds and defined stereo equal to RDKit, digits per row in the golden data); 286 rows with stereo elements and 111 with at least one unspecified, counted from the golden data.
- Intended limit: the reader does not decide whether an atom without a sign could be stereogenic (`with_stereo_sites` does), so it never creates `unknown` elements; it keeps an `@` even on a non-stereogenic centre, which RDKit cleans.
- Writer: every golden record (no `unknown` elements) and the 625 rows, rewritten and reread, equal the record renumbered in text order; a rewrite gives the same text. `renumbered` with RDKit's canonical permutation gives RDKit's canonical record (`tests/record.rs`). Trap: explicit `[H]` stay atoms (4 `refs`, `implicitH` false), so the hash differs from the one with implicit H.
- Molfile reader (`tests/molfile.rs`, `tests/data/golden_molfile.json`, generated by `confronto.py --golden-molfile`): 92 molecules in V2000/V3000 with wedges, with type 4 bonds and without wedges, compared with RDKit (atoms, bonds modulo Kekulé, `defined` elements, V3000 groups); the sign of the wedge parity is fixed by this data. A molfile without wedges gives no `defined` centre (RDKit marks it `unknown`: `with_stereo_sites` does it).
- Limit: the NH tautomers of an aromatic system without a valence field are not recoverable (the number of H is compared). Double bond with the "either" sign → `unknown`; without a sign the configuration comes from the drawn coordinates (like RDKit).
- OpenChemLib trap: with an explicit `[H]` OpenChemLib removes the atom and reorders the others (e.g. `C[C@]([H])(O)CC`), so its labels do not match the written order; the reader instead keeps the received order (`ocl_ordine.mjs --json` gives the golden data).
- Stereogenicity (`tests/stereo.rs`, `tests/data/golden_stereo.json` from `confronto.py --golden-stereo`): the sites of 717 SMILES (20 + 72 + 625 rows of composti.csv) coincide with RDKit's `FindPotentialStereo` except 3 exceptions listed in the test: the azide N=N+=N- (linear, no cis/trans) and two trialkyl phosphates with three equal arms (they have only two arm types, so two always coincide: the phosphorus is not stereogenic).
- An imino N-H is a site but stays `unknown` (no neighbour to name in a defined element). The sites of the 92 molfiles coincide with RDKit's `defined`/`unknown` states (also without wedges).
- Aromaticity (`tests/stereo.rs`): the aromatic bonds of the 717 SMILES coincide with RDKit's (golden data `golden_stereo.json`, third column) and 9 textbook rings (benzene, quinone, tropylium, azulene, pyrrole, ...) have the expected count.
- Canonical order (`tests/stereo.rs`): each SMILES and its version with the sites keep canonical JSON and `can` under three random permutations; enantiomers, E/Z, defined/unknown and ring cis/trans differ in `can`.
- Size rules: file ≤ 500 lines, functions ≤ 80 (`tools/controlla.py`).
- View for the engines (`crates/mzlab-feat/src/chem/graph_record.rs`, feature `record` of `mzlab-feat`): `grafo::Mol::from_record(&rec, aromatic)` builds the mzFinder graph from the record: atoms and H of the record, bonds ordered by pair; with `aromatic` the rings perceived by `aromatic_bonds()` take the flag and order 1,5, without it they stay Kekulé. Isotopes and radicals do not enter; like `parse` it rejects elements without a normal valence and more than 200 atoms, accepts detached pieces.
- Test `tests/grafo_record.rs` (`--features record`, line in `rust.yml`): same graph as `parse` and `from_molfile` on study cases, extras, composti.csv and molfiles (set of bonds). The V2000 reader of `grafo::Mol` does not know radicals and adds the charge's H shift to that of the valence: the record is right and the test allows the difference on the H of charged or radical atoms only.
- `grafo::Mol::from_molfile`, `cut/1`, `mech/1` and `selection/1` (`mol sel`) read V2000 text; a V3000 file is first written as V2000 by `chem/molfile_read.rs` (own small reader: atoms with CHG/RAD/MASS/VAL/CFG, bonds 1-4 with CFG, `MRV_IMPLICIT_H` groups, counts line checked; no `mzlab-mol` reader, so `grafo.wasm` grows ~16 KB raw, not 60).
  Above `MAX_ATOMS` (200) it is `error.grafo.tooBig`; a file cut short or without atoms is `error.grafo.molfile`: never an empty molecule. Anything the graph cannot hold is refused by name with `error.grafo.molfile`: bond types above 4, COLLECTION, RGROUP, LINKNODE, other blocks, every SGROUP but `MRV_IMPLICIT_H`, other atom or bond keywords, atom maps, stray lines.
  Tests: `chem/sel_v3000_tests.rs`, `mzlab-cli/src/chem_v3000_tests.rs`, `tests/test_feat_grafo_wasm.py`.
- Consumers on the record (feature `record`, in no `.wasm`): `posiz/1` (`positions_record_json`, `docs/agenti/positions.md`) and `frag/1`/`parz/1` (`frammenti::fragments_record_json`, `partial_record_json`: cuts ordered by atom pair, perceived aromaticity; test `tests/frammenti_record.rs` on the V2000 molfiles of the golden data). `interp/1` (`docs/agenti/interp.md`) and `mol3d_from_record` (`docs/agenti/reactivity.md`).
- Descriptors (`src/descr.rs`, `crippen.rs`, `crippen_tab.rs`; Wildman-Crippen 1999 tables by hand): `Mol::crippen_logp()` (graph types, implicit + explicit H), `tpsa()` (Ertl, N and O only), `hbd()` (N/O with H), `hba()` (O and N with a lone pair, our definition), `molecular_weight()` (None if a weight is missing), `descriptors()` = all at once (`logp`, `mr` the Crippen molar refractivity, `tpsa`, `hbd`, `hba`, `rotors` the RDKit "strict" rotatable bonds, `mw`).
- Test `tests/descr.rs`: 45 molecules against RDKit values (logP, MR, TPSA, rotors, MW); the 625 rows of `TP_Mine/data/composti.csv` match. Crippen logP is not the measured one (caffeine −1.03 versus −0.07).
- `descriptors/1` (`chem/descriptors.rs`): `descriptors()` + `analysis/1` of a V2000/V3000 molfile: ONE function for mzDraw, `molkit`, `feat/grafo`, `mol measure`; parity `tests/dati/descriptors_disegno.json`.
- Transformations `trasf/1` (`src/transform.rs`, `transform_chain.rs`, `delta.rs`; spec `docs/studies/TRANSFORMATION_RECORD.md`): `Transformation` = parent → product with the atom map on the canonical indices of the two `mol/1`, identified by the core hash and never copied. `from_json`/`to_json` (fields in the order of the spec, `level` and `stereo_rule` optional), `validate()` without molecules, `validate_against(parent, product)` with the hashes, sizes, elements (equal except at the centre) and the stereo rule.
- Certain product: `product` + complete `map` (one `[parent atom, product atom or null]` per atom, in order) + `added` + `centre`, without `sites` or `region`. Uncertain product (the change only): no `product`, `map`, `added`, `centre`; `sites` (distinct atoms, p in (0, 1]) and a `region` that includes them. A `product` is never given to an uncertain TP.
- Stereo: `touched_stereo(parent)` gives the parent's elements on the `centre`; the product does not have them `defined` unless `stereo_rule` is `retention` or `inversion` (test in `tests/transform.rs`). `product_atom`/`parent_atom` follow an atom along the map; `compose(next)` composes two certain steps (map, `added`, `centre`, delta; a lost atom stays lost; the evidence of the first parent and the second product stays); `canonical(parent, product)` rewrites everything on the canonical order and the hashes of the renumbered records.
- `delta` (`mzlab_mol::delta`): `+O`, `-H2+O`, `-Cl+OH` as signed element counts; `add` sums two deltas and writes them in a single form (gains then losses, Hill order). No consumer outside `mzlab-mol` uses `trasf/1` (interp, Draw and posiz/1 read the `grafo::Mol` view or the record); the crate stays out of every `.wasm`.
