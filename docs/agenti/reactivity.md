# Parent reactivity in Tabui: 3D viewer and panel (`viewer3d.js`)
Administrator mode only (`uipAdminOn()`); texts in English without `lang/` keys. Validation on experimental sites, protomer and discarded experiments: `docs/agenti/reactivity-studies.md`. Every value is "Prev." (prediction for the isolated molecule in implicit water): no Schymanski level, no `posiz/1` class reordered or removed.
- **Contract** `reatt/1` (study §8): `atoms[{i, el, heavy_index|null, x, y, z, q, f_plus, f_zero, f_minus, dual}]`, `bonds[{a,b,order}]` and `hbonds[{donor_h,acceptor}]` refer to the field `i`; `global`, `conformers`, `scc`, `notes`, `cite` optional. Optional panel field: `ch_sites[{class, heavy_index, n_h, f_minus, rank, tie_first}]` (C–H sites per symmetry class; the table appears only if present).
- **Form** (`protomer`, optional): `{ph, smiles, charge, changes[{group, heavy_index, action, pka}], note}`, present only with `protomer:"ph7"`; the "Info" tab shows Form, Total charge, Protonation changes and the note.
- **`TPREATT`** (`reattivita.js`): `mount(host, reatt)` draws the panel (false without administrator: no node); `state(host, {state:"waiting"|"computing"|"failed", error_key?, message?})` shows "waiting for TPs", "computing…" or "failed: …" (message from the `error.chem.*` key); `xyz(r)`, `csv(r)`, `mapHeavy(mol, r)`, `svg2d(...)` exposed for tests. Hook-in: `20_tpmine.js::structure` puts `#tp-reatt` under the structure of the "Parent compound" box (state "waiting for TPs" if the SMILES is present, administrator only); computation and `mount`: `TPREATTAUTO` (section "Automatic start").
- **Tabs**: "f(−)" (default), "f(0)", "f(+)" (2D with halos, 3D model, threshold, table), "Info" (method, solvent, engine, SMILES, conformers, HOMO/LUMO/gap, IP, EA, μ, η, ω, SCC convergence), "Notes" (fixed limits: "not evidence", f(0) fails on deactivated rings, TiO2, no level; then `reatt.notes`), "JSON" (`reatt/1` read-only). Tabs with arrows, Home, End.
- **Threshold**: slider and numeric field linked (0-0.15, step 0.005, default 0.05); atoms with a value ≥ threshold have a thick outline and the written index (2D and 3D) and the table row highlighted with "▲". Index shown: 1-based among the heavy atoms (as "Substitution position"), `H<i>` for hydrogens. The per-atom table is sortable (buttons in the headers) and every figure has its own `TDATI`.
- **2D**: OpenChemLib coordinates (`Molecule.fromSmiles`), drawn by the panel in SVG to keep the halos aligned; the order of OpenChemLib's heavy atoms matches `heavy_index` (a removed test on paracetamol and carbamazepine); if the graph does not match, `mapHeavy` looks for the correspondence by element and neighbors.
- **Viewer** `TPV3D.create(host, {atoms, bonds, hbonds, label})` (`viewer3d.js`): Canvas 2D, spheres and sticks sorted by depth, halo per atom (radius and opacity ∝ value of the tab), dashed H bonds, H switch. Buttons ← → ↑ ↓ (15°), + −, "Reset view"; same keys with the canvas focused (`+ − 0 Home H`); dragging is only an addition; no automatic rotation. View state in the canvas `data-*` (`yaw`, `pitch`, `zoom`, `showH`, `over`). Colors from `--v3-*` variables (Okabe-Ito, light and dark theme).
- **Exports** with `window.dl`: `.xyz` (N+2 lines, comment with method, engine and SMILES), `.smi`, `.json` (`reatt/1`), `.csv` (per-atom table). "How to cite" lists `reatt.cite` (texts and DOI).
- **Loading**: the three files (`viewer3d.js`, `reattivita.js`, `reatt-auto.js`) sit in `TP_Mine/lazy/` (packed in `static/mzfinder/lazy/`, names in `indice.json` under `lazy`), not in the Tabui startup; `TPLAZY.load("viewer3d.js", "reattivita.js", "reatt-auto.js")` (in `tpmine-loader.js`) runs them once at the first parent structure (`reattPanel` in `20_tpmine.js`). a removed test fail if they return to `js/`.

## Automatic start
- **When**: `20_tpmine.js::reattPanel` loads the three files only with `uipAdminOn()` (without administrator it downloads and computes nothing) and calls `TPREATTAUTO.attach(host, smiles)`; the "Dig" handler calls `tpsStart()` at the start, `tpsDone()` when the TPs are finished (LR and HR) and `tpsEnd()` if the Dig ends with an error or is canceled (then nothing starts). With TPs finished and a valid SMILES the computation starts with `requestIdleCallback` (timeout 3 s, fallback 400 ms).
- **TPs take precedence**: `tpsStart()` stops the computation (worker terminated, late replies discarded by the generation number) and puts back "waiting for TPs"; "Recompute" is disabled while the TPs run. One computation at a time; a new request cancels the queued or running one. Changing the parent SMILES stops the computation and returns the panel to "waiting".
- **Worker** (`chem-worker.js`, module, created from a Blob of `QTOOLS.ctx.files`): at the first message it downloads the `chem` `.wasm` from the manifest (`wasm-feat/manifest.json`, entry `chem`, SHA-256 verified), then calls `reactivity(smiles, opts)`; it replies `{ok, json, version, ms}` or `{error:{error_key, params}}`. The worker does not live between one canceled request and the next: it is recreated. The engine does not give the steps (geometry, neutral, cation, anion): the state is only "computing…".
- **Session cache**: key = OpenChemLib canonical SMILES + the engine's `version()` + solvent + conformers + form; no durable data and no new database name.
- **Bar** (`#tp-reatt-ctl`, under the panel): state (`role="status"`: "waiting for TPs", "computing…", "done in X s", "done (kept from this session)", "failed"), `Solvent` (Water / None), `Conformers` (1-16, default 4), `Form` ("As drawn" default, "Most probable at pH 7"), "Recompute" and, after an error, "Retry". Texts in English without `lang/` keys.
- **Desktop**: same sequence with `pool.reactivity(smiles, opts)` of `feat-desktop-pool.js` (route `feat/grafo`, `kind:"reactivity"`), one native call at a time; a native call cannot be stopped, if canceled its reply is discarded.
- **Measurement** (38 synthetic files, Chromium, minimum of 2 Digs): without reactivity 1630 ms, with reactivity armed 1706 ms (+76 ms, no computation runs during the TPs). `chem` `.wasm` after `wasm-opt -Oz`: 568,809 B.

## Engine: 3D geometry (`crates/mzlab-chem`)
- **Purpose**: from a SMILES to reliable 3D coordinates, in Rust, for the reactivity engine (section "Engine: GFN2-xTB"). Geometry in `crates/mzlab-chem/src/`: `mol3d.rs`, `embed.rs`, `lengths.rs`, `minimize.rs`.
- **Numbering**: `Mol3d::from_mol(&grafo::Mol)` (does not use sci-form's SMILES reader): the heavy atoms in the order of `grafo::Mol` (= `heavy_index` of `reatt/1` and `posiz/1`), then the H of each heavy atom in turn. Fields: `el` (atomic numbers), `xyz`, `bonds` (a, b, order; 4 = aromatic), `heavy_index`, `charge`, `arom`; `to_xyz(comment)`. Elements: H C N O F P S Cl Br I; others give `error.chem.element` (`{error, error_key, params}`).
- **From the `mol/1` record** (feature `record` of `mzlab-chem`, in no `.wasm`): `mol3d_record::mol3d_from_record(&mzlab_mol::Mol)` = `from_mol(Mol::from_record(rec, true))`, same atoms, H, charges, aromatic flags and bonds (as a set: the record orders them by pair); a Kekulé parent takes the flags of its lowercase twin. Test: `tests/mol3d_record.rs`.
- **Geometry**: `embed(&Mol3d, seed, n) -> Result<Vec<Conf>>` calls sci-form 0.15.2's ETKDG (exact dependency `=0.15.2`, MIT, `default-features=false`; license in `LICENSE-sci-form` and `LICENZE-TERZI.md`) with seeds `seed + k*997`, regularizes the bond lengths and sorts by increasing energy. Text to use: "ETKDG geometry, no force-field refinement". `DEFAULT_CONFORMERS` = 4, `n` stays a parameter. If no conformer succeeds: `error.chem.embed` with `reason`.
- **Why not MMFF94**: sci-form 0.15.2's `Mmff94Builder` is not a real MMFF94 (angles at 109.5° with k = 0.5, generic torsions and vdW): with that relaxation the aromatic bonds come out at 1.53 Å and the median RMSD vs RDKit rises to 0.72 Å (UFF: 0.83; pure ETKDG: 0.32). A real MMFF94 remains a future optional package.
- **Regularization** (`embed.rs::regularize`, `lengths.rs`): every bond is pulled to the ideal length (textbook values in `lengths.rs`: aromatic 1.40, C-H 1.09, nitro N-O 1.23, amides and anilines 1.35-1.40...) with a weak spring that keeps the atoms close to the ETKDG position (L-BFGS of `minimize.rs`); it is not a force field (no angles, torsions, non-bonded). The energy of `Conf` is a single-point UFF, only to sort the conformers.
- **Reference**: `tools/cc_reference.py` (RDKit ETKDGv3 + MMFF94, seed 42, and tblite GFN2 charges) writes `crates/mzlab-chem/tests/data/cc_ref.json` (20 public compounds, versions and command in `meta`). Tests: `cargo test --release -p mzlab-chem` (`tests/numbering.rs`, `tests/geom.rs`).
- **Outcome (seed 42, 4 conformers)**: all 20 compounds succeed; no distance between non-bonded atoms < 0.9 Å; maximum bond deviation from RDKit ≤ 0.078 Å (limit 0.1); same seed = same geometry; heavy-atom RMSD after Kabsch (with mirror image) of the best conformer: median on the rigid ones 0.039 Å (threshold 0.5), on all 0.325 Å.
- **RMSD per compound** (Å, best conformer / minimum-energy conformer): rigid: phenol 0.011/0.011, aniline 0.036/0.039, toluene 0.021/0.024, chlorobenzene 0.016/0.023, naphthalene 0.040/0.040, nitrobenzene 0.039/0.039, caffeine 0.190/0.196, benzoic acid 0.067/0.067.
- **RMSD of the flexible ones** (do not block; Å, best conformer / minimum energy): anisole 0.025/0.025, bisphenol A 0.221/0.221, benzophenone-3 0.325/0.504, sulfamethoxazole 0.364/1.184, carbamazepine 0.430/0.855, atrazine 0.475/1.329, metoprolol 0.512/1.800, triclosan 0.532/1.258, diclofenac 0.535/1.344, paracetamol 0.665/0.665, ibuprofen 0.820/1.529, imidacloprid 0.846/1.560. For the flexible ones the comparison depends on which conformer comes out, not on the quality of the bonds.
- **Maximum bond deviation from RDKit** (Å): phenol 0.011, toluene 0.010, aniline 0.007, chlorobenzene 0.019, triclosan 0.019, naphthalene 0.016, anisole 0.022, benzoic 0.027, benzophenone-3 0.028, diclofenac 0.028, ibuprofen 0.031, paracetamol 0.032, atrazine 0.033, metoprolol 0.036, sulfamethoxazole 0.037, bisphenol A 0.042, carbamazepine 0.050, caffeine 0.061, nitrobenzene 0.068, imidacloprid 0.078.
- **Native time** (release, one CPU of the container): paracetamol (20 atoms) 52-66 ms with 4 conformers and 115-121 ms with 8; diclofenac (30 atoms) 153-158 ms with 4 and 285-311 ms with 8. The study's expectation (≤ 50 ms for 30 atoms and 8 conformers) is not reached: the work runs in the background (B4).
- **Trap**: `grafo` calls a bond aromatic when both atoms are written aromatic, RDKit perceives the rings (caffeine's six-membered ring is not aromatic for RDKit): in the numbering test the orders are compared only where neither says aromatic.

## Engine: GFN2-xTB, Fukui and `reatt/1` (`crates/mzlab-chem`, `chem` `.wasm`)
- **Method**: GFN2-xTB in `src/xtb/` (`basis.rs`, `aes.rs`, `scc.rs`, `alpb.rs`, `sasa.rs`, `water.rs`, `mod.rs`) with sci-form 0.15.2's parameters (`gfn2_params`, MIT), checked number by number against dxtb 0.4.0's `gfn2-xtb.toml` (Apache-2.0) for H C N O F P S Cl Br I (`tests/parameters_gfn2.rs`). Only difference: `mprad` of Br (3.9 in sci-form, 4.0 in dxtb); 4.0 is used (bromobenzene charges as tblite within 6e-6, with 3.9 at 4e-4).
- **SCC**: `System::new(el, xyz, solvent)` builds once the integrals, H0, γ, multipoles, D4 and ALPB matrix, common to neutral, cation and anion; `solve(charge, guess)`: Fermi at 300 K per spin channel (α = ⌈n/2⌉, β = ⌊n/2⌋, no spin polarization, as tblite with `uhf = nel % 2`), Broyden (damping 0.4; if the residual blows up it restarts from the best with halved damping), at most 250 iterations, `converged` always in the output. The ions start from the neutral; no environment variable.
- **Water (ALPB)**: Onufriev-Bashford-Case Born radii, P16 kernel and shape term as dxtb 0.4.0 and tad-mctc 0.9.2 (Apache-2.0); water parameters for GFN2 (Born scale 1.474, probe radius 1.134 Å, descreening and H-bond strength per element) from the public ChemRxiv supplement of Ehlert et al. 2021 (`param_alpb2_water.txt`, in `xtb/water.rs` with the source).
- **Solvent H bond**: −c²·(exposed fraction)·q² on the diagonal of the ALPB matrix; exposed fraction from the accessible surface (`sasa.rs`: D3 van der Waals spheres + probe, smooth step of 0.3 Å, Fibonacci sphere of 230 points). Surface tension (CDS) and the constant shift are excluded: they do not depend on the charges, so they change neither charges nor Fukui. Text in the notes: "ALPB for GFN2-xTB (Ehlert et al. 2021 water parameters)".
- **Fukui** (`fukui.rs`): for each atom, H included, f(+) = q(N) − q(N+1), f(−) = q(N−1) − q(N), f(0) = mean, dual = f(+) − f(−) (Mulliken charges). C–H sites (`ch_sites`): unsaturated C with H, mean of f(−) over the class of `posizioni::symmetry_classes`, in decreasing order with `rank`; `tie_first` if the gap from the first is < 0.005. f(−) also on the 3 lowest conformers: `f_minus_spread` = maximum difference of an atom.
- **EHT fallback** (`eht.rs`, sci-form's extended Hückel): only if one of the three SCCs does not converge; `method:"EHT"`, fixed note `eht::NOTE`, `q` and `f_zero` null, `scc` `{converged:[true,false,false], iterations:[1,0,0]}`. `PUBLISH_GFN2 = true` in `react.rs`.
- **`reactivity_json(smiles, opts)`** (`react.rs`): opts `{"solvent":"water"|"none", "conformers":1-16, "seed", "protomer":"none"|"ph7"}` (empty string = defaults); output `reatt/1` with `engine` `mzlab-chem/1`, `method`, `solvent`, `atoms` (4 decimals), `bonds` (aromatic 1.5), `hbonds`, `global` (HOMO, LUMO, gap in eV; `ip_ev`, `ea_ev`, `mu_ev`, `eta_ev`, `omega_ev` null), `conformers`, `scc` (three values: N, N+1, N−1), `ch_sites`, `notes`, `cite` `{text, doi}`.
- **H bonds** (`mol3d::hbonds(m, xyz)`): H on N or O, acceptor N, O or F different from the donor, H···A ≤ 2.5 Å and angle D–H···A > 120°. Errors of `reactivity_json`: `error.chem.smiles` (with `reason`), `error.chem.element`, `error.chem.embed`, `error.chem.charge`, `error.chem.opts` (with `field`), `error.chem.scc`; the panel has a message for each and shows "—" for null values.
- **`chem` `.wasm`** (`crates/mzlab-chem-wasm`): `version()` = `mzlab-chem/1`, `reactivity(smiles, opts)`; errors as a JSON string `{error_key, params}`. Entry `chem` in `tools/build_feat_wasm.sh` (`static/wasm-feat/chem/`, manifest) with its own cap 569,344 B; measurement after `wasm-opt -Oz`: 551,415 B. Memory: a call allocates and frees its matrices, the output is a single JSON string. `getrandom` with `js` is needed only for linking (the generators have the seed).
- **Desktop**: the route `feat/grafo` with `kind:"reactivity"` (`smiles`, `opts` as object or text) calls the same `reactivity_json` (`crates/mzlab-desktop/src/feat_graph.rs`; test `crates/mzlab-desktop/tests/responsiveness.rs`).
- **Parity with tblite 0.7.0** (`tests/parity_gfn2.rs`, `cc_ref.json`, same geometry): 20/20 converge (N, N+1, N−1) in gas and in water, |Δq| ≤ 2e-3 and |Δf| ≤ 3e-3. ## "Prev. f(−)" in the position classes (`posiz/1`) and reading in Attributio
- **Engine**: `mzlab_feat::chem::positions::positions_prior_json` (`grafo` `.wasm`: `positions_prior`) receives the parent's `reatt/1` as text and writes `prior` in `posiz/1` (contract and ranks in `docs/agenti/positions.md`); `mzlab-feat` does not depend on `mzlab-chem`. Test: `crates/mzlab-feat/tests/positions_prior.rs` (phenol from `cc_ref.json`, ALPB water: para 1, ortho 2, meta 3; JSON with `prior` removed identical to the one without).
- **Page**: `TPREATT.mount` and `TPREATT.state` send the event `tp-reatt` (`{smiles, reatt}` or `{smiles, state}`, SMILES = `dataset.smiles` of the box); the "Substitution position" panel (`44_posizioni.js`) listens to it and fills the "Prev. f(−)" column without redrawing the rows.
- **Attributio** (Bench 2b, when it exists): reads `prior` of `posiz/1` as is, next to the classes, as "Prev." information; it does not use it for levels, scores, order or exclusion of classes and does not recompute it. Without `prior` (no `reatt/1` or a transformation without an estimate) it shows nothing. The AT packages do not change.

## Protomer at pH 7 (`crates/mzlab-chem/src/protomer.rs`, option `protomer`)
- **Function**: `protomer::assign(smiles, &Mol) -> Protomer {mol, smiles, changes, charge}` puts the parent in the most probable charge form at pH 7 with a table of typical pKa (no quantum pKa); `reactivity_json` with `protomer:"ph7"` computes Fukui and charges on that form and writes the `protomer` field and a note. A single protomer, no enumeration; with several groups the note declares it ("Several ionisable groups…"). Charges written in the SMILES are respected.
- **Option off by default** (`PROTOMER_DEFAULT = false` in `react.rs`): validation on known sites does not improve (below). The panel offers it in the `Form` menu.
- **Acids** (deprotonated if pKa < 7): carboxylic 4.5; sulfonic/sulfuric −1; phosphoric/phosphonic 2.0 (first OH only); tetrazole N–H 4.9; phenol 10.0 − 2.8 for each nitro and − 1.3 for each Cl/Br/I in ortho or para (2,4-dinitrophenol and pentachlorophenol anions, 4-nitrophenol and triclosan neutral).
- **Bases** (protonated if pKa > 7): aliphatic amine 10.0 (N with only single bonds to sp3 C); amidine 12.0; guanidine 13.6 (without electron-withdrawing groups or heteroatoms on the N). Every cation already present within 3 bonds lowers the pKa by 3.5 (within 5 by 1.5): ethylenediamine stays mono-cationic, lysine dicationic with a carboxylate.
- **Left as written**: aromatic amines, amides, ureas, pyridine and azole nitrogens (imidazole 7.0), N–H sulfonamides (including N-heteroaryl sulfa drugs and bentazone, which are actually acids), thiols, quaternary ammonium and nitro.
- **Validation before/after** (`validation.rs`, `baseline` and `baseline_ph7`; C–H sites, 32 rows; all atoms in parentheses): top-1/3/5 → top-1 19 → 19 (1 → 1), top-3 29 → 27 (10 → 10), top-5 30 → 30 (18 → 19). Zero or negative gain: that is why the option stays off.

## HOMO and LUMO share per atom
- **HOMO/LUMO per atom** (`System::frontier_shares`): every atom of `atoms` has `homo` and `lumo` (share of |ψ|² by Mulliken partition, each list sums to 1); the Tabui table shows the columns "HOMO share" and "LUMO share", also in the CSV and in `TDATI`. With the EHT fallback the fields are missing.
- **Tests**: `tests/frontier.rs` (HOMO/LUMO sums = 1, shares of phenol and acetate).

## Calculations for the TPs (choices)
- Everything stays "Prev.", without a Schymanski level, and never excludes position classes. Fukui, conformers and HOMO/LUMO (without isosurfaces) are done. C–H BDE, polarity for elution order and redox potential are shown only after validation on measured data (polarity: right order in ≥ 8/10 isomer pairs with RT); pKa only qualitative (ionizable groups, expected charge); CCS waits for IMS data in use. Not done: docking, proteins, phonons, IRC and transition states, surfaces, solubility, ADME-Tox, NMR.
