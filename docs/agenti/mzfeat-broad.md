# Broad bands (`crates/mzlab-feat/src/broad.rs`)
Bands with FWHM 30-300 s (overloaded basic compounds, interconverting species) that `centwave.rs` (scales 5-30 s), `trace.rs` (> `long_trace` = background) and `untargeted/resolve.rs` (threshold at the 0.85 quantile, duration ≤ 1 min) miss. Off by default: with `BroadParams::default()` tables and fingerprints do not change (the `Debug` of `CentWave` prints `broad` only if on).
- `is_broad_ridge(y, lo, hi, dt_s)`: Ricker response at the apex at 15, 22, 30 s (FWHM); true if it grows (and `r_30 > 0`) = ridge of a wider structure, not to be cancelled; false = a true narrow peak.
- `inpaint_linear` (same math as `arpls::bridge`) under the narrow peaks only with a false ridge; contiguous or overlapping regions merge before filling; `decimate(rt, mz, y, k)` gives `(rt, y, mz)` in blocks of `k` (8).
- The Ricker responses use `Kernel` (taps per scale tabulated once, same sum and same bits as the direct computation).
- `detect_broad(rt_s, mz, y, narrow, p)`: six log-spaced scales in `width_s` (30-300 s) on the residual ÷8; candidates = maxima of the response (the strongest one stays, neighbors within one sigma drop). Noise = 1.4826 × median of |first differences| / √2 (not `REACH`), base = lower quartile.
- Filters of `detect_broad`: passes if height above the bridge ≥ `snr` (8) × noise and ≥ `noise` (500), and the width at half height ≥ 0.75 × `width_s.0`, measured on the smoothed signal above the local bridge (`half_width`, blocks inside the edges).
- Edges of `detect_broad`: the smoothed signal falls to 3 % of the height (or to 2 noises), or rises by 4 noises, or meets another valid candidate; bridge = straight line between the edge blocks; `area` in scans, `area_s` in seconds, height of the residual ÷8.
- Front and area (`broad_front.rs`): a candidate is discarded if the left half-width at half height (apex minus the point where the inpainted trace, before G(t), in block means, falls below half between the base `y[lo]` and the apex) is ≤ `BroadParams.front_s` (20 s; 0 if there is no flank on the left): it is the tail of a narrow peak. `area` and `area_s` are the trapezoidal integral of the inpainted trace before G(t) over the linear bridge alone between `y[lo]` and `y[hi]`. `front_s` appears in the `Debug` only with `enabled`.
- Hooks: `CentWave.broad` (`refine`: the wide segments are appended; with `broad` on, a band > `long_trace` is not background) and `untargeted::resolve::resolve_with_broad` (`UntParams.broad`; the band is searched on the trace BEFORE the quantile threshold).
- Common mode (`broad_gt.rs`, `BroadParams.gt`, off by default, needs `enabled`): `TraceBank` (`offer` counts and decides the sample, `push_row` adds: unsampled traces are not built) keeps at most 512 persistent traces (≥ 400 scans or ≥ 30 % of the run), uniformly sampled, decimated ÷8 and divided by their median. G(t) = pointwise median per block, then a moving median over 9 blocks (removes the shared narrow peaks), interpolated per scan.
- `detrend` (in `detect_broad_at`, after the inpainting): fits each trace with [1, t, t², G] by asymmetric least squares (weight 0.05 on positive residuals, 0.95 on negative ones, 8 iterations) on the residual ÷8 and subtracts the background from every scan. Without `common` or with a singular fit it does nothing.
- G is collected with a first pass: `trace::common_mode_params` (CentWave, unshifted grid) and `pipeline::common_mode_params` (ADAP); the result is `BroadParams.common` (in the recipe text it appears only with `gt`).
- Limits: with `gt` off a drift reads as a band (do not try `enabled` on real data first); with `gt` a band present in more than half of the traces enters G; a jagged crest gives a false ridge and the flank of an intense band can give a true ridge; peaks with sigma ≥ ~5 s pass the ridge and only the width filter stops them; ADAP (`min_highest`) discards low traces upstream; no `FLAG_BROAD` bit (`flags` is full).
- Marking and blank: a broad band carries `quality::REASON_BROAD` (bit 3 of `qreason`, not a quality defect: `RowQ::apply` keeps it, `interp/filters.rs` ignores it in `bad_quality`) from `Seg.broad` → `Row.broad` (`post.rs`) and from `untargeted::Feature.broad` (`to_file_features`); `rowmeta` ORs it onto the row.
- Blank filter: `Rule::BroadBlank(r)` (`filters.rs`, JSON `bianco_larga`): for rows with the bit and at least one `blank` file, max(sample areas) ≥ r × max(blank areas) is required (3.0 recommended), otherwise the row is discarded; without a blank, without summaries or without the bit everything passes. It is not among the parameters of the `features.filter` step (it is used in the JSON `filtra`).
- Switch: boolean parameter `broad_peaks` (default false) of the `features.detect` step, only with `algorithm=centwave` (with masstrace the error is exit code 2); `recipe_map.rs` carries it to `CentWave.broad.enabled`; in the `resolve` block of the untargeted recipe `"broad": true` turns on `Params.broad.enabled`. The page shows it as a checkbox in the step panel and, if off, the key does not enter the recipe (the hashes of the predefined recipes do not change); from the CLI `--set detect.broad_peaks=true`. `broad.gt` stays off.
- Pointwise blank (`broad_blank.rs`, `BroadParams.blank_pointwise`, off by default, needs `enabled`): `trace::detect_with_blank(index, blank, p, progress)` first collects the blank's traces (≥ `min_scans` scans, the 4096 most intense, decimated ÷8) into `BroadParams.blank`.
- Subtraction (`broad::detect_broad_at`, after the inpainting): the blank trace with the nearest m/z within `blank_ppm` (10) is interpolated in time and subtracted per scan (clip ≥ 0); with `blank_scale` it is multiplied by the median of the sample/blank ratios per block, in [0, 1]. Without a bank, option or trace of that ion nothing changes; no batch step calls it yet.
- Not done: `attributio/review/propose.rs` `blank_ratios` (tables without `qreason`).
- UV/PDA trace (`uv_bands.rs`, step `chrom.uv_peaks@1` in `flow_uv.rs`): `uv_peaks(rt_min, y, &UvParams)` gives the narrow ones from `peaks::detect_peaks` (unchanged, area ×60 in signal×s) and, with `broad`, the bands of `detect_broad` on a single trace (`gt` and blank off, `noise` 0, block 3 s). Without `remove_line` (line of the lower background, asymmetric fit) the drift stops the edge walk; the narrow ones are merged and widened by 50 % per side before the inpainting.
- Band width and height from the smooth trace over the bridge of the detector's edges, area = `area_s`. CSV `file,kind,rt_apex_min,rt_lo_min,rt_hi_min,fwhm_s,height_mau,area_mau_s,snr`; without a DAD matrix `err.pda.none`; each parameter has its own `--set`.
- Measures on `tests/uv_bands.rs` (40 min run, 5 bands FWHM 60-240 s, 12 narrow ones, drift 0→5 mAU, noise 0.05 mAU; tolerances: apex ≤ 12 s, FWHM ±20 %, area ±45 %): at 2 Hz all 5 found, apex ≤ 10.5 s, FWHM −3…−17 %, area −9…−38 %. The area bias comes from the bridge between the edges of `broad.rs` (same error without narrow peaks or drift): future SPAL candidate.
- Known limits: at 10 Hz two close bands merge (the test only checks the isolated ones); a low band (2 mAU) in a run 90 % covered by bands does not exceed the base (lower quartile) and is not found; the narrow peaks above the bands have the area within 3 %; 0 false bands on linear and quadratic drift.

## Options of the targeted pass (`target`)

Off by default: tables and `to_bytes()` identical. The rest of `target` is in `docs/agenti/mzfeat.md`.

- `TargetParams::estimate_points` (`Option<u16>`): a peak with fewer than `min_points` but at least this many points is accepted as "estimated" (`Cand::estimated`, bit `quality::REASON_ESTIMATED` in `qreason` of the row of `FilePeaks::peaks`); with fewer points it is discarded, as always.
- `TargetParams::shape_tol_pct` (`Option<f64>`, /\ check in `target/extract.rs::rises_then_falls`): the trace rises up to the apex and falls after it; a step back of up to that % of the height is noise, beyond it the peak is not accepted (comparison with the running maximum before the apex and with the running minimum after it).
- `TargetParams::merge_overlap` (`target/merge.rs`): rows with m/z within `ppm` and overlapping RT windows become one (the first by mass); `TargetRow::merged` lists the other hypotheses, `TargetResult::name(reg, riga)` joins the labels with " / ". Per file the detected cell wins, then the largest; `found` counts the hypotheses found before the merge; the `Registry` format does not change.

## RT correction and alignment (`rtmap.rs`, `rtwarp.rs`)

- `rtmap.rs`: `build(&Aligned, &RtParams) -> RtMap` (port of `rtalign.build`): anchors = strong rows (median height ≥ 10 × `min_height`), isolated (no other row within 5 ppm and 0.5 min), with a deviation between files ≤ 0.5 min, seen in all files (or in 75 %); piecewise linear curve per file between the medians of groups of anchors; `at`, `expected`, `reference`, `rms` per file. Without at least 3 anchors the map is empty (`ok()` false, deviations 0).
- `rtwarp.rs`: RT correction between files (`WarpParams`, `Method` off|piecewise|loess|ransac; recipe `align.warp`, step `features.align` `rt_warp`, `rt_coarse_gap`, `rt_span`, `rt_inlier`, `rt_seed`).
- `rtwarp::warp_map`: coarse alignment (`coarse`: `rt_gap` ≥ `coarse_gap`, no isomer splitting), anchors from `rtmap`, per file the curve (apex − reference RT) at 33 nodes: block medians (`rtmap::build`), local linear tricube LOESS with 2 robust passes (≤400 anchors) or a RANSAC line (200 samples, fixed seed).
- The `rtwarp` map goes to `align_grouped` (`adj`): in ion mode `famalign::align_ions` (thresholds of `r.rt`), in family mode it replaces the `rtmap` map; in slabs `slabs::first_map`. `off` (default) does not change the bytes.
- MEASURE align_deriva_ioni (`crates/mzlab-feat/tests/align_drift.rs`, synthetic series of 20 files, drift +0.008 min per injection, two isomers at the same m/z 0.14 min apart, FWHM 0.06, a minor third in 5 files; 45 true features): defaults (`rt_warp=off`, `max_rt_span=None`) in ion mode 1 row (the three isomers merged: the drift fills the 0.14 min gap, `rt_gap` 0.1 chains them); `piecewise` and `loess` 3 pure rows; `max_rt_span=0.25` 8 rows (6 pure: the cap cuts the groups in half); `split_gap=0.1` without `rt_warp` stays 1 row (`MIN_SPLIT_FILES`=9 is not enough with the three merged).
- MEASURE align_deriva_famiglie (same series, each isomer the root of a family): defaults 3 pure rows (the `rtmap` map already corrects the drift), `piecewise`/`loess` 3, `max_rt_span=0.25` 3. In this series family mode does not lose products with `rt_warp`: the absorption in `for-agents.md` needs non-root members, not measured here.
- `quality::keep_row` / `reason_text`: a row of the aligned table stays if at least one file has a good peak; the discarded ones carry the code of the most frequent reason (`Q_SHAPE<0.5 in 13/13 files`). Alignment does not live in this crate: whoever aligns calls it.

## Kinetics as evidence (`kinetic.rs`, `kinetic/`)

- `kinetic.rs` (+ `kinetic/`): kinetics as evidence (with four shapes). `FormBank::new(tempi)` builds once the formation curves `1-e^{-kt}`, rise-and-fall (Bateman), flat and decreasing on a grid of k (28 values, 0.003-3 /min); `fit(y)` fits them in the square-root space (closed-form scale) and ranks them by AICc (`choice` = shape and gap from the second).
- `kinetic::stability(y, Resample::LeaveOneOut | Bootstrap{draws, seed})` gives the share of fits with the same shape (valid if ≥ 80 %; SplitMix64 generator). `generation(&FormBank, &ChainBank, y, MARGIN)`: AICc of A→B→ against A→I→B→ (Padé, `ChainBank` ≈ 3 700 curves), "first / second / undecidable" with `delta`.
- The two k of the rise-and-fall and the three of the chain are symmetric (formation and decay swap; the rows are increasing pairs and triples): which one is the formation is told by the parent, not by the shape. Answers frozen in `tests/golden/feat/cinetica_*.json`, test `tests/kinetic_parity.rs`.
- `kinetic/evidence.rs`: `parent_kinetics` (decay k of the parent; if the flat-then-collapse part of `parent_saturation` decays at less than a third of the speed after the plateau it is saturation: the isotopic series is used if it does not saturate, otherwise the times after the plateau) and `evidence_from_kinetics(&Setup, y, controlli) -> Evidence {kind "cinetica", value, threshold, points, sign, source, version, params}` (`kinetic_report` also gives shape, stability, generation, controls and coupling).
- Rules of `kinetic_report`: controls (absent or reduced ≥ 3× relative to blank, dark and t0, noise = `pseudo`); shape and stability (one point excluded, ≥ 80 %); coupling: the k closest to the parent's within ×2 is "coherent", above ×2 "too fast", below "too slow"; almost instantaneous formation = not comparable. "In favor": formed, stable, coherent, not 2nd generation; "against": too fast or present in the controls; otherwise informative.
- Points of the "controls" evidence (`livelli-1.js`, `punti.prove.controlli`): log2 of the treated/blank ratio ≥ 3 → 2 points, ≥ 1 → 1; a feature present in the blank does not get them (the evidence is "against", never compensated) and drops in the Dig order.
- `POINTS_MAX = 0`: kinetics is informative only (measured on V1 in `tests/kinetic_v1.rs`): right shape in 96 % of the products above 10× the noise, right generation or "undecidable" in 99 %. Coupling, however, does not separate true TPs from random-k decoys if V1 is as it is (formation k independent of the parent: "against" 84 % of TPs, 48 % of decoys); it separates only if the generator assumes the coupling (92 % against 16 %).
- `params.points_if_weighed` says what the evidence would be worth (maximum 1). 1000 identities: 0.45 s (O3) – 0.6 s (release profile `z`), banks built once.
