# Spectrum cards: peak box, highlighted stick, keys, pinned labels
`mzlab/web/peak-info.js` (`window.PEAKINFO`, in the start list of `carica.js`) is the interface half; the box itself is `p._a.tipOf(b)` in `drawSpec` (`picchi.js`), shown by `putHover` (`grafici.js`).
- **Box**: any peak, labelled or not, within 14 px of the pointer (24 px on touch, `ptr-touch`) at any height; the nearest peak wins. First lines are the old ones (m/z, intensity with the other unit in brackets: % in cps mode, cps in % mode). PEAKINFO adds, for MS² and MSn, the shift from the precursor and the loss, `+N close` (peaks within 6 px, found by `spanOf`, a binary search; no per-pixel loop), the formula applied to the peak (`p.fm`) and the notes near it (`p.anns`). Contaminant lines stay as they were.
- **Numbers**: `PEAKINFO.top`, `dmz`, `nl` are the ones the peak table uses (`tabella-picchi.js`), so box and table agree. The engine has no peak-table function: both are plain JS on the peak list as drawn.
- **Profile data**: the box snaps to the peak list (`d.mz`, the local maxima the engine returns), never to the profile line.
- **Speed**: `hoverFrame` in `eventi.js` shows the first move of a burst at once and folds the rest of the frame into one `requestAnimationFrame` step. No box while a drag is running.
- **Stick**: a thick `--accent` bar (`.pkhi`) over the peak, hidden with the box.
- **Keys** (canvas focused, `attach`): Left/Right = previous/next peak in the window by m/z; Shift+Right/Up = next taller, Shift+Left/Down = next shorter; Enter or Space = pin/remove the label; Esc or blur closes. The same box opens, and its text goes to the `role="status"` region of the card (hover is never announced).
- **Pinned labels**: a click within 6 px of a peak (14 px on touch) toggles its label when no ruler or XIC step is pending (`specClick`, `spettro.js`). `p.lblOn` (pinned, always drawn, not counted in «labels») and `p.lblOff` (removed) belong to `spec.display` (undo, saved with the card). Tests: `tests_e2e/e2e_picco_info.py`.
