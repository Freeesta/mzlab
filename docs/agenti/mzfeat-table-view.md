# Feature table in the Pipeline (steps 5-6): QC and table

View of the mzFinder table in the browser; the rest of the browser work is in `mzfeat-wasm.md`.

- **"Feature table" tab, steps 5-6** (`36_feature_qc.js` = `PIPEQC`, `37_feature_table.js` = `PIPET`; they appear under the list of the "Run" results): `PIPEQC.open` loads the `.wasm` of the table (SHA-256 of the manifest) and opens a session on the `.mzfeat` (`qc`, `view`, `rows` = rows of the sheet `xlsx_rows`, `file`); `PIPEQC.render` draws the quality control from `qc/1` (D/T/F/A cells per file with chart and `TDATI`, CV of the replicates, CV of the standard, state of the gate).
  - With `vista_tabella` open `PIPET.mount` draws the bar (chips "Not in blank" = rule `blank` at 3, "Detected in ≥ 2 files" = `min_detected` 2, "With MS2" = rule `ms2`, button "More filters" that opens the numeric fields, normalization dropdown), the counter "N of M rows" (`#ft-n`) and "Rejected by each filter" (`#ft-why`) from `view`/`qc`, and a virtualized `TPTAB` (`#ft-t`, rows from `session.rows(spec)`, sorting on the model).
  - **Closed row**: fixed columns `level` (badge from the identities of `evid/1` of the row, empty without) and `identity` (formula of the hypothesis or "m/z 199.0536"), then `mz` (4 decimals), `rt`, `trend` (areas of the samples of `samples/1` in time order, empty without times), `cells` (states ● ◐ ◌ ○) and `max`; from the "Columns" menu: ID, charge, "Detected in N of M", peak edges. Legend of the symbols under the table; "Export view" of the component.
  - **Expanded row**: the click opens it in place with `TPRIGA` (header, Overview, Samples, Identity, Quality); `session.row(id)` (`riga/1`) once per row, `session.traces(id, ppm)` (`riga_tracce/1`) at the Overview tab: XIC of all the files from the `.mzidx` of the series (no file open in Data), MS1 at the apex with the observed M+1 peak and MS2 if present. "XIC in Data" above the XIC opens the XIC of the m/z in Data with the file of highest area. `PIPER.detail` (`#pv-det`) stays only for the list of the identities.
  - **"Compare with library"** (`37_feature_table.js::compare`, under the MS2 of the Overview tab; proof `e2e_specchio.py`): visible only with an installed library (`LIB.call("list")`) and a row with MS2; the click launches `LIB.call("search")` (precursor = m/z of the row, tolerance and polarity of the page, fragments 0.01 Da) and draws the first result with `LIB.mirror`; the first 5 are `aria-pressed` buttons (name, entropy). Without results: "No library spectrum within the tolerances". The answer stays per row; it is not an identification and does not change the level.
  - **Sample matrix**: Samples tab, a matrix with a small square per file (`.tpr-cell`, focusable) per group (`.tpr-grp b`): each has its own help from `TPHELP` (own text in `data-tt-text`) and below is the color/symbol legend (`.tpr-mxleg`, symbols of `TPTAB.legend`).
  - **Cells column** (`TPTAB.cells(states, max, info)`): with `info` (`{file, type, time, area}` per state) the samples go in the order blank/dark, t0, increasing times, each symbol is a focusable small square with help and a white background (no area) or green `--tpr-g0..4` ever darker with the area (log scale on the row maximum, `TPTAB.areaScale`/`paint`, the same as the matrix); the symbol stays neutral, the legend says "white = none, darker green = larger area"; the low-resolution TP table passes it from `kinetics`.
  - Under the quality control `PIPEQC.render` shows "Plausibility of the values" (`plausibleHtml`, from `session.plausible()` = `plausible/1`): a `role=status` line ("N of M checks of the values flag rows" or "The values look plausible") and, only for the checks that flag rows, a list with "Warning:" or "Note:", the number of rows and the first ids; the checks not done are listed apart.
  - No color alone in the box (border `--warn` plus text); an engine error leaves the rest of the QC. Proof: `tests_e2e/e2e_tabui_plausible.py` (synthetic texts with suspect values and with good values, light/dark axes, 390 px) and the real box in `e2e_pipeline.py`.
  - `qc/2` also carries `blanks` (blank files, rows considered, rows with a peak in a blank), `m1_over_m` (rows with M+1/M, minimum, median, maximum; `null` without row summaries) and `discarded` (rows rejected by each rule of the view, in order); the page shows them in the quality control and next to the table counter, with the reason of each rule in `PIPEQC.WHY`.
- **TP drawing on the parent** (`TP_Mine/js/46_tp_mol.js`, `TPMOL`; proof `e2e_tabui_tp_disegno`): `TPMOL.svg(smiles, drawing)` draws the `drawing` of `interp/1` (`mode`, `atoms`, `ends`, `highlight`, `zones`, `kept`, `uncertainty`; indexes in the order of the SMILES of the parent) with the 2D coordinates of OpenChemLib (never the canvas of Draw). Each drawing is `role="img"` with `aria-label`.
  - Shapes: `structure` = the TP with the new atom on the outer bisector (the lost part is gray dashed), `multicenter` = a single dashed bond from the center of the ring to "OH" leaving from the middle of a bond (small circles on the candidates only if they are not the whole ring), `bracket` = bracket `parentesi.js` with the Δ; the fragment zone has halo and dashing, the class zones have their own number and stroke;
  - `TPMOL.molfile` writes the V3000 with the S-groups `MZLAB` and `MZLAB_ENDPTS` (Ketcher reads them back) and `TPMOL.openInDraw` loads it in Draw (opens the view and then `TPDraw.load`).
- **"Structure" tab** (`TP_Mine/js/47_tp_struttura.js`, `TPSTRUCT.fill(panel, interp, why)`, fifth tab of `TPRIGA`): reads an `interp/1` row (`m.interp`, from `session.interp(id)` when the series has the block; without it, the tab says why in `missing.structure`).
- **Content of the "Structure" tab**: the head sentence from the confidence of `site` (`Site: C8`, `Region: ring A`, `Position uncertain · 3 classes · relative support 0.6 / 0.3 / 0.1`, `Cannot say: <reason>`), the drawing of `TPMOL` (shape from `drawing.mode`, `TDATI` with the classes), "Open in Draw", the table of the classes with the state in words, the equal/shifted nodes of the tree, the limit and the suggestions (MS3, H/D exchange, standard).
- **Link of `interp/1`** (`34_pipeline_run.js::interpInput`, `36_feature_qc.js::interpFeed`): with the proof table (`out.evid`) and a parent (SMILES or neutral formula, adduct from the polarity, RT) the session has `interp(id)` and `interpCached(id)`; the block is built once, at the first row asked, with `pool.interpBuild` (parent, `out.registry`, `.mzidx` in `mzlab/idx/`, infusion position or -1) and the rows are read with `pool.interpRow` on the table with the block.
- **Link of `interp/1`, app and limits**: in the app the held table changes SHA-256 after the build: the native session names the new one. Without a parent or without proofs the tab says "not built".
- **"Identity" column**: `TPSTRUCT.identText(row)` gives `+O`, `+O (ring A?)` with `region`, `+O (?)` with `uncertain`; `PIPET.mount` uses it if `o.interpText(id)` provides it (the page reads the rows already open: `session.interpCached`). Proof: `tests_e2e/e2e_tabui_struttura.py` (synthetic `interp/1` rows, 390 px, dark theme, axe).
- **Hooks for other pages** (`PIPET.mount`, `TPRIGA.hook`; proof `e2e_tabui_extra.py`): `o.extraCols` adds columns after those of the table (same shape: `key`, `label`, `cell(row)`, optional `sort`, `text`, `num`, `off`); `o.extraTabs` (`[{key, label, help?, fill(panel, row)}]`) adds tabs to the expanded row after "Structure"; the result of `mount` also has `redraw()` that redraws the rows with the new values of an added column. Without these options the output is identical. Used by the graphical Flow.
- **Report with the products** (`39_tp_report.js` = `TPREPORT` for `PIPEX.reportHtml`; proof `e2e_tabui_rapporto`): at the top the provenance (`mzlab-feat`, rules, profile, SHA-256 of recipe, table, samples, inputs, date); with `tp/1` (`session.tpTable` = `tptab/1`) the section "Transformation products" of the rows in view (formulas from `interp/1`, "Prev. N", proposed level and `TPDEC` separate), "Formed, not explained" and for the first 50 products by relationship an SVG with the data table below. One file, no scripts or URLs.
