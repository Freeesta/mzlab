# Peak integration in the Data view
Files: `picchi.js` (integration, `autoEdges` = async request to `QUALITA.edges`, `autoAll`, parameter window), `eventi.js` (tool clicks), `qualita.js` + `peaks-worker.js` (Rust peak engine), `crates/mzlab-core/src/peakq_auto.rs` (HR) on top of `peakq.rs`.

- **Integration**: only XIC and MRM (TIC/BPC/PDA point to the XIC); automatic or manual, never overlapping; table with S/N, optional chromatographic parameters and quality measures (Gaussian similarity, asymmetry at 10 %, noise score: computed by the Rust engine in `qualita.js`/`peaks-worker.js`, they are measures to discuss, never "good/bad"; without the `.wasm` the cells stay empty); Excel. The calibration line is not in the view: it is the Flow step `quant.curve` on the exported integrated peaks (`calibration.md`).
- **Automatic tool** (`iauto` button of the panel): at low resolution one click integrates the peak under the cursor (`autoInt` with the edges of `autoEdges`; without the engine: notice `int.autoOff`); on a trace of a high-resolution file (`hrTrace`: `HR.isHr` of the file) one click integrates all the peaks of the shown stretch (zoom included) in one undoable step (`autoAll`).
  `autoAll` asks `QUALITA.autoIntegrate` for the peaks of `auto_integrate` (`peakq_auto.rs`; engine `.wasm`, same in the desktop app): AsLS background (lambda 1e7) raised to the median of the background points, Savitzky-Golay smoothing with half-window 30 % of the FWHM of the highest peak (2-30 points), apexes from `detect_prominence` (prominence, `local_snr` 5), edges where the smoothed signal above the background drops to 1 times its noise, or at the valley shared with the neighbour, or, past half height, where the slope stays below 3 % of the steepest (sloping background), at most 5 FWHM.
  Each HR integration carries `base = [t0, y0, t1, y1]`: the baseline is the line through the two background ends, `integ()` subtracts it (also after dragging an edge) and `grafici.js` draws it; without `base` (one click at low resolution) the line passes through the means of 3 points at the edges.
- **Manual window** (tool `man`, drag between two bars; record with `zero: true`): the area is the one under the trace between the two bars, cut vertically at each bar down to zero, with no baseline and no correction for a nearby peak (`integra-zero.js`: trapezoids with linear interpolation at the exact bar times, height = highest point, RT = that point); `grafici.js` fills the same region.
- **Bar handles** (`barre-maniglie.js`, `BARRE.sync` called by `cursorLine`): each bar has a 44 px handle (`p._bar` in `eventi.js`: finger/pen pointer, arrows one sample, Shift five; `touch-action:none`, `aria-label` `int.bar.a/b`); with the cursor the `.clh` dot is hidden while the manual, automatic or "delete" tool is active. Test: `e2e_integra_barre`.
- **Ends on the baseline** (`integra-estremi.js`, module loaded by `BARRE.sync`, `INTEND.sync`): where the area touches its line (zero, edges or line) each window has two 24 px buttons `.iep` (`role=slider`, `data-e` 0 start / 1 end, `aria-label` `int.end.a/b`): mouse, finger or pen drag them along the trace, left/right arrows one point (Shift five); where a point of a custom line (`.ibp`) sits, that point wins. Area and line follow at every step; text `.iep-live` (`role=status`) with end and area; one undo step per gesture; rows `tdati.int.a/b` in the data table.
- **Two anchors (SIM/MRM panels, `ESP.mrm`)**: no bars and no `.ibh`/`edgeAt`; an integration is just the two `.iep` with `it.snap` = {a, b} in `curve` (on the trace) | `zero` | `man` (`it.my` = level in counts). At every draw `snapBase` (`picchi.js`) derives `bm`/`bl`: two `curve` = `edges`, two `zero` = `zero`, otherwise `line` between the anchors; table, CSV and engine stay the same. Up/down arrows, dragging 16 px off the line or the menu (`.iep-menu`, `role=menu` of `menuitemradio`, context key or Shift+F10) give `man`; S cycles the modes.
- **Anchors, accessibility**: `title`/`aria-label` `int.anchor.a/b` (time, intensity, mode), 24 px; `.sel` (thicker ring) = chosen integration (`p.isel`); the mode of the pair is the "Baseline" column, never text on the plot. Test: `e2e_sim_wiff`.
- **Several integrations per trace (`addInt`)**: a new drag adds a window (identical = no duplicate, overlapping = notice `int.clash`), one undo step each; the new one inherits the modes (and the manual levels) of the last anchor changed by the user (`it.st`, `snapNew`), otherwise `curve`/`curve`; the program never chooses the baseline.
  Where the end goes is Rust's job: `mzlab_core::intwin::move_end` (on the nearest point, at least one point before/after the other end, up to the edge of a nearby integration without crossing it), wasm export `window_end`, operation `wend` of `peaks-worker.js`, `QUALITA.windowEnd`; CLI `mzlab-cli peak-window TRACE.csv --end MIN --other MIN (--at MIN | --step N) [--min MIN] [--max MIN]` (same JSON bytes `{t, i}`). Tests: `intwin_test.rs`, `peak_window_cmd_tests.rs`, `e2e_integra_estremi.py` (page = CLI, keyboard, mouse, finger 1024x768, light and dark theme).
  Windows that would overlap an existing integration stay out; edges are draggable as always. Without the engine: notice `int.autoOff`. Method choice: "HR method choice" below (bench `crates/mzlab-core/tests/integration_bench.rs`); test `e2e_integra_hr` (page area = Rust area), Rust tests `auto_integrate_*`.
- **Edges at low resolution**: `peakq_lr::lr_edges` (Rust), wasm export `lr_edges`, operation `edges` of `peaks-worker.js`, `QUALITA.edges`; CLI `mzlab-cli peak-edges TRACE.csv --at MIN [--set NAME=VALUE]`; test `crates/mzlab-core/tests/peakq_lr_parity.rs` against the golden values of the old JS version (`crates/mzlab-core/tests/golden/picchi_lr.json`). `integ()`, `chromPeak`, `noiseSD` (`cromato.js`) and `snipBaseline` (`fondo.js`) stay in JS.
- **All peaks at low resolution** (`integra-tutti.js`, lazy; ▾ button `data-a=iall` next to the automatic tool and menu item "Integrate all shown peaks" of the trace menu; on click only): `peakq_lrauto::lr_auto_integrate` (Rust), wasm export `lr_auto_integrate`, operation `lrauto` of `peaks-worker.js`, `QUALITA.lrAuto`; CLI `mzlab-cli peak-auto-lr` (same bytes, `e2e_integra_tutti`).
  - Detection on a copy: background of `peakq_auto::background`, 3-point median against spikes, quadratic Savitzky-Golay (half-window `lr_half`; 0 = 40 % of the FWHM of the highest peak, 2-30 points, `AUTO_FRAC`); smoothed apex >= 5 x noise (`lr_sn`).
  - Valleys: local maxima merged in pairs starting from the weakest until the valley between two drops by at least 10 % of the lower one (`lr_valley`) and by 3 times the noise of the smoothed copy; edges at half height extended until the copy returns to the background or to the valley (at most 5 FWHM).
  - Baseline: each end is the background (or the smoothed level at a cut or a cap), never above the raw trace and never below the zero line of the trace shown (raised to 0, `clamp0`/`clamp1` = `none|trace|zero`; on a trace shown with the background subtracted, 0 is the subtracted background). The line only runs from one end to the other. Always the engine's line: the dialog's "baseline" setting does not apply here (`params.baseline: "engine"`).
  - Area on the RAW trace (trapezoids, a stretch crossing the line is split where it crosses): `area` = only the part above the line, never negative; the part below the line is cut off and reported in `below`; `area_zero` = the part above zero. Two touching peaks share the valley point, with the perpendicular (`drop`: line between the outer ends of the group) or valley to valley (`valley`).
  - JSON `{params, peaks}`: `half`, `half_auto`, `valley`, `sn`, `split`, `baseline`, `noise`; per peak `t0`, `t1`, `rt`, `b0`, `b1`, `area`, `below`, `area_zero` (counts x min, never negative), `height`, `sn`, `end0`/`end1` (`background|valley|cap`), `clamp0`/`clamp1`.
  - Page: each peak is a record with `bm: line` (SIM/MRM: two `man` anchors on the engine line) and `auto` (every choice: smoothing, valley, S/N, split, `baseline`, `b0`/`b1`, `end0`/`end1`, `clamp0`/`clamp1`, and the engine areas `area`, `below`, `area_zero` in counts x s); the area of the record stays the page's signed measure for a line (above minus below, the part below in "Below the line" of the table), that is the engine's `area - below`.
  - Page, notice and dialog: one undo step; the notice states smoothing, valley, S/N, noise, split, that the line is the engine's, how many ends were lowered onto the trace and the part below the line. Dialog: "Low resolution: all peaks" (`lrSmooth` points, 0 = from the peaks; `lrValley` 10 %; `lrSn` 5; `lrSplit`).
  - Tests: `peakq_lrauto_test.rs`, `peaks_auto_lr_cmd_tests.rs`, `tests/test_lr_auto.py` (150/150 peaks on the jagged traces of `tools/synthetic_data_jagged.py`, areas = independent trapezoid), `e2e_integra_tutti`.
- **All peaks of all shown files** (SIM/MRM, item "Integrate peaks in all selected files", `runFiles` in `integra-tutti.js`): same `lr_auto_integrate` and numbers as the window, for each file; peaks at the same time (apex within 0.10 min) form a group, the one without a counterpart is marked; engine, routes and tests in `transizioni.md`.
- Peak CLI: `mzlab-cli peak-edges` (one peak, low resolution) and `mzlab-cli peak-auto TRACE.csv [--from MIN --to MIN] [--set NAME=VALUE]` (all HR peaks of `auto_integrate`, test `peaks_auto_cmd_tests.rs` against the engine).
- **Peak detection**: `PK` / `PK_DEF` (`mzlab.picchi`), window "Peak detection" (`peakParams`): seven settings in two side-by-side boxes, one help line each, "Restore defaults"; it fits on one page without scrolling at 1280x720 and 1366x768 (`e2e_picchi`). Box "One peak per click" (`autoEdges`, `LrParams::from_dialog`): `edgeFrac` 3 %, `win` 1.2 min, `tail` 1.5 min, `snMin` 0 (no threshold), `multi` 50 %. Defaults in the engine (`LrParams::default`, `AutoParams::default`), repeated in `PK_DEF`; `PK` reads only the seven keys.
- **HR parameters** (box "High resolution: all peaks", `AutoParams::from_dialog`): `hrSmooth` (apex smoothing window, 5 points, odd) and `hrSn` (minimum prominence S/N, 5). CLI: `peak-edges --set` `win`, `edge_frac`, `sn_min`, `multi`, `tail`; `peak-auto --set` `hr_smooth`, `hr_sn`. Fixed in the engine and adjustable only from the CLI: low resolution `area_noise` 3, `noise` mad, `min_half` 2, `constrain` 0, `nr0`/`nr1` (`peak-edges --set`); HR `hr_prom` 1 %, `hr_scans` 3, `hr_half` 0, `hr_slope` 3 % (`peak-auto --set`).
- **Baseline** (`mzlab_core::baseline`): `zero` (y = 0, `DEFAULT`: the mode of an integration that names none, in the CLI, in Flow, in the Data view and in the SIM Table; integrating from zero includes in the area any background under the peak), `edges` (each engine's line between the edges, the numbers of records made before) or `line` (a level or a line through two points, also inside the peak).
- **Baseline, measure**: against `zero` or `line` the area is the signed integral of the trapezoids of y - line (a stretch crossing the line is split where it crosses), `below` = the part below the line (positive, never subtracted), height = highest point above the line (negative if the whole stretch is below), RT = that point; edges = nearest points as `nearIdx`.
- **Baseline floor** (`Baseline::at`, `baseline.rs`): a `line` is never below zero, anywhere (between its points, past them, a level): it is held at 0 wherever the straight line would be negative, so area, height, p/v and the drawn fill never include a part under the zero line. The HR route (`peakq_auto.rs`) floors its ends at 0 too. A handle cannot be dragged, typed or restored under zero: `put` and the dialog (`integra-base.js`), the anchors (`integra-estremi.js`, `my`), `ibFloor` on a record of a saved session, `edge3`/`baseAt` (`picchi.js`).
- **Baseline floor, notice**: `peak_area_json` adds `"clamped"` (counts × s: what the part under zero would have counted) only when the line was held; the page puts it in `it.clamped` and in the label box; the CLI `peak-area` prints the same bytes. A handle at zero gives no `clamped`; the "Below the line" column is then only the trace itself under the line (a noisy trace under zero), never the baseline's own triangle.
- **Extended baseline, in the page**: an `edges` record with `base` (automatic HR, or without `bm`) whose edge went past `base` where the line would be negative (`ibDips`, `integra-base.js`) is measured and drawn as `line` on that `base` (`QUALITA.peakArea`, mode 2); inside its two points it stays `integ()`. The polygon follows the line sample by sample, so it is the number.
- **Baseline floor, tests**: `baseline_test.rs`, `peakq_lrauto_test.rs`, `peak_area_cmd_tests.rs`, `peak_area.rs` (wasm), `e2e_integra_estesa.py`, `e2e_integra_zero.py` (a handle dragged under the axis stops at zero, the area is unchanged).
- **Baseline, where**: same function for the page (`peak_area` of `mzlab-wasm`, operation `area` of `peaks-worker.js`, `QUALITA.peakArea`: JSON text `{baseline, area, below, height, rt}`, counts x s), `mzlab-cli peak-area TRACE.csv --from MIN --to MIN [--set baseline=zero|line] [--set base_y0=.. base_t0=.. base_y1=.. base_t1=..]` (same bytes; without `baseline` = `zero`) and `peak-auto [--set baseline=zero|edges|line]` (all peaks, default `zero`; each peak carries `baseline`, with `zero` and `line` also `below`; area in counts x min).
  `edges` of the Data view stays `integ()` in JS. Tests: `baseline_test.rs` (Gaussians with analytic areas, also on a sloping background), `peak_area_cmd_tests.rs`, `peaks_auto_cmd_tests.rs`.
- **Baseline in the Data view** (`integra-base.js`, loaded by `grafici.js` when a record has `bm`): each new integration writes `bm` = `zero`|`edges`|`line`, with `bl` = [t0, y0, t1, y1] and `lvl` (level) for `line`; `edges` and a record without `bm` (earlier sessions) stay `integ()` with their numbers. For `zero` and `line` the number is Rust's (`QUALITA.peakArea`, cached per record, never saved); the part below the line is hatched, the line is dashed, the label states the mode ("A = ... (from zero)").
- **Baseline, commands**: key B or "Baseline..." in the menu (`area-copia.js`, action `int.baseline`) opens the record dialog (mode, the two points as numbers, horizontal; invalid numbers: row `#ib-msg` role=status inside the dialog); the two points are 24 px buttons `.ibp` (`data-k` 0/1) to drag with mouse, finger or pen, left/right arrows one sample and up/down arrows 1 % of the axis (Shift x5 / 10 %), one undo per gesture.
- **Baseline, new integrations**: `PK.bmode` (0 zero, default as `baseline::DEFAULT`; 1 edges; 2 line from the edges) = mode of the new automatic integrations, also `autoAll`; of the old key `base`, saved with every setting, only the line (2) carries over. The manual window stays `zero:true`. The dialog says that from zero any background under the peak enters the area.
- **Baseline, table**: columns "Baseline" and "Below the line" always on screen, a note when a part lies below the line; Excel/CSV add them at the end when a record has `bm`, that is with every new integration (exports of earlier sessions identical). S/N, w1/2, N and tail use the same line (`chromPeak` with `bl` or [0,0,1,0]). Test: `e2e_integra_modi.py` (new record from zero, record without `bm` = `integ()`, three modes, mouse, keyboard, touch 1024x768, light and dark theme).
- **Area copy** (`area-copia.js`): click on the "A = ..." label (rectangle `copy`/`int` in `p._a.lbls`, set by `grafici.js`) or menu item "Copy value" (action `int.copyArea`, also from the keyboard) copies to the clipboard the area at CSV precision (`I18N.sciE(area, 5)`, no unit).
- **Area copy, notice**: "Copied!" (or `bn.noClip` if the clipboard refuses, `.bad`) in a DOM element `.areacopied` fixed next to the pointer (child of `body`, or of the `dialog[open]` if a dialog is open, so it stays on top; `AREACOPY.put`, `role=status`, `aria-live`, visible 1.2 s then a 150 ms fade, none with `prefers-reduced-motion`; a new copy restarts from scratch), never in PNG/PDF.
- **Area copy, fallback and notice**: if `navigator.clipboard.writeText` refuses, it falls back to a hidden textarea + `execCommand('copy')` (removed at once, focus returned) and only if that also fails does it show the error. The label tooltip is only `int.copy.tip`.
- **Area copy, pointer**: mousedown on the label starts no drag and does not move the cursor, except within 6 px of an edge (which is dragged even under the label). A press-and-release that moves no more than 5 px copies (also with a redraw in between or at an edge); beyond 5 px it is a drag and does not copy. Tests: `e2e_area_copia`, `e2e_integrazioni`.
- **Grabbing edges and cursor** (`tol` in `eventi.js`): the edges of an integrated peak and the cursor line are grabbed within 6 px with the mouse, 14 px with a finger or pen (class `ptr-touch` of `touch.js`); the nearest edge wins. Test: `e2e_tocco`.

## Method choice and two-anchor proposal
- **HR method choice** (bench `crates/mzlab-core/tests/integration_bench.rs`, synthetic traces with known areas): `auto_integrate` beats the page click, prominence and CWT (mean area error 5.4 % against 24.5-37 %, no false peak); shoulders with Rs <= 0.5 do not separate (the group is integrated together). The thresholds are tuned only on synthetic traces. At low resolution the click tool stays at one peak; all peaks with ▾ (above).
- **LR method choice** (`python3 tools/lr_auto_misura.py all`, reproducible, ~20 s: jagged 3200 QTRAP-style traces of `tools/synthetic_data_jagged.py` plus a narrow pair and a small neighbour, 20 seeds, 1 % extra spikes; two sets of 760 peaks, "mild" 4 and 8 % jitter, "harsh" 15 and 25 %). "Merged" = windows with two true apexes (the shoulder also counts, which is intended as one window), "false" = windows without an apex, error = |area/true - 1| of the peaks alone in the window.
  - `lr_auto_integrate` defaults: mild 760/760, 15 merged, 2 false, median error 2.3 % (p90 8.8 %); harsh 756/760, 17 merged, 5 false, 5.7 % (p90 17.7 %). HR `auto_integrate` (`baseline=edges`): mild 758, 25 merged, 13 false, 2.7 % (p90 9.5 %); harsh 743, 65 merged, 11 false, p90 56.6 %. `lr_edges` click at every true apex (150 peaks): 110 apexes in a window, 50 pairs of overlapping windows.
  - Variations (same sets): S/N 3 gives 39 false (mild) and 24 (harsh), S/N 8 loses 22 harsh peaks; valley 5 % merges less (mild 2, harsh 8) with the same false in mild (2) and more in harsh (9), 15-20 % merges more (28-37 mild); fixed half-window 3 points: mild 14 merged 0 false, harsh 14 merged 2 false (slightly better than automatic), 4-5 points merge more. The 40 % FWHM fraction (`AUTO_FRAC`) was not varied. Defaults approved by the owner on 16 Nov (40 % FWHM, valley 10 %, S/N 5, on click only).
- **Unimplemented proposal: two-anchor model** (the Rust module `intanchor` is missing; in the page the two anchors exist only in the SIM/MRM panels): an integration = anchors A and B, each with a time and a mode (`curve` on the trace, `zero`, `manual`), baseline = line through A and B, today's modes as pairs of modes.
- **Rest of the two-anchor proposal**: signal below the line cut and hatched (option `signed`), split with a vertical only on command, propagation to other samples marked `propagated`/`unconfirmed`, Ph. Eur. S/N 2H/h, an `int/2` record, site = CLI parity test. In LR no integration without a user gesture.

- Question 3: propagation with the same times or shifted by the apex, and driven by the internal standard?
