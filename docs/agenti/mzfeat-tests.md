# mzFeat: proofs, points and the identity graph
Area of the `mzlab-feat` crate, modules `evid*`, and measurements of the row area. The rest of the crate: `docs/agenti/mzfeat.md`.

- `evid.rs` (+ `evid/points.rs`): the proofs of an identity as data (`evid/1`, schema `docs/schemi/evid-1.json`). `Evidence` = {type (`massa` mass, `isotopi` isotopes, `MS2`, `frammenti` fragments, `rete` network, `cinetica` kinetics, `controlli` controls, `RT`, `frequenza` frequency, `esterne` external), value, threshold, points ≥ 0, sign (`a favore` for | `contro` against | `neutra` neutral; the literal values of the schema), source, version, parameters} = an entry of `evidence[]` of `mzid/1`; `Evidence::graded` gives the points from the table, `from_kinetic` carries the `kinetic` proof.
  - Sign values are the literals above (`a favore` = for, `contro` = against, `neutra` = neutral).
- Points of `evid`: `PointsTable::bundled()` reads `data/livelli-1.json` (section `punti` of the default profile of `livelli/1`: steps in the style of Alygizakis 2023, thresholds named after an entry of `soglie`; `massimo` 0 for kinetics, which is informative only; `weighed` turns it on at 1).
- Source of the points: the JSON is written by `python3 tools/export_levels.py` from `mzlab/web/livelli-1.js` (single source; `--controlla` and `tests/evid.rs` check that they match).
- Contributions of `evid`: `breakdown` = column of the contributions in type order, total (only the "for" proofs; it is 0 if the only proofs for are frequency and controls (next line); an "against" counts 0 and is not offset, on equal total the identity with fewer proofs against goes first, `Breakdown::rank`), N and 1/N (`indistinguishable`: same mass within ppm and MS2 not separated by the margin), reason codes (`EV_AGAINST_OPEN`, `EV_INDISTINGUISHABLE`, `EV_NO_MS2`, `EV_WEAK_MS2`, `EV_KINETICS_INFO`). The total orders and does not assign the level: the cascade stays `LIVELLI.assess`.
- Frequency and controls say that the signal is real and say nothing about the identity: if they are the only proofs for, the total is 0 and the identity is "discarded", with the code `EV_ONLY_FREQUENCY` (frequency only) or `EV_ONLY_CONTROLS` (controls, with or without frequency); with a proof of the identity they also count in the total.
- `evid/graph.rs`: `build_graph(&Inputs) -> Vec<Identity>` on the producers already merged (aligned rows, `Facts` per row, family edges, hypothesis registry, library entries and external results as plain structures, `Kinetics`). Nodes = identities and members (with role); edges = adduct, ISF, isotope, "indistinguishable", parent→product, identity→library, identity→external.
- `Identity` (of `graph.rs`): hypothesis, members, `Breakdown`, edges, maximum area and `max_level` ("4" without library MS2 and at unit resolution, "3a" with an in-silico library, "2a" only with an experimental library: `LibHit.experimental`). Order: total descending (on a tie fewer proofs against, `Breakdown::rank`), then maximum area descending, then row order (stable).
- `match_hypotheses_in`: a row represents its family; the hypothesis is confirmed first by the root ion (role M among the `member_*`), then by the reference ion of the row (e.g. an [M+Na]+), then by the others. In the Dig (`legacy/dig.rs::candidate`) `mz` and `ppm` of the candidate are those of the ion that matches the hypothesis: its MS2 is searched on that. The example `mzidx` uses `upload::prepare` (like the browser: `hr1` true on HR files).
- `evid/arcs.rs`: `arcs_from_families(aligned, files)` carries the edges of `Families` of each file onto the aligned rows (m/z ±5 ppm, RT ±0.3 min; on a tie it keeps the better `r`); `proof_evidence` turns a `Proof` into `Evidence` (coelution → RT, the others → network). The "network" proof = share of MS2 fragments equal to or shifted from the parent, given by `Facts.network`.
- The "against" proofs stay flagged in the column, not offset and with no effect on the level (cascade of `LIVELLI.assess`); the order that uses them on a tie is in `Breakdown::rank`.
- `evid/proofs.rs`: the proofs of a row graded from the table (`graded`, `frequency`, `kinetic_proofs`, `rt_proof`).
- `evid/producers.rs`: per-row measures that feed `Facts`: `isotope_score` (observed M+1/M ratio, areas of the window around the apex in the file where the ion is strongest, against the one expected from the formula of the hypothesis with `isotope_ratios`; 1 − |diff|/expected; M+1 absent where expected = 0; `None` if expected < 0.08) and `network_share` (share of the MS2 peaks of the child above 5 % that the parent explains, equal or shifted by the mass difference). Fragments explained by formula: no per-row producer (`Facts.fragments` stays empty).
- MEASUREMENT isotopi_alogeni (`crates/mzlab-feat/tests/iso_halogen.rs`, 8 true formulas with Cl2, Cl3, Br, Br2, S, S2, Cl+S and a CHNO control; each isobaric CHNO formula within 5 ppm on the [M+H]+ is compared with the observed pattern of the true formula, M and M+1 for the row score, the pattern merged at resolution 20000 for `mzlab_core::isoscore` with the options of `mzlab-target`): the true formula gives 1.00 in both. For Cl3 and Br2 no CHNO formula falls within 5 ppm (large mass defect).
- MEASUREMENT isotopi_alogeni, isobars: the row score (M+1/M only) gives the wrong formula from 0.34 to 0.99 (maximum 0.99 for the compound with S2, 0.82 for Br, 0.71 for Cl2, 0.76 for Cl+S, 0.94 for the CHNO control), the core score (with M+2) from 0.16 to 0.66 (0.38 for S2, 0.31 for Br, 0.16 for Cl2). The row score does not see M+2, so Cl, Br and S2 do not lower it enough; no default changes.
- `evid/out.rs`: `graph_json(ids, table)` writes the `evid/1` document (identities with contribution table, nodes, edges, reason codes); `store::write_table_with_evid` puts it in the `evid` section of the `.mzfeat` and `store::read_evid` reads it back. Same inputs = same bytes.
- `cargo run --release -p mzlab-feat --example checks -- FILE.mzidx… --parent-formula F [--truth V.json] [--out X.mzfeat] [--neg] [--twice] [--library X.msp]`: full path on real indexes (isotopes, purity and network from the MS2 spectrum, with `--library` the best library entry per row of the strongest file; `--json` writes the `evid/1`); with the truth it reports rank by area and by graph; `--twice` checks identical bytes.

## Measurements of the row area
- **Measurements of `area_from`** (`tools/area_synthetic.py` + `tools/area_diagnosis.py`: 60 peaks, jitter 0-3 ppm, drift, DDA; median log2 area/XIC over the scans of the row): `channel` −0.06 (±1 ppm), −0.19 (±2 ppm), −0.48 more with DDA; `window` 0.000 without DDA, −0.48 with DDA (MS1 scans farther from the apex than the median); `xic` 0.000 in every scenario (p95 ≤ 0.003, 0.22 only at ±3 ppm, points outside ±6 ppm). In `flow_area_tests.rs`: Gaussians at ±2 ppm, median |log2(area/true)| 0.17 `channel`, 0.012 `window`; irregular scans, `window` 1.1 and `xic` 0.000 from the XIC.
- MEASUREMENT bordo_canale (`crates/mzlab-cli/src/flow_area_tests.rs`, `ions_on_a_channel_border_lose_area`: the same 6 ions on the border of the 4 ppm channels, of one grid or the other; Gaussian noise on the m/z with sigma = 0.8 + 2.5·√(apex height / hour height) ppm, at most 3 ppm; 400 regular scans, fixed seed; the biggest row of each ion is read). Control: without noise 1 row per ion and |log2(area/true)| ≤ 0.03.
- MEASUREMENT bordo_canale, results (median and maximum of |log2(area/true)|, grid not shifted / shifted): `channel`+`strict` (default) 0.83 and 3.05 / 0.83 and 3.05; `channel`+`wide` 0.72 and 1.50 / 0.63 and 1.50; `window`+`strict` 0.76 and 3.05 / 0.76 and 3.05; `window`+`wide` 0.60 and 1.18 / 0.51 and 1.18. Rows per ion from 1 to 3 (the two most intense have 2-3 with every choice), no ion lost. `window` and `wide` lower the error but not below 0.5 of median: the limit is the dispersion of the m/z beyond the channel, not the area computation.
  - After the measurement the HR default is `window`+`wide`; the unit one stays `channel`+`strict`.

## Frozen answers of the old Python
- The answers of the old Python mzFinder engine are in `tests/golden/feat/` (and `tests/golden/finder*.json`, `subpeaks.json`, `tpmine_irls.json`, `lib/all_pairs.json`): the tests read them and never call a Python reference. The synthetic inputs are written by `tools/feat_synthetic.py` (numpy and `mzlab`, no engine): without python3 and numpy the tests that need them are skipped (`MZLAB_GOLDEN_STRICT=1` makes them fail).
- `python3 tools/feat_synthetic.py OUT` writes five synthetic files (invented ions: weak, intense with an FT lobe at 60 ppm, two ions at 3 ppm, continuous background, empty file) and their `.mzidx`; `tests/trace_parity.rs` compares them with `detect.json`. In the "background" file `long_trace` is 60 because the synthetic traces do not reach 400 scans.
- `python3 tools/feat_synthetic.py OUT --solo identita` writes the small synthetic identity series (seed 7) in `OUT/identita/`, the `.mzidx` `OUT/ri_<name>.mzidx` and `OUT/identita.mzidx` (= SIN_t10min); it is used by `iso_ms2_parity.rs`, `gapfill_parity.rs`, `family_parity.rs`, `export.rs` and `row.rs`.
- `tests/iso_ms2_parity.rs` (`iso_ms2_identita.json`: isotope groups and MS2 linked with purity, computed with simple loops): same indexes, purity within 1e-5, and the truth of the synthetics (purity within 0.05, isotopologues grouped ≥ 95 %).
- `tests/family_parity.rs` (`famiglie_coppie.json`: grid of steps, MS2 and ratios for `edge_evidence`; `famiglie_identita.json`: peaks, traces and spectra of the identity series with pairs, edges, roles, families and alternatives): correlation at 1e-6, double run and small slabs same bytes.
- `cargo run --release -p mzlab-feat --example families -- FILE.mzidx... [--neg] [--truth verita.json] [--mz M]... [--rt R] [--json FILE]` prints features, families, roles, edges and time; with the truth it judges bases and derivatives (isotope, adduct, ISF, undeclared false ISF, open NH4). `--mz M` (with `--rt R`, row within ±0.15 min) prints role, parent and family of the rows near M.
- `--json FILE` of `famiglie` writes the `famiglia/1` records (`famrecord.rs`: per family members with m/z, RT, role and parent row; per edge step, correlation, readings and proofs; the size is `size`), one line per file; proof `family_record_json` in `tests/family_parity.rs`.
- MEASUREMENT famiglie_identita (synthetic series `identita`, `--truth`): 988 features, 20 families with more members, 11 edges; 16/16 bases with the right family, isotopologues 12/12, adducts 5/5, ISF 3/3, false ISF never declared ISF 3/3 (2/3 left undetermined, the third stays an M of its own), NH4 with the two readings open 2/2, 0 foreign rows.
- MEASUREMENT famiglie_degdma (`python3 tools/feat_synthetic.py OUT --solo degdma`, `tools/valida/sintetici_degdma.py`: molecule with weak [M+H]+, [M+Na]+ 6×, [M+K]+ 2.5× and [M+NH4]+ 3× higher, in-source fragment base peak 12× with MS2 proof of the parent, same m/z from a product that elutes 6 min later): 18 bases found, 17 with the right family, 0 foreign rows; the fragment of the product stays an M of its own (never an ISF of the molecule, 4/4 undeclared false ISF).
  - With `Orient::Factor` (`--orient factor`) Na and K are missing (not merged) and the base-peak ISF (not merged), and the [M+H]+ stays "undetermined" under the NH4: in `roles.rs::orient` a dependent must be at least `parent_factor` (2) times weaker than the parent, and here the adduct and the fragment are stronger.
  - With `Orient::Evidence` (default) Na, K, ISF and NH4 are in the family of the molecule (Na and K as adducts, the ISF as ISF; the NH4 stays root with the two readings open), 7/8 adducts and 4/4 ISF, the fragment of the product stays an M of its own; on the series `identita` families and roles are identical in the two modes. Each edge of the `famiglia/1` record says `admitted_by` (`factor` or `evidence`).
  - `tests/families_degdma.rs` fixes the right unions, the product fragment kept separate, no extra link on `identita`, the text of `FamParams` that names `orient` and the same bytes. On real files the measurement is done only on the Mac.
- `tests/align_parity.rs` (`allinea_sintetico.json`: synthetic series with RT drift, isomers, chains of ions at 3 ppm and at 0.08 min, FT lobes; `align` with three parametrizations and the RT map): same groups (m/z 1e-9, RT 1e-6, areas 1e-9 relative) and same curves within 1e-4 min.
- `cargo run --release -p mzlab-feat --example align -- FILE.mzidx... [--neg] [--tsv FILE] [--family] [--truth FILE] [--dump FILE]` detects, aligns and prints groups (all, in ≥ 3 files, in all), anchors and times; with `--family` it also runs quality, isotopes, MS2 and families and the alignment by family (rows, members, SHA-256 of two runs); `--truth` (lines `id<TAB>m/z<TAB>RT`) counts the ions of the list found as reference or as member.
- `cargo run --release -p mzlab-feat --example series -- FILE.mzidx... [--neg] [--ion] [--out FILE.mzfeat] [--twice]` builds the table of a series (analysis, alignment, filling) with the times per step, the cells per state, the SHA-256 of the `.mzfeat` and the re-read check; `--twice` compares two builds.
- `tests/gapfill_parity.rs` (`riempimento.json`, on the identity series): same filled cells and areas within 1e-4 with and without the map; `tests/store.rs`: identical write and re-read, equal bytes, 64-byte arrays, damaged files rejected, fingerprints; `tests/scale.rs`: 50 files × 80 000 features (`cargo test --release -p mzlab-feat --test scale -- --ignored`), table < 100 MB.
- `tests/famalign.rs`: synthetic series built in the test (RT drift, adducts, isotopes, ISF, fragment shared by two compounds, base missing in some files): families in one row (≥ 98 %), no merge through the shared fragment, states coherent with the peaks, two identical runs and frozen answer in `tests/golden/feat/famalign_sintetico.json` (`MZLAB_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1` rewrites it).
- `tests/hypo.rs` (`ipotesi_regole.json`: hypotheses of three invented formulas with `TP_Mine/data/regole_tp_3.json`): same formulas, steps and masses (1e-6) and the same SHA-256 of the registry in two runs.
- `tests/tp_ms2.rs` and `legacy/network/golden.rs` (`tp_ms2.json`: parent-product pairs): the three components of the MS2 similarity (equal, shifted, common losses) within 1e-9 with counts and typed pairs, and the edges of the mass-difference network (source, product, kinetic coherence, Δm within 1.5e-5).
- `crates/mzlab-desktop/tests/e2e_shim_finder.py` (`finder_msn_e2e.json`: nodes of the synthetic MSn standard, tree, calibration, `frag_tree`, comparisons and localization of the Dig candidates): the `finder_msn` `.wasm` and the desktop engine give the same answers.
- `tests/deconvolve_parity.rs` (`deconvolve.json`), `mzlab-core/tests/subpeaks_parity.rs` (`subpeaks.json`), `mzlab-core/tests/tpmine_irls_parity.rs` (`tpmine_irls.json`) and `mzlab-lib/tests/all_pairs_parity.rs` (`lib/all_pairs.json`): inputs and answers in the same file.
- `tests/trace_parity.rs`: same number of features, apex and edges identical, m/z within 1e-6 relative, areas within 1e-4, same flags; slab of a thousand peaks (budget 1) = big slab; two runs = same bytes.
- Predecessors of the network (`predecessors`) and verdict of the hidden products (`isf_hidden`): frozen answers of the Python reference in `tests/golden/finder_funzioni.json`, read by the tests `legacy/network_pred/golden.rs` and `legacy/hidden_fam/golden.rs`; the names of the transformations are not compared.
