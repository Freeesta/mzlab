# Memory contract of the WASM modules

The lines apply to every crate that runs in WASM: build ceiling 4 GiB per instance (`.cargo/config.toml`, `--max-memory=4294967296`: reserves nothing, limits `memory.grow`; initial 32 MB), working budget chosen by `budget_auto` (minimum 192 MB, never above 3072 MB in the browser: margin for code, stack and fragmentation under the 4 GiB of wasm32, 32-bit `usize`).

- `mzlab-core::budget`: `MemBudget` (`reserve(fase, byte)` → `Guard` that returns the bytes when dropped; `in_memory(stima)` = the estimate fits in half of the free space; `from_request(mb)`: NaN/0 = 192 MB, native 1024 MB), `refusal`/`refuse` (error `error.memoryBudget` with `{fase, stima_mb, budget_mb, libero_mb, eccesso_mb}`: `eccesso_mb` is the estimate minus the FREE space, not minus the ceiling; with `fase` the text is `error.memoryBudgetFase`, chosen by `I18N.err`)
- Other parts of `mzlab-core::budget`: `try_vec`, `WriteAt` (write at offset: `Vec<u8>` in tests, OPFS handle in the browser), `Hashing` (in-order sink that counts the bytes and computes the SHA-256), `sha256_hex`; `sha256_hex_at` (a `ReadAt`) and `sha256_hex_read` (a `Read`) compute the SHA-256 in 1 MiB blocks, with no whole copy: the table of `table.filter` (`view_json_origin`), the table search of a view (`table_by_sha`) and the MSn graph index.
- Rule: every phase ESTIMATES before allocating (with `saturating_*`: a huge estimate is a refusal, never an overflow) and picks a route: in memory, in blocks (same bytes) or an orderly refusal with the key. Never a silently different result: no raised thresholds and no excluded files.
- Estimate before allocating (`mzlab-core::stima`): `scantable::read_in` and `upload::prepare_in` compute the scan table (`TABLE_PER_SCAN` 640 B per scan at peak, measured 556-563) from the count of the offset index or, without an index, from the progress of the walk (every `EVERY` 4096 scans); `build_mzidx` estimates the index (`INDEX_PER_SCAN` 220 B beyond the JSON of the rows, plus half of the peak budget).
- The orderly refusal: outside the instance budget, `error.memoryBudget` with `fase` (`table`, `index`, `indexPeaks`, ...), `stima_mb`, `budget_mb`, `eccesso_mb`; `I18N.err` and the CLI (`error_text.rs`) show it with `error.memoryBudgetFase.<fase>` if the phase has its own text, otherwise `error.memoryBudgetFase` with the name `memory.phase.<fase>`; app and CLI use `.native` (computer memory; `tests/dati/avvisi_memoria.json`); the CLI adds the next step (`--set ISTANZA.budget=`, `--budget-mb N`). No worker dies.
- Detection estimate (`mzlab_feat::trace_estimate`), from the index header and the sampled histogram: 68 MB + 3,250 B per MS1 scan + 13 B per peak above `floor` (+ the slab beyond the default one). Fitted on 5 synthetic files (800-30,000 scans, 0.15-14.6 M peaks kept): every estimate ≥1.15× the .wasm peak, always above native (ratios 1.31 / 1.15 / 1.27 / 1.37 / 1.17).
- `detect_fits` asks for it before `detect` in the .wasm (`detect_file_to`, page budget, not the quota), in the app (`feat/detect`, `step_budget`) and in `flow run` (work budget): refusal `error.memoryBudget` phase `detect`. `tests/dati/stima_detect.json` drives the four tests: `mzlab-feat-wasm/tests/estimate_detect.rs`, `flow_budget_tests.rs`, `mzlab-desktop/tests/estimate_detect.rs`, `tests/test_stima_detect_wasm.py`.
- Estimate for reading a whole mzML (`mzlab_core::mzml_estimate`), from one pass over the tags before decoding (base64 has no `<`): 16 MB + 640 B per spectrum or chromatogram + 14 B per point (`defaultArrayLength`) + 9 B per point of every additional `f64` array (64-bit intensities, also from a group, ion mobility) + the bytes of the files kept; for `spectra.ms2` in addition the MGF text (24 B per MS2 point + 256 B per MS2 spectrum) or, in DIA, the `f64` copy of the intensities (9 B per point).
- Reading, calibration: 4 synthetic shapes (`examples/read_estimate.rs --write`: dense, 64-bit, 40,000 small scans, profile): .wasm peak 244/284/303/322 MB against estimates 311/351/373/397 (margin 20-27 %), always above the native peak (`mzml::read` ≈ file + 12 B per point + 330 B per spectrum). `ms2_text` reserves the estimated MGF text (`reserve_exact`): no doubling of the `String`, about 100 MB less per file in the .wasm.
- Reading, refusal: `spectra.ms2` and `formula.ms1` ask for it before `mzml::read`, in the .wasm (`budgetMb`), in the app and in `flow run`: `error.memoryBudget` phase `read`; the page asks once for "more memory" if it fits in `alto` (`holdRead` of `flow-web-ident.js`). `tests/dati/read_estimate.json` drives `mzlab-feat-wasm/tests/read_estimate.rs`, `flow_budget_tests.rs`, `tests/test_read_estimate_wasm.py`.
- `error.featMemory` (the `memoryBudget` refusal as seen by the `feat-worker.js` pool, with `files`) also states the free space: "{stima_mb} MB, {libero_mb} MB free of {budget_mb} MB"; `libero_mb` comes from the engine.
- Whole reading, other steps: `chrom.transitions` (`transitions_text`, `transitions_packed`; `flow_transitions` with `budgetMb`) and `chrom.uv_peaks` (`uv_text`, app only) ask for the same estimate for every mzML before reading it (kept: all the files, the reader copy, the channels already read); `spectra.chimeric` asks for `text_bytes` (16 MB + 3 × the MGF bytes, table and library) and reads the offset indexes. Refusal `error.memoryBudget` phase `read`; tests: `flow_transitions_tests.rs`, `flow_uv_tests.rs`, `flow_chimeric_tests.rs`, `e2e_flow_transitions.py`.
- Compact table rows (`scantable::Row`): `filter` and `native` are interned `Arc<str>`, `an` is `&'static str`, the rest (precursor, CE, activation, path, `precs`) lives in `Option<Box<Detail>>` and the "where" field of the readers in `Option<Box<Dove>>`; the JSON is written in 1 MB pieces (`json::drain_json`) and joined at the end. The `.mzidx` stays identical byte for byte (SHA-256 in the `mzidx` line of `examples/mzml_memory.rs`).
- Adaptive budget (`mzlab-core::budget_auto`, wasm `budget_choice(device_gb, cores, small, manual, ios, probe_mb)` and `budget_down(corrente_mb, morti)`): Automatic mode gives `{mb (= safe_mb), safe_mb, high_mb, levels, mode, reason, confidence, hard_mb, max_scans, inputs}`.
- Budget, levels: `safe_mb` is the budget the engine works with without asking anything, `high_mb` the one usable only after a warning. Browser levels 96, 128, 192, 512, 1024, 2048, 3072 MB; safe/high: `deviceMemory` ≤1 → 96/96, 2 → 128/192, phone/tablet or Safari iOS/iPadOS → 192/192, computer with `deviceMemory` 4 → 512/1024, 8 with fewer than 8 cores → 1024/3072, 8 with ≥8 cores → 2048/3072, unknown (Firefox, Safari) → 512/2048.
- Feature pool (`feat-worker.js`): `budgetOf(opts)` = `opts.budgetMb` (number or function), otherwise the safe budget of the page engine (`MZLAB_RUST.choice.mb`, or the level the supervisor lowered it to), otherwise 0 (= 192); `setBudget(mb)` changes it and returns the old one. Steps running together (alignments, tests) split it (`shareOf(budget, jobs running or queued)`, never below 64 MB); a step alone has all of it. Test: `tests/test_feat_pool_budget.py`.
- Native: `flow run` divides RAM/4 among the jobs running together (`flow_out::Budget`, `budget_auto::share_mb`, at least 128 MB; `--budget-mb` stays as it is); the app feature steps use the same (`feat.rs::step_budget`).
- "More memory" warning (`feat-run.js`, `runFeatPipeline`): if `align` or the tests are refused (`error.featMemory`) but the estimate fits in `alto` (`choice.high_mb`, or the lowered level), it asks once `askMore({fase, stima_mb, budget_mb, libero_mb, high_mb})` (default `window.mzlabAskMore`, set by `rust-budget.js`; the dialog is `memoria-avviso.js`, default "Cancel").
- Warning, outcome: yes = the phase reruns at `alto` for the rest of the run, then the pool returns to safe; no, beyond `alto` or nobody to ask = the refusal with its numbers. Tests: `tests/test_feat_pool_budget.py`, `tests_e2e/e2e_memoria_avviso.py`.
- Budget, probe and descent: the growth probe (`probe_mb`, 0 = absent) can only lower `high` (not below 192), with `safe ≤ high`. Native: a quarter of the RAM between 1 and 16 GB, safe = high.
- `budget_down`: a death for memory drops one level, the second within a minute to 192 at most, the third stops (0).
- Growth probe (`rust-sonda.js`): a throwaway worker grows a `WebAssembly.Memory` with the build ceiling in steps of 256 MB up to 3072 MB or the first refusal (2 s at most), then closes; `probe_mb` enters the budget choice, stays in `sessionStorage` (`mzlab.memProbe`) and `rust-budget.js` waits for it at most 800 ms. It measures what the browser grants, not the free RAM.
- Computers only: heavy data (HRMS, large `.raw`) and mzFlow are not supported on phones and tablets; there the budget stays at 192 MB (or less) with no high level. Levels and the 4 GiB ceiling are tuned on computers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari macOS).
- Manual choice and registry: the manual choice (`rust-budget.js`: 96, 128, 192 MB) has no interface, it always stays Automatic and a value saved in `mzlab.memBudget` is ignored and cleaned; the registry is read-only in the "Diagnostics" section (administrator only) of Settings. Every choice, with reason, confidence and data used, is in the registry `mzlab.memBudget.log` (last 40, who: program, user, supervisor).
- Path: `rust-worker.js` receives the choice with `{op:"policy"}` and passes it to `prepare_upload` and to the index (`cap`); the supervisor's level descent enters the registry. Desktop app: `idxbuild.rs` reads the RAM (`/proc/meminfo`, `sysctl hw.memsize`) and refuses in order an index that does not fit.
- Large outputs: they do not return as `Vec<u8>`; they are written at offset on a `WriteAt` and return `{bytes, sha256}` (`store::write_file_to`, `write_table_to`; the container is written in order, column by column in pieces of 8192 elements). The `evid` section is a `store::Section` (known length, bytes emitted in pieces).
- Large inputs: read by offset (`store::read_file_from` with `Want{lean, mz}`, `read_table_from`), never the whole file in one buffer; `Want::lean` reads only the 10 alignment columns (39 bytes per row instead of 91); the m/z range is found by halving the column (`mz_rank`) and a slice reads only its rows from `Stored::part` (file on disk, page `Uint8Array` without copy), not the whole file.
- `mzlab-feat::slabs::align_and_fill_stored` / `align_and_fill_spilling` (L4-L6 from stored `.mzfeatf`, one file at a time through `Stored`): in memory if the estimate fits in half of the free space, otherwise in m/z slabs cut where no pair of features of the series is closer than the alignment ppm (no group crosses a cut); same bytes. Weight: 39+40 bytes per work row, 21 bytes per cell, 4 bytes per `group_of` row.
- "Family" in slabs (`slabs::family_slabs`): two passes of groups per slab (the second with the RT map), then votes and links one file at a time; ion table for `1.5 × the widest` rows, slab = half of the rest next to the table (and the copy, if it fits twice in the free space); the growth copy is checked on the real groups of the slab.
- Tests (L8-L9): `run::evidence_to` builds the whole graph (`build_graph` + `graph_json`) if `rows × 2400 + table` fits in half of the free space, otherwise `evid::GraphStream`: two passes, every identity (a pure function of the row) is rebuilt, one for the order and length of the JSON, one for the bytes; only one identity stays in memory. Same bytes. Hypotheses (L7): `target::run_loading` loads the table of one file at a time.
- Contract test: `tests/test_feat_wasm.py` (SHA-256 of `.mzfeatf` and `.mzfeat` with `evid` equal in memory and at reduced budget, native and in Node; orderly refusal with the previously saved phases), `crates/mzlab-feat/tests/{slabs,evid_graph}.rs`.
- Guards (CI, `rust.yml`): `tests/test_guardie_memoria.py` measures the peak linear memory of the .wasm after each phase (detect, align, hypotheses, tests; engine: index open, TIC+XIC, scans, map, `subxic`, second engine at a tiny budget with the same bytes and zero cache) on the synthetic series `piccolo` and `completo` (seed 2) with `tests/guardie_memoria_node.js` and compares it with `tools/guardie_memoria.json` + 10 % (`MZLAB_UPDATE_GUARDS=1` to update it, justified in the PR);
- Guards, large series: `completo_blocchi` is `completo` at 24 MB (`BUDGET_MB`): align and tests go in slabs and the two `.mzfeat` have the SHA-256 of the in-memory route. Almost all values are the 32 MB floor; `completo` at the tests rises to 43 MB, in slabs it stays at 32. It takes about 3 minutes.
- `memory_bytes()` is exported by `mzlab-wasm` and `mzlab-feat-wasm`; `tools/controlla.py` rejects an exported WASM function that returns a whole `Vec<u8>` if it is not in `tools/api_byte_ammesse.txt`, and `indexedDB.open` outside the known files.
- Persistence: `qqq-db.js` has `SCHEMA` (version of the whole archive) and `DB_VER` 4 in the database `NOMI.DB` (`mzlab_lab`, old name `qqq_lab`) with the store `manifest` (one row per OPFS file: `{path, tipo, bytes, creato, usato, formato, versione_formato, crate, sorgente_sha256}`; unknown fields `null`).
- `migra.js` runs numbered, idempotent and repeatable steps under the exclusive lock `mzlab-migra`, before the engine opens the files (`rust-worker.js`, wait at most 5 s if another tab holds the database); `kv.schema` says at which step the data are.
- Step 3 fills the manifest from the files in OPFS (`files/`, `idx/`, `feat/`); `archivio.js` notifies every write and removal (`watch`) and the manifest follows them.
- The libraries live in the OPFS root of `mzlab/`, not in `lib/` (IndexedDB fallback `mzlab-libs` only without OPFS).
- Archive (b): `archivio.js` writes one file at a time per tab (`locked`, Web Lock `mzlab-file:<folder>/<name>`; beyond 2 s `error.fileInUse`), checks the space before a write above 50 MB (`ensureRoom`: needs 1.8 × the bytes, otherwise `error.quota {serve_mb, libero_mb}`), asks for `persist()` only once (`persistOnce`, also from the libraries window with `import()`) and notifies the other tabs with `BroadcastChannel("mzlab")` (`listen`).
- Index across tabs: the index of a file is built by one tab at a time (`archivio.exclusive`, Web Lock `mzlab-job:idx/<name>` with no wait limit); the tab that waited reuses the finished index (`lrIndex`). An OPFS handle taken by another tab is `error.fileInUse` (not fatal), never an engine restart. Test: `e2e_due_tab_indice`.
- `pulizia.js` (with the engine stopped) removes `.part` older than 1 h, indexes without a source unused for a week and derived files in a format older than the current one, never a source.
- Space (settings): it also counts the pipeline, says when the browser does not guarantee the data and, on iPad/iPhone outside "Add to Home Screen", that a week without use may delete them; it updates if another tab changes the files.
- Derived-files manifest: `manifesto.js` (`infoDi`) reads from the file (`.mzidx`, `.mzfeatf`, `.mzfeat`) `versione_formato` (index; `feat_version` of the pipeline), `crate` and `sorgente_sha256`; `CORRENTI` fixes the current values (`tests/test_manifesto.py` compares them with `FEAT_VERSION`). `rust-worker.js` writes them on every write (`infoOf`; new index: `crate` = engine version), `pulizia.js` completes the rows without them, also for the files that `feat-worker.js` writes without an archive.
- Freeing the derived files (no button, only `POST api/spazio?derivati=1` → `lr_space` with `free:"derived"`, `lrFreeDerived`): removes indexes and pipeline files, rebuildable from the sources; without the parameter only the orphan indexes (`lrFree`).
- Orphans (`lrFree`, also before a large copy): an `idx/` entry is orphan if its file is missing in `files/`, key `.cen.key` or `.part` being written (`ARCH.idxOwner`). `lr_remove` removes the indexes and key of its own file; a single `lrFree` + `nativo.sweep` starts 300 ms after the last removal of a series (`tidySoon`).
- "Reduce every peak to a single point" (settings, `centroidAll` in `settings.js`): for every low-resolution profile mzML file of `ST.files` it writes `<name>.centroidi.mzML` (op `mzml2cen`, `rust-centroidi.js`) and loads it with `upload()` like any file; the original does not change. The copy is a normal file (removed from the list), not a derived one: "Free the derived files" does not touch it. Space requested first with `ensureRoom(1.1 × original)`; progress "N of M" and Cancel.
- Engine (`mzlab-wasm`): a single byte LRU (`routes::trim_cache`): whole maps, per-scan tables (`ScanArrays`) and m/z blocks of all files, oldest use out first (tick of `routes::next_tick`); they fill from `idxread`, the answer does not change after eviction. Budget `Engine.set_budget_mb(mb)` (default 192): cache = `min(5/6, 512 MB)`, the rest is work (192 MB → 160/32), `cache_bytes()`, `trim_cache()` (`rust-worker.js` calls it before every answer).
- Work: an mzML opened whole (`Engine.runs`, without an index) is not evicted, counts on the work and above 32 MB `open` gives `error.memoryBudget` (phase `open`); after the check it discards the 64-bit intensities (`wide`): the routes of `Engine.runs` use only `intensity`.
- Panic: `Engine::new` installs a `panic::set_hook` (`panic.rs`) that writes the message in `__mzlabPanic` before the abort; the worker's fatal answer carries `panic` and `mem`, the supervisor keeps the last one in `lastPanic`.
- Recycling: `rust-worker.js` answers with `mem` (bytes of linear memory); `rust-bridge.js` recycles the worker (`stats.recycled`) when `mem` exceeds max(budget in use, 192 MB) and the engine has been idle for 30 s or the page goes to the background, and reopens the files as after a failure, without counting it or warning.
- Supervisor: a death with the out-of-memory signature (`unreachable`/`rust_oom` above 64 MB) drops one level (`budget_down` of Rust, message `{op:"down"}` and answer `budget-down`; `stats.lowered`, warning `error.engineLowered`, session level in `mzlab.memLevel`); the second within a minute drops to 192 at most, the third (or a death at the lowest level) turns it off with `error.engineOffMemory`; the other deaths follow the rule `error.engineFallback`.
- Compilation: the `.wasm` is compiled once in `rust-bridge.js`; the same `WebAssembly.Module` goes to `rust-worker.js` (`{op:"module"}`, `null` = compile by itself) and to the pools.
- Index: `idxpool.js` passes that `Module` (or one compiled once with `compileStreaming`) to every worker with `{op:"module"}`, which `idx-worker.js` waits for. The index and `.wiff` pools share a gate (`makeGate`, `poolSize`: at most 2 compute workers together on iPad/phone, `min(core − 1, 4)` on computer): whoever frees a place wakes the other pool.
- Index helpers (`idx-aiuti.js`, only with isolation): each is an engine instance (initial `.wasm` memory plus the arrays of one spectrum, outside the builder's peak budget); the window (4 per helper) keeps one `SharedArrayBuffer` slot per job, 1.25 × the maximum of base64 text and answer (16 B + 20 B per peak: 8 m/z, 12 intensities; in multiples of 4 KiB): ~150 KB for an HR spectrum of 6000 points, 2 MB for 3 helpers. Slots and helpers close at the end of the build.
- Weak layer of `align` (`series::finish`, `spill.rs`): the weak tables of all files stay in memory (29 bytes per row) until they exceed half of the free space; beyond that they go to a `Spill` (`put(k, bytes)`/`get(k)`, table sorted by m/z) and are aligned in m/z slabs, keeping the m/z (8 bytes per row) and the recurring rows. Without a `Spill` the phase is refused with `error.memoryBudget` (phase `align`); a `Spill` inside the module is counted on the budget.
- `align_series_to(..., budget_mb, spill)`: OPTIONAL `spill` `{put(k, Uint8Array), get(k)}` (`spill_js.rs`, JS arrays of the worker outside the module memory); absent = in memory, `feat-worker.js` does not change.
- JS `Spill` test: `tests/test_feat_wasm.py` with the series `debole` (`tools/feat_mirato.py`: `piccolo` plus 5000 weak features per file); at 1.5 and 0.5 MB `tests/feat_wasm_node.js --spill 1` counts `spill puts 6` (one per file) and `gets` 42 / 120 (slabs × files), same SHA-256 as the in-memory route; without `spill` or with a `get` that throws: `error.memoryBudget` phase `align`.
- Known limit: the mzML of a large `.raw` stays in memory as a Blob during the conversion.
- Maps and XIC: regions have at most 2048 (RT) × 1024 (m/z) `f32` cells (8 MB; without `nrt`/`nmz` 400, the session grid does not change: zoom asks for a new region); 3D sticks at most 200,000 points, chosen in Rust (`sticks`); `subxic` does not load the peak table of an overview (tens of millions of peaks): for every ppm window it reads only its peaks from the index (`each_peak`), same JSON.
- The data folder pointed to by the browser is a handle in `mzlab_lab`, store `kv`, key `cartella.puntata` (nothing in OPFS until a file is opened, then the rest of this file applies); `docs/agenti/data-folder.md`.
- Peak of the `.mzidx` of a vendor file in the browser (`build_mzidx_native`): pass 1 keeps at most `budget / 4` (`builderBudget`) of peaks plus 8 MB of scratch partition buffers; the bucket rounds keep at most `budget / 2` (32 MB with the scratch) minus the fixed part of the builder; the per-scan index tables and the JSON of the rows (~35 MB on 100,000 scans) stay.
- The large file is never in memory: in OPFS live the copy, the scratch (~20 B per peak) and the index being written.
- Format newer than the program (`error.newerFormat`, read-only, never overwritten): `docs/agenti/new-format.md`.
- Tab measurement: `python3 tests_e2e/e2e_memoria_misure.py` (no threshold; `MZLAB_E2E_JSON=<file>` writes the JSON) prints at 5 moments (page open, engine ready, demo Full Scans open, after the map, after Tabui) PSS of renderer/GPU/browser (`/proc`), JS heap and WASM memory of page and workers, bytes copied or transferred to the workers, canvases (w×h×4 B, also hidden or off the DOM), estimate of `CACHE`, `SC.m` and other globals; then the table in MB with a proposal per item.
- Measurement, limits: the PSS of a dedicated worker cannot be separated from that of the page that creates it: for workers, heap and WASM memory count. The proposals are not fixed MB ceilings (they would take resources from an upload): data in OPFS in windows, `f32`, zero-copy transfers, idle workers closed (WASM memory does not shrink), hidden canvases at low resolution, caches limited in bytes and in share of the free space.
- Reductions: `closePanel` takes the panel canvases to 1×1 before `p.el.remove()`; the `feat-worker.js` pool (`IDLE_MS` 20 s, `idleRest`) closes workers and archive when the queue is empty and recreates them on the next request (`pool.workers`).
- Reductions: `impronta.js` closes the hash worker after 10 s without jobs; the `SC` cache also has a byte limit (`SC.maxBytes`, `scTrim`: oldest out, never the scans just downloaded by the same request). Per-state test: `tests_e2e/e2e_memoria_riduzioni.py`.
- Not reduced: `tpmine-worker` (holds the Dig state), `CACHE` (1.7 MB), canvases of panels open but hidden (flicker without a visual test).
- Effect of the reductions (MB, one run, noise ~±25 MB on the renderer): hidden canvases 1.4 (without 5.9); GPU from Pipeline with panels closed 44.9 and 39.0 (without 53.8 and 53.4); renderer with panels closed 305.9 (without 269.3; drop from Pipeline −35 against −44: within the noise); workers alive after 40 s 4 (without 5).
- Read block of the `.wasm` engine (`BLOCK` in `mzlab-wasm/src/blocks.rs`): 4 MiB, a single constant; the JS buffers (`readerFor`, `openSource`) grow to the requested length. The block held by `BlockReader` counts 4 MiB in the peak per open file; the write queue of `BlockWriter` is also 4 MiB.
- Buffers returned by `recycle` (`bufpool.js`, used by `rust-worker.js`): the next answer takes the smallest that suffices (best fit); at most 8 and 64 MB are kept, the oldest leave first, one larger than 64 MB does not enter. A single `FileReaderSync` serves all the file readers of the worker.

- When "file too large" appears, measurements by format and number of files, files open together: `docs/agenti/memoria-limiti.md`.
