# Panel actions (`azioni/1`) and keyboard menu

- **Registry** (`mzlab/web/azioni.js`, classic script after `esperimenti.js`): every entry of the right-click menu, of a panel's bar and of the palette is an action `{id, group, views, nome(c), voce?(c), tip?(c), svg?(c), available?(c), attiva?(c), visible?(c), menu?, barra?, esegui(c)}`, registered in its module's file with `AZIONI.registra` (a duplicate id or unknown group throws an error). `c` is the request context: `{vista, p, …}` plus what the module needs (the map passes its adapter).
- **Icons** (`azioni-icone.js`, before `azioni.js`): `AZIONI_ICONE.DISEGNI` (key → 16×16 SVG strokes, `currentColor`), `POR_ID` (action id → key) and `svg(key)` (decorative, `aria-hidden`). `registra` writes `icona` from the table if the action does not carry one.
- **Icon in the menu**: `AZIONI.icona(a, c)` gives `a.svg(c)` (the map keeps its own) otherwise the table's icon; `per` puts it in `icon` and `menu()` (`explore.js`) draws it on the left in `span.ic`, with the check mark on the right (`span.ck`) in the toggleable entries.
- **Layout** (group `disp`, views `*`, `arrange.js`): in the right-click menu only `disp.su` and `disp.giu`, in ONE row: consecutive entries with the same `fila` become an icon-only `div.mrow` (`title`, `aria-label`; `per` groups them, `menu()` draws them).
- **Layout, the rest**: `disp.arrange` (one entry for every other panel), `disp.intera`, `disp.uguali`, `disp.ripristina` have `menu: false`: they stay in the palette (active panel, `barra.js`) and in the ⠿ handle menu (with «Restore layout»); they are also done by dragging. Grey when not needed; absent on the map (`mappa.js`).
- **Hints** (`suggerimenti.js`, `SUGG.chiedi(id, mostra, attesa)`): single queue of tours, «The tools are here» and «Drag here to tile»; only one is open, the others wait until it is closed.
- **New action**: adds its id to `POR_ID` (`test_azioni.py` and `e2e_menu_coerenti` check that every action has an existing icon).
- **Menu** = `AZIONI.per(c)`: the view's actions in group order (`AZIONI.GROUPS`, a single table for all panels; within a group `ordine`, then registration order), a separator between groups; an action with `voci(c)` gives several rows with the same id (one per open XIC, per measurement…).
- **Unavailable entries** (`off`): not shown at all in any menu; `AZIONI.pulisci` (a single function, called by `menu()`) removes them and removes the separators left first, last or doubled; an informational row (`info: true`) stays.
- `menu()` (`explore.js`) writes `data-azione=<id>` on every entry: it is what the tests compare. 
- **Execution**: `AZIONI.esegui(id, c)` runs only an action of the view and available; `AZIONI.elenco(c, "barra")` gives the actions with `barra: true`.
- **Who registers**: the 2D and 3D map (`mappa.js`, views `map` and `map3d`, `data-map.md`).
- **Spectrum** (view `spec`, context `{p, a, m, has, lab, e, px, py, pol}`): the core in `spettro.js` (copy, XIC and overlay, MS2 of an LR peak, measurements, annotation, isotope profile, freeze/relink), the modules in their own files: `dda.js` (MS2 of the HR peak, DDA trio, MS2 beside), `liste.js` (contaminants), `elementare.js` (formula), `visualizza.js` (marks, bench options), `libreria.js`, `workflow.js` (MGF/MSP), `fair.js` (MassBank/SIRIUS).
- **Click-on-a-peak rule** (`spec.peak` in `spettro.js`, double click on the Full Scan spectrum; `e2e_ms2_clic`): opens the MS2 if an MS2 scan of that precursor exists (Bench tolerance in HR, 0.6 in unit resolution), otherwise the XIC; never just one of the two silently (the menu always has XIC, and MS2 grey if missing).
- **Method inclusion list** (view `metodo`, context `{f, v, i, ms2}`, `metodo-incl.js`): `metodo.xic` (group `xic`) and `metodo.ms2` (group `ms2`, grey without a suitable MS2); the same actions as the «XIC» and «MS2» buttons of every row, of the right-click and of Shift+F10 (the `#ctx` menu goes inside the modal `<dialog>` while it is open).
- **Chromatogram, XIC, MRM** (views `chrom`, `xic`, `mrm`, context `{p, x, k, near, px, py, e}`: RT under the pointer, nearest file, nearby annotation): `eventi.js` registers spectrum at RT and average (`chrom.specAtRt`, `chrom.avgSel`; not in MRM, `ESP.spettri(p)`), XIC from the TIC, «add ion» (`xic.addIon`) and «annotate the point»; the modules their own: `visualizza.js` (bench), `gradiente.js` (pressure, gradient), `dda.js` (MS2 beside), `libreria.js` and `composizione.js` (identify all, MSn tree), `picchi.js` (integration), `grafici.js` (background of a stretch).
- **Trace menu**: `chrom.specAtRt` and `chrom.avgSel` have `menu: false` (click, drag, double click and Enter give them); «Integrate the peak» (`picchi.js`, `canInt`) exists only on XIC, MRM and MS² chromatograms, never on totals (TIC, BPC, UV).
- **Conditional entries and experiment**: `visible` = the action does not concern that data (HR only, bench only, MS² only, without pressure) and the entry is absent; `available` = a condition is missing right now (no peak, no selection, no isotope profile, no zoom) and the entry is not shown. `ESP.di(p)` gives a panel's experiment (`"mrm"` for MRM, otherwise its tab).
- **Common to spectrum, chromatogram, XIC and MRM** (`eventi.js`): `view.resetZoom` (group `reset`, at the top, grey without zoom), `ann.edit` and `ann.delete` (group `ann`, at the end, «Edit/Delete the note «text»»: present only with a nearby annotation, `c.near`; delete removes the user's note, edit = double click). **Double click**: `cv.ondblclick` calls `AZIONI.doubleClick(c)` (with `c.dbl`), which runs the first available action with `doubleClick(c)` true by `doubleClickOrder` (annotation 1, zoom 2, main action 5-9: `chrom.specAtRt`, `spec.full`); the map keeps its own (`MAPPA.dbl`).
- **`ctxFor`** (`eventi.js`) adds the «m/z …» row (spectrum) or «RT …» (traces) and the nearby annotation `near`; the rest comes from the registry.
- **Keyboard menu** (WCAG 2.1.1): Shift+F10 and the Menu key on the focused chart open the same menu as the right-click (`kbdMenu`, `eventi.js`): at the chromatogram cursor if visible, at the highest visible peak of the spectrum, otherwise at the middle of the chart; focus goes to the first available entry, Esc returns it to the chart. The 2D map and the 3D viewer have their own handler (`mappa.js` `key`, `mappa3d.js`) that opens the menu at the crosshair point.
- **Tests**: `tests/test_azioni.py` (order, rows of an action, grey entries, unique ids, it/en texts of the map and spectrum actions); `e2e_menu_coerenti` (map: right click = Shift+F10 = registry, bar and palette only with registered actions; spectrum, chromatogram and MRM: Shift+F10 = right click at the same point, focus inside and returned; every spectrum, chromatogram and MRM entry has `data-azione` of its view).
- **Keys** (`shortcut` field of an action: `{key|code, mod?, alt?, shift?, where: "chart"|"view", label}`; `azioni.js` `TASTI`, added to `AZIONI` as `testo`, `premuto`, `conTasto`): ONE `keydown` on the document. `where: "chart"` = letter on the focused chart (never in `input`, `textarea`, `select`, `[contenteditable]`, never with a `dialog[open]`), context = RT of the cursor (traces) or highest visible peak (spectrum), like Shift+F10; `where: "view"` = Ctrl/⌘ or Alt+digit (`e.code`) in the whole view.
- **Existing key wins**: a chart handler that has already done `preventDefault` (map: F, M, S, L) is not doubled.
- **Registered keys**: X (`spec.xic`, `chrom.xic`, `xic.addIon`), Enter (`chrom.specAtRt` spectrum at cursor, `spec.peak` MS2 or XIC of the peak), I (`chrom.intAuto`), R (`spec.measureKey`), F (`view.fullscreen`, all panels), ⌘/Ctrl+O (`app.apri`, file choice), ⌘/Ctrl+E (`app.esporta`, report; only with at least one file open, otherwise the key stays with the browser), Alt/⌥+1-3 (`app.dati`, `app.disegno`), `?` (`app.scorciatoie`).
- **Active chart keys**: Backspace (`chart.intera`: full view of the active chart, with a selected peak it deletes it), Del (`chart.elimina`); those without a module of their own are registered in `azioni-tasti.js`. `AZIONI.testo(s)` writes the key for the system (⌘O on Mac, Ctrl+O elsewhere).
- **«Shortcuts» panel** (`?`, `window.SCORCIATOIE`, `azioni-tasti.js`): `<dialog id="scorc">` generated from the registry (icon, short label `scorc.*`, key); first «On this chart» (the active panel), then «Everywhere»; without a focused chart the chart keys are under «On charts (with focus on the chart)» (`scorc.grafici`), never under «Everywhere»; one row per label and key; «Other keys» opens the old help (arrows, space, zoom). The palette shows the key of the commands with an `azione` field (`report` → `app.esporta`).
- **Key tests**: `tests/test_azioni_tasti.py` (no duplicate key in the same view, none reserved by the browser, Mac/Windows text, `premuto`); `e2e_scorciatoie` (every key on its chart, no effect in fields or without focus, `?` with axe, key in the palette). Adding a key = `shortcut` field + `scorc.*` key in `lang/`.
- **Single Esc** (`azioni-esc.js`, `ESC`): ONE capturing `keydown`; one press does ONE thing, the first open by priority: menus, tooltips and windows (`dialog[open]`, `#ctx`, `.corrm`, `#subpop`, `#helppop`, `#uipset`, `#stmenu`, palette) keep their Esc and `ESC` stops; then `ESC.registra({id, prio, aperto, chiudi})`: 30 pending measurement/first step (`eventi.js`), 40 scan playback (`scroll.js`), 50 panel fullscreen (`pannelli.js`).
- **Focus after a window** (`azioni-esc.js`): when a `dialog` closes, if focus stays on the body or on a hidden control (the opening came from an entry of a menu that closed, e.g. the header «⋯» → XIC), it goes to the last focused control still visible outside the windows (the «⋯»), otherwise to the first visible control of the nearest container. The floating calculator (`#calcdlg`, not a `dialog`) closed by Esc or × returns focus to `#np-calc2` (`calcBox.close`, `eventi.js`). Test: `e2e_dialogo_fuoco`.
- **Esc and drawer**: the side drawer (`barra.js`) closes only if `ESC` did not act (event already `defaultPrevented`). A new entry is registered with `ESC.registra`, never with another `keydown` on Esc. `app.esc` gives the «Esc» row to the Shortcuts panel.
- **Downloads and Options**: the downloads (`download: true`, `menu: false`: MGF, MSP, MassBank, SIRIUS) are not in the right-click but in the panel's download button (`AZIONI.scarichi(c)`, `pannelli.js`); «Options…» (`spec.disp`, `chrom.disp`, gear icon) opens the `visualizza.js` panel with scan markers, contaminant removal and list search; `e2e_menu_limpio`.
