# Statistics in HR (feature table, blank, difference from the reference, PCA)
The Bench and the rest of the HR world: `high-resolution.md`; the common statistics sections: `data-tools.md`.

- **Statistics in HR** (sections of `STAT` with `hr: true`, only with `BANCO.on()`; the common sections: `data-tools.md`; synthetic data `ds.hr_series`; tests `e2e_statistica`, `tests/test_statistica.py`, `tests/test_tabella.py`). **Feature table** (`tabella.js`, `TABELLA`): the feature × file matrix that blank and PCA work on.
  The program does not detect features: the table comes from a CSV/TSV export of mzmine (columns `row m/z`, `row retention time`, one area column per file, recognised by the file name; mzmine 2 and 3), from an "m/z; RT" list (each ion measured in all files with `PROFILO.measure`, at most 300) or from the mzFinder table (`UNTUI.state.table`); it stays in memory, `TABELLA.matrix()` returns it in file order.
  **Blank subtraction** (`bianco.js`, `BIANCO`; `statistica.blank_filter`, `/api/stat/blank`): threshold = blank level (maximum or mean of the blank-type files) × the user's factor; kept if at least N non-blank files ("dark" files do not count) are above the threshold; two visible lists (kept, discarded) with blank level, threshold, maximum in the samples and count; click on a row = time profile of the ion; Excel with the two sheets. Parameters in `qqq.bianco`.
  **Difference from the reference** (`statistica.reference_difference`, `/api/stat/reference`; golden data `tests/golden/differenza_hr.json`, tests in `tests/test_statistica.py`): a single pure function per feature × file. Reference = explicit choice or, by default, time ≤ 0, blank or dark (same rule as `split_columns` of TP_Mine); treated = the other files that are not blank, dark, QC or standard.
  Per feature: reference level (maximum or mean), maximum of the treated and its file, ratio, log2FC with pseudocount = 3 × the median of the positive blank areas (otherwise of the reference, otherwise `FLOOR`; never below `FLOOR`) or given, "absent in the reference" (level ≤ pseudocount), shape over time (Spearman ρ on the per-time mean of the "sample" files with a time, time of the maximum; labels `rises`, `rises_falls`, `falls`, `flat` only to sort and filter),
  replicates of the maximum (mean, CV, detections) and the `blank_filter` threshold (level × factor) with the kept (`absent_in_reference` | `above_threshold`) and discarded (`no_signal` | `below_reference` | `too_few_files`) lists. No blank and no time-0 file: clear error. Another table row with the same m/z and shifted RT stays a row of its own.
  **Interface** (`riferimento-corpo.js` (lazy; registers `riferimento.js`), `RIFERIMENTO`; section "Difference from the reference" of `STAT`, `hr: true`, after the blank; tests `e2e_differenza_hr`): works on the feature table (`TABELLA`), recomputes itself when the table or the sample table changes (events `tabella`, `samples`). The default reference comes from the metadata (`SAMPLES`: time ≤ 0, blank, dark), it is changed by ticking the files and "Default" restores it; reference, treated and pseudocount (with its origin) are always written above the table.
  Parameters (factor, maximum or mean, minimum of files, pseudocount "auto" or number, `FLOOR`) in `qqq.reference`. Table sortable (button headers with `aria-sort`) and filterable by state (kept by default, discarded, all) and by shape or "absent in the reference"; names `row ID`, `row m/z`, `row retention time` as in mzmine; at most 500 rows shown, with the count.
  "Same identity as…" shows (does not remove) adducts and in-source fragments from `statistica.identity_links` (`ionfamily.ADDUCTS`, `NEUTRAL_LOSSES`, ESI+ only); the "In your list" column (name, Δppm from `LISTE.userMatches`) exists only if the user has loaded a list (`LISTE.hasUser`). Click or Enter on a row: `PROFILO.show` (time profile), `xicDirect` (XIC in all files of the panel), MS1 spectrum (`newSpec`) at the apex in the file with the maximum (RT from the profile) and, for a DDA file, `DDA.make` + `DDA.openMs2` on the MS² scan of the ion.
  TSV (lines `# parameter<TAB>value` at the top) and Excel (sheets "Features" and "Parameters") with the reference and the parameters used.
- **HR spectrum, markers and menu** (`picchi.js`, `dda.js`, menu actions in `actions.md`; test `e2e_spettro_hr`): the triangles of the MS² scans on the Full Scan spectrum (`dda-marcatori.js`, module loaded by `DDA.decorate` at the first drawing of the trio) are all visible with the "Marker" switch (`p.mk`, `markOn`; on by default up to `MARK_N` = 20), otherwise only the one of the peak under the mouse or finger (`hoverMark`, `p._mk`); the m/z labels of the highest peaks do not overlap (distance = text width).
  The empty MS2 panel says why (`DDAMARK.why`, computed when the Full Scan is drawn; same text for the screen reader, `p._say`): averaged Full Scan (`dda.why.avg`/`avg0`), precursors outside the m/z zoom (`dda.why.zoom`), only ions fragmented in nearby scans (`dda.why.near`), no MS2 within ±1 min (`dda.why.none`), file without MS2 (`dda.why.file`); test `e2e_dda_ms2_vie`.
  "N MS²" button in a group of its own before "XIC" in the Full Scan (`dda-elenco.js`, loaded by `dda-marcatori.js`; with a narrow header it goes into "⋯"): how many MS2 started from the scans shown and their list (listbox: ↑ ↓ Home End, Enter opens the most intense MS2 of the precursor, Esc closes), one group per precursor within the profile tolerance; from `api/dda_window?k&r0&r1[&hr]` (`ddafinestra.rs`, reference `tools/dda_reference_window.py`, parity in `hr_parity.rs` and `parity_routes.rs`).
  An MS2 without precursor intensity in the file (Thermo `.raw`) takes it from the highest point of its parent Full Scan in the isolation window, at most ±0.5 Da (route with the source, `mzml_route`; at most 400 Full Scans per window: beyond that it stays null and the response carries `pintCut`, which the "N MS²" list and the button title state with `dda.win.cut`); site and app/CLI equal byte for byte (`thermo_synthetic_hr.rs`, `parity_routes.rs`).
  With `mz` (and `tol` in Da) `api/dda_window` keeps only the MS2 of that precursor: "Follow this ion" and the precursor list of `dda.js` (`pints`) read only their parent Full Scans and pick the most intense MS2 even on a `.raw` (test `e2e_dda_pint`).
  On an averaged Full Scan each group has its own numbered flag ▼: the click opens its MS2 and leaves the average. CLI: `mzlab-cli spectrum spectrum dda-window FILE --from MIN --to MIN [--set hr=1|0] [--out PATH]` (`dda_window_cmd.rs`, same bytes as the route, `dda_window_tests.rs`).
  Header of the MS2 panel: ‹ i/N › (`dda-naviga.js`, loaded by `dda-marcatori.js` at the first MS2 drawn) goes to the previous/next MS2 of the same precursor (profile tolerance, acquisition order); Shift+click and ← → go to the previous/next MS2 in time (`DDA.step`), which from an empty panel starts from the chromatogram cursor (if missing, from the Full Scan shown).
  Click (or tap) on a Full Scan peak (`DDA.click` → `DDAMARK.peak`, before the ruler and the measurement, never with an XIC pending): its MS2; if it was not fragmented in that scan, the nearest MS2 in time within ±1 min with precursor in tolerance, with the warning `dda.peak.near`; on an average, the best member of the group. Flag hit areas 24 px. "▲ MS²" (`DDAELENCO.tri`, group `.hdkeep` that `fitHd` does not move into "⋯") turns on the MS2 triangles on the chromatogram of the trio (`ms2tri`).
  DIA file (`DDAMARK.dia`, `f.acq` with "DIA"): the MS2 are isolation windows, not ions; no flag on the Full Scan, the empty MS2 panel says `dda.dia.why`, the "N MS²" list names each window (`dda.win.dia1`/`diaN`), the click on a peak opens the MS2 of the narrowest window that contains it (band on the Full Scan, no precursor line on the MS2); the list closes on `pointerdown` outside (a tap on iPad gives no `mousedown`); test `e2e_dda_dia`.
  Right click on an HR peak: after the header, "Open the MS² scan (precursor …)" (`DDA.ms2For`, Bench tolerance) if it exists, then Generate formula, Marker, Remove contaminants, Display options, then the groups with separators: XIC, contaminants, tools (measure, annotate, isotopes), DDA, library/exports, freeze (the entries carry a `g` tag and an `aria`; `menu()` accepts `aria` and `check`).
  "Remove contaminants" (`p.noCont`, `LISTE.keller`, ppm = `UIP.hrPpm`): peaks compatible with the Keller list leave the peaks shown while the entry is on (note in the legend); same entries in "Display options" and in the palette (`marker`, `nocont`, `openms2`).
- **Exploratory PCA** (`pca.js`, `PCA`; `statistica.pca_analysis`, `/api/stat/pca`; tests `tests/test_statistica.py` with the golden data `tests/golden/statistica_pca.json` meant for the Rust port, `e2e_statistica`): a view, not a test (no p-value, never "significant"). Off below 5 samples (non-blank and non-dark).
  Fixed order: (1) feature filter: above the blank (same parameters as `BIANCO`; skipped and declared if no file is a blank), maximum area ≥ intensity threshold, present (area ≥ threshold) in at least k samples; (2) declared imputation (1/5 of the feature minimum, count shown); (3) log2 and Pareto scaling by default (auto and none selectable, with the note that "auto" inflates noise with few samples);
  (4) SVD, explained variance of each component (on the axes of the two plots and in the list), sign of the components fixed (largest loading positive); (5) stability: PCA repeated leaving out one sample at a time (scaling recomputed, components realigned by cosine), `1/(1+σ_loading/typical loading)` per component, weighted by the square of the loading; unstable loadings are paler; (6) 95 % ellipse for the replicate groups with at least 3 samples.
  The scores are joined in time order (centre of the replicate group) with the viridis gradient; replicates have the same colour. The "?" explains the arch effect in two lines. A click on a row of the loadings table or on a plot point opens the XIC of the ion (a single reused panel, `p.pcaIon`) and the time profile (`PROFILO.show`). Excel with scores, loadings and variance. Parameters in `qqq.pca`.
- **Kendrick mass defect** (`mzlab_core::kendrick`, route `POST api/kmd`; `kmd-calc.js` keeps only `UNITS`; `kmd.js`, `KMD`: section "Kendrick mass defect" of `STAT`, `hr: true`, after the PCA; tests `e2e_kmd`, `tests/test_kmd.py`): works on the feature table (`TABELLA`). KM = m/z × (nominal mass / exact mass of the unit), KMD = round(KM) − KM; units CF2 (default), CF2O, C2F4, CH2. The series are CANDIDATE (never an identification): KMD window (mDa) with at least N different nominal Kendrick masses.
  Evidence of a series: members, steps in units (1 = consecutive, 2 = a member is missing), KMD span, Spearman ρ between mass and RT. KM versus KMD plot (colour and shape per series, 24 px targets, data table `KMD.plotTable` via `TDATI.watch`; beyond 2000 ions `kmd-nuvola.js` draws it as one path per series, see below), series table (click = only its ions), ion table; click on a point or a row = XIC (`p.kmdIon`) and time profile. Parameters in `qqq.kmd`; Excel (Features, Series, Parameters).
  Dense plot (`KMDCLOUD`, beyond `CLOUD` = 2000 ions; test `e2e_kmd_denso`, measurement `tools/bench_table_hr.py`): one `path.kmd-cloud` per series (same shapes, colours and transparencies; points on the same tenth of a unit written once), click and pointer hover = nearest ion within 12 units (`#kmd-hov` with its tooltip), the chosen ion stays a button, the keyboard goes through the tables; a new choice leaves the plot in place and changes only dimming and chosen ion.
- **PFAS: MD/C and diagnostic fragments** (`pfas-calc.js`, pure arithmetic as an ES module, node tests in `tests/test_pfas.py`; `pfas.js`, `PFAS`: ES module `m:pfas.js` with `STAT.add` `pfas`, `hr: true`, after the feature table; tests `e2e_kmd`): for each ion of the table (the first 300 in table order; if it has more a note under the summary says so, `pfas.capped`) it asks `/api/composition` (Rust) for the formulas with ≥3 F (C 1-30, H 0-10, O 0-6, N 0-1, S 0-1, editable ppm) and keeps the one with the smallest error, as a CANDIDATE with the number of formulas left.
  m/C = m/z ÷ C and MD/C = (m/z − round(m/z)) ÷ C; plot (square = at least one MS2 evidence, colour = KMD series, table `PFAS.plotTable` via `TDATI.watch`).
  The MS2 of the open HR files (`DDA.get`) that isolated the ion within ±0.5 min of its RT (the 3 nearest) are searched for diagnostic fragments (CF3, C2F5, C3F7, C4F9, FSO3, SO3; ion according to polarity), neutral losses (HF, 2HF, CO2, HF+CO2, CF2, C2F4, C3F6, SO3) and pairs of peaks at a CF2 distance; the scan with the most evidence counts.
  "Evidence" = fragments + losses + 1 if there are CF2 pairs, with the error in ppm: hints, never a label. Click on a point or a row = XIC (`p.pfasIon`) and MS2 scan (`p.pfasMs2`). Parameters in `qqq.pfas`; Excel (Features, MS2, Parameters). Without files with MS2 the evidence table says so; the two tables show 200 rows, with the note `kmd.more` (the rest in the Excel).
- **In Rust as steps**: the PCA is `mzlab_core::pca` (step `table.pca`) and the KMD is `mzlab_core::kendrick` (step `table.kmd`, parity `tests/golden/kmd.json`); the Welch/ANOVA tests are `table.stats` and have no view (`stat.md`).
- **`table compare` (candidate transformation products, low and high resolution)**: `mzlab-cli table compare TABLE --ref SEL --blank SEL [--samples FILE.json] [--quantity area|height] [--factor F] [--pseudocount auto|N] [--format json|csv] [--dry-run] [--out PATH]` and the Flow step `table.compare@1` (port `report` = `compare/1`, schema `docs/schemi/compare-1.json`) put every feature of a time-course table against its reference (t0) and its blank.
  Engine `mzlab_core::statcmp` wraps `statref::reference_difference` (levels, pseudocount and its origin, shape, rho); `mzlab-feat/src/flow_compare.rs` reads the table (mzmine full CSV: areas only; `.mzfeat`: area or height) and the optional `samples/1`; `.wasm` `flowstat` (`flow_compare`) and the native executor (`flow_tablecmp.rs`) give the same bytes as the CLI.
  Selectors: comma lists of case-insensitive globs over the column names, plus `role:blank`, `role:reference`, `time:0` (these need `--samples`). Nothing matched: exit 5 (`err.compare.selector`); a column in both lists: exit 2 (`err.compare.overlap`); unreadable table or samples: 4; `--out` unwritable: 6. `--dry-run` prints `compare-plan/1` (the columns of each role, the time of each column, the choices) and computes no row.
  Per feature: ratio `(x + pc) / (y + pc)` and log2FC against the reference level and against the blank level (maximum of the blank columns), `blank_rho` (Spearman of the timed blank columns, null under 3), and a class that only sorts and filters, first rule that matches: `no_signal` (maximum ≤ pseudocount), `blank_artefact` (ratio to the blank under the factor, or the blank itself rises with time), `candidate` (absent in the reference or ratio to the reference ≥ factor), `decreasing`, `unchanged`; every row keeps its `reasons`.
  The CSV of `--format csv` is one row per feature with the same keys. A table read from a CSV is rounded by the export, so the rows match the `.mzfeat` of the same run in ids, class, reasons and shape but not digit for digit. Tests: `statcmp_test.rs`, `table_compare_cmd_tests.rs`, parity `tests/test_compare_wasm.py`, golden `tests/golden/compare_lr.json` (`MZLAB_UPDATE_COMPARE=1`).
