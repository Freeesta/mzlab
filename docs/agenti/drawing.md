# Draw (`draw.js`, Ketcher in `vendor/ketcher`)
The core is in CLAUDE.md.

- The editor fills the full width and height below the header (`#kframe` with no border or margins, `calc(100svh - var(--hh))`, falling back to `vh`); the panels sit on the right or, on narrow screens, below.
- Ketcher loads only when the tab is opened; OpenChemLib on the first property calculation. The `<style>` injected by `hideMacro` adapts the toolbars and hides Ketcher's help and info; initial zoom 100% (`fitZoom`). Right click: "Copy SMILES", "Center drawing".
- **Zoom** in the top bar, after the gear, full screen and crosshair (centre only): "Zoom in", "Zoom out" (x1.25 / x0.8, 0.2-4) and "Reset" (100% and centred, `fitZoom`), `data-testid` `zoom-*-tp` (`addZoomButtons`); no +/-/0 keys because Ketcher uses them for the charge.
- **"New"** (Ketcher's `clear-canvas` and Ctrl/Cmd+Del or Backspace; `disegno-nuovo.js`, test `e2e_disegno_nuovo`): with something on the canvas it asks "Clear the drawing?" (`confirmBox` of `memoria-avviso.js`, "Cancel" has focus, Esc and a click outside cancel), then clears with Ketcher's action (undo unchanged); empty canvas = no question. `TPDraw.load` (tray, infusion graph, Tabui) replaces without asking.
- **Editor tools** (`TOOLS`; choice in `qqq.disegno.strumenti`): on are rings, arrows, chains, reorder, file; shapes and images always; off are aromaticity, stereochemistry, S/R groups, check/3D, mapping, generic atoms, biology. A new group = one `TOOLS` row with the `data-testid`s. `applyTools` hides a toolbar group only if none of its buttons has a box (`getClientRects`); a bare button (periodic table) is a group of its own. `fit()` enlarges the bars up to 1.2 (side) and 1.05 (top), on touch up to 1.55 and 1.3 (40 px).
- **Atom symbol size** (`disegno-font.js`, ES module; "Editor tools" tab): small 13 / medium 16 (default) / large 20, in `qqq.disegno.font`; applied with `ketcher.editor.setOptions({fontsz})` (`setSettings` has no effect) at start and on change. The exported SVG does not follow the size. Test in `e2e_strumenti_ketcher`.
- **Search a compound in "Quick draw"** (`disegno-cerca.js`, ES module, started by `initCerca`): the `#ex-smi` field ("Paste a SMILES or search a compound") is also a combobox (`field()` of `molecules-field.js` with the `input` option) on the molecule database (`search` of the engine: name, synonym, formula; equal, prefix, word start, substring; accents stripped from the typed text).
  A text made only of SMILES tokens (`looksLikeSmiles`) is not searched and is drawn as usual with "Draw" or Enter. Choosing a suggestion (arrows+Enter or click) puts its SMILES in the field and presses "Draw"; the `molecole/catalogo.csv` catalogue loads on the first focus of the field.
  Enter or "Draw" on a name (neither SMILES nor `InChI=`) draws the first suggestion (`nameToSmiles`); with no results nothing is sent to Ketcher (`addFragment` would draw query atoms) and under the field "Compound not found and not a SMILES" appears. The same notice appears when pasting a name onto the drawing (`guardPaste`, `paste` event of the iframe, single lines that are not SMILES/InChI/molfile/KET). Tests `e2e_disegno_cerca`, `e2e_disegno_veloce_nomi`.
- **Dropdown bar** (`disegno-barra.js`, ES module, `mountBar(d, t, own)` called by `hideMacro`): a click on the button opens the menu AND selects the tool shown on the button, a second click closes; a proxy button replaces groups of Ketcher buttons (registry `GROUPS`: rings, charge +/- and radical; `role=toolbar`/`menu`, keyboard, 40 px on touch, `draw.bar.*` tooltips); the choice does `.click()` on the real hidden button (the "selected" class is mirrored by a `MutationObserver`).
- **Dropdown bar, self-check**: a group with a missing `data-testid` stays native; if the bar disappears from the page Ketcher's bars come back (`html[data-tp-bar]` = `menu`|`native`). The "rings" group follows the `rings` tool (`update(choice)` from `applyTools`). Arrows, shapes and bonds stay native: they are Ketcher dropdowns whose members are not in the DOM until opened. Test `e2e_disegno_barra`.
- **Radical** (`disegno-radicale.js`, "Radical •" entry of the charge menu; entry with `icon` in `GROUPS`, action from `own`): with atoms selected it adds or removes the dot (lone electron, `radical: 2` in the `.ket`, one undo step; Ketcher removes an H) (keyboard route). Without a selection it turns the tool on: a click or tap on the nearest atom (within 0.45 bonds) adds or removes the dot without drawing; Esc or another tool turns it off. The dot appears in the molfile (`M  RAD`), in the SMILES (`[CH2]`) and in the labels (`label/1`, `r`). Test `e2e_radicali`.
- **Right-click menu** (`#ctx`, `disegno-menu.js`, row registry, Information..., PubChem/ChEBI/BioTransformer): `docs/agenti/drawing-menu.md`.
- **Empty canvas and tablet**: exporting an empty canvas shows "Nothing to export" (`#ex-warn`, `role=status`, removed at the first canvas edit) and downloads nothing; the Quick draw notices (`#smi-warn`, `role=status`, colour `--bad`) are announced; without the `.wasm` of the graph the menu does not offer "All breaks..."; with the tabs below the canvas (<=1000 px or touch) an edit made from the tabs (Quick draw) brings the canvas back into view; checkboxes, radios, summaries and the "i" button are at least 24x24 px.
- **Touch targets** (`@media (pointer:coarse)`, test `e2e_disegno_bersagli`): in the panels labels, summaries and "See an example" are 40 px high (checkbox and radio 20 px) and the `#ctx` rows 40 px. In Ketcher `disegno-tablet.js` brings the "Copy" triangle (with `aria-label` `draw.copy.options`) and the window close button to 24 px, and hides "or drag file here" and the image tile.
  A long press (500 ms, no movement) on a button of Ketcher's bars or of `#tp-bar` shows its name in `#tp-tip` (`aria-live=polite`, fade off with `prefers-reduced-motion`) and does not activate it. The `.tp-skip` button ("Go to Quick draw", before the iframe, visible on focus) moves focus to `#ex-smi`.
- **Right panels** can be hidden together (`qqq.disegno.riquadri`; `#side-toggle` looks like `#sb-toggle` of Data: icon + word, above the panels or, if hidden, in the top right corner, centred on the top bar with a free band `--tpill`; below 600 px icon only: `placePill`); labels under the molecules always dark on a white canvas; right click on the label = "Copy the formula" and "Copy the mass"; `overscroll-behavior-x` and horizontal `wheel` blocked.
  One by one (`disegno-riquadri.js`, test `e2e_disegno_riquadri`): arrow in the title (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`); at the top and open Quick draw, Expected properties, Export the canvas, the others closed; choice in `qqq.disegno.schede`, applied before paint by the script after the `aside`. A text in one of its `role=status` or the `mzlab-card-open` event opens it without saving it. Explanations as `title` of the title and hidden `aria-describedby`.
- Automatic labels under the structures (formula, mass, "m/z" if charged) and above the arrows (neutral loss and Δm: OPTIONAL, off by default, `#lb-a`; students do the calculation themselves), in an SVG group above the drawing. Name and "TP m/z" are written by the student. SMILES and estimated properties (logP, TPSA...) as estimates. PNG/JPEG/SVG/.ket export.
- **Export panel** (`#exp-card`): images (+ "Transparent"), `.ket`, chemistry (MOL/SDF) in `role=group`; the SDF adduct `#ex-ion` sits behind `#ex-sdfopt`, saved as `NB.drawIon`. Page only.
- **Label numbers** (`disegno-etichette.js`): formula (isotopes `[13C]`/D, every element), charge, exact and nominal mass (sum of the mass numbers), m/z come from `label/1` of `chem/labels.rs` (`labels` of the `grafo` `.wasm`, `feat/grafo` of the app, `mzlab-cli mol label REQUEST|- [--out PATH]`; masses in `mzlab_core::nuclides` from `tools/genera_nuclidi.py`), kept per request: the labels appear when the answer arrives. Arrow: "isotopologue" if only isotopes change; copied formula with |q|>1 `[C3H2O4]2-`. Test `e2e_disegno_chimica`.
- **Formula analysis** (`disegno-analisi.js`, closed `<details id="anal-card">`, imported on opening): of the selected structure (or the only one drawn, otherwise it asks to select one) formula with "Copy", masses (monoisotopic, nominal, average), m/z if charged, mass % per element; "Show the isotope pattern" opens the theoretical sticks M, M+1, ... (keyboard, "Data table" for `TDATI`), titled as formula theory; in the HR world a button opens Composition with `ions[0]`.
  It also opens from the "Formula analysis" row of the right-click menu (`menuAdd` id `analysis`, order 70, in `draw.js`): it opens the card, or recalculates it if open, on the selection or the only structure, and moves focus to the title.
  Numbers from `analysis/1` of `chem/analysis.rs` (`analysis` of the `grafo` `.wasm` with `isotopi-tutti`, `feat/grafo` kind `analysis` of the app, `mzlab-cli mol anal REQUEST|-`): the fields of `label/1`, `average`, `composition` `[{el,count,pct}]`, `ions` (the drawn ion or [M+H]+/[M-H]-), `pattern` `[{off,mz,rel,abund}]` per mass number, sticks >=0.01% of the highest; independent reference `tools/analisi_riferimento.py` -> `tests/dati/anal_golden.json` (22 molecules).
- **SMILES of the selection** (`pieceSmiles`; molfile from `molfileOf` of `disegno-molfile.js`, ES module): each connected piece of the selected atoms goes to Indigo as V2000 in Ketcher's atom order with `M  CHG`, `M  ISO`, `M  RAD`, wedge/hash bonds and atom parities (chiral flag 1 if every centre is absolute), so the SMILES keeps [13C], [2H], radicals and @/@@ like the canvas one. Test `e2e_disegno_chimica`.
- **Bond cutting** (`#cut-card`, `disegno-taglio.js`): the cut line (`#cut-line`, layer `#cut-layer`, Esc turns it off) (one stroke can cross several bonds, ring bonds too) breaks them in the chosen way: homolytic (default, radical dot on each end), heterolytic (the menu asks which piece takes the +; single bonds, two pieces) or "+H". One undo step (`.ket` edit); the pieces move apart, each with its label (formula with •, exact mass, m/z if charged). Candidates: no scores and no comparison with the spectrum. Test `e2e_taglio`.
  Engine `cut/1` of `chem/cut.rs` (`cut` of the `grafo` `.wasm`, `feat/grafo` of the app with `kind: "cut"`, `mzlab-cli mol cut MOL|- --set bonds=A-B[,..] [--set mode=homolytic|heterolytic|hydrogen] [--set cation=..] [--set gap=N]`; parity in `tests/test_feat_grafo_wasm.py`): receives molfile, bonds (0-based positions) and mode, returns for each touched atom charge, radical (`M  RAD` code), H and `pin` (H to pin with `implicitHCount` where Ketcher's valence rule would give another value, e.g. N+ of a nitrenium), and for each piece atoms, shift and label. Errors `error.grafo.cut.*`.
  `label/1` reads `r` (Ketcher's radical code) per atom and gives per label `unpaired`, `electrons` (sum Z + H - charge), `odd` and `kind` (neutral, radical, cation, anion, radicalCation, radicalAnion); the label (and the cut note) puts one • per unpaired electron (•• for the triplet carbene that a cut leaves next to a dot) or one • if only `odd`; the radical ion is "CH5N+•" with m/z = mass - e (M+•). `Mol::from_molfile` removes the H of the `M  RAD`.
  Its implicit H follow the charge (`smiles::valence_for`): a singly charged N, P, O, S takes the valences of the element with the same electrons (N+ 4, S+ 3 or 5, O+ 3, O- 1), so tetramethylammonium, a sulfonium, a nitro group or an N-oxide drawn with separated charges get no extra H; an aromatic O+ or S+ with only its two ring bonds (pyrylium, thiopyrylium written with type 4 bonds) gives a ring electron like a pyridinium N+ and has no H; the H of an aromatic N comes from Ketcher's `MRV_IMPLICIT_H` data group (tests in `sel_tests.rs`, `cut_tests.rs`).
- **Drawing check** (`#chk-card`, `disegno-verifica.js`, ES module, one row in `draw.js`): "Check" sends the canvas molfile (`molfileOf`, query atoms kept) to `check/1`; each warning: a row with written level ("Error"/"Note") and atoms (C1), one click selects them, "Why?" (`aria-expanded`, or the `check-why` row of the menu, `TPDraw.menu.add`) opens problem, why it matters, next step (`draw.check.<code>.what|why|next`); status in `#chk-status` (`role=status`). Only the drawing, never data or spectra. Auto-check off by default. Test `e2e_disegno_verifica`.
  Engine `check/1` of `chem/check.rs` (feature `check`, records of `Mol::drawing_from_molfile`): codes valence, valenceCharge (`fix` = the charge that makes the valence right), charge, isotope, kekule, aromaticH (atom given an aromatic H that another could carry), wedgeMultiple, wedgeNoCentre, wedgeUndecided, query, queryBond, pieces; sulfones, nitro, phosphates, N-oxides, azides without warnings.
  `molkit` `.wasm` (`mzlab-mol-wasm`, loaded at the first check), `feat/grafo` of the app with `kind: "check"`, `mzlab-cli mol check REQUEST|-`; parity on `tests/dati/check_disegno.json` (`test_molkit_wasm.py`, `labels.rs` of the app). Indigo's check stays hidden.
- **Substructure search** (`#sub-card`, `disegno-sottostruttura.js`, ES module, one row in `draw.js`; the card is created at first use, after `#chk-card`): menu rows `sub-find-sel` (`when: "selection"`) and `sub-find-whole` (`when: "whole"`, `TPDraw.menu.add`) take the molfile of the selection or of the whole drawing (`molfileOf`) as query; source = catalogue (`static/molecole/catalogo.csv`) or a chosen file (SDF, SMILES list, CSV with a `smiles` column), read in memory and never saved; optional "Stereo".
  "Search" sends `{query, db, stereo, max: 100}` to `substructure_db/1` (`substructure` of `molkit` loaded with `carica()` of `disegno-verifica.js`; in the app `feat/grafo`, `kind: "sub"`). Outcome in `#sub-status` (`role=status`): candidates, never identifications; list of the first 200 (border `--lv1`), "Data table" (`TDATI.table`, all candidates and unreadable entries). "Show" draws the candidate and selects the atoms and bonds of its matches; "Back to my drawing" restores the previous `.ket`. Test `e2e_disegno_sottostruttura`.
- **Evidence colours** (`#ev-card`, `disegno-evidenza.js` loaded at the first request, `e2e_disegno_evidenza`): `TPDraw.highlight(layers)` (`moiety`, `sites`, `cuts`) sends molfile, selection and world (`BANCO.on()`) to `highlight/1` (`mol.md`) and paints with `editor.highlights` in the `--ev-*` colours (Okabe-Ito; dark, high contrast and `forced-colors` variants); legend in words (`aria-label`), "Data table" (`TDATI`), "Remove the colours"; an edit of the drawing removes them. In LR only the selected atoms.
  A theme change repaints them in the new colours; the `mzlab-ev-cleared` event (number of atoms of the drawing) says when they go away. They are requested by "Show" of the substructure search (`moiety`) and "Show" of the cuts of the MSn graph (`cuts`, `infusion-graph.md`).
- **Fragments** (`disegno-frammenti.js`, `crates/mzlab-feat/src/chem/fragments.rs`): right click on a bond (`editor.findItem`) = two rows with formula, mass and m/z of [F+H]+ (decimals of `#lb-dec`; a click copies them) and "All breaks..." (table `TDATI.table`); a ring bond says it does not break alone.
  The piece goes as a V2000 molfile (`pieceMolfile`, Ketcher's atom order) to the `grafo` `.wasm` of `wasm-feat/` (`fragments`, `frag/1`; each side takes one H per bond order; `M  CHG`/`M  ISO` count: charged side = the ion itself, m/z = mass - e, formula "H4N+"; isotopes "13CH4"; a side with an odd number of electrons, i.e. with a drawn radical, has `odd` and the row and the table write "CH3O•": its [F+H]+ is an odd-electron ion).
- **Mechanism** (`#mech-card`, `disegno-meccanismo.js`, test `e2e_meccanismo`): full-head or half-head arrows dragged on `#mech-layer` (Esc) or from the keyboard (`#mech-key`), lone pairs `#mech-lone`, "•+" `#mech-ion`; `#mech-list` one row per arrow. DAT S-groups `MZLAB_MECH` ("pair|single", start `L|R<k>`/`B<k>-<l>`, end `A<k>`/`B<k>-<l>`) and `MZLAB_LP` ("lp N"), hidden; `draw()` and `addExportMech` draw them.
  `mechanism/1` (`chem/mech.rs`; `grafo` `.wasm`, `kind: "mech"`, `mzlab-cli mol mech`; fixture `mechanism_mclafferty.json`): form only; lone pairs beyond what is possible with `ok: false`.
  Without the `.wasm` the row says "Fragments not available". Test `e2e_frammenti` (skipped without `site/static/wasm-feat`); radicals with labels, cut and fragments in `e2e_radicali_ms`, parity of `frag/1` on the fixture `tests/dati/frag_radicale.json` (`.wasm` and app route).
- **Partial structures** (`disegno-parziali.js`, overlay `parentesi.js` reusable by mzFinder): selected atoms + right click "Bracket with Δ..." = Ketcher DAT S-group on the atoms (fieldName `MZLAB`, fieldData the Δ "+O -H2", context `Fragment`), written in the `.ket` (`getKet`/`setMolecule`, atoms by coordinates). `drawBrackets` draws brackets and Δ (the text of Ketcher's DAT stays hidden even when Ketcher redraws it on a selection or a zoom: `MutationObserver`).
  The label under the structure counts the Δ (structure formula and nominal mass + Δ: key `deltas` of `label/1`); on the bracket (atom, line or inside) the menu gives the same formula and mass, m/z [M+H]+ (`parz/1`) and "Remove the bracket". A click on the line selects it (thicker stroke), Del/Backspace removes it; adding and removing can be undone and redone. A Δ is a candidate, not an identification. Test `e2e_parziali`.
- **Multicentre** (attachment on any one of several ring atoms): candidate ring atoms + the substituent atom (the only one without ring bonds, bonded to a candidate) selected, right click "Attachment on one of these atoms" = DAT S-group `MZLAB_ENDPTS` (fieldData `ANY`) on the same atoms (`multiShape`). `drawMulticenter` of `parentesi.js` draws the dashed bond to the centre of the candidates and a small circle on each; "Remove the variable attachment" removes it. Ketcher has no variable-position bonds: the V3000 with atom `*` and `ENDPTS=(n …) ATTACH=ANY` is written by `multiMolfile`.
- **Export for mzFinder**: `TPDraw.smiles()` strips Ketcher's CXSMILES extension "|…|" (`plainSmiles`); `TPDraw.partial()` = `{smiles, delta, level, positionUnknown, molfile}`: `level` 3 with a Δ or a multicentre, otherwise `null`; `molfile` is the V3000 with ENDPTS only with the multicentre. The exported images (PNG/JPEG/SVG) have the brackets, the Δ and the dashed attachment: `image()` removes the DATs from the `.ket` (`ketWithoutPartials`) and redraws them with `addExportPartials` (same `drawBrackets`, position from `exportOffset`, widened image).
- **Highlighting bonds**: `TPDraw.mark([[a, b], …])` selects in Ketcher the bonds between the atom pairs (indices as in the SMILES just loaded with `TPDraw.load`) and returns how many it found; used by the fragment panel of the MSn graph.
- **Uncertain toward mzFinder**: `TPDraw.partial()` adds `info` = `{formula, mass, mz}` of the drawing with the Δ (`parz/1`, `null` without a Δ). Tabui's "Use this structure", with a Δ or an attachment on several atoms, puts the SMILES in the SMILES field and `info.formula` in the formula field; the preview row gives formula, mass, m/z and the note "Δ +O, position uncertain, level 3" (a candidate, never a certain structure; Draw passes `{smiles, delta, level: 3}`); editing formula or SMILES removes the note. Tests: `e2e_tabui_incerto`, `e2e_parziali`.
- **Draw tour** (`TOUR.disegno()` in `tour.js`, chapter 3, 7 steps `d_*`, texts `tour.d_*`): same engine as the Data tour (spotlight, balloon, keyboard); starts from the gear when the view is Draw and does not touch `qqq.tour.lr`. The zoom buttons are inside Ketcher's iframe: `frameBox` gives a target with the rectangle shifted by the iframe's; the steps on the panels show them if hidden and hide them again at the end (`prep`). Test `e2e_tour_disegno`.
- **Pitfalls**: `zoomAccordingContent` reduces the zoom after each paste; the visible zoom label is the second `[data-testid=zoom-selector]` (the first belongs to the macromolecule mode, hidden).
- **Finger and Apple Pencil** (`touch.js`, `TOUCH`; tests `e2e_tocco`, `e2e_tocco_disegno`, `e2e_tocco_penna`): touches become mouse events (one touch = `mousedown`, `mouseup` and `click`, on charts too: the diamond of a contaminant acts on `click`, test `e2e_contaminanti`); the mouse/`click` events added by the browser after a touch are stopped (one touch = one bond).
- **Pen and palm** (`touch.js`): touches ignored with the pen down and for PALM ms after its last event; the pen cancels a touch already resting and has no long press (menu: side button). The menu of a finger long press stays open after release (`menuUp`) and `ctxShow` moves it next to the finger. No key bar: on the chromatogram the cursor moves with the dot.
- **Charge +/- on touch** (`touch.js`, `snapCharge` in `disegno-tablet.js` as `tpSnap`; test `e2e_tocco_disegno`): the charge acts on the touch `click`, not on `mouseup`. With charge +/- active `editor.findItem` looks for the nearest atom within 24 px of the finger (probe rings, the smaller `dist` wins), highlights it and taps it; on empty space "Tap an atom to charge it" appears (`draw.tap.atom`).
- **Stable loading**: `#kframe` stays `visibility:hidden` (class `kwait`, `app.css`) with the note "Loading the editor" until `hideMacro` and the first fit are done, so the bars do not jump; the side panels always stay visible (test `e2e_disegno_caricamento`).
- **Tablet** (`disegno-tablet.js`, mounted by `draw.js`): in the iframe the `.cliparea` textarea is `position:fixed` at top left (no scroll), with `tabindex=-1` and `inputmode=none` (put back by an observer) (no `focus()` before the first gesture, `e2e_disegno_focus`; the guard is `MZLAB_FOCUS_GUARD` in `first-paint.js`: a direct `#/draw` starts the frame before `draw.js`); below 600 px the top bar wraps onto two rows. On `pointer:coarse` the fields of `#v-draw` are 16 px (no iOS zoom); `#ex-smi`: autocapitalize/autocorrect off, enterkeyhint go. Test: `e2e_disegno_tablet_ketcher.py`.
- **Finger targets** (`touch.js`, `app.css`): the height grip (`.rzg`, only `html.touch`) is 72x24 px at the centre of the bottom edge; name and x of a trace in the legend are 24 px high (test `e2e_tocco`).
- **Early start**: on `#/draw` `first-paint.js` starts Ketcher's iframe at once and remembers `ketcher-ready` in `MZLAB_KETCHER_READY`; `start()` adopts it. From the other pages (`first-paint.js`): low-priority `link rel=prefetch` (never read into memory) at idle, 3 s after load and with no file loads in progress (event `mzlab-index`); not on phones, nor with data saver or offline; mouse or focus on "Draw" calls it at once (`MZLAB_WARM_KETCHER`, `shell.js`). Tests `tests/test_ketcher_warm.py`, `e2e_disegno_guscio`.
- **Start**: `start()` also runs from a direct address (`#/draw`, Cmd+Shift+R) without waiting for the engine; if Ketcher does not answer in 60 s the error "Ketcher did not start" appears but the attempt is discarded, so Quick draw, the exports and the card retry. The saved options (formula, mass, decimals) apply at `nbloaded` and the labels are redrawn after the first zoom fit.
- **Final first paint**: a direct `#/draw` address marks `<html class="in-draw">` already in the inline script of the `<head>` of `index.html` (app.css hides `#start`, the Data screen), and `#prop-card` is born visible with "Select a molecule" (the resting state), so nothing moves when Ketcher and OpenChemLib arrive. Test `e2e_cls` (CLS <= 0.02 with a 4x slower CPU).
- **Indigo queue** (`indigo()` in `draw.js`): every Ketcher conversion (SMILES of a piece, `getSmiles`, `partial`, `generateImage`) goes through a single queue (Indigo hangs with two overlapping calls); a slot is freed after 20 s at most; a SMILES of a piece beyond `SMILES_MS` (20 s) gives the note `draw.selection.slow`, not "no SMILES". A new call to Indigo goes inside `indigo(() => ...)`, never inside another `indigo()` (it would block itself).
- **Ketcher weight** (`tools/ketcher_estrai_worker.py`): Indigo's worker in base64 inside the Vite bundle (21 MB) goes to `assets/indigo-worker-<hash>.js` and `assets/indigo-<hash>.wasm` (synchronous XHR from the worker), no `atob` on the page thread; page bundle (same name) 7.9 MB. Idempotent; the `prefetch` links in `index.html` are used by the warm-up of `first-paint.js`.
  Guards: `tests/test_ketcher_worker_estratto.py` and `e2e_disegno_longtask` (cap on the longest long task, to be lowered in later steps). Ketcher shares the page thread: at 4x CPU what remains is mostly module evaluation (~2 s).
- **Ketcher without monomers** (`tools/ketcher_senza_monomeri.py`, after the worker extraction): the monomer library (3.5 MB of JSON, ~3 s of long task at 4x) is not loaded: the script adds `disableMacromoleculesEditor:!0` to the `render` call and empties the library; the page bundle drops to 4.4 MB. Draw never uses peptides, RNA or DNA. Guards: `tests/test_ketcher_worker_estratto.py`, `e2e_disegno_longtask`.
- **Full or blocked storage** (`disegno-storage.js`, imported by `draw.js`): Ketcher saves settings with `localStorage.setItem` without try/catch, so with full quota start-up threw (empty canvas). The module wraps `getItem`/`setItem`/`removeItem` of `Storage` in the iframe from the parent, before the deferred bundle (values kept in memory if the call throws); `vendor/ketcher` is not modified. A failed start says "Ketcher did not start" (`draw.error.ketcherNotStarted`), never Ketcher's own English. Test `e2e_disegno_storage`.
