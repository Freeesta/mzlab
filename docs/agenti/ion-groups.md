# features.group@1 and features.companions@1: ions of the same compound (correlation, isotopes, ion identity networking; unit-mass companions)

- **What it does**: reads a per-ion table (`features.align` with `mode=ion`, or its `features.gapfill`) and says which rows are the same compound: isotopologues, different ion species ([M+H]+, [M+Na]+, …) and multimers. No row is merged or removed: every row has a `group`, and when the evidence gives it a species (`ion`) and a neutral mass (`neutral`). The family mode of `features.align` instead merges isotopes and adducts into one row and can lose true rows; the "Untargeted analysis" template of the Flow therefore uses `ali.mode=ion` + `features.group`.
- **Code**: `crates/mzlab-feat/src/ionnet/` (`pairs.rs` correlation, `net.rs` isotopes, species and groups, `ions.rs` species library, `mod.rs` parameters and text). Single entry point `ionnet::group_text(params, bytes of the .mzfeat)`: called by `mzlab-cli` (`flow_group.rs`, also the desktop app via `run_plan`) and the `.wasm` `flowtab` (`flow_group` in `mzlab-feat-wasm/src/run_tab.rs`); descriptor `steps/group.rs`.
- **Algorithm**: (1) pairs of rows with RT within `rt_tol`; in files that detect both, the apexes are at most `apex_fwhm` × the width of the wider peak apart (in at least half of those files); with at least `min_corr_samples` files measured in common, Pearson of the heights ≥ `min_corr` (metaCorrelate of mzmine; `groupFWHM`/`groupCorr` of CAMERA). From the table only: there is no shape correlation on the raw data.
  (2) Isotopes: step 13C, 13C2, 34S, 37Cl, 81Br divided by the charge 1..`max_charge`, within `ppm` (minimum `mz_abs`); median height ratio M+1/M ≤ 2.5 × 0.000475 × mass (averagine, at least 0.1), +2 steps ≤ 1.2. Each heavy row takes the light partner with the smallest error.
  (3) Ion identity: for each correlated non-isotopic pair, two different species of the list that give the same neutral mass within tolerance. Each row keeps the species with the most partners (on a tie the list order); an edge stays if both ends keep its species (conflict resolution like the IIN of mzmine). (4) `group` = connected components of isotope and identity edges; `corr_group` = components of the correlation edges; ids numbered from the first row.
- **Cost**: pairs are searched in the RT window (rows sorted by RT); correlation edges are added only for pairs not already linked, searched by bisection in a sorted list. Measurement (4 synthetic files, defaults, median of 10): 1 651 rows 7 ms, 3 538 rows 35 ms, 6 981 rows 136 ms, 12 730 rows 0.53 s.
- **Memory**: before keeping the pairs it counts them; step estimate = 128 B per row + 48 B per correlated pair (+200 B per correlation edge written). Over the budget (`MemBudget`: `--budget-mb` / runner budget in `flow run`, the page's in the browser, 192 MB if it asks for nothing) it refuses with `error.memoryBudget` `{fase:"group", stima_mb, budget_mb}`; pairs over the limit are counted without keeping them.
  In the browser the step works within the page's safe budget (`budgetMb`, else `choice.mb`); a refusal that fits the high budget waits for the yes like the alignment (`permission.memory`, card "Allow more memory: Run now", window "ion grouping" at Run) and the yes redoes it with the high budget; otherwise `error.featMemory` ("Not enough memory for the "group" phase"). Measurement (1 dense file, 12 000 scans, 20 000 compounds, 33 173 rows): 9.5 s, `ionnet/1` 1.9 MB, run peak 538 MB.
- **Parameters** (all with `--set INSTANCE.NAME=VALUE`): `polarity` (auto = that of the table's files), `ions_pos`, `ions_neg` (comma-separated lists, species of the library; unknown name or wrong polarity = `err.flow.group.ion`), `rt_tol` 0.1 min, `apex_fwhm` 0.6, `min_corr` 0.75, `min_corr_samples` 3, `ppm` 5, `mz_abs` 0.001 Da, `max_charge` 2. The `table` port asks for `hr` data.
- **Sources of the thresholds**: `apex_fwhm` 0.6 (`perfwhm`), `min_corr` 0.75 (`cor_eic_th`) and 5 ppm are the CAMERA defaults (Kuhl et al., Anal. Chem. 2012, doi:10.1021/ac202450g); the species and the edge names follow Ion Identity Molecular Networking (Schmid et al., Nat. Commun. 2021, doi:10.1038/s41467-021-23953-9). `rt_tol` 0.1 min is a choice for UHPLC peaks a few seconds wide. Both sources are in the descriptor's `references`.
- **Output** `groups` = `<instance>.json`, `ionnet/1`: `polarity`, `rows`, `groups`, `ions` (the list used), columns of one entry per row `id` (the table's row id), `group`, `corr_group`, `charge`, `iso` (13C steps above the lightest member), `ion`, `neutral`; `edges` with the columns of GNPS's supplementary pairs `ID1, ID2, EdgeType` (`MS1 annotation` identity, `MS1 isotope`, `MS1 correlation`), `Score` (Pearson, `null` if unproven), `Annotation`.
- **Few files**: with fewer files than `min_corr_samples` no pair can be proven: no `MS1 correlation` edge (pairs still drive isotopes, species and `corr_group`) and the warning `warn.flow.group.corrFiles` in the record (`flow run`) and on the step card in the browser ("done, with a note"). From `min_corr_samples` files up the text does not change.
- **Browser** (`flow/lazy/flow-web-group.js`, `F.webGroup`): pool `ident("flowtab", "flow_group", …)` on the alignment table in OPFS; without a table `block` with `platform.computer`; result cached under the job key. It passes the page's `budgetMb`; warnings go in `x.notes` (card) and in the cache with the text.
- **Proof**: `crates/mzlab-feat/tests/ionnet.rs` (synthetic set with truth, three seeds: ARI ≥ 0.8 against the compounds, identity-edge recall ≥ 0.8, right species ≥ 0.9 of the named rows, no row lost, same bytes; squashed RT, 40-900 compounds: frozen SHA-256 and one edge per type and pair), `crates/mzlab-cli/src/flow_group_tests.rs` (plan = engine text with the parameters as the page sends them, `--set`, species error), `tests_e2e/e2e_flow_web_group.py` (same SHA-256 as `flow run`, second run from the cache).
  `e2e_flow_web_group.py` also with `min_corr_samples` 7 on 6 files (native text, note on the card), with a tiny budget (refusal of the `group` phase) and with a tiny safe budget and a wide high one (the window at Run, the yes gives the native text, the no leaves the refusal). `crates/mzlab-feat/tests/ionnet_parts/files.rs`: 1 and 2 files without correlation edges with `corr_group` unchanged, refusal with the same estimate at every budget; `flow_group_tests.rs`: warning in the prov, `--budget-mb 0.0001` = `error.memoryBudget` phase `group`.

## features.companions@1 (AU-10): companion ions at unit mass
- **What it does**: `features.group` needs `hr`. For a unit-mass table (`lr` port) this step finds, for each row, the ions that co-elute and differ by a nominal mass, with the numbers behind each pair. It never merges or removes a row, and a mass difference alone never makes an edge: the Pearson of the heights over the files must reach `min_corr`.
- **Code**: `crates/mzlab-feat/src/companions/` (`mod.rs` pairs, edges, parents; `evidence.rs` numbers; `list.rs` mass differences; `text.rs` JSON). One entry point `companions_text(params, .mzfeat bytes)`: CLI executor `flow_companions.rs`, `.wasm` `flowtab` `flow_companions` (`run_tab.rs`), site `flow/lazy/flow-web-companions.js`. Schema `docs/schemi/companions-1.json`.
- **Edges** (`from` = lighter row, `to` = heavier): `isotope` (+1, +2 Da; `carbons` range from the M+1/M ratio at 1.08 % per carbon, `hint` `cl_br_s` when M+2/M >= 0.25), `relation` (adducts of [M+H]+ 17 NH4, 22 Na, 38 K, 41, 63; in-source losses 17, 18, 28, 42, 44, 46; `relations` lists every name that fits, so 17 Da is `adduct_nh4` and `loss_nh3`), `coelution` (correlated, no mass relation, one per row and linked group).
  Each edge: `r`, `n_files`, `rt_offset`, `apex_shift`, `ratio` (median, lo, hi of `to`/`from`), `parent` and `rule` (`lighter` for isotopes and adducts, `heavier` for losses, `intensity` when both fit).
- **Row columns**: `companion_of` (id of the parent from the best edge by r) and `companion_via`; `null` when none. The table itself is unchanged: removing companion rows is a follow-up (`table.filter` option).
- **Choices logged**: `params` echoes every value used; `counts` has pairs, edges per kind, `weak` (a relation fits, r between `weak_corr` 0.5 and `min_corr`), `untested` (too few shared files or a constant level), `implied` (co-elution edges left out because other edges already link the rows) and `dropped` (past `max_edges`). `weak` lists up to 200 near misses with the same numbers; fewer files than `min_corr_samples` gives no edge, the warning `warn.flow.companions.corrFiles` and every fitting pair in `weak`.
- **Parameters** (`--set INSTANCE.NAME=VALUE`): `rt_tol` 0.1 min, `min_corr` 0.9, `weak_corr` 0.5, `min_corr_samples` 3, `da_tol` 0.4 Da, `adducts`, `losses` (comma lists of Da; a bad item is `err.flow.companions.list`), `max_edges` 20000. Defaults are a choice for UHPLC peaks, not a published value.
- **Proof**: `crates/mzlab-feat/tests/companions.rs` (M+1 at 11 % gives one isotope edge whose carbon range holds 10, the loss of 18 and the NH4 adduct point at M, an unrelated ion at +1 gets no edge), `crates/mzlab-cli/src/flow_companions_tests.rs` (plan = engine text as the page sends the parameters, the browser path), `flow_view_tests.rs` (reads a `table.filter` view). Follow-ups: ESI- list and dimers (2M-1, 2M+21), the `table.filter` option, `raw companions` (AU-16) sharing the list.
