# mzFinder low resolution in Rust

The mzFinder Dig on a unit-resolution series (3200 QTRAP, full scan) runs in Rust: the table rows are the peaks of the `crates/mzlab-feat` pipeline (profile `lr`: detection, alignment, L7 hypotheses with `regole_tp_3.json`), then an adapter writes the JSON of the `run` and `detail` calls, which the worker routes (`TPCALL`) return unchanged.

## LR Dig: files
- `legacy/lr/cond.rs` (view, score and generation per condition) and `groups_out.rs` (`gruppi` block of the output): `docs/agenti/groups.md`.
- `crates/mzlab-feat/src/legacy/lr/`: `plan.rs` (files, columns, parent, settings; `plan_json` = `{rust, lc, ms2, mrm, minutes, formula}`), `rows.rs` (calibration on the parent, candidates from the hypothesis registry, isomers, unexpected ions, isotopes), `labels.rs` (criteria and score of `score_candidate`), `kin.rs` (parent decay, kinetic generation), `summary.rs` (`summary`, `live`, levels 4/5), `detail.rs` (`detail` with the XICs from the `.mzidx` files, `frag_tree`, `frag_tree_compare`), `mod.rs` (`dig`).
- `crates/mzlab-feat/src/legacy/lr/ms2link.rs` and `ms2link/{exp,rules,json}.rs`: matching of the MS2 experiments to the rows and the `ms2_links/1` contract (weight of `finder-lr` in `build_feat_wasm.sh`).
- `crates/mzlab-finder-lr-wasm`: a `.wasm` of its own (`wasm-feat/finder-lr/`, `finder_lr` entry of the manifest, limit 741 376 B in `build_feat_wasm.sh`, with `chem` for the tree and the MRM): `finder_lr_plan`, `finder_lr_mrm(files, read, sizes, ends, rt_tol)` (reads the `mrm` files from their mzML with `mrm::file_from_mzml`, the same as the desktop, and calls `mrm::analyse`),
  `finder_lr_dig` → `FinderLrDig{summary(timing), live(), detail(id, read, sizes), frag_tree(), frag_tree_compare(id), ms2_link(overrides), relink(overrides), links_json()}`. Native example `finder_lr` (same pipeline as the browser, for the e2e).
- `TP_Mine/files/finder-lr.js` (module imported by `tpmine-worker.js`): `run(ctx, args)` → JSON of `api.run` or `false` (the worker replies with an error); `ofDig(ctx, fn, args)` for `detail`, `frag_tree`, `frag_tree_compare` when `last === "rust-lr"`.
## LR Dig: when it runs in Rust
- All files of type `full` (and at most `ms2` and `mrm`), each with its `.mzidx` in OPFS (`mzlab/idx/`), parent with formula or SMILES (`plan.rust`), default transformations. The `ms2` files (EPI) are not columns: `plan_json` lists them in `ms2` and the worker opens their index after that of the columns.
- `mrm` files: no index; `plan_json` lists them in `mrm`, `finder-lr.js` reads their mzML from the bytes sent by the page (`held` of the worker) and `run` adds `mrm` to the summary. A parent known only by m/z stays in Rust (no hypothesis: its row and the unexpected ions, empty registry); the user's transformation list (`a[3]`) is merged into `regole_tp_3.json` with `finder_transformations`, as in HR.
- Order in the worker: detect (phase `calibration`), hypotheses (`candidates`), alignment and Dig (`xic`, with the `live()` load of the candidates that pass), then `unexpected`, `ms2`, `tables`.

## LR Dig: semantics
- Columns (`plan::columns_for`; `plan::table_columns` reads the count from the table for the app, which opens the files by name): the table's files are `plan::columns` (samples, blanks, dark controls; the standard only with several conditions) when it has their number (the page aligns only those), else every full-scan file of the plan (a Flow plan aligns them all): standards (`std`, `calibration`) and `other` (`qc`, `pool`, `infusion`) are then columns that are never kinetic points. Any other count is an error naming both counts and the roles.
- Split rows (`lr/merge.rs`, before the peak check): rows within `merge_da` (0.3 Da) and `merge_rt_min` (0.3 min) are one ion unless a file has a detected peak in both with apexes more than half the wider peak apart (two resolved peaks); the row with the larger detected area keeps, per column, the detected (or larger detected) cell; the absorbed row keeps no peak. 0 switches it off. `summary.merged` logs each merge: `kept`/`absorbed` {row, mz, rt, areas, states} as they were, `delta_mz`, `delta_rt`, `both_detected`, `files_taken`, `reason`.
  - Candidates come from an m/z index (rows sorted by m/z, a window of `merge_da`), visited in the order of the pairwise loop: the same merges in the same order (`merge_tests.rs` against an independent copy of the pairwise loop: crowded random tables, duplicate m/z, ±`merge_da` exactly and one ulp off, tied totals, permuted rows); 20 000 rows 32 ms instead of 1.2 s. A row with a non-finite m/z or RT is never merged.
  - Why 0.3 min (`lr_robust.rs`, `isobaric_products_0_3_to_0_5_min_apart_are_never_merged`): two isobaric products 0.3-0.5 min apart, co-eluting, handing over in time or never in the same file, stay two rows under 0.3 and 0.2; 0.3 also joins a step drift of 0.25 min that 0.2 leaves split (k = 3 + 3). Limit: isobars closer than the alignment tolerance (0.15-0.2 min) that are never detected in the same file look like one split ion under either value; `summary.merged` shows them.
  - Formed together 0.4 min apart, the second isobar already has no row of its own in the alignment (merge off or on): a limit upstream of the merge, not of `merge_rt_min`.
- Evidence per row (`lr/points.rs`): `table_row`, `points`, `k_points`, `controls`, `cond`, `by_cond`.
  - `points`, per column: `file`, `role` of `samples/1`, `cond` (its `series`; empty with fewer than 2 conditions), `time`, `area`, `snr`, `state`.
  - `state` uses the shared words `mzlab_feat::EVIDENCE` (`aligned::evidence_word`): `detected` = passed the peak check, `forced` = a peak the check rejected (or the targeted pass), `filled` = the alignment's gap filling, `absent`.
  - The S/N of a filled cell is the XIC's at the row RT (`peakchk::forced_snr`).
  - `k_points`: timed samples with a detected peak; the others enter the kinetics as zeros.
  - `controls` {`blank`, `dark`, `standard`}: largest detected area in those columns / largest detected treated area; 0 when only forced or filled areas are there; null without such columns or without a detected treated peak (nothing to divide by).
  - `cond`: the condition whose criteria the row carries (`Entry.cond`). `by_cond`, only with ≥ 2 conditions (else `[]`): per condition {`cond`, `k_points`, `blank`, `dark`} on the columns of `cond::members`, from the one alignment of every file.
- Calibration: parent row within 0.6 Da (maximum area); offset = observed - calculated, applied if `auto_offset` and |offset| ≤ 0.6.
- Chromatographic peak (`lr/peakchk.rs`, before calibration, candidates and unexpected): for every DETECTED cell of the rows used, XIC of its file (row m/z ± `tol_da`) and `find_peak` at the apex; the cell stays a peak only with S/N ≥ `min_snr` (5; noise = 1.4826 MAD of the far points, or the 90th percentile of the deviations if the trace is almost all zero), FWHM ≤ `max_fwhm_s` (60 s), ≥ `min_points` scans (5), apex after `dead_factor`·`rt_min` (1.5) and edges inside the run; otherwise it becomes FILLED. Criterion "chromatographic peak".
- Parent (`parent_check` in `lr/parent.rs`): "parent found" = a true peak in one sample; "parent falls over time" = maximum before the last time and last area ≤ 50 % of the maximum, in at least one condition if the series has more than one (n/a with fewer than 2 samples with time or `parent_decay` false). If one fails: `parent_ok: false`, row status "Parent not found", notice above the table, and every candidate stays in the table but weak (score ≤ 40, criterion "parent of the series" failed).
- Candidates: `[M+H]+` hypotheses of the registry (sources rule/cleavage, steps ≥ 1) per (steps, m/z); a hypothesis within the parent's tolerance becomes its alternative. Isomers: up to 4 peaks (≥ 10 % of the strongest), id + 10000·j, name "label @ RT min".
- Criteria on the table cells (state DETECTED = peak): peak in the treated, persistence (peak in at least `min_files` treated, 3 by default, "Min. samples" field of the page, fewer if the series is shorter, or in two consecutive ones with the apex within `rt_tol_min`; 0 switches it off; the unexpected ions must pass it), blank and t0 ≤ 0.2, growth (Spearman ≥ 0.8 up to the maximum, rise ≥ ×2); score = share passed (≤ 20 without a peak); strong ≥ 75, possible ≥ 50, weak.
- A row without a chromatographic peak, not persistent, or that neither is missing at t0 nor grows is weak with score ≤ 40.
- Coincidences (`lr/chance.rs`, `summary.chance`): for each number of steps of the hypotheses, how many hypothesis masses cover a free ion (`rows::free_rows`) against the mean over 8 decoy shifts (±11, ±13, ±17, ±19 Da); `p` = Poisson tail. With `p` ≥ 0.05 the class does not beat chance and its candidates have `by_chance`. A two-step candidate or one `by_chance` is at most possible (score ≤ 74).
- Known limit: with `LR_FLOOR` 3e3 and `LR_MIN_HEIGHT` 7e3 (recalibrated on the bench) a product at 4 times the intensity of a noise ion (synthetic series: amplitude 1.2e4 with noise ≈ 3e3) is shown, at 3 times (9e3) it is in the table but weak, at 2 times (6e3) it is not detected by the pipeline (`recipe_lr`); detection false positives 0.17 per trace on noise only. Measure: `tests/lr_recall.rs` with `--ignored`; the bench `bench_recall_false_positives` (products at 2-10 times the noise and three noise-only series) prints recall and false positives per `(floor, min_height)` pair.
- Check of the unexpected: those chosen on the pipeline table, then the peak check; without a true peak or not persistent they stay weak, after those that pass, within the `max_unexpected` limit. "Weak evidence" chip of the table (off by default, one click): all weak rows, unexpected included.
- Isotope: stronger ion (area ≥ 2×) at -1.00335 / -2.0067 / -1.99580 Da within tolerance and RT: criterion failed, score ≤ 40, weak.
- Unexpected ions: free rows that pass the criteria, ids from 100001, at most `max_unexpected` by score and area. `insource` = unexpected ion lighter than the parent within 0.5·`rt_tol_min` of it; `isf` = verdict of `lr/isf.rs` (`isf_eval` on the full scan columns); with the verdict `insource` also requires `probs.isf ≥ 0.5`.
- Co-elution (`lr/coelute.rs`, optional key `coelutes` {id, mz, delta_mz, delta_rt} of the unexpected-ion rows and of the strong or possible candidates): the strong or possible candidate heavier by at least 3 Da with RT within half of `rt_tol_min`, the closest in RT; on a candidate it means "to be checked" (possible in-source fragment of the heavier one; on the flufenacet demo the 2 strong ones beyond the 3 documented at 13.44 and 13.46 min), it is a measure that removes no rows, does not enter the score nor the order.
- Specificity (`lr/spec.rs`, only in `detail.specificity`, does not enter the score): in the file of the strongest cell and in the time window of its peak, the signal at the ±1 and ±2 Da windows (neighbours) and at ±0.5 Da (decoys) next to that of the ion, with the count of those at least as strong as the ion.
- MS2 (`lr/ms2link.rs`, `lr/ms2.rs`): an experiment is (file, polarity, precursor at 0.1 Da, CE at 0.1 eV or none) from the level-2 scans of the `ms2` files and of the columns; its trace is (RT, TIC) from the index. `ms2_link(overrides)` matches the experiments to the rows and keeps the result in `d.links`; `relink(overrides)` recomputes it with the user's corrections without redoing the Dig.
- Matching rules: polarity; precursor within 0.5 Da of the row's calculated, extracted or read m/z; isotope only if the isotope's trace is ≤ 0.5 of the row's, and removes only the pairs of its peak; time = trace peak with S/N ≥ 3 within `rt_tol_min` of the expected RT plus the file's drift (median of the peaks of the rows alone at their precursor); a peak with more rows goes to the closest (`ambiguous`); in-source fragment `isf`. States: `auto`, `ambiguous`, `isf`, `isotope`, `user`, `stale` (a correction that no longer applies).
- `ms2_links/1` contract (in `summary.ms2_links` and `links_json()`): `links` (matches) and `unmatched` (experiments without a row, with `reason`: `no_row`, `no_feature`, `polarity`, `before_rt_min`, `no_peak`, `user`, ...); `rows[].ms2` counts the matches, `detail.ms2_links` has one per match.
- The pool (`ms2::pool`) takes the scans inside the peak of the row's matches, of the CE group with more scans; without matches it is empty (no window around `ref_rt`). Fragments less than 1.5 Da from the precursor excluded, mean intensity per scan in 0.3 Da classes, ≥ 5 % of the maximum, at most 6.
- For a product every fragment has `loss` and `note` ("shared with the parent" within 0.5 Da, or shifted by the mass difference), `detail.transitions` has one row per fragment (recommended Q3 = observed − offset).
- MRM (`lr/mrm.rs`, `lr/peak1d.rs`): `analyse(files, rt_tol)` integrates the SRM transitions of each file around the quantifier's time (name "quant…", otherwise the first by Q1 and Q3; apex of the strongest file): area, time, S/N, qualifier/quantifier ratio against the median of the standards (±30 %), no calibration line (`calibration: null`). `find_peak` (test in `tests/lr_mrm.rs`). Linked to the Dig by `finder_lr_mrm`; the concentration columns exist only if `calibration` exists.
- No level emitted: `level` is always null (the chemist decides it, `TPDEC`). `summary::pred` gives `pred` = prediction grade of the rule (`hypotheses/2` `level`, Löffler 2-4) to the strong or possible rows with a formula; `summary::evidence` writes in `level_text` what the data support: "Proposed formula" only for formula + strong + one step + class above chance (`evidence().0`, used by `lr_demo.rs`), otherwise the reason (two steps, class at chance, mass only).
- Without MS2: levels 4/5 as `confidence`, `ms2` empty, `frag_tree` with the note "no MS2 of the parent in the files". With MS2, `lr/tree.rs` collects the 12 strongest fragments of the parent (`ms2::pool`) for `lrisf::tree::lr_tree` and `compare_lr` (`chem` feature of the wasm crate).

## LR Dig: tests
- `cargo test --release -p mzlab-finder-lr-wasm` (`tests/lr_dig.rs`): the series of `TP_Mine/tests/lr_synth.py` rewritten in Rust; keys and types equal to `tests/golden/finder_lr_contratto.json` (frozen), ≥ 95 % of the parent/strong/possible rows of `tests/golden/finder_lr_riferimento.json` found within ±0.35 Da and ±0.25 min, every candidate with its evidence, offset ≈ 0.30, isotope and blank not promoted, determinism.
- `tests/lr_robust.rs`: a plan with a standard, a table that matches no files (error text), one product split by an RT drift merged (`k_points` 6, log), S/N and states per point. `mzlab-cli` `flow_lr_tests.rs`: a plan with a standard gives the page's JSON.
- `tests/lr_demo.rs`: the five demo FullScans of the site; carbamazepine (not in the files) and 6 decoy parents without strong candidates or proposed formulas, flufenacet with its proposed formulas. `tests/lr_recall.rs`: weak synthetic products close to the dead volume, with the defaults as visible as without filters; `-- --ignored --nocapture` prints the threshold → recall and threshold → noise candidates tables.

## ISF verdict of the LR rows (`crates/mzlab-feat/src/legacy/lrisf/`)
- Pure functions with parity ≤ 1e-9 with the frozen replies (`tests/golden/finder_lrisf.json`, test `crates/mzlab-feat/tests/legacy_lrisf.rs`; `legacy_lrisf_eval.rs` tests `isf_eval` on a synthetic index). The verdict (probability) lives only here, never in `mzlab-core`. The model parameters (`isf_model.MODEL`) are constants in `isf.rs`; `calibrate`, `fit_logistic` and `families` (MCR-ALS) are not ported.
- Interface for the LR Dig: `isf_eval(samples: &mut [IsfSample], parent_mz, ions: &[f64], formula_p: Option<&str>) -> Vec<Option<IsfVerdict>>` = `classify_ions(..., fam=False)`, one result per ion in the given order, `None` if the ion cannot be analysed (never a panic). 
- `IsfSample` = `mzlab_core::origin::Sample` (label, time in minutes, `.mzidx` index and full scan section): it is what the pipeline has for each file. 
- `IsfVerdict` = `row.isf` (`p_inherited`, `isf_ness`, `probs`, `doubtful`, `reasons`, `explanation`, `calibrated`); `row.insource` is computed by the caller (`probs.isf ≥ 0.5` and lighter ion).
- `classify_report(report, formula_p, ramp) -> Option<Value>`: the full JSON of `isf.classify_report` (with `evidence`, `notes`, `features`); MS2 and DP ramp contributions with heuristic weight, `calibrated: false`. `isf_split.rs` = roles by mass difference (heavier than the parent is not a fragment), `isf_text.rs` = English texts and Python's number formats.

## LR "supposed" tree and comparison (`lrisf/tree.rs`, `compare.rs`)
- `lr_tree(ms2: Option<&ParentMs2>, mz_x, ion_formula: Option<&str>, smiles: Option<&str>) -> String` = `fragtree.lr`: on top of `fragtree::two_level` (same code as HR) with `ppm = 0`, tolerance `LR_TOL = 0.5` Da, `supposed: true`, `mode: "lr"`. 
- `ParentMs2` = fragments (m/z, intensity: the strongest, ≥ 5 %, at most 12), number of scans and precursor m/z (0 or absent → `mz_x`). `ion_formula` is the formula of the [M+H]+ ion (`None` for other adducts or without a formula: then the alternatives are the common losses only). 
- Without MS2 of the parent: `{"ok": false, "mode": "lr", "note": "no MS2 of the parent in the files", "nodes": []}`. 
- `resolve_smiles(field, neutral text)` gives the SMILES as in Python (the neutral text counts as SMILES if it is not a plain alphanumeric formula).
- `compare_lr(tree: &Value, tp_mz: &[f64], dm: Option<f64>) -> String` = `compare_lr` + `_tally`: a product fragment within 0.5 Da of the node is `same`, within 0.5 Da of the node plus `dm` is `shifted`; without fragments `{"ok": false, "note": "no MS2 of the product", "marks": {}}`.
- Golden data: keys `tree` and `compare` of `tests/golden/finder_lrisf.json` (the reference calls `fragtree.lr` with a fake experiment). An element outside the HR list makes the Python raise; the Rust draws the tree without formulas.
- Test sizes (wasm32, `opt-level z`, lto, without `wasm-opt`; base with only the JSON reader 88 KB): `classify_report` +40 KB, `lr_tree` + `compare_lr` +112 KB (chemical graph and formula space), `isf_eval` with `origin_report` of `mzlab-core` +145 KB; all together 378 KB.

`lr_tree` and `compare_lr` (modules `tree`, `compare`) exist only with the `chem` feature (they use `fragtree`); the ISF verdict also compiles without it, like the site build.

## MS2 targets
- An MS2 trace peak without a row (`no_row`, `no_row_at_rt`) that has a feature in the aligned table (read m/z within the tolerance of P, apex of the trace peak inside the feature's peak: `docs/agenti/mzfinder-ms2.md`) becomes a row "MS2 target m/z P @ RT min" (id from 200001, `kind` `unexpected`, `rows[].ms2_target: true`) with the criteria and score of the unexpected but without their filter, and is matched (`auto`, "row made from this MS2"); without a feature in the table the peak is `unmatched` with reason `no_feature`.
  Details, cases and diagnosis: `docs/agenti/mzfinder-ms2.md`.

## MS2 similarity to the parent (LR)
- `lr/ms2sim.rs` (`ms2_link` computes it after the matches, `Links::sims`): for every row with matches, if the parent has MS2 (`parent.has_ms2`), consensus spectrum of the row and of the parent (`ms2::pool` with `K` = 32 fragments, m/z at 0.1 and peaks merged within `TOL_LR` = 0.5 Da, then `similarity::prepare`) and `similarity::pair_components_tol(..., TOL_LR)`.
- `detail.sim` has the HR shape (`delta`, `cos`/`ent` for `equal`/`shifted`/`loss` and `total`, `n`, `pairs`), `rows[].sim` = `cos.total` to 3 digits; without MS2 of the row or of the parent the two keys are missing.
- It does not change the level (D6 = no). Tests: `crates/mzlab-feat/tests/lr_ms2sim.rs` (synthetic spectra, noise at ±0.3 Da) and `lr_ms2link/sim.rs`.

## Row names and columns in words
- **Names and columns in words** (`legacy/lr/names.rs`, Rust only; tests in the module; row fields `tp_name`, `net_change`, `modification`): `net_change` = neutral formula of the TP minus that of the parent, "−C2H3 +O3" (C, H, then alphabetical; losses then gains; "—" equal, empty if the formula is missing or for an unexpected ion); `modification` = the rule name without "_" or " @ RT", with initial capital, consecutive losses joined ("Loss of C4H8 and SO2"), "—" for the parent.
  `tp_name` = "TP " + the ion's m/z rounded to the integer (the parent is "Parent"). Same integer with a different formula (without a formula: m/z different by 0.01): "_" + the digits after the point of the theoretical m/z (2, or 3-6 if two coincide in the group), for example "TP 362_31" and "TP 362_22". Same formula with a different RT (isomers): " A", " B"… in RT order. It depends only on m/z and RT, so it is the same in every file and at every reload.

## Results report on the flufenacet demo
- `cargo test --release -p mzlab-finder-lr-wasm --test lr_demo -- --ignored --nocapture report` (~20 s) prints on the five demo FullScans: features per file after detection, aligned rows, times, memory peak, entries per label, rank of the three documented products, the first twelve in table order, reasons of the non-strong ones, strong unexpected ions that co-elute (≤ 0.1 min) with a heavier strong candidate, and the false positives of seven decoy parents.
- Same report on a series of one's own: repeat `demo()` with the files and times of the series (no path or name in the public repository) and read only the counts.

## From the demo to the result (Tabui, flufenacet)
- Input: the five demo FullScans as columns in time order, parent flufenacet (neutral formula `C14H13F4N3O2S`), default transformations; without the right parent (`parent_ok` false) no strong candidate comes out.
- Table with the default chips (Strong, Possible, Unexpected): the strong candidates come first, ordered by score and area; the parent is pinned on top.
- Expected result: the three documented products (m/z 362.06 at 13.02 min; 378.05 at 13.57; 396.06 at 13.41) are the first three candidates with "Proposed formula"; they are evidence to read, not verdicts.
- To check: a candidate with "Co-elutes with" filled co-elutes with a heavier one (possible in-source fragment; on the demo m/z 322 and 338 at 13.44-13.46 min); a strong one with the same m/z at another RT is an isomer, not a fragment.
- Strong unexpected ions: they are rows without a hypothesis; "Co-elutes with" says of which candidate they are probable fragments (18 of 40 on the demo).
- Open row: criteria ✓/✗, XIC and kinetics per file, MS2 if the `ms2` files are there; the failed criteria explain why a row is weak.
- Check without interface: the `lr_demo` report (section above) prints the same numbers; it must stay the same after every engine change.
