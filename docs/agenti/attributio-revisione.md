# Attributio: review explanations and proposals

Review evidence (explained fragments, isotopes, purity, co-elution, RT against logP, fragmenter) and proposal classes of `crates/mzlab-feat/src/attributio/review/`. The rest of Attributio is in `docs/agenti/attributio.md`.

- **Review explanations** (`attributio/review/`, AT-06): `explain` (`parse_formula`, `match_adduct`, `subformula_table`, `assign`, `prep_ms2`, `removed_reasons`, `explain` → adduct, ion ppm, explained fragments with formula and loss, explained intensity share and "decoy" share), `isotopes` (`pattern`, `clusters`, `compare`: pruned multinomial pattern as in Attributio, different from `crate::isotopes`).
- **Purity and raw data**: `purity` (isolation purity, impossible losses, unexplained signal, `candidate_scores`, `badge`), `raw` (trait `RawSource`, `ms1_metrics`, `peak_quality`, `coelution`), `rtlogp` (`fit_line`, `fit_mlr`, `calibrate`, `check`). `RawSource` (`scan_rt`, `scan_info`, `xic`) is supplied by the caller (the campaign's `.mzidx`); without raw data every proof is `None` ("not available"), never an error. `round_to` rounds like Python's `round`.
- **Fragmenter** (`chem/fragmenter.rs`, republished as `review::fragmenter`; available with the `chem` feature alone): `fragment_molecule(smiles, pol, picchi, ppm, max_tagli)` cuts every non-ring single bond of the mzLab molecular graph (`chem::graph`, no RDKit; a non-ring aromatic bond counts as single), adds one H to each side and compares ion, radical and losses (-H2O, -2H2O, -CO, -CO2, -NH3) with the peaks. It does not draw: `atoms` of each side tells the page what to draw. A mixture (salt) is not cut, as in Attributio.
- **Fragmenter measurement**: on 40 structures (`frammenti.json`) 225 explained fragments out of 228 are equal to those of Attributio (0.987; threshold 0.90) and 230 bonds out of 230 are cut. The 3 differences are in two structures (a sulfonamide and a dinitroaniline) where the charge or the H of the nitro/sulfonamide group makes a different side get chosen.
- **Proposals** (`review/feature.rs`, `propose.rs`, `proposal.rs`): `review_feature(&Env{s, lib, st, raw}, &Feature, &Inputs{mgf, sirius, gnps}) -> Proposal{class, proposed, reasons, best, alternatives, evidence}`; `class` ∈ `3-keep`, `2b-strong`, `2b-check`, `2a-robust`, `2a-unverified`, `2a->2b`, `2a->3` (and `1`); `Proposal::to_json()` writes the JSON that the Bench reads (hand-written writer: `serde_json` is only a dev-dependency). It is an aid for sorting, never a verdict.
- **Candidate structures**: the `Structures` trait (`formula`, `ik1`) has `GraphStructures` (mzLab graph: formula yes, InChIKey no, identity comes from `inchikey_skeleton` of the sources) and `NoStructures` (as Attributio without RDKit: formulas come from `formula`/`mzlib_formula`); proposal parity is proven with `NoStructures`. A substituted aromatic nitrogen (three bonds) carries no H.
- **Proposal references**: exact (`LibraryIndex::exact`, the best `top_n`, on equal cosine the same structure first), analogs (`analog_candidates`, Δm and Δm formulas) and tool hits (SIRIUS, GNPS2, mzMine) attached per entry; without the spectrum in the library they stay without cosine (`available: false`). Not ported: in-source relations, polarity partners, RT siblings, previous decisions, plugins, USI download; mzMine gives neither entry nor USI (they are not typed columns).
- **AT-06 proofs**: `cargo test --release -p mzlab-feat --features attributio --test attributio_proposte --test attributio_spiegazioni`; `proposte.json` (11 features: same class, proposed level and reasons), `spiegazioni.json` (10 formulas, 10 isotope patterns, purity, raw data, RT-logP at 1e-9 relative; `assign` with ppm within 0.005), `frammenti.json` (measurement ≥ 0.90). Stages `spiegazioni` and `frammenti` of `tools/attributio_reference.py` (`--only spiegazioni frammenti`; `frammenti` needs RDKit).
- `LogPValues::from_smiles` (`rtlogp`) gives Crippen logP and descriptors from `mzlab-mol` (feature `crippen` or `record`); the Bench RT-logP check uses them in the `.wasm` as in native (parity: `tests/test_attributio_descr_wasm.py`).
- `rtlogp::check_terms` opens the residual of the RT-logP check (`y - pred`) as a sum: observed (weight 1), intercept and one term per descriptor with the negated coefficient (`score/rt-residual`); `rt_logp` writes it as `score_terms` with the setting `rt_logp_terms = 1` (off by default). Proof: `tests/score_terms_rt.rs`.

## Name curation, canonical names and identity (AT-03)

Modules in `crates/mzlab-feat/src/attributio/` (feature `attributio`, no `.wasm` carries them yet). Proof: `cargo test --release -p mzlab-feat --features attributio --test attributio_identita` (parity with `tests/golden/attributio/sintetica/{identita,nomi,membri}.json`: equal text, numbers at 1e-9 relative).

- **Curation tables** (`[curation]` of `campaign.toml`: `name_map`, `category_override`, `identity_formula_override`, paths relative to the campaign folder; columns `pattern;canonical_name;category` and `identity_key;corrected_formula`, separator `;`): NOT in the repository; without tables curation passes the cleaned names with no category and gives no error. The synthetic campaign has its own, invented, in `tests/data/attributio/campagna_sintetica/curation/`. The path enters the annotation fingerprint (`fingerprint.rs`).
- **`curation.rs`**: `TableCuration` (implements `Curation`: `match_cec` = first row of the map that matches, file order; `curate_identity` = curated contaminant, then `category_override` (substring), then "Unidentified…" placeholder, then `natural`/`ambiguous`), `from_texts`, `load(campagna, io)`, `audit_name_map` (duplicates, patterns outside the dialect, order shadows), `clean_library_name`, `classify_noncec`, `unnamed`, categories and labels (`CEC_CATEGORIES`, `cec_category_label`, `noncec_category_label`). `BasicCuration` (without tables) uses the same functions.
- **`pattern.rs`**: dialect of the regular expressions of the tables with no dependencies (`regex` has no lookaround and the map uses them): literals, `.`, classes, `\b \w \d \s`, `^ $`, groups, alternation, `? * + {m,n}`, `(?= ) (?! ) (?<= ) (?<! )`; `Pattern::search` = `re.search`; a pattern that cannot be read never matches (the audit says so).
- **`names.rs`**: `canonical_name(identity, formula)` (verified synonyms, PEG series grouped by formula, unresolved library codes, "isomer, RT x.x min" suffix kept), `clean_raw`, `split_rt_isomer`, `subscript_formula` (plain text: exports are AT-08, the page uses the mzLab format).
- **`identity.rs`**: `curated` (identity and group of every level 1-3 feature: `identity_override` of the RT rule, `group_override` of the chemist), `identity_table` (one row per identity, sorted by name; `best_level_sub` `b` only if all features of the best level are 2b), `identity_table_by_structure` (key = first InChIKey block, for comparison: the chemical review of this union is not done), `level_counts` / `LevelCounts::pairs`, `feature_level_counts` (levels 4 and 0 stay feature counts), `load_confident_inventory`, `inchikey_skeleton`.
- **`coherence.rs`**: `coherence_report` (hard invariants and diagnostics). `check_identity_drift` and `update_identity_baseline` are not included (they write command-line files).
- **`members.rs`**: `EvidenceExtras::load` (columns that the typed rows do not keep: CSI adduct, structure and reference of the SIRIUS spectral match, adduct/library/spectrum of the best GNPS2 hit), `feature_evidence` (source that gave the name → structure, adduct, outcome of `structure_fit` on the precursor of the mzMine library), `identity_members`, `lookup_name`. Suspect lists (`suspect_members`) are not included.
- **`untid.rs`**: `untid_column` gives every feature the mzLab identity (neutral mass and adduct of `RowIdent::compute`, `rowident.rs`) and `FeatureEvidence::untid` reports it as evidence alongside, never as truth. The mzMine table has no hypothesis registry: without hypotheses every row is read as the ion of its polarity ([M+H]+ in POS, [M-H]- in NEG); whoever has a registry passes its `Hypothesis` values.

## Review matching (AT-05)

Code in `crates/mzlab-feat/src/attributio/review/` (feature `attributio`); it ports `review/{matching,libraries,sources,settings}.py` of Attributio 1.12.0 on top of `crates/mzlab-lib`. Golden data: stage `abbinamenti` (`tests/golden/attributio/sintetica/abbinamenti.json`).

- **Matching**: `matching.rs`: `clean`, `match_spectra`, `compare` (GNPS cosine and modified cosine with `scores::gnps_match_prepared`, extracted from `gnps_cosine`, which is not duplicated), `delta_formulas` (common Δm + CHNOPS grid, `round_ties_even` like numpy).
- **Review: libraries and sources**: `libraries.rs`: `sniff`, `find_library_files` and the parsers are those of `mzlab-lib` (`parse_all`); `LibraryIndex` replaces `FileIndex`/`build_file_index`/`load_file_index` (one in-memory `.mzlib` per file: `exact` = precursor mass, `analog_candidates` = `flash_scores` with hybrid, order different from the per-bin counts of Attributio; records with raw peaks stay apart because the `.mzlib` cleans and quantizes). `adduct.rs`: `adduct_key`.
- **Review: sources and settings**: `sources.rs`: `sirius_hits`, `sirius_formulas`, `csi_top5`, `network_edges`, `gnps_hits` on the file text (`SourceTexts`; pandas typing is in `tsv.rs`). `settings.rs` (+ `fields.rs`, macro and types): `ReviewSettings` (Attributio defaults and keys, `[review]` of `campaign.toml`, unknown key = error with the same words). `UsiFetcher` is not ported.
- **Intentional matching differences**: polarity is also read from `CHARGE`; the `entry` of a JSON is also `lib_id`/`usi`, that of an MSP also `DB#` (keys read in `parse.rs`).
- **Matching proof**: `cargo test --release -p mzlab-feat --features attributio --test attributio_matching` (30 pairs from `abbinamenti.json`: cosine and matched at 1e-9 relative, same peak indices; library records, adduct keys, source tables, settings).
- **`.siriusdb`**: not read. It is not SQLite but an H2 MVStore archive with Nitrite and Java serialization; no new dependency. In the site and in the index a `.siriusdb` file leaves the warning ".siriusdb is read by the desktop app" (planned reader behind the `siriusdb` feature of the desktop app alone, to be decided).
