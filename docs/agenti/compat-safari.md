# Safari (macOS, iPadOS) and WebKit: APIs used, minimum version, fallback

- Site floor: **Safari / iPadOS 16.4** (SIMD128 of every `.wasm`, `opfsSync` in workers, lookbehind of the `calcola.js` regexes). Below it: the engine is off with the common warning of `capacita.js` (`motore`) or with `load.err.oldBrowser`; no second `.wasm` without SIMD (it would double the weight and the parity work), the desktop app remains the way. The engine uses no wasm threads, wasm exceptions, tail calls or memory64.
- `compat.js` (ES module, first import of `capacita.js`): fallbacks for missing APIs, feature detection only. `installa(g)` returns the names installed (`window.__compat`). Node test: `tests/compat_node.mjs` (`tests/test_compat.py`); in the browser: `e2e_safari_mac` (Mac, pinch) and, with an emulated iPad, `e2e_safari_ipad` (also targets ≥24×24 px: the windows' X, "Skip the tour", the file box of the samples table, 24 px with `pointer:coarse`).
- Touch (`app.css`): buttons, fields, `select`, `label`, `summary`, links and button/tab/menuitem roles have `touch-action:manipulation` (no zoom on double tap and no wait for the second tap; page pinch stays, WCAG 1.4.4); canvases, handles and `#sb-header` stay `touch-action:none`. With `pointer:coarse` the sidebar handle takes 24 px and paints 8; `summary`, `label`s with a checkbox, the `.seg` buttons of the Lists, the × of the XIC rows and the panel title are at least 24 px. Test: `e2e_safari_ipad`.
- On-screen keyboard (`tocco-controlli.js`, `app.css`): iOS only shortens the `visualViewport`; with more than 100 px covered and no pinch, `html.kb` and `--vv-top`/`--vv-bottom` keep every `dialog:modal` in the band above the keyboard, with inner scrolling and the active field in view. Test: `e2e_safari_ipad` (fake visualViewport: Playwright's Chromium and WebKit have no on-screen keyboard).
- Real WebKit: not available in the cloud (Playwright has only Chromium); at night `notte.yml` runs `--fumo` with `--browser webkit` on macOS, `e2e_safari_ipad` uses WebKit when present and otherwise Chromium with an emulated iPad (it says so in its output). `webkit-mac.yml` (manual) diagnoses the engine startup.

## Memory on WebKit (Safari, iPad, iPhone)
Safari closes a tab long before the device runs out of RAM (iPad about 1-1.5 GB, iPhone less) and does not expose `navigator.deviceMemory`. Here: which budget the site uses on WebKit, which large paths end in the calm refusal `error.memoryBudget` (`budget::refusal`: phase, estimate, budget in MB) and which test proves it with a fake small budget. General contract: `docs/agenti/memory.md`.

### Budget on WebKit
- **Device estimate**: `rust-budget.js` recognises iOS/iPadOS (`iPad|iPhone|iPod`, or `MacIntel` with `maxTouchPoints > 1`) and treats it as a small device: `budget_auto` gives 192 MB safe and 192 high (`smallDevice`). Safari on Mac (no `deviceMemory`) starts from 512/2048 MB, lowered by the growth probe (`rust-sonda.js`, `probe_mb`).
- **Shared memory**: `SharedArrayBuffer` and the threads of the index builder are used only with `crossOriginIsolated`; without it, the same code runs in a single worker with the same bytes.
