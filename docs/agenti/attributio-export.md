# Attributio: exports (evidence, Schymanski folder, publication table, quantification, gradient)

The chemist takes the campaign results away. Cascade in `docs/agenti/attributio.md`; Bench 2b in `docs/agenti/attributio-banco.md`. Parity on the golden data of `tests/golden/attributio/esportazione/`.

- **Model** (`crates/mzlab-feat/src/attributio/export/cells.rs`): `Sheet{name, head, rows: Vec<Vec<Cell>>}` with `csv()` (`;`, UTF-8, `\n`), `json()` and `xlsx_rows(&[Sheet])` (`{"hojas":[{"nombre","filas"}]}`, which the page wraps into an XLSX). `pyfmt.rs`: `py_float` (the `repr` of a Python float), `join_distinct`, `toml_text`; `wide.rs`: wide tables per site; `sha1.rs`: `sha1_hex`.
- **Context** (`export/ctx.rs`): `Loaded::load(c, io, matrix)` (sources, cascade, curation, matrix sites), `feature_rows` (`build_feature_frame`: curated identity, provenance of the GNPS2 match, areas per site, RT-logP flag). The matrix is the campaign's `[diagnostics] matrix` or the first one.
- **Schymanski folder** (`export/schymanski.rs`): `build(c, io, matrix) -> Vec<Sheet>` and `notes`; the level label is "(L1)/(L2a)/(L2b)/(L3a)/(L3b)" where Attributio writes "(Lvl N)"; the risk columns are the fixed record "Not assessable" (the hazard registry, RDKit and the checks are not in Rust) and the check columns stay empty.
- **Publication table** (`export/publication.rs`, `publication_ids.rs`, `publication_readme.rs`, `publication_text.rs`): `canonical_identities` (one row per canonical name), `feature_frame`, `build -> Publication{identities, excluded, counts, sites, short, sorbents, blank_fold}`, `readme(&ReadmeInput) -> Sheet` (title, description, column dictionary, level criteria, presence rule, provenance). Sheets "Identities", "README", "Excluded" and the identities CSV.
- **Quantification** (`quantify.rs`): `load_calibration`, `quantify_lvl1`, `quantify_lvl1_environmental` (environmental concentrations with a warning when data is missing); sheets "Level 1" and "Environmental".
- **Gradient** (`gradient.rs`): `compute_gradient_areas(_by_sorbent)`, `same_sorbent_default`, `identity_gradient -> (Vec<GradientRow>, GradientSummary)`, `feature_cloud_gradient`; sheets "Identity gradient", "Feature cloud", "Summary". Two sites are needed in the matrix.
- **Exported evidence** (`review/evidence.rs`, `evidence_svg.rs`): `decided_features` (the last row with a level in `feature_decisions.csv` is `2b` or `2`), `settings_digest` (sha1 of `k=v;…` over the thresholds, 12 digits), `export_evidence(io, root) -> Vec<(name, text)>`: for each decision `<campaign>_<pol><id>_<identity>.svg` (hand-written mirror plot: on top the spectrum with the explained fragments, below the reference, shifted by Δm for an analogue, violet = pairs found with the Δm) and `.csv` with the chart data (`side;mz;drawn_x;relative_intensity;matched;fragment;ppm`).
- **Evidence summary** (`review/evidence.rs`): `evidence_<campaign>.csv` (26 columns) and `evidence_<campaign>.json`. No PNG or matplotlib; the `corroboration` column carries only the GNPS component.
- **Output for the page** (`export/run.rs`): `run(io, root, kind, &Options{matrix, generated}) -> String` with `kind` = `schymanski | publication | quantify | gradient | sirius`; JSON `{"files":[{"name","text"}|{"name","rows"}],"warnings":[]}`; `review::evidence::evidence_json` gives the same for `evidence`.
- **`.wasm`** (`crates/mzlab-attributio-wasm`): `export_to(read, names_json, kind, write)` (`names_json` may carry `matrix` and `generated`); `evidence` serves the review, the other five the export cascade: in the merged module `attributio-all` (feature `all`) `export_to` serves all six; separately they live in `attributio-rev` (feature `rev`, `evidence` only) and `attributio-export` (feature `export`, `attributio_export` entry of the manifest).
- **Worker and pool** (`feat-worker.js`, `feat-desktop-pool.js`): `attributioExport(files, root, kind, extra)`; operation `attributio_export`; desktop app: route `feat/attributio/export` (`feat_attributio.rs`).
- **Page** (`TP_Mine/js/53_attributio_export.js`, `ATTRIBUTIO_EXPORT.mount(host, ctx)`; `// @ts-check` in `tools/tipi/pagina.json`): the campaign "Export" menu in Flow (administrator) with six entries; a sheet downloads as XLSX, the evidence, the publication table and the SIRIUS input as `.zip`.
- **Tests**: `cargo test --release -p mzlab-feat --features attributio --test attributio_export` (parity with `tests/golden/attributio/esportazione/*.json`: identical texts, numbers 1e-9 relative; digest, choice of decided features and SVG/CSV of the evidence); `python3 tests_e2e/e2e_attributio.py` (the five files download, the XLSX opens with the expected headers).
- **Reference**: `python3 tools/attributio_reference.py --solo quantificazione schymanski pubblicazione gradiente` regenerates only these stages (campaigns `campagna_esportazione/` overlaid on the synthetic one and `campagna_gradiente/`, made up).
- **Formula**: no header is "formula" alone: the Schymanski table has `neutral_formula`, the publication one "Neutral molecular formula"; the formula of an ion carries the adduct next to it (test: `tests/test_formula_etichette.py` and `attributio_export`).
- **SIRIUS input** (`export/sirius.rs`, `kind` = `sirius`): `collect` reads from the FBMN MGF all the MS2 blocks (with `COLLISION_ENERGY` if present) and the richest MS1 of each feature, `write_ms` and `write_mgf` write `sirius_<campaign>.ms` and `.mgf` (UTF-8, one compound `feature_<pol>_<id>` per feature with at least one MS2; `>rt` in seconds, `>collision <E>` or `>ms2` without energy; MGF with `PEPMASS`, `CHARGE=1+|1-`, `RTINSECONDS`, `MSLEVEL`, `FEATURE_ID`, plus informational `ADDUCT` and `COLLISION_ENERGY`).
- **SIRIUS adducts**: `ionization()` admits only `[M+H]+`, `[M+Na]+`, `[M+NH4]+`, `[M-H]-`, `[M+Cl]-` (adduct from the mzMine match, then GNPS2); a feature with no adduct or with another one is excluded and a warning gives their number and ids (never file or sample names). No reading of SIRIUS results. Test: module tests (re-read within 1e-9) and `sirius_export_of_the_synthetic_campaign` in `attributio_export`.
