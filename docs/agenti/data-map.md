# Data view: RT x m/z map and navigation
Part of the Data view (`docs/agenti/data.md`). One entry per topic.

- **RT x m/z map** (`drawMap` in `grafici.js`; mouse, keyboard, crosshair and true zoom in `mappa.js`, `MAPPA`; choices in `qqq.map`): 2D in the panel and 3D in the viewer (no Ridge view). Gestures and commands: see the entry "2D map navigation".
- **Engine**: `map`, `mapsnap`, `mappunti` and `mappunti3d` answer from the Rust indexes for every file with an index (unit, profile, HR), without Pyodide (`lr-map.js`, `maproute.rs`; test `tests/map_parity.rs`, `e2e_lr`, `e2e_hr_mappa`); Python only without an index.
- **True zoom**: 150 ms after the last change `/api/map?rt0=&rt1=&mz0=&mz1=&nrt=&nmz=` (`Item.ionmap_region`, at most 1000x1000, never more bins than scans nor coarser than the whole map; colours on the same scale as the whole map).
- **"Smooth" / "Detail" rendering** (map options, `PREF.rend`, default Smooth, remembered in `qqq.map`; `liscia`, `splatOf` in `mappa.js`, `MAPREND.splat` in `mappa-render.js`; test `e2e_mappa_liscia`, `tests/test_mappa_render.py`): applies to the normal map and to the difference, only when zoomed.
  Smooth: each cell of the enlarged region spreads as a Gaussian (maximum, not sum: heights stay those of the matrix) as wide as the m/z resolution (LR 0.7 Da at half height, HR 15 ppm) and as the distance between scans in RT, so the blob of a peak stays continuous; the matrix `s.reg.v` read by crosshair, XIC and points does not change, only the `off` image; the enlarged whole map while waiting for the region is drawn with interpolation.
  Detail: one cell per value, no interpolation. Cost: the splat runs once per region (about 25 ms on 1000x1000), pan and zoom redraw the ready image.
- **Map data** (`/api/map`, `Item.ionmap` and `ionmap_region`): `agg=max` (default: a peak is not diluted in the bin) or `mean` (mean per scan; default with `ref`, the difference); LR on nominal masses (bin [n-0.2; n+0.8], cells of at least 0.5 Da in whole Da: `map_cells`), HR in 3 ppm cells (`HR_CELL`) that shrink when the view is narrow (`mzscale=log`: equal ppm cells, `dlog` in the response; the response always states the grid used: `mz0`, `mz1`, `dmz`, `nmz`);
  `bg=1` removes the constant background (median of each m/z cell along the whole run: `map_background`, `subtract_constant`); `/api/mappunti3d` = the true points of the region (RT, m/z, intensity; the most intense up to `max`, cap 200 000: `Item.map_points`). Test `tests/test_mappa.py`.
- **Crosshair** (button or M; on in full screen, F; the button shows the true state with `aria-pressed` and class `on`, `mirBtn`): cross, readout, spectrum of the scan on the side (same m/z axis) and XIC below (same RT axis; LR [n-0.2; n+0.8], HR ppm of the profile); below 700 px only cross and readout. With Shift held while the crosshair moves with no buttons pressed (`shiftSight`, `s.sl`) the crosshair locks on the axis chosen by the first pixels (`ASSE.THR`): horizontal = same m/z, vertical = same RT; releasing Shift (`shiftUp`) frees it again. Test `e2e_mappa_mirino_maiusc`.
- **Plot bitmaps at the margins** (`sp`, `xc`, plots of the extraction box): `MAPMARGINI.sized` (`mappa-margini.js`) sizes the bitmap on the LAYOUT box (`clientWidth/clientHeight` x `devicePixelRatio`), never on `getBoundingClientRect`: during the FLIP animation of the panel (`transizioni.js`) the rectangle is scaled and no ResizeObserver repairs it afterwards. `rel` = origin of the map in the margin; `watch` redraws when the box or the pixel ratio changes. XIC: zero on the axis, 8% above the maximum. Tests: `test_mappa_margini.py`, `e2e_mappa_margini`, `e2e_mappa_mirino`.
- **Crosshair with a subtracted file** (the map shows A - B): the side spectrum is the full difference of the two scans nearest to the RT (`MIRINO.diffPeaks`: peak of A minus the most intense peak of B within the XIC window; bars `--diff-pos` right of zero, `--diff-neg` left); the XIC below is A - B in time (`MIRINO.diffTrace`, B interpolated on the RT of A) with area `--diff-pos`/`--diff-neg`, and the TIC difference (`fetchTic`) as a dashed line on its own scale. Tests `e2e_mappa_mirino_diff`, `tests/test_mirino_differenza.py`.
- **Whole view** (`mapView` in `grafici.js`, `Item.data_extent`, `extent` in the `/api/map` response for the whole map; `e2e_mappa_intervallo`, `tests/test_map_extent.py`): it is the union, over all the open files of the group and level (the axes do not change when changing file; `e2e_mappa_assi_fissi`), of the intervals with data — RT of the first and last scan with peaks, m/z of the lowest and highest peak — with a 1% margin inside the grid; same limits for zoom, pan, "Whole view", Backspace, arrows, crosshair and 3D viewer (`_a.full`, `_a.fullY`);
  Widened to the scan window the method declares (`run.scan_window` of the index, `declared_survey_window` in `maproute.rs`, `Item.declared_window`) for level 1 at unit resolution, also in the common grid (centroids have points only where there is a peak, so without the window the m/z axis would change from file to file; HR and MS2 stay on the peaks; `e2e_mappa_finestra`).
  the bin grid (`_a.grid`) stays the common one, so the maps stay comparable; no extrapolation.
- **Crosshair path** (`mirino.js`, `MIRINO`, pure functions tested by `tests/test_scan_axis.py`; `mappa.js`; same with Python and with Rust): the point is redrawn once per frame (`frame`);
  the nearest scan is found in the page on the RT axis of the file (`/api/scan_axis`, once per file and level, `MIRINO.nearest` = same rule as `nearest_scan`), the spectrum is `/api/spectra?i0=i&i1=i` (same arrays as `/api/spectrum` on a window with that single scan) and is requested only if the scan changes, the XIC only if the window changes (nominal m/z in LR); one request at a time (`MIRINO.latest`: the most recent point wins) and a response for a point already left is not drawn;
  what is missing is drawn at once as a **preview** (row and column of the map matrix, or of the zoom region, grey and labelled "preview", without m/z labels; no preview in the A - B difference) and when the response arrives it replaces it; spectra and XIC live in an LRU cache (64 MB, 32 on iPad, phone or `deviceMemory` <= 4) that is emptied with `CACHE.clear()` and when the file is removed (`MAPPA.dropFile`); once answered, in idle, the next two scans in the direction of movement are requested in one go.
  m/z labels of the side spectrum (`sideLabels` in `mappa.js`, choice in `MAPREND.pick`, `mappa-render.js`): 12.5 px font (follows the text size), beyond the bar tip with a halo of the panel colour; if they do not fit they go above the bar to the left of the tip, never on another label. The dashed crosshair guide goes from the edge to the tip of the bar of the marked peak (with a closing dash; without a peak, full width) and is drawn under the labels, which it never touches (`s.gd`, tested by `e2e_mappa_etichette`).
  Score = local S/N (intensity / noise level in the neighbourhood: median + 1.4826 x MAD of the other peaks within +-max(15 Da, a quarter of the window), excluding the peak and its isotopes within `GROUP_DA` = 2.5 Da); never below S/N 5; a peak with a more intense one within 2.5 Da (M+1/M+2) is not labelled. Order: the crosshair peak (XIC window, always, first), then the best S/N of each of the N equal bands of the visible window (`quota` per band), then the best remaining; recomputed at every zoom.
  Option "few / medium / many labels" (`PREF.dens`, `DENS`: 4/8/14 labels, 2/4/6 bands, quota 1/1/2). The label tooltip says m/z and S/N (`mappa.lbl.tip`); the plot data table does not change. Comparison of strategies (bands with quota, S/N only, old 5 % rule): `tools/compare_labels.py`. Tests `e2e_mappa_etichette`, `e2e_mappa_ritocchi`, `e2e_mappa_espandi`, `tests/test_mappa_render.py`.
  Cursor (`e2e_mappa_cursore`): the label of the peak under the sight is drawn first by `MAPCURSORE.label` (`mappa-cursore.js`): bold, beyond the tip when it fits, otherwise above the bar and the guide, always inside the panel, never dropped (`lbls[].cur`); its bar is drawn 2.5 px in the ink colour. A chip (`.mzchip`, aria-hidden, pointer-events none) beside the pointer or the key-moved point reads `RT .. min · m/z ..` and `peak ..`, flipping at the edges; a dot on the XIC at the cursor RT (`.mzlive`, geometry `s.xg` from `drawBottom`). mark / snap / sight is one segmented control as tall as the 3D button (CSS at the end of `app.css`), two active neighbours one fill; all three buttons have `aria-pressed`.
  Side spectrum: if the scan is in profile (the response has `pmz`/`py`) the continuous line with area is drawn (`profSide`: all points while there is at most one point per pixel row, otherwise the maximum of each row), with the peaks (`mz`/`y`) only for the labels; for centroids the bars stay; test `e2e_mappa_profilo`.
  The XIC below the map has the intensity y axis (`drawBottom`); the two side plots have no "Data" button (`TDATI.addButton/series(..., quiet)`): the table stays on the T key of the focused plot.
  `?perf` records in `window.__mir` the cost of the last exact response; `python3 tools/bench_crosshair.py [--site]` measures the crosshair (table in `measurements.md`). Test `e2e_mappa_mirino`.
- **Lock**: menu item "Lock the crosshair" or L (no padlock in the 2D bar; in 3D yes) (the state is visible: closed padlock and `aria-pressed` on the button, dot with dark outline and accent fill exactly on the point, `lockDot`). Arrows: left/right scan, up/down one bin, Shift = XIC apex / spectrum peak above 3x the MAD noise.
  With the menu open (`holdSight` in `mappa.js`) the crosshair stays on the right-click point (`s.frz`: neither hover nor leaving the map changes it); when the menu closes it returns to the pointer, unless the lock item was chosen. Esc or the same item unlock. Item "Mark/Unmark the point" (`mapmark`, 2D only): adds or removes a marked point (`addPointAt`/`delPoint`). Test `e2e_mappa_blocco`.
- **Difference** A - B computed by the server (`/api/map?ref=&norm=&rttol=`, `diff_tolerant` in `explore.py`: B replaced by its local maximum over +-0.1 min, so background and small drifts cancel; in HR also over +-`mztol` ppm (5 by default) along m/z, in cells of the log axis (`wm` in the response, at most 20; LR unchanged)), Okabe-Ito colours vermilion #D55E00 (in texts "orange") <-> white <-> blue #0072B2,
  short legend on the two sides of the bar (▲ more in the sample · ▼ more in the reference, no numeric range; orange/blue explanation in the tooltips; the map starts as a difference from the session reference `S.ref`), checkbox "Also show decreases" (checked by default, `PREF.neg`, `data-mz="neg"`) and noise threshold (`qqq.map`). Sign: 2D shows increases (orange) and decreases (blue) by default; unchecked only the orange (`diffOpt().show` `all`|`up`, `grafici.js`); 3D always shows both signs. Test `e2e_mappa`.
- **Marked points** ("mark" or S): snap to the local maximum (RT: about 5 px of the zoom, between 1.5 scans and 0.2 min; m/z: +-1 bin; `/api/mapsnap`, pure function `apex` in `mapcells.rs`; optional parameters `drt` and `wm`; the "snap" button turns it off: `PREF.snap`), draggable, Del removes them, saved in `NB.mappaPunti` per file pair.
- **Cells per scan** (`mapscan.rs`): the first map of a file on the session grid (1 Da cells with integer edges, or `n - 0.2` at unit resolution) builds once, in one pass over the peaks, the maximum of each scan in each cell (f32, cap 64 MB, in the same LRU as maps and pyramids); whole map, background and regions with whole cells are rows of that table, and peaks within rounding distance of an edge sit in a separate list put back into the cells with the grid arithmetic, so the result is bit-identical to the one from the peaks.
- **Cells per scan, test**: `mapscan_parity.rs` compares every request with the same one with the table off (`set_scan_cells(false)`).
- **Columns of a window** (`mapzoom.rs`): a region (maximum, linear or log) whose m/z window contains at least 2 M peaks and that arrives for the second time with the same window (`mz0`, `mz1`, `nmz`, axis) builds a scan x cell table (one per file, in the engine LRU, at most 32 MB and the table cap); later requests of the same window with another RT stretch are a fold of the table, without rereading the peaks. The cells use the arithmetic of `region_map`: same bits. Test: `mapzoom_parity.rs`.
- **Cells per scan, warm**: `api/map?...&warm=1` builds the table and sends nothing: the page requests it file by file at rest (`mappa-caldo.js`). Measurements on a file of 1500 scans x 9000 points: `cargo run --release -p mzlab-core --example map_times -- X.wiff`.
  Checkbox "align to the first" of the table (`PREF.align`): each point of a group takes the RT of the first marked point, so they sit on the same vertical line, and the measures are redone there. Test `e2e_map_groups`.
  Point shapes and the table under the map live in `mappa-tabella.js` (classic script before `mappa.js`, which calls `MAPTAB(ctx)` once in its closure passing the local helpers).
  Table under the map (the panel grows by its measured height with `grow(p, "tab", px)`, the map never changes size; beyond 210 px it scrolls inside) with ONLY measures (`/api/mappunti`: A, B = maximum of the XIC within +-0.1 min, A - B, A/B, S/N, RT group = apexes within a default of about one peak (5 scans, 0.02-0.15 min: `grpTol`), editable (`PREF.grp`), Δm from the most intense point of the group or from the point the student chooses as reference with the "Ref." button of the row (`q.ref` in the notebook; computed in the client in `tableNow`), note).
  The shape of a point (circle, square, triangle, diamond, in rotation) is decided at creation (`shapeNew` in `mappa-tabella.js`, field `shape` of the point in the notebook) and never changes: a point within the RT window of the groups (`grpTol`) of an earlier one takes its shape, a new RT takes the shape after that of the last created point.
  Old points without a shape receive one in order (`grpShape`, `shapePath`; never colour alone, also in 3D) and the table gathers the members under a collapsible group row (`groupedRows`, `s.gcol`; not sorted: by RT then by intensity). In 3D the marker sits on the cell that contains it, at the height of its maximum (`markCell`). Tests `e2e_map_groups`, `e2e_mappa_apice`.
  Δm -> Filtered neutral losses (or Adducts), Excel with the metadata, copy with tabs. No automatic search (it lives in mzFinder). Tests `e2e_mappa`, `tests/test_mappa.py`.
- **Panels** (`mappa.js`, end): `.pgrip` handles on the TOP and BOTTOM edge (pointer events, also touch; double click = 300 px; arrows on the focused handle); at the top, between stacked panels, it moves the boundary with the panel above; no `resize:both`, width always that of the column (`clamp`).
- **File arrows ‹ ›** (next to the 2D map selector, `data-a="mapnav"` in `pannelli.js`; `MAPVICINO.vicino` in `mappa-vicino.js`, test `tests/test_mappa_vicino.py` and `e2e_mappa_frecce`): move to the previous/next file of the selector list keeping the difference reference and window (zoom); grey, not hidden, if there is no file before/after; the reference is skipped. No keyboard shortcut. The drawing is `chevron()` (`explore.js`), the same as the file arrows of the header and of "Subtraction".
- **2D panel**: default height 360 px; header on ONE row (`.pnl .hd` without wrapping; control groups do not shrink (`flex-shrink:0`), so `fitHd` moves into the "⋯" menu what does not fit, as in the other panels, except the +/-/? bar (`.mznav.hdkeep`); no guide text under the map: the gestures are in the "?" sheet) with [file · level: the sample selector is always there; with "Only the selected" it also chooses the file of the bar] [Correction ▾] [Options ▾ + chips for what is not default: normalisation and "show" of the difference;
- **Fixed scale** ("fixed scale" checkbox under "Colours...", `PREF.fix`, not in the difference; `mapImage` in `grafici.js`, `MAPREND.ref`; `e2e_mappa_assi_fissi`): the colour maximum is the highest among the open files of the group (same 99.5th percentile of each), not that of the shown file.
- **MS / PDA map selector** (`pannelli.js`, `pdaMapOk` in `grafici.js`): the PDA choice (RT x wavelength matrix, `/api/map?kind=pda`) appears only if the route really answers, probed once per file (`f._pdaMap`); the PDA trace alone (`f.pda`, chromatogram flag) is not enough and a file without a matrix (trace only, converted mzML) shows the plain MS level; with the matrix (`.wiff` read by the engine, `docs/agenti/dad.md`) the map has the axis in nm, there is no crosshair and the data table (`TDATI_P`) gives RT, nm and mAU. `e2e_mappa_pda.py`.
  under "Colours..." (disclosure) colour scale — linear by default, square root, log, gamma with exponent —, viridis/cividis palette and noise threshold] [mark · snap · crosshair] [3D] (the return to the whole view is the common header button); palettes in `MAP_PAL` (`explore.js`; viridis default, Okabe-Ito difference), choices in `qqq.map` (`pal`, `thr`, `bg`, `show`); legend with colour bar and values (0 ... 99.5th percentile, ± for the difference) and one line of instructions.
- **Correction** (a single menu, `corrWidget` in `pannelli.js`, keys `corr.*`, in the spectrum and in the map; in the chromatogram/XIC/MRM the same items sit in the two groups of the "Subtraction" menu: None · Subtract the file: name · Background of a stretch · Automatic background — the latter is not in the spectrum; one at a time; one-line descriptions): in the map the state is `p.corr` (`""` = follows the session reference `S.ref`, `none`, `file` with `p.ref`, `tract` with `p.bgr = [rt0, rt1]`, `auto`), saved in the notebook;
  a panel saved with the previous format converts at the first draw (`corrInit`: `p.ref` -> `file`, the global switch `PREF.bg` -> `auto`); the current file is not offered. `auto` = `&bg=1` (median of each m/z cell along the run, `map_background`); `tract` = `&bgrt0=&bgrt1=` (`Item.map_stretch`: mean per scan of each m/z cell in the interval; chosen with Alt+drag on the map, `corrPick`); `file` = sample - reference difference (`/api/map?ref=`, abs/max/tic normalisation in "Options").
  In the spectrum `p.bg` stays `""`/`x`/`w`/number; the time window ("stretch") has fields `bw0`/`bw1`. The 3D viewer takes the automatic background of the panel it is opened from (`prefs.bg`). Tests `e2e_mappa_correzione`, `e2e_intestazioni` (includes the map), `tests/test_mappa.py`.
- **3D viewer** (`mappa3d.js`, `failure3d`, viewer commands and menu, preload `preload3d`): `docs/agenti/map-3d.md`.
- **2D map navigation** (`mappa.js`, section "navigation"; tests `e2e_mappa_nav`, `e2e_gesti` for mouse and keyboard, `e2e_gesti_tocco` for touch).
- **Commands = actions of the `actions/1` registry** (`azioni.js`, `actions.md`): `mappa.js` registers the 13 `map...` actions (views `map` and `map3d`; `MAPPA.CMDS` is their list) that feed the right-click and Shift+F10 menu (`itemsFor` = "where" row + `AZIONI.forContext(adapter)`), the bar on the map (`BAR`), the Ctrl/⌘K palette (`barra.js` adds the `map...` items to `CMD`, visible with a 2D map in the tab) and the gesture sheet; the texts are `cmd.<id>.{lab,name,menu,tip,kw}` (`cmd.maplock.off.name` = "Unlock the crosshair").
  Each command acts on an "adapter" (`ad2(p, at)`): the point is the locked crosshair, otherwise the point under the pointer when the menu opened (from the keyboard, Menu or Shift+F10: the crosshair or the centre of the view; the first row of the menu says which).
- **Menu** (right click, 550 ms long press, Menu key / Shift+F10; arrows, Enter, Esc; short on purpose): row with the point (RT, m/z, intensity), XIC (`xicDirect`: unit window, in HR the ppm of the Bench), Spectrum (`spectrumAt`: scan nearest to the RT), Lock/Unlock the crosshair, Reset zoom (with the four-corner icon of the "whole view" button of every panel), Copy coordinates, Gestures; in 3D also From above / Side RT / Side m/z.
  Items without a point are grey; commands with `menu: false` (+, -, mzFinder features) stay in the bar and the palette, not in the menu. Shift+right click = XIC dialog with its window; zoom per axis: double click, Esc, wheel with Shift/Alt.
- **Bar** `.mznav` in the panel header (group `.tbg` with `.bt` buttons, like the other panels; `role=toolbar`; nothing above the plot): +, -, mzFinder features (HR only with a result), "?" (gesture sheet: table action x mouse/trackpad/touch/keyboard, `mapnav.h.2d.*`); the reset is the "fit" button of the header. In the 3D viewer the same bar (with Reset) sits under the title of the side column. Test `e2e_mappa_barra`.
- **Mouse**: the wheel alone scrolls the page (`MAPPA.wheel` does not take it); Ctrl/⌘+wheel or trackpad pinch = zoom on RT and m/z around the pointer (`eventi.js`), Shift+wheel = RT only, Alt+wheel = m/z only, drag = box zoom, Shift+drag = pan, Alt+drag = spectrum of the interval (select), double click = whole view (`MAPPA.whole`), click on a point = chooses it, "Mark" + click = adds.
- **Touch** (`touchInit`, `touch-action: none` on the map): one finger draws the zoom box (same `.zr` element as the mouse, `boxZoom` on release), two fingers pan and pinch (`pinch`), double tap = whole view, long press = menu; after a gesture the emulated mouse events are ignored (`MAPPA.touched`).
- **Axis lock** (`asse.js`, `ASSE`; test `e2e_asse`): with Shift held during the gesture (panning, which Shift already starts, needs Ctrl/⌘ + Shift), or with the latch X / Shift+X (RT only) and Y / Shift+Y (m/z only; same key or Esc = free), zoom box, dragged marked point, panning and, in 3D, panning move on a single direction; when held, the axis is chosen from the first 4 px and stays until release.
  The box extends over the whole other axis, the point keeps the other coordinate (also after snapping), a dashed line (`MAPPA.guide`) and "RT only" / "m/z only" in the panel readout show it, the announcement goes to the `#sb-live` region; with the latch the perpendicular arrows do nothing. Row `mapnav.h.2d.axis` / `3d.axis` in the gesture sheet.
- **Keyboard** (with focus on the map): 0 or Home = whole view, Backspace = whole view, Esc unlocks the crosshair (does not undo the zoom; Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes, `history.js`), + / - = zoom, ? = gesture sheet, Menu or Shift+F10 = menu, M crosshair, L lock, S mark, F expands the panel in the page (like the others, never the browser full screen), arrows move the crosshair.
- **How it is extracted** (`mappa-estrai.js`, ES module loaded on first use by `MAPPA.explain`; opened from the mzFinder markers (`selectFeat`) or from `MAPPA.explain(p, true)`; no button or command; test `e2e_mappa_estrai`):
  on the 2D map a box (m/z window x RT window, `st(p).ex.box`; starting on the crosshair or at the view centre, XIC window of the program — [n-0.2; n+0.8] in LR, the profile ppm in HR — and RT at least 4 scans wide) that is dragged, resized from the edges and corners (grab area of at least 18 px; with a finger 16 px tolerance), moved with the arrows (Alt+arrows widen it).
  It uses the same scheme as the crosshair: spectrum on the right and XIC below, with the same axes; the XIC is `/api/xic` on the m/z window of the box (sum of intensities with m/z in the window, as a function of RT), the spectrum is `/api/spectrum` on the RT window (mean per scan, 0.1 Da cells); the integrated zone is coloured (`--accent`), dashes from the box edges to the two axes and from the maximum point (RT and m/z of the maximum) to the axes.
  Under the map `.mzex`: window, area (trapezoids in counts·s, no background: same formula as `measure`), height (maximum of the XIC in the window), RT and m/z of the maximum (`<output data-k>`), the rule as formulas and "Close". The panel grows by the height of the block (`MAPPA.grow`). LR: no label, no marker, the position is chosen by the student (principle 1).
