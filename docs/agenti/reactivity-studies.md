# mzLab · reactivity: validation and discarded experiments

Measurements and negative results of the reactivity engine (`crates/mzlab-chem`); the current state of the engine is in `docs/agenti/reactivity.md`.

## Validation on experimental sites (`crates/mzlab-chem/tests/validation.rs`, `tests/data/reattivita/`)
- **Set**: `validazione.json` has 23 molecules and 57 rows (one experimental site per row) from `docs/letteratura_cc/` (48 rows with DOI, `tier` `literatura`) and from the CC-05 draft table (9 rows without DOI, `tier` `bozza`, textbook ·OH regiochemistry). Reactions: ·OH, ozone, metabolism; no site data for chlorine. Format and excluded rows: `tests/data/reattivita/README.md`. The number of molecules is the maximum derivable from the repository data.
- **Test**: `reactivity_json(smiles, "")` (defaults: water, 4 conformers, seed 42) on every molecule, then the rank of the best symmetry group that contains an experimental atom; two measures: on the engine's C–H sites (`ch_sites`, rows `scope` `ch`) and on all heavy atoms sorted by class-mean f(−) (any row). The top-1/3/5 counts per `reazione/tier` are locked in the `baseline` field (updated only if the engine changes on purpose). They are not verdicts and do not appear in the interface.
- **C–H sites, ·OH, literature** (n = 20): top-1 8, top-3 17, top-5 18. CC-05 draft (n = 9): 9/9/9. Metabolism (n = 2: 5- and 4'-hydroxydiclofenac): 1/2/2. Ozone (n = 1, metoprolol ortho to the ether): 1/1/1. Wrong at first place (rank): diclofenac 4' (2) and 3' (6); benzophenone-3 C2' (6 of 6), C4' (4), C5 (2); bentazone C8 (2); carbamazepine 2-OH (3) and 3-OH (2); propranolol 5-OH (3); isoxazole C4 of sulfamethoxazole (3); benzoic acid (2) and nitrobenzene (2) against the ortho/para products of the literature (the draft says meta and the engine gives meta at first place).
- **All heavy atoms** (class-mean f(−)): top-1 0 of 39 ·OH literature rows, top-3 4, top-5 9; ozone 0/0/0 (n = 4), metabolism 0/0/1 (n = 5). Sites that are not unsaturated C–H (N- and O-methyls, aliphatic C, C–Cl, S–N bonds and ethers) come out low because f(−) measures the loss of an electron, not H abstraction: the engine does not publish a ranking for these sites and none should be read from `atoms`.

## Discarded experiments (not in the code)
- **Hirshfeld charges discarded**: Hirshfeld populations (integration on Becke grids) in place of Mulliken for the Fukui did not improve the C–H sites (19/29/30 identical, top-1/3/5), cost about 12 kB of `chem` `.wasm` and are not in the code. CM5 not tried (constants not verifiable).
- **Boltzmann average over conformers** tried: no gain on C33 (19/29/30), not adopted.
- **C–H radical energy (option `bde`, PR #1038, code only in the PR history)**: a second indicator independent of Fukui for the `ch_sites` sites. Method: GFN2-xTB, same geometry and same solvent; one of its H is removed from the first atom of the class (same total charge, odd electrons, no spin polarization); ΔE = E(radical) − E(molecule) in kcal/mol, lower = H· lost more easily (not a BDE: the energy of H· is missing). Score = 0.5·f(−) min-max + 0.5·(−ΔE) min-max, with the two contributions shown per site.
- **Result**: on the 32 validated C–H sites, top-1/3/5 from 19/29/30 (Fukui only) to 20/26/29; all heavy atoms unchanged. Other energy weights: 0.1-0.25 → 19/29/30; 0.3 → 20/29/30; 0.4-0.5 → 20/26/29; 0.6 → 12/27/29; 0.75 → 10/25/29; radical only → 9/23/29. No robust gain and the code cost about 5 kB of `chem` `.wasm` (margin below the cap reduced to 117 B): discarded, the code is not in `main`.

## Decisions
- **Precomputed reactivity** (`crates/mzlab-chem/examples/precalc_react.rs` measures the cost on `composti.csv` with the app's options): no precomputed table; the panel computes the parent on the fly (one per session, in the background, administrator only, no cost for the Dig). It is reconsidered only if reactivity leaves administrator mode with many parents; the key would be canonical SMILES + hash of the `chem` `.wasm` (the engine's `version()` does not change with the numbers) + solvent, conformers and protomer.
- **Fukui, what they say** (`tools/fukui_valida/valida.py`): f(−) orders the sites only within a π ring, for electrophilic substitution. It is not proof of position and does not hold for sp3 C alpha to N/O (f(−) of the C negative or zero, order that changes when the H are added), for H abstraction, cleavages or deactivated rings (meta and ortho almost equal). It is shown only as "Prev.", never to reorder the C–H positions or to change confidence; for aliphatic C–H another indicator with a dedicated reference set is needed.
