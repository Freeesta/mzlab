# Chemical classes from rules (`classi/1`)

- **What it is**: from an HR MS/MS spectrum (m/z and intensity peaks, precursor m/z, polarity, tolerance in ppm) it lists candidate chemical classes with the diagnostic fragments and neutral losses found. Explainable hypotheses, not an identification: maximum level 3 ("3a" or "3b"), never "identified". No neural network.
- **Code**: `crates/mzlab-feat/src/classes/` (`mod.rs`: table and constants, `evaluate.rs`: comparison and JSON), API `Table::bundled()`, `evaluate(&Table, peaks, precursor_mz, Polarity, ppm)`, `to_json(&Report)`. Tests: `cargo test -p mzlab-feat --test classes`. Not hooked into any `.wasm` nor the interface.
- **Table** `crates/mzlab-feat/data/classi-1.json` (`classi-regole/1`): classes with rules `{id, kind: frag|loss, formula, polarity: +|-|both, role: diagnostic|support, weight, why}`. Formulas only: m/z and Δm are computed with the masses of `mzlab-core::elements` (a positive fragment loses an electron, a negative one gains one; the formula of a fragment is the composition of the ion). A new rule needs a row in `LITERATURE` of `tests/classes.rs` with the published value to 4 decimals.
- **Decision rules** (constants in `mod.rs`): a class appears only if at least one `diagnostic` rule is satisfied and the score is ≥ `SCORE_MIN` (0.3). The `support` rules (unspecific losses, shared ions) raise the score but are never enough alone; H2O, NH3, CO2, CO are not classes. Score = satisfied weight / (strongest diagnostic rule + all the support rules), at most 1. Level "3a" with at least two diagnostic rules, score ≥ 0.6 and no ambiguity; otherwise "3b".
- **Tolerance**: a fragment matches within `ppm` of its theoretical m/z; a loss within `ppm` of the precursor m/z (observed Δm = precursor − peak). The closest peak with intensity ≥ `MIN_REL` (1 %) of the base peak is chosen. The noise case: 30 random peaks sometimes give a class by chance (9 spectra out of 2000 with 5 ppm, test `random_noise_rarely_lists_a_class`, threshold 1 %); peaks 2-3 tolerances away from the rules never give one.
- **Ambiguity**: if the same peak satisfies a rule of another class (within its tolerance) the evidence carries `also` and the class `ambiguous_with`; all the classes involved are in the output, with level "3b".
- **Output `classi/1`**: `{format, polarity, precursor_mz, ppm, classes:[{id, name, score, level, ambiguous_with, evidence:[{rule, kind, role, formula, theoretical, observed, peak_mz, ppm, rel_intensity, why, also}], unmet:[{rule, kind, role, theoretical, why}]}]}`; `theoretical`/`observed` are m/z (fragment) or Δm (loss); `ppm` is relative to the theoretical m/z of the fragment, or to the precursor for a loss.
- **Sources** (short citations; the fragment formulas are recomputed, the values in `LITERATURE` are those of the papers; reference check on PubMed: all exist with the right volume and pages; the content is confirmed only where written, the rest is "to be verified" against the text). Level scale: Schymanski et al., Environ. Sci. Technol. 2014, 48:2097 (confirmed).
  - phthalates m/z 149: McLafferty & Tureček, *Interpretation of Mass Spectra*, 4th ed. 1993.
  - Conjugates (glucuronides, sulfates, phosphates, sulfonic acids: losses and ions HSO4−, H2PO4−, PO3−, SO3−•): Holčapek et al., J. Chromatogr. A 2010, 1217:3908 (typical neutral losses, in general; the HSO4−/H2PO4− ions to be verified).
  - Glycosides 162/146/132: Cuyckens & Claeys, J. Mass Spectrom. 2004, 39:1 (plausible, to be verified against the text); HexNAc 204/168/138: oxonium ions of glycoproteomics.
  - Phosphocholines 184: Murphy, *Tandem Mass Spectrometry of Lipids*, RSC 2015; sulfonamides 156/108/92: Klagkou et al., Rapid Commun. Mass Spectrom. 2003, 17:2373 (to be verified).
  - PFAS CF3−/C2F5−/C3F7−/FSO3−: Barzen-Hanson et al., Environ. Sci. Technol. 2017, 51:2047 (PFAS classes, does NOT by itself confirm the ions: to be verified; the m/z values are recomputed from the formulas and match LC-MS/MS application notes and PMC12419500); glutathione: 272 in negative mode confirmed by Dieckhaus et al., Chem. Res. Toxicol. 2005, 18:630, the losses 129/75/307 to be verified; taurine 124: Griffiths & Sjövall, J. Lipid Res. 2010, 51:23 (to be verified).
- **Bench 2b**: `review_json` adds the key `classes` (`classi/1` on the feature's MS2 block, ppm of the Bench fragments; `null` without MS2).
- **Table on the site**: `review_json_with` receives the table as text: on the site the file `classi-1.json` sits next to the `.wasm` `attributio_rev` (entry `classes` of the manifest, `classesUrl` of the pool) and the worker downloads it at the first review, the `.wasm` contains no data; the native app uses the included table) and the page shows the table "Chemical classes from diagnostic fragments and neutral losses" (English only, admin only): it is a probe, it does not tick the checklist and does not assign 2b.
