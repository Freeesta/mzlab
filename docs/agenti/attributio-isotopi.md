# mzmine 4.10 "Isotopic peaks finder" in Rust (`untargeted/isofinder.rs`, `isofinder/`)

- Works on the SPECTRUM of each feature and writes a pattern (charge, peaks, score); unlike the 13C filter in `isotopes.rs` it does not remove rows. Module `isofinder` with `envelope.rs` (expected envelope and upper limit), `ladder.rs` (ladder on the 13C grid) and `score.rs` (score parts); the isotope masses come from `mzlab_core::isoscore::isotopes_of`.
- `Engine::candidates(z, mz, y, target)`: the point closest to the target, then descent from the top of the spectrum and ascent (a point is kept if adding or subtracting an isotopic difference of the elements lands on a candidate), stopping at `max_diff` beyond the last accepted one. Tolerance `Tol`: the larger of 0.005 m/z and 15 ppm.
- `Engine::score_charge(z, mz, candidates, polarity)`: `None` if the candidates are fewer than `min_signals(z)` (2, 3, 4, 5 for z 1-3, 4-5, 6-9, ≥10) or if "Require 13C" finds no gap-free ladder up to the anchor or the M+1/M ratio falls outside the limits for carbon atoms per dalton (1/20, 1/14, 1/12). Otherwise `ChargeEval`: `raw` = quality × (1 + 0.1 × kept peaks), with sliding-cosine fit to the carbon Poisson envelope, coverage, self-consistency (only z > 1), intensity agreement and spacing regularity.
- `select::detect(engine, mz, y, mz_feature, polarity)` tries charges 1..max, keeps those with `raw` > 0, sorts them by `raw` (ties by quality) and returns `Detection`: best charge, `IsotopePattern` (charge, kept peaks, score = quality × intensity agreement, probability) with the winner always present and the alternatives with quality ≥ best − 0.15 and ≥ 0.1, and the probabilities of ALL charges (quality / sum of qualities, sum 1; an alternative that is not kept does not appear in the patterns).
- The winner goes by `raw`, so it does not necessarily have the highest probability. `None` if no charge counts.
- `auto::detect(signals, charge, tol, candidates)` (Cl, Br, S, Si): pairs of peaks with spacing close to the M+2 defect of each element, median of the spacings, fingerprint of 29Si on M+1; returns the elements not excluded, the best first (`Found`: symbol, indicative atoms ≤ 40, confidence), empty for a plain CHNO.
- `Config::mode` (`ElementMode`, default `UserPlusAuto` as in mzmine): if it is not `UserDefined`, `select::detect` runs `auto::detect` on the raw window around the winning pattern (±2.5/charge), rebuilds the envelope with the atoms found (with the user's elements only in `UserPlusAuto`) and recomputes that charge; the winner stays first and `Detection::composition` reports the elements.
- Not done: filter of explainable signals, refinement across scans, mobility.
- Tests: `tests/untargeted_isofinder.rs` and `untargeted_isofinder_auto.rs` with synthetic spectra (exact binomial envelope; [M+H]+ with exact fine structure of Cl2, Br, S2, Si and of a CHNO) from `tools/isofinder_reference.py` → `tests/data/untargeted/isofinder/{casi,elementi}.json`; parity with mzmine 4.10 not available.

## Elemental composition from the isotope finder (`untargeted/formula_id.rs`, JSON `formula_id/1`)

- Thin layer on top of `mzlab_core::composition::compose` (exact-mass search, RDB, nitrogen rule, Seven Golden Rules, isotope score of `isoscore.rs`): it does not redo the engine. `identify(mz[], y[], mz_feature, Opts)` runs `isofinder::select::detect` on the feature spectrum, takes its charge, the peaks of the winning pattern and the heavy elements not excluded (`auto::detect`), then calls `compose` with those peaks as `obs` and reads the candidates.
- `Opts` (from JSON with `Opts::from_json`: `ion`, `z`, `tol_ppm`, `elements`, `nitrogen`, `rdb_min`, `max_results`): `z` absent = the finder decides (1 if it finds no pattern, `source` "assumed"); above charge 1 the formulas are those of the ion (`[M]+`). Elements: the given text is searched as is; with no text `C:0-50,H:0-100,N:0-8,O:0-15,P:0-2,S:0-3` plus the finder's heavy elements (from 0 to atoms+1, at most 8).
- Choice and why: candidates are sorted by score (mass and isotopes; with no pattern only the mass term, a Gaussian with expected error 3 ppm); `confidence` = share of the score over the sum of the list (never "identified"); `chosen` is the index chosen by the program (decision 78: the caller may change it), `alternatives` those within 0.15 of the best, `why` the reasons as codes (`massError`, `rdb`, `isotopeFit` or `massOnly`, `rulesOk`, `ruleBroken`) with the number, which the interface writes out.
- Output `formula_id/1`: `charge{value,source(given|finder|assumed),probability}`, `observed[[mz,int]]`, `elements_found[{symbol,atoms,confidence}]`, `candidates[{formula,neutral,mz_calc,error_ppm,rdb,score,confidence,terms{mass,isotopes},rules_ok,rules_failed,why}]`, `chosen`, `alternatives`. The caller gives the m/z of the monoisotopic peak.
- Route: `.wasm` `untargeted` (`untargeted_formula_id(mz, y, feature_mz, params)` in `mzlab-feat-wasm`) and desktop twin `feat/formula_id`; the pools (`feat-worker.js`, `feat-desktop-pool.js`) expose `formulaId`. In Tabui the "Elemental composition" block of the Identity card (`46_tp_composizione.js`, English only: "Neutral formula", "Ion formula") uses the MS1 at the apex of the row (`riga_tracce`), starts only on click and always lets the user pick another composition, saying that the choice is the chemist's and which one was the program's.
- Tests: `tests/untargeted_formula_id.rs` with ions of known molecules (glucose, caffeine, paracetamol, atrazine, bromazepam; one doubly charged ion) whose pattern is computed from the abundances (`tools/formula_id_reference.py` → `tests/data/untargeted/formula_id/casi.json`): the true composition is first, the confidences sum to 1.
- Option `score_terms` (off): each candidate carries `score_terms`, the weighted mean of mass (weight 1) and isotopes (weight 2, the weights of `compose`) with `formula_ref` `score/composition` (the mass term is the unrounded one that `compose` averaged: the value rounded to 4 decimals is the score shown); with no pattern only the mass. The other fields do not change. Test: `score_terms_open_the_combined_score_and_leave_it_unchanged`.
- `compose` grid (`composition/grid.rs`): product of the ranges of the elements other than C and H, cap `MAX_GRID` = 3 000 000. Before counting, each maximum drops to the number of atoms whose mass alone exceeds ion mass + tolerance, plus one; the limit uses `t` and the tolerance of the results test, so it holds for every sign, charge, marking and adduct atom, and the rows removed were never results.
- Lossless test: `composition_cut.rs` and `test_composition_taglio.py` compare the response byte for byte with and without the cut (`Opts.uncut`). Beyond the cap `err.comp.many` carries `n` and `max`; in `formula.ms1` the feature has `grid` and `max_grid` and the view note says that no formula was searched for that m/z.
