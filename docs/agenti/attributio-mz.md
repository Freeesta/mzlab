# MZ track: mzmine-style detection (`untargeted`)

Part of `docs/agenti/attributio.md` (AT track: Attributio step). Resolver and the reasons for agreement with mzmine: `docs/agenti/attributio.md`, "Decisions in force".
- **Purpose**: the feature table from raw files when the mzMine one is missing; it is the alternative entry point of the Attributio cascade (`attributio/from_untargeted.rs`, re-exported as `untargeted::to_attributio`). The mzmine 3.9.0 stages (ADAP, local-minimum resolver, join, gap filling, isotopes) are rewritten from scratch in Rust, each with parity on the golden data; the only reference is mzmine's outputs, never its code.
- **Modules** (feature `untargeted`, `crates/mzlab-feat/src/untargeted/`): `adap.rs`, `resolve.rs`, `join.rs`, `gapfill.rs`, `table.rs` done; `isotopes.rs`, `ident.rs`, `pipeline.rs` (`.wasm` `untargeted`) and `attributio/from_untargeted.rs` done; `compat = V3_9 | V4` only where the versions differ (resolver, join organisation).
- **Join aligner** (`untargeted/join.rs`, `align(lists, &JoinParams) -> Rows`, feature `untargeted`): like the `JoinAlignerTask` of 3.9.0. At each round the list with the most pending features is the base (stable sort) and the others are matched by decreasing score (m/z weight 3, RT weight 1, closed intervals ±max(0.0015 Da; 5 ppm) and ±0.2 min, RT in `f32` like a `float`): one feature per file and row, final rows by RT with mean m/z and RT.
  - Our own deterministic tie-break: order of the pending lists, of the feature, of the base row. Optional checks not ported (charge, identity, isotopes, spectra, mobility).
  - Parity on `tests/data/untargeted/mzmine39/` (`tests/untargeted_join.rs`): 13 rows out of 13 with the same features, m/z within 2·10⁻⁵ and RT within 10⁻⁴ (the precision printed by the CSV); plus the hand-made cases.
- **Gap filling** (`untargeted/gapfill.rs`, `fill_file`, `fill`): like `Gap` of 3.9.0 on the mass list (peaks ≥ `noise`, MS1 of one polarity) read from the `.mzidx` by m/z window; it does not touch `crate::gapfill`. One point per scan in the RT window (the highest peak in the m/z range, or zero), apex = highest interior maximum, edges that fall within `intensity_tol` and close at the first zero, accepted with ≥ `min_points` points.
  - Feature: weighted m/z, RT and height of the highest point, trapezoidal area in `f32` (intensity × min). Test `tests/untargeted_gapfill.rs` (recreates the 4 mzML with `oro_mzmine.py --genera`; skipped without python3/numpy): the 3 filled cells of the golden data are equal, area and height within 6·10⁻⁴ (the golden CSV has 4 digits).
- **Table** (`untargeted/table.rs`, `to_aligned(&Rows, &[String]) -> Aligned`): one row per ion (no `member_*`), area × 60 (counts · s), cells `CELL_DETECTED` / `CELL_FILLED` / `CELL_ABSENT` (area 0, apex and width NaN); `meta` and `tp` stay empty; `iso` and `ident` are written by `run_series` (below). With the real data `tests/untargeted_join_data.rs` compares the per-file lists of mzmine (`risolutore_<file>.mzML_*.csv`) with `join_Aligned_feature_list.csv` of the `MZLAB_MZMINE_FASI` folder (or `$MZLAB_DATI/HRMS/mzmine39_fasi/`), threshold 0.97; it is skipped without them.
- **Parity with mzmine** (mzmine 3.9.0 in the cloud, parameter names, golden data, 4.10 reference, `arpls.rs`, `smooth.rs`, `localmax.rs`, 4.10 outcome per stage, known differences): `docs/agenti/attributio-mz-parita.md`.
- **Local-minimum resolver** (`untargeted::resolve`, rewritten from `MinimumSearchFeatureResolver.java` of 3.9.0): `resolve(&Chrom, rt_min, &MinParams) -> Vec<Feature>`. `Chrom {mz, y}` is the trace over ALL MS1 scans, with zeros where the point is missing; `MinParams::default()` has the values of `predefinita.json` (threshold 0.85, search ±0.1 min, ratio 1.7, height 3·10⁴, `min_points` 5, duration 0-1 min).
- **Resolver features**: `Feature {mz, rt_apex, rt_start, rt_end, height, area, area_s, scans}`; trapezoidal area in intensity·min, `area_s` = area × 60; m/z = intensity-weighted mean; apex = first maximum. One function per step: `quantile` (like `calcQuantile`: mean of the values at positions floor and ceil of (n−1)·q), `regions` (the walk of the source).
- **Resolver rules the study does not state**: a minimum closes the region only if it is ≥ `search_rt` from the start, no point within ±`search_rt` is lower (point 0 is not looked at) and the maximum is ≥ `min_ratio` × the minimum; the closed region passes the filter on both edges, otherwise it is discarded and the walk restarts from the minimum.
- **Zeros and threshold in the resolver**: a zero always closes; a zero at the apex leaves two halves with one high edge and therefore no peak. With more than 15 % of non-zero points the threshold also cuts the base of the peaks. With RT steps wider than `search_rt` every point is a minimum.
- **Resolver test**: `cargo test --release -p mzlab-feat --features untargeted --test untargeted_resolve --test untargeted_resolve_cases`. The 45 chromatograms of `adap.csv` (points in `adap_scansioni.csv`, axis in `adap_asse.csv`, written by `python3 tools/bench/oro_scansioni.py` from the synthetic mzML) give the 49 features of `resolved.csv`: start, end, apex and m/z within 1e-4, height and area within 1.1·10⁻³ (the mzmine CSV has 4 digits).
- **Hand-made resolver cases**: `untargeted_resolve_cases.rs` (valley at 40 and 70 %, isolated zero, duration, filters, dense base, quantile). The mzmine lists on the real data (private) are not yet compared.
- **Test**: `python3 -m pytest -q tests/test_concordanza_ioni.py` (no dependency on mzmine, cargo or private data).
- **What mzmine does on that data** (to be re-read in the sources before MZ-01…03):
  - the resolver also splits the pair with a nominal 70 % valley (in the data the valley falls to about 37 % of the apex, ratio 2.7 above the `minRatio` 1.7; a true 70 % is not split, see the hand-made cases);
  - the trace with the hole of a zero is built by ADAP and discarded by the resolver (no row); the background trace gives only the peak that sticks out; the join puts the four versions with ±0.05 min drift in one row; the gap filling gives the area of the missing peak a small value (state `ESTIMATED`); the isotope grouping finds charge 1 and 2 and removes the isotopologues (M+1, M+2).
- **Gate protocol, per area**: `cancello_misure.agreement_mzmine` (reported next to it): mzmine rows with maximum area ≥ 10 × threshold (mixed units: intensity × min versus counts × s), our table per family, a single scale for Spearman and |log2|.
- **Gate protocol, per ion**: `agreement_mzmine_ions(native, mzmine, columns, min_height)`: comparison per ion row (recipe `{"mode": "ion"}`, `Mode::Ion`), COMMON height floor in counts on both sides (a row below the floor drops out of both), mzmine areas × 60 (counts × s) before Spearman and |log2| (`log2_mediano` without scale factor, `log2_centrato` like the per-area protocol), matching `tools/valida/abbina.py` (1:1 greedy, 5 ppm, 0.1 min). `Table` carries `altezza` (read by `nativo.read_series` and `read_mzmine`).
- **Reasons for rows without a partner**: `python3 tools/bench/motivi_mzmine.py MZML_DIR WORK_DIR [--rapporto FILE.md] [--altezza-min H]` runs our pipeline (ion and family) and mzmine 3.9.0 on the folder, writes the table per series and per file (both protocols) and the breakdown of the reasons (`below`, `family`, `background`, `split`, `align`, `other`, plus the pairs with areas beyond 2×); `classifica` is the pure function. With the real data the report goes in the PRIVATE repo (`scratchpad/consegne/ATTRIBUTIO_AT00.md`), here only the aggregate numbers.
- **Measurements**: on the 4 golden synthetics (floor 2·10⁴) per-area protocol recall 0.538, precision 0.875, Spearman 0.321, |log2| 0.080; per-ion protocol recall 0.692, precision 0.900, Spearman 0.683, |log2| 0.028; mzmine rows without a partner: 1 `align` and 3 `other` with no feature of ours in any file (250.2000 isolated at 2·10⁵, M+2 of z=1 and of z=2, lost by the detection), ours without a partner: 1 `other` (the trace with the hole, which mzmine discards). On the real data `HRMS/Timolol/` of the private repo: not measured with the per-ion protocol (command above, `MZLAB_DATI`).

- **ADAP builder** (`untargeted/adap.rs`, feature `untargeted`): `build(&[Ms1Scan], &AdapParams) -> Vec<Chrom>`; `build_slabs(.., budget_bytes)` gives the same output in m/z slabs separated by a gap > 2 × tolerance (`slab_count`). Scans = positions in the slice; `Chrom.mz` = intensity-weighted mean (the one mzmine lists), `mz_mean` = simple mean.
- **ADAP rules** (like the Java): points by decreasing intensity, at equal intensity m/z decreasing, stable sort; half-open intervals ±max(Da, ppm) around the point, cut against the neighbours; one point per scan; kept with ≥ `min_consecutive` consecutive scans ≥ `min_group_intensity` and maximum seen so far (never reset between stretches) ≥ `min_highest`; then a zero on both sides of each stretch.
- **ADAP test**: `cargo test --release -p mzlab-feat --features untargeted --test untargeted_adap` (45/45 chromatograms equal on the 4 synthetics, m/z within 1e-5 and height within 6e-4 = precision of the golden CSV) and `--test untargeted_adap_data` (skipped without `MZLAB_DATI` or without `scratchpad/mzmine39/adap_<file>.csv`, which `python3 tools/bench/oro_mzmine.py --reale MZML_DIR DEST` writes).
- **`untargeted` pipeline** (`untargeted/pipeline.rs`): `UntParams` (`from_json`: `{engine:"untargeted", polarity (only 1, -1 or null, anything else `error.badRecipe`), adap{noise,min_consecutive,min_group_intensity,min_highest,tol_da,tol_ppm}, resolve{chrom_threshold,search_rt,min_ratio,min_abs_height,min_rel_height,min_points,duration:[lo,hi]}, join{tol_da,tol_ppm,w_mz,w_rt,rt_tol}, gap{intensity_tol,tol_da,tol_ppm,rt_tol,min_points,noise}}`).
- **Recipe rules**: missing key = mzmine default, negative number or wrong type = `error.badRecipe`; `gap.noise` follows `adap.noise`.
- **Pipeline stages** (`pipeline.rs`): `run_file` (the MS1 peaks of `Index::each_peak` flow into `adap::SlabStream` in slabs of `slab_bytes`, never all in memory; the chains of the first pass stay for the second if ≤ `slab_bytes`/4; a single reused `Trace`; then resolver, features sorted by m/z and rounded to what the `.mzfeatf` holds; the stream steps are in `pipeline_stream.rs`), `run_series` (join → gap filling → `Aligned`), `to_file_features`/`from_file_features` (area ×60 / ÷60), `From<&resolve::Feature> for Feature`.
- Fields of the `untargeted` `.mzfeatf`: apex, `scan_lo`/`scan_hi`, points, S/N and FWHM go into the file (S/N and FWHM are 0 for narrow peaks). Test: `--test untargeted_pipeline` (on the golden synthetics 13 rows out of 13 and 52 cells out of 52 equal to mzmine).
- **`.wasm` `untargeted`** (`mzlab-feat-wasm`, feature `untargeted`, 256 174 B): `detect_untargeted_to` (one `.mzidx` → `.mzfeatf`) and `align_untargeted_to` (the `.mzfeatf` and the indexes → `.mzfeat`), same formats as mzFinder; bytes equal to those of the hand-run stages (`tests/untargeted_wasm.rs`), independent of the sink; rejected budget = `error.memoryBudget`. Pool: `detectAllUnt`/`alignUnt` in `feat-worker.js` and in `feat-desktop-pool.js` (routes `feat/untargeted/detect|align`); `feat-run.js` uses them if the recipe has `engine:"untargeted"` (no hypotheses or proofs).
- **Pipeline panel**: the "Engine" choice (mzFinder | mzmine-like (Attributio)) exists only in administrator mode (`uipAdminOn()`), the parameters carry the name of the mzmine module in square brackets; test `tests_e2e/e2e_attributio_pipeline.py`.
- **Example and gate**: `cargo run --release -p mzlab-feat-wasm --features untargeted --example untargeted -- OUT_DIR INPUT... [--params JSON] [--csv F]` (inputs `.mzidx`, `.mzML` or folders; writes the `.mzfeatf`, `series.mzfeat`, sha256, time and memory, and a CSV like the mzmine one).
- **Gate**: `python3 tools/bench/corri_mzlab.py --engine untargeted ...` brings it into the `bench/1` format; `python3 tools/valida/cancello_untargeted.py --mzml-dir FOLDER --mzmine FINAL_CSV [--altezza-min 1e5]` writes the `untargeted` section of `TP_Mine/data/cancello.json` (`ok` true only with recall, precision, Spearman ≥ 0.95 and |log2| ≤ 0.10 with the `ion/height-floor/counts-s` protocol and with the old protocol within 0.90 / 0.80 / 0.90 / 0.25); without the two arguments the section stays `ok: null`. The real data are measured locally by the owner.
- **Level cascade** (`attributio/annotate/`, AT-02): `load_annotations(c, &Sources, Option<&[String]>, &dyn Curation, Comparer) -> Result<Annotations{features, warnings}>` gives one typed row (`Feature`) per detected feature, with level, sub-level, basis, `name` (CSI name), `identity()`, `level_why` and `level_decisive` equal to Attributio 1.12.0, with no tolerance, on the synthetic campaign (`tests/attributio_livelli.rs`).
- **Gate order** (the first that holds wins): standard (L1); spectral library 2a (cosine ≥ 0.70, ≥ 4 ions, precursor at 15 ppm); putative library (off); CSI:FingerID (COSMIC ≥ 0.64, L3); suspect-RT (L0); none (the CANOPUS class ≥ 0.70 is shown separately and gives no level). 2b arises only from the chemist's decision.
- **Modules of `annotate/`**: `settings` (`AnnotationSettings::for_campaign`, `label`, `basis_level`), `join` (`detected`, `remap_sources`, `join`), `gates` (`apply_gates`, `is_propagated`, `inchikey_block`), `naming` (`rank_library_hits`, `name_features`: conflicts with margin 0.05 → L3 `name conflict`), `decide` (`apply_decisions`: name, `3` = review, `2b` = diagnostic evidence).
- **Other modules of `annotate/`**: `rt` (`rt_coherence`, `apply_rt_coherence`: tolerance 0.20 min, `isomers not discriminated`, `RT isomer`), `reasons` (`level_reasons`), `standards` (`reference_standard_matches`, `gnps_comparer` = GNPS cosine of `mzlab_lib::scores`), `fingerprint` (`annotations_fingerprint`: 24-digit FNV on files and settings, no clock).
- **Curation** (`annotate/curate.rs`): the `Curation` trait (`match_cec`, `is_headline`, `clean_library_name`, `curate_identity`, `canonical_name`, `formula_override`) leaves to AT-03 the CEC map, the synonyms, the PEG and the forced formula; `BasicCuration` curates nothing.
- **Not ported**: MS2 Engine and MS2DeepScore (warning if `use_ms2_engine`), toxicity, `categories` and `toxicity` checks; `gnps_mass_tol_ppm` ≠ 15 gives only a warning (the GNPS2 results are read at 15 ppm).
- **Formulas** (`attributio/formula.rs`): `formula_mass`, `mass_of`, `parse_adduct` (from `review/adduct.rs`: 36 elements like Attributio; the 14-element table of `mzlab-core` differs by up to 1.2·10⁻⁸ in relative terms and has no Fe, Cu, Zn), `common_adducts`, `formula_charge`, `neutral_equivalent`, `ion_mz`, `ppm`, `best_common_adduct`, `structure_fit(formula, adduct, precursore, pol, tol)` (20 ppm for the library structures).
- **Formula test**: 20 formulas/adducts, 7 `structure_fit` and 8 names against `tests/golden/attributio/sintetica/formule.json` (`tools/attributio_reference.py`, stage `formule`; `tests/attributio_formula.rs`).
- **Checks** (`attributio/checks.rs`, `rt_logp.rs`): `Context` (registry, members, evidence, PubChem, tolerances, fit, `mzlib` rows) → `Finding{check, severity, campaign, identity, inchikey_first_block, detail, value}` with the Attributio texts: `check_coverage`, `check_mass` (ppm of the declared adduct, otherwise the nearest), `check_names` (MERGE, SPLIT, name outside the PubChem synonyms, name structure against the loader, mzMine entries against their own precursor).

- **Review explanations and proposals** (AT-06): `docs/agenti/attributio-revisione.md`.
- **Curation, names and identity (AT-03)**: `docs/agenti/attributio-revisione.md`. `untid` (mzLab identity: adducts and in-source fragments of a hypothesis, `rowident.rs`) and the Attributio identity (curated name and InChIKey skeleton) give different results when one name covers two unrelated masses (one identity, two `untid`), when two names cover one structure (two identities, one `untid`), and always for features without hypotheses, which `untid` reads as [M+H]+ / [M-H]-; the two are not merged.

- **Isotopes per ion** (`untargeted/isotopes.rs`, `group_items(&[Item], &IsoParams) -> IsoGroups`, `group(&Aligned, ..)` on the table rows with height = mean of the present cells): like the `IsotopeGrouperTask` of 3.9.0 but without removing rows. From the most intense free row, for each charge 1..`max_charge` the free rows are searched at n × step / z above and (unless `monotonic`) below, within max(Da; ppm) and RT (interval in `f32`);
  - ALL the candidates of a step are taken and the search stops at the first empty step; the charge with the most rows wins, the lowest on a tie; the representative is the most intense row or the one with the lowest m/z.
  - Intentional differences: step 1.0033 like the Java constant (not 1.003355); `monotonic` is the mzmine option (it only removes the search below the starting row, it does not check that the heights fall); no mobility and no "never remove with MS2".
  - Output: groups `{representative, members, charge}` of at least two rows; `per_row(n)` gives `(group, charge)` per row.
- **Identity** (`untargeted/ident.rs`, `ident(&Aligned, &IsoGroups, polarity, &IdentParams) -> Idents {ident, family}`): two ions with RT within `rt_tol` (0.1 min) whose neutral masses coincide (window `win`, 5 ppm) when read with two different adducts of the polarity (positive [M+H]+ [M+NH4]+ [M+Na]+ [M+K]+, negative [M-H]- [M+Cl]- [M+HCOO]-) are a family; the anchor is the most intense ion, read with the adduct that explains the most ions (the first on a tie).
- **IIN: no parity with mzmine** (see `ident.rs`): by default only neutral mass + RT; the 4.10.90 golden data has no IIN output.
  - Option `ident.min_corr` (0 = off, default; bytes and `Debug` of the recipe as before): a link to the anchor is kept only if the Pearson correlation of the areas between the two ions, over the files where both are detected, is ≥ `min_corr` (at most 1).
  - With fewer than 4 common files, or with a flat profile, the link is kept. Same code in the `.wasm` and in the app (`UntParams::from_json`), with a byte-for-byte test in the tests of the two crates.
  - Each linked ion gives a hypothesis (source `SRC_NETWORK`, RT of the ion) to `RowIdent::compute_in`; an ion without a link has no hypothesis and is read as [M+H]+ ([M-H]- in negative). Isotopologues are neither anchors nor links: they copy neutral mass, adduct and family from the representative. By default the correlation between samples is not used.
- **Marks on the table**: `Aligned::iso` (`IsoBlock {group, charge, family}`, 1-based, 0 = alone) and `Aligned::ident` are optional columns of the `.mzfeat` (`iso_group`, `iso_charge`, `family`, meta `isotopes: 1`; a table without marks does not write them and its bytes do not change). `run_series` computes them with `"iso"` and `"ident"` of the recipe (`iso`: `tol_da`, `tol_ppm`, `rt_tol`, `max_charge` 1-6, `monotonic`, `representative` = `most_intense` | `lowest_mz`; `ident`: `rt_tol`, `win`, `min_corr`); `"iso": null` or `false` omits them. No page JS reads them.
- **Cascade entry** (`attributio/from_untargeted.rs`, feature `untargeted` + `attributio`): `build(&Aligned, &IsoGroups, &Idents, &[Option<Spectrum>], polarity) -> (Campaign, Sources)` builds the in-memory campaign (one site `F<k>` per file, two sorbents `U` and `V` because the cascade is made for dual-SPE campaigns; `V` never detected) and the joined table (`id` = 1-based row, `mz`, `rt`, `iso_group`, `charge`, `family`, `neutral_mass`, `feature_state` and `area` per site and sorbent;
  - DETECTED for detected or filled cells). `most_intense_ms2(&Aligned, polarity, open)` reads from the `.mzidx` the most intense MS2 spectrum (TIC) of the isolation window and of the peak in the file where the ion is highest. Absent sources (SIRIUS, GNPS2, mzmine libraries, standards, decisions): like a campaign without those folders.
