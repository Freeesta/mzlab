# mzlab-feat at low resolution (full scan, unit resolution)

The pipeline of `crates/mzlab-feat` also runs on LR data (SCIEX 3200 QTRAP, Q1 full scan, MS2 EPI) with a recipe of its own; the tests use only synthetic data (`tests/lr_fullscan.rs`).

## Profile choice
- `Recipe::for_index(&mut Index)` (`recipe_lr.rs`): if `Index::hr1()` is true the recipe is the default one (HR), otherwise `Recipe::lr(defect, LR_FLOOR, LR_MIN_HEIGHT)`. The wasm crate chooses it by itself (`run::recipe_for`) in detection, preview, alignment and hypotheses; `build_evidence*` uses the recipe passed in.
- In the Flow steps (`flow_base.rs`, `run::recipe_for_estimated`, `Recipe::for_index_estimated`) the minimum height at high resolution is estimated from the file noise (`params_height::estimated_min_height`: 10 × the mode of the noise lump, to two digits, never below 2e5; without a lump it stays 2e5; `Recipe.min_height_basis` carries mode and sigma, outside the recipe text; an explicit value in the plan replaces it, `flow run` reports it among the estimates; site and panel use `recipe_for`, with 2e5).
- `Recipe::for_index_in` and `for_index_estimated_in` do the same on the MS1 scans of a given polarity (`features.estimate` on a negative file, also LR).
- The Flow record of `features.detect` on LR files reports the preset `lr` (`Outcome.preset`, also from the cache and from the site), not the default `hr`.
- `spectra.match` and `table.match`: with `tol_da` 0 (default) the fragment window is 0.02 Da; a query with the note `# unit resolution` (written by `table.match` for LR files, like the unit MGF) takes 0.5 Da and, if `precursor_da` is 0, 0.7 Da of precursor (the peak width of the 3200 QTRAP); `flowmatch/resolution.rs`, the same Rust in the site.
- Mass axis: `MassAxis::{Ppm, Da{defect}}` (`axis.rs`). A negative tolerance means "Da": `half(mz, tol)` is `-tol` if `tol < 0`, otherwise `mz·tol·1e-6`. Constants: `LR_TOL` 0.35 Da, `LR_TOUCH` 0.1, `LR_TRACE` 0.3, `LR_STITCH` 0.2.
- In Da the channels are 1 Da wide, with coordinate `mz − defect + 0.5` and two grids offset by 0.5. The mass defect is the circular mode of the fractional parts of the strong peaks (`estimate_defect`); the preview reports it in `"defect"` (Da only).

## What changes compared with HR
- Isotopes: `+1` and a single `+2`, charge 1; `M+1/M` is not defined (`NaN`) in the row summaries (`rowmeta::compute_in` with a window in Da).
- MS2: without `parent` the MS2 scan is tied to the previous MS1 by RT; purity with the tolerance in Da.
- Families: at nominal mass an adduct or an in-source fragmentation with no MS2 on either the light or the heavy one stays `Undetermined` (`Rules::nominal()`).
- Alignment, gap filling, RT map and quality use `half`; `fourier_artefacts` does not apply in Da.
- Hypotheses (`HypoParams.nominal`): they merge by nominal mass, adduct and formula; the alternatives appear as `formula (label)`.
- Targeted (`TargetParams::lr`): windows of 0.35 Da; `TargetParams.mz_offset` is the calibration error measured on the parent (`target::offset::estimate`, median observed − calculated, limit ±0.6 Da, RT window 0.3 min) and shifted onto all hypotheses.
- Dig estimates from the data: the preview (`params::preview`, profile `lr`) reports `da` (half m/z window: twice the measured tolerance, that is 3 robust sigmas of the deviations of the strong peaks from their trace, 0.1-0.5 Da), `rt_tol` (twice the median FWHM of the strong peaks, 0.05-1 min) and `dead` (end of the unretained front of the TIC in the first fifth of the run, null if there is none); functions in `params_unit.rs`.
- The page receives them from `tpmine-worker.js` (`estimate`) and fills XIC ±Da, RT tolerance and "Ignore RT before" in yellow until the user types. Proof: `lr_fullscan` (`unit_preview_*`) and `params_tests.rs`.
- RT drift of the parent (`params_drift.rs`, export `drift_series(files, parent_mz, base_rt_tol)` of `mzlab-feat-wasm`, operation `drift` of the pool): on the `.mzfeatf` of all the full files, per file the highest feature within ±0.6 Da of the parent m/z and ±2 min of the RT of the strongest of the series; drift = maximum − minimum of the RTs (at least two files). `estimate(names, mz)` of `tpmine-worker.js` returns `rt_tol = max(2·FWHM, drift)` and `drift {value, n}`; the page redoes the estimate when the parent m/z changes.

## Noise rows
- LR recipe of a file (`Recipe::for_index`, never `Recipe::lr` alone: MRM and mzFinder LR do not change): `detect.min_snr` 3 (`LR_MIN_SNR`; `--set det.min_snr=`, 0 = off, off in HR) and `detect.min_width` 0 (`LR_MIN_WIDTH`, optional; `--set det.min_width=`). After quality (`pipeline::analyse_with`) `lrnoise::reject` removes the row whose apex is below `min_snr` times the noise of the flanks and, if `min_width` is on, the one narrower than `min_width` times the median FWHM of its 80 highest rows.
- The width gate is off by default: with 0.5 the synthetic precision rises (centroids 0.50, `as_is` 0.49) but it cuts a narrow, documented product of the demo series (`lr_demo`). With fewer than 5 strong rows there is none. The fields are in the recipe text only if different from 0.
- Per-point threshold on profile scans (`recipe_lr::profile_floor`): if at least half of the first 50 MS1 scans are profile, the floor is the mode of the noise lump of the intensity histogram (if below `LR_FLOOR`); the intensity of a centroid is an area and keeps `LR_FLOOR`. The 3-sigma floor cut the peak flanks, with split or lost rows.
- Blank rule: already in `table.filter` (`blank_ratio`, `blank_quantity` = `height`; `--set flt.blank_ratio=3`), with the aligned table of sample and blank; detection is per file and does not see the blank.
- Proof: `flow_lr_bench_tests` (generator `tools/lr_synthetic.py`, jitter 0.05; the thresholds are the measured numbers minus 10 %): centroids path precision 0.35, recall 1.00, 140 rows on the blank; `as_is` path precision 0.38, recall 0.98, split 0.55, 91 rows on the blank.
- Proof, blanks: a noise-only blank (`LR_bench-noise-s1.mzML`) gives 0 rows in both. With the blank rule on the aligned table the precision is 0.34 (centroids) and 0.33 (`as_is`), against 0.22 and 0.26 without.

## Levels
- Profile `lr` of `livelli/1` (hidden): never above 3 (`tetto`: all passed gates give 3), 4 if the nominal formula alone explains the ion, otherwise 5. The profile has no `punti`: the table `PointsTable::bundled()` reads only the default profile.

## Proof
- `cargo test -p mzlab-feat --test lr_fullscan`: synthetic series (5 times + blank, 270 Q1 scans, deterministic noise, calibration shift +0.30 Da) built in Rust with `MzmlWriter` → `upload::prepare` → `mzidx`.
- L8 proofs: `PointsTable::with_unit()` (chosen in `run::evidence_to` from the recipe text in the table meta, `recipe::is_unit_text`) puts the m/z windows in Da (`PointsTable::window`, `match_hypotheses_in`, `RowIdent::compute_in`, `parent_row`, `counts_of_n`), removes the mass proof (a nominal mass is not evidence) and brings the maximum level with MS2 to 3 (`MAX_LEVEL_UNIT`). The views that do not pass through here stay in ppm: `tp/` (products view), `evid/arcs.rs` and `evid/producers.rs` (not used in the site flow).
- Export: `export_ms::mgf_unit` (format `mgf_unit` of `ms2_file`) writes the same MGF with the line `# unit resolution, tolerance 0.5 Da`; it is chosen explicitly and there is no SIRIUS version.

## MRM (`crates/mzlab-feat/src/mrm.rs`)
- An MRM file has no scans: the unit is the transition. `read_srm` reads from a `Tracce` (`srm::leggi`: the mzML or a producer sample, not the `.mzidx`) the `SRM` chromatograms with Q1 and Q3; `detect` looks for the peaks of each with the resolver of `mrm/resolver.rs`. The 3200 writes multiples of a count (40 cps), one point every 0.06 s, mostly zeros: the MAD of the differences is 0.
- MRM resolver: the chromatogram is averaged over half of `MrmParams.min_width_s` (3 s, the narrowest LC peak at the base), the count noise (Poisson, quantum = smallest positive value, plus the MAD if the noise is continuous) is made uniform with the generalized Anscombe transform (`Stabilise`).
- An MRM peak rises by `MrmParams.snr` (10) standard deviations above its valleys, the limits are at the feet (where the trace stops falling), it is at least `min_width_s` wide and has area > 0; height and area from the averaged trace, then `MrmParams.min_height`. No isotopes, MS2 or families. The row `mz` is the Q1 and `q3` the Q3: optional column of `FileFeatures` and of `Aligned` (absent in ion files, so the HR bytes do not change), kept in `.mzfeatf` and `.mzfeat` only if not empty.
- `align` groups by transition (Q1, Q3 to the hundredth) and by RT (`MrmParams.rt_gap`, a single peak per file in each row); the rows are in order (Q1, Q3, RT). `fill_file` integrates the cells with no peak on the chromatogram of the transition (threshold 3 times the noise); `file_bytes` and `table_bytes` write `.mzfeatf` and `.mzfeat` with the unit recipe (`recipe`).
- No wasm bridge for MRM: in mzFinder the MRM series goes through `mzlab-finder-lr-wasm` (`finder_lr_mrm`); `mzlab_feat::mrm` stays the library (`read_srm`, `detect`, `file_to`, `table_to`) for the native example and the tests.
- Proof: `cargo test -p mzlab-feat --test lr_mrm` (six levels and a sample with continuous noise, mzML written and re-read, calibration table) and `--test lr_mrm_radi` (Poisson counts in steps of 40 with ≥ 80 % zeros at 0.06 s: one row per transition, detected at every level with growing area, coherent cells in the samples, no peak from noise alone).
- Outside this module: the level (cap 3 with a standard compared in RT, otherwise 4) applies in the interface; precursor ion and neutral loss are not read.
