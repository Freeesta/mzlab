# Spectral study: MS/MS comparison ideas measured on synthetic data

Measures, against the engine, the four ideas from DAG-OT-IDF and MORPHO-MS that the review judged worth measuring (calibrated null, coincidence p-value and information weights, sub-formula validation, multi-shift analogs; optional E: Tree-W1 on collision energy). Measurements only: no default, score, format, step or site piece changes. The experimental arms live only in test and example code; `crates/mzlab-lib/src/` stays byte-for-byte identical.

## Where
- Modules in `crates/mzlab-lib/tests/studio_spettri/`: `gen.rs` (generator), `scen.rs` + `analogue.rs` (scenarios E0-E6 with ground truth), `arms.rs` (base arms), `null.rs` (arm A), `idf.rs` (arm B), `subf.rs` (arm C, with `deconv.rs` of `mzlab-feat` included by path), `shift.rs` (arm D), `metrics.rs`, `mod.rs` (shared setup, pinned numbers).
- Tests `crates/mzlab-lib/tests/studio_spettri_<scenario>.rs` (base arms), `studio_spettri_null.rs` (arm A on E6, E1, E3), `studio_spettri_idf.rs` (arm B on E1, E2, E0), `studio_spettri_subf.rs` (arm C on E3, E0) and `studio_spettri_shift.rs` (arm D on E5, E0): library of 600 entries, 120 queries, seed 1, the numbers pinned with tolerance 1e-3 (rounding to 4 decimals); each test runs twice (JSON identical byte for byte) and checks that the entropy arm gives the same best target and the same q as `decoy::search_fdr`. Under 10 s each in debug.
- Example `cargo run --release -p mzlab-lib --example studio_spettri -- <scenario> <n_lib> <n_query> <seed> [base|null|idf|subf|shift]`: one JSON line per arm (totals, per subgroup and per prefix before `/`; `ms` of the whole set of arms divided by the arms; `det`: scenario built twice with the same library bytes and the same queries, arms rerun with the same JSON).
- `python3 tools/studio_spettri.py [--arms base|null|idf|subf|shift] [--scenarios e0,...] [--n-lib 20000] [--n-query 2000] [--seeds 1,2,3] [--json OUT]`: runs the example for 3 seeds and prints the `MISURA studio_<scenario>` lines (`studio_null_<scenario>` for arm A, `studio_idf_<scenario>` for B, `studio_subf_<scenario>` for C, `studio_shift_<scenario>` for D; mean, with min-max of Recall@1 and AUPRC in brackets). About 1 minute for the base arms, 2 for arm A, 3 for B.
- An engine or generator change that moves a number updates the test and the MISURA line together.

## Generator (`gen.rs`)
- Randomness only from `decoy::XorShift` with a fixed seed, one stream per object (a base library entry has its own: a subsample keeps the same entries); masses from `mzlab_core::elements`, exp/ln/cos from `libm`.
- Precursors: neutral CHNO formula with P (5 %), S (10 %), Cl (12 %), integer RDBE >= 0 (Senior: even valence sum), [M+H]+ 120-700.
- Fragmentation tree: depth 2-5, branching 1-3 (the root always branches, other ions with probability 0.6), at most 25 fragments; each child is the parent minus a loss from a fixed list of 18 (H2O, CO, CO2, NH3, CH2O, C2H4, HCN, CH3OH, C2H2O, HCl, SO2, CH4, C3H6, C2H2, CH2O2, H2S, C4H8, C2H3N); an ion is valid with C >= 1, H >= 1, RDBE >= -0.5, m/z >= 50, never twice in the same tree.
- Kinetic intensities: each edge has (A, E0), A log-uniform 10^0.5-10^3, E0 0.9-2.6; k = A·exp(-E0/T(CE)), T = 0.15 + 0.012·CE. An ion decays in unit time into its children in proportion to k; what remains is seen. Branching ratios change with CE (no invariance assumed).
- Noise: m/z 2 ppm (sigma), lognormal intensity 8 %, 0-5 noise peaks at 0.5-2 % of the base peak. A set of 40 "scaffold" ions (80-200) is shared by 30 % of the entries (the root is scaffold + a remainder, with the root -> scaffold edge).
- Library: n_lib entries at CE 35 (test 600, example 20 000) with formula, accession `SYN-<id>`, plus the entries each scenario adds; each target has its own `DecoyKind::Fragments` decoy (that of `spectra.match decoys=spectrum`), `.mzlib` built with `build::build_in_memory`.

## Scenarios (truth per query)
- **E0** clean replicate: new measurement at CE 35 of an entry.
- **E1** small: [M+H]+ < 200, 2-3 true fragments; the library entry also has 5 peaks at 1-2 %; two isobaric competitors (same formula) in the window: one with all fragments but one (own kinetics), one independent.
- **E2** positional isomers A and B in the library: common base peak at 90 % intensity, 2 diagnostic ions at 3 % each, the other common fragments at 4 %; the query is A.
- **E3** chimeric (1-eta)·Q + eta·I, eta 0.1-0.6, isobaric interferer (delta precursor 0.02-0.5 Da) or isomer, absent from the library; subgroups `eta=.../isobaric|isomer`.
- **E4** query at CE 10/20/50/65 against the library at CE 35.
- **E5** analog with two modifications on disjoint subtrees: +O on one, -Cl+H (if all ions of the subtree have Cl) or -CH2 on the other; the root and ancestors carry the modifications of their descendants. The modified compound is not in the library; the truth of the analog search is the unmodified entry.
- **E6** for styles E0-E3, half of the queries are in the library and half are traps (same formula as an entry, other tree, absent); subgroups `eN/true|trap`.

## Metrics (`metrics.rs`)
- Recall@1 and MRR on the queries with truth in the library; `hit` share of queries with at least one candidate; `score` median score of the first candidate.
- `pauprc`: area under the precision-recall curve of the first candidates ordered by score (equal scores grouped) up to the last cut with true FDP <= 1 %; the maximum is the recall at FDP 1 %.
- `recall_q01`: share of correct hits accepted at q <= 0.01 on the queries with truth in the library (the power of the null: a null that rejects everything has ECE 0). `q_auc`: probability that a wrong first candidate (trap or error) has a higher q than a correct one, ties counting half; 1 separates fully, 0.5 not at all.
- Calibration: at 20 log-spaced thresholds tau in [0.001, 0.1] the hits with q <= tau are accepted; `ece` is the mean of |true FDP - highest accepted q|, `fdp_q01` the true FDP at q <= 0.01. q = `decoy::lib_fdr` on the best target and the best decoy of each query, as `best_scores_with`.
- `gate`: share of queries whose first candidate passes the `shared` gate of `livelli-1.js` (>= `peaks_min` 6, or 3 when the entry has fewer than 6 peaks).
- `q_true` / `q_trap`: median q of the true hits and of the traps. Per arm: `ms` (example only) and `det`.

## Base arms
- `entropy` (default), `cosine`, `modified_cosine`: all candidates of the 10 ppm window (`search::identity_with`, fragments 0.02 Da, the default values of `spectra.match` on an HR query), ordered by quantized score (`decoy::quantize`).
- `analogs`: `search::analogs_with` with default `AnalogOpts` (hybrid Flash, modified cosine, `min_delta` 0.5 Da); measured on E5 and on E0, where every analog returned is false.

## Arm A: calibrated null (`null.rs`)
- Ordering is always the engine's entropy (Recall@1 and AUPRC do not change by construction); only the null changes. Each decoy kind builds its own `.mzlib` (targets plus one decoy each, accession `DECOY:` as `decoy::make_decoy`); q from `decoy::lib_fdr` and, for each kind, `<kind>+1` with q = (1 + #decoys >= s) / max(1, #targets >= s), then the running minimum.
- Kinds: `fragments` (the default), `precursor` (the engine's other kind), `swap_near` (peaks of the nearest entry by precursor, also an isomer in the window), `swap_out` (the nearest beyond 20 ppm), on the donor's peaks below both precursors - 1.6 Da; `subformula` (as many random valid sub-formulas of the root as peaks, never on a true peak, intensities kept); `sead` (consecutive losses from the precursor shuffled and re-summed, each rank keeps its intensity; the lowest fragment stays fixed).

## Arm B: coincidence p-value and information weights (`idf.rs`)
- On the engine's 10 ppm window candidates (same peak pairs, same decoys, q from `decoy::lib_fdr`). Background B: the targets in the 10 ppm window (`_win`) or within 10 Da (`_wide`) of the query precursor, with the clean peaks of the `.mzlib`; N(f) = entries of B with a peak within 0.02 Da of f; coincidence probability (N + 0.5) / (|B| + 1), without the candidate itself.
- `binom`: -log10 P(X >= k), X binomial over the n query peaks with the mean probability; `idf`: entropy with each intensity (query and candidate) times I = -log2((N + 0.5) / (|B| + 1)); `surp`: sum of -log2 of the probability over the paired peaks. `<arm>@50`: background of 50 % of the entries (a fixed bit per entry), search on the whole library; `top_changed` is the share of queries whose first candidate changes.

## Arm C: sub-formula validation as de-chimerization (`subf.rs`)
- On the engine's 10 ppm window candidates. For each candidate the query keeps only the fragments (below precursor - 1.6 Da) within 5 (`sub5`) or 10 ppm (`sub10`) of a sub-formula of the candidate's ion (`mzlab_core::subformulas::submasses`; a decoy uses its target's formula), then the engine's entropy with the candidate's peaks; `sub5x` / `sub10x`: the same times the share of fragment intensity kept. Beyond `MAX_COMBINATIONS` sub-formulas nothing is filtered (`skip`).
- `nnls` (reference, `spectra.chimeric`): `deconvolve` of `ms2_engine/deconv.rs` on the query with columns the targets within +-1 Da and the decoys of the window; score is the candidate's share. `peaks` (only `sub5` / `sub10`, filter of the true entry): share of true fragments lost (`true_lost`), of interferer removed (`int_removed`), of noise removed (`noise_removed`), queries without a filter (`skip`).

## Arm D: multi-shift analogs (`shift.rs`)
- Window, minimum shift, 300 candidates, >= 4 pairs and top 10 as in `AnalogOpts::default`. Candidates from an in-memory index with 0.01 Da bins: peaks, losses from the precursor and differences between peaks (the edge channel), ordered by shared bins (+-1). delta1 from the maximum of a vote histogram of the query - candidate differences (outside 0.5 Da from 0 and from delta precursor; a bin also sums the votes of delta precursor - delta), delta2 = delta precursor - delta1.
- `nl`: cosine of the differences between peaks (weights sqrt(Ii Ij)); `mc4`: one-to-one cosine as `modified_cosine` with the shifts {0, delta1, delta2, delta precursor}; `sankoff`: 2-shift DP on the maximum-likelihood arborescence of the candidate (parent of a peak: the heaviest peak or the precursor whose difference is the library's most frequent loss), states {0, delta1, delta2, delta precursor}, a child carries a subset of its parent's modifications;
- `mc4_flash`: `mc4` on the engine's hybrid Flash candidates, without the edge channel. In E0 every analog returned is false (`hit`); `hit_s90` / `recall1_s90`: with score >= 0.9.

## Measures
The full per-scenario tables are regenerated by `python3 tools/studio_spettri.py` (3 seeds, n_lib 20 000, 2000 queries) and pinned at library 600 / 120 queries by the tests; the findings below are what they show. All arms are deterministic (`det` true for every arm, scenario and seed).

## Findings
- The `Fragments` decoys do not separate the traps that share fragments with the entry of the same formula: in E6 the median q of the E1 and E2 traps is 0 and the true FDP at q <= 0.01 is 0.37 (ECE 0.34). The traps without common fragments (E0, E3) stay at q 1. This is the lower bound already written in `lib.fdr.note`; target of arm A.
- Small (E1): entropy gets 4 % of the first candidates wrong and the errors sit at the top (AUPRC@1 % 0.07); cosine gets 1.6 % wrong (AUPRC 0.32, very variable across seeds). The `shared` gate lets 0.03 % through (3 shared peaks against 6, because the entry has 5 more weak peaks): same outcome as MISURA livelli_ms2_piccoli. Target of arm B.
- Chimeric (E3): the first candidate stays right up to eta 0.6, but entropy drops from 0.92 (E0) to 0.84 at eta 0.1 and to 0.60-0.61 at eta 0.6, with no difference between isobaric interferer and isomer. Target of arm C.
- Two-shift analogs (E5): the analog search finds the parent in 29 % of cases and in 68 % returns no candidate (`hit` 0.32: fewer than 3 hybrid pairs or fewer than 4 paired peaks); in E0 it returns a false analog for 16 % of the queries. Target of arm D.
- Energy (E4): the first candidate stays right (>= 0.99 at every CE), the score drops to 0.65 at CE 10 and the `shared` gate passes 16 %.
- Positional isomers (E2): with 2 ppm and 8 % noise the two diagnostics at 3 % suffice for all three scores (Recall@1 1).
- Arm A: no decoy kind calibrates the traps. The best in E6 is `sead` (true FDP at q <= 0.01 from 0.37 to 0.21, ECE from 0.34 to 0.15, `q_auc` from 0.85 to 0.96) and it pays in power: `recall_q01` from 0.993 to 0.934, 0.90 on chimeric (0.73 at eta 0.6), 0.83 on small; `swap_out` drops to 0.24 with less loss (0.971). The isomeric traps (E2 style, 90 % of the base peak in common) stay at FDP 0.47-0.5 with every decoy: they are not random errors and no decoy null sees them.
- Arm A: `swap_near` is unusable: the decoy of an isomer in the window carries the query's true peaks, median q of true hits 0.20 in E6 and 0.33 in E3, `recall_q01` <= 0.06. The +1 correction moves the FDP by <= 0.011 with 2000 queries; the ECE too, except `sead` in E1 (0.077 -> 0.017, because the lowest thresholds stay empty).
- Arm A and the rule: Recall@1 and AUPRC are those of entropy by construction, so no kind "beats" the base under the rule; the calibration gain of `sead` and `swap_out` is real (3 seeds, `det` true) but stays far from FDP 1 % and costs power. The owner decides.
- Arm B: the binomial p-value pushes the errors of the small ones to the bottom: AUPRC@1 % of E1 from 0.07 to 0.44 (`_wide`) and 0.37 (`_win`), on each of the 3 seeds (minimum +0.26), with E0 and E2 unchanged; Recall@1 does not move (+0.1 points), so under the rule it does not "beat" the base. Entropy with IDF weights changes nothing (AUPRC 0.065-0.097); the summed surprisal loses 1.7 points of Recall@1.
- Arm B, library dependence: with half the background the first candidate changes in 5 % of the E1 queries and Recall@1 loses 2.7 points for binom and surp (the right entry outside the background and the competitor inside do not have the same probability); `binom_win` returns to AUPRC 0.065, `binom_wide` keeps 0.32. Such a score depends on which other entries the library has.
- Arm B, if the owner wanted `binom_wide` (not a winner): it would need N(f) per precursor +-10 Da at search time, i.e. a fragment count per precursor interval in the `.mzlib` (format change, or a scan of ~700 entries per query as here: on average 1.2 s per arm and 2000 queries on 20 000 entries, against 0.05-0.06 s for identity); lgamma/log/exp must be replaced by tables or integers and the score quantized as `decoy::quantize`; `score_terms` would show k, n, the mean probability and |B|.
- Arm C: the sub-formula filter removes the isobaric interferer (0.73 of its fragments at 5 ppm, 0.62 at 10 ppm) and the true entry's score at eta 0.6 rises from 0.60 to 0.88 (`sub5`), on each of the 3 seeds; the isomeric interferer has the same formula, each of its fragments is a sub-formula and the filter removes none (score 0.61 -> 0.62). At 5 ppm 1 % of the true fragments are lost (2 ppm noise on the tail), at 10 ppm none.
- Arm C and the rule: Recall@1 of E3 is already 0.9995 with entropy and AUPRC 0.9995, so no filter "beats" the base (sub5 -0.1 points, sub10x -0.03); E0 loses <= 0.02 points. The gain is only in the score of the right entry (thresholds and gates), not in the ordering. The weight by kept intensity (`x`) lowers the score below entropy (0.41 at eta 0.6 isobaric) because it counts the removed interferer as a loss.
- Arm C, NNLS reference: deconvolution on the entries within +-1 Da loses 2 points of Recall@1 in E3 (4.2 at eta 0.6 isomer) and 1.3 in E0, AUPRC 0.87 and 0.94: similar columns share the intensity and a decoy or an isomer takes the share. It is not an identity score.
- Arm C, if the owner wanted `sub5` / `sub10` as a threshold (not a winner): it would need the ion formula of each candidate in the `.mzlib` and the enumeration of `subformulas::submasses` in the `librerie` wasm (`mzlab-core` code absent there, bytes not measured); the filter uses only f64 sums and comparisons (no exp/ln), so it is the same between native and wasm; `score_terms` would show the removed peaks, their intensity and the ppm tolerance.
- Arm D: `mc4` finds the E5 parent in 48 % of cases against 29 % for the analog search (+19 points, AUPRC +0.23, on each of the 3 seeds); without the edge channel (`mc4_flash`) +9 points and AUPRC +0.13. The tree constraint (`sankoff`) loses 11 points against `mc4`: the estimated arborescence gets parents wrong and the vote gets delta wrong in 38 % of cases; the overlap of differences (`nl`) alone does not separate (AUPRC 0). The ceiling is the library: 14 % of the parents have fewer than 4 peaks.
- Arm D and the rule: no arm "beats" the base, because in E0 the false analogs rise from 0.16 to 0.66 (`mc4`), 0.63 (`sankoff`) and 0.47 (`mc4_flash`), well over 1 point; two free shifts pair four peaks of unrelated entries. With the 0.9 threshold (chosen after the measurement) `mc4` keeps Recall@1 0.44 against 0.12 for the base at the same threshold, with 2.75 % false analogs against 0.07 %: still over the guard. The owner decides.
- Arm D, if the owner wanted `mc4` (not a winner): the edge channel adds to the `.mzlib` about 3.4 times the peak index entries (21.7 bins per entry; format change), `mc4_flash` none; in the `librerie` wasm the vote and the DP are a few hundred lines without exp/ln (bytes not measured); the score must be quantized as `decoy::quantize`; `score_terms` would show delta1, delta2, delta precursor and the pairs per shift.
- Decision rule for arms A-D (no action from the study, the owner decides): an arm "beats" the base only if on its scenario Recall@1 rises by >= 5 points and AUPRC@FDR<=1 % by >= 0.05, with E0 losing <= 1 point, on all 3 seeds and byte-for-byte identical on rerun.
- For each winner, write down: extra bytes in the `librerie` wasm (cap and measured size in `tools/moduli/librerie.toml`), a `.mzlib` format change if needed, the cost of making the arithmetic deterministic between native and wasm (no raw f64 exp/ln in a score; quantize as `decoy::quantize`), how each term would show in `score_terms`.
