# Undo/Redo and "removed" (`tolto`) entries (`storia.js`, `STORIA`)
- **Action registry** (`comandi.js`, `COMANDI_TAB`): one row per state-changing action (id, i18n name, module, files that change it `dove`, fields, parameters, key, steps, undo type: `istantanea`, `comando`, `tolto`, `layout`, `flow`, `draw`, `nessuna` + reason, `da-portare`). 
- **Generated page**: `docs/azioni.md` is generated with `python3 tools/doc_commands.py`, never by hand. `tests/test_comandi.py`: well-formed table, handlers and calls with registered ids, no registered field assigned outside the files of `dove`, `da-portare` only decreasing, page up to date. A new state-changing action = one row there, then it goes through `COMANDI`.
- **Command** (`COMANDI.gestore(id, {leggi(p), applica(p, snap)})` in the owning module; `COMANDI.fai(id, p, cambia, {unisci})` runs `cambia`, compares the before/after snapshot and records ONE step `{tipo:"cmd"}`; `COMANDI.inizia(id, p)` -> `fine()` for drags; a command inside the same command is part of the outer step; steps with the same `unisci` on the same panel within 0.8 s are one). The ruler (`spec.ruler`: `p.meas`, `p.rul`; click, button, R, Esc) is the first; the zoom entry also carries the ruler and the view button.
- **Keys and fields**: Ctrl/⌘+Z and Ctrl+Y/Shift+Ctrl/⌘+Z are left to the browser only in text fields (textual `input`, `textarea`, `contenteditable`) and with a `dialog[open]`; with focus on a button, checkbox, slider or `select` they reach the stack.
- **Single Undo/Redo stack**: entry = `{n, p, tipo, prima, dopo}` with small snapshots (zoom, JSON of `p.ints`, fields `bk/bg/fondo/smooth/corr/ref/bgr`; never traces); 100 entries / ~2 MB, no persistence. `pushZh`/`pushIh`/`pushCampi`/`undoZoom` are wrappers; buttons `#np-undo`/`#np-redo`.
- **Undo/Redo keys**: Ctrl/⌘+Z undoes the last entry of the visible tab (in full screen only that of the panel), Shift+Ctrl/⌘+Z or Ctrl+Y redoes, a new action cuts the Redos; ignored in input/textarea/select/contenteditable/open dialog; in Data always intercepted (empty stack = notice); in Draw it forwards to `TPDraw.undo`.
- **`tolto` entry**: removing a file (`removeFile` in `lista-file.js`), closing a panel (X of `chiudiPannello` in `pannelli.js`) or clearing the map marks (`mappa-tabella.js`) asks no confirmation: it records in `STORIA` an entry `{tipo:"tolto", what, tab|p, dati, undo, redo, scade}` with no visible notice (announced in the `#sb-live` region and undone with ⌘/Ctrl+Z or `#np-undo`). The entries have `undo`/`redo` closures; `dati` (small JSON) counts in the weight; never traces or spectra.
- **Remove all**: `removeAllFiles()` (`lista-file.js`): a single `tolto` entry without `tab` (no confirmation or notice) whose undo puts all the files back; `fileRemoval(f)` gives the three steps (remove, put back, free) shared with `removeFile`.
- **Visibility**: an entry with `tab` (closed panel: `p.tab`) applies in its tab and never in full screen; a removed file has no `tab` (the list shows the files of all tabs) and is undone from any tab, never in full screen; an entry with `p` (marks) applies as long as the panel is open. ⌘/Ctrl+Z and Shift+⌘/Ctrl+Z treat them like the other entries.
- **No notice**: no user action shows a notice with "Undo" (not even panel tiling, `affianca.js`, which is announced in `#aff-live` and undone with the stack); if the entry has `scade` (file) a 6 s timer starts, undoing stops it.
- **Files**: in the browser the OPFS copy and the indices (`api/remove`) are freed when the 6 s pass without an undo; then the entry leaves the stack (`scade`). An origin shared with another sample that was not removed stays.
- **Last panel**: closing it (panel X or the bar X `closeAllPanels`) unticks all the files (`untickAll`) and leads to the empty area; the `tolto` entry remembers the ticks (`visNow`/`visBack`) and its undo puts them back before reopening the panels; the bar X is a single entry ("panels") that reopens all the panels with the links; the world change (`closeAllPanels(true)`) records nothing. Test: `e2e_ultimo_pannello`.
- **Panel**: `panelRec(p)` (`explore.js`, the same record as `uiSave`) and `riapriPannello(sp)` (the part of `restoreUi` that creates the panel) reopen it with zoom, integrations and settings, in front of the panels that moved up into its place (`relayout` sorts by `y`); `STORIA.segui(vecchio, nuovo)` reassigns to the new panel the zoom and integration entries of the old one.
- **Confirmations that remain** (`yesno`): writes to OPFS, New session, reset, and the dialogs of `picchi.js`, `settings.js`, `workflow.js`.
- **Alt+Shift+←/→** (sidebar side, `barra.js`) is ignored in `input`, `textarea`, `select`, `[contenteditable]` and with a `dialog[open]`.
- Tests: `e2e_annulla_azioni` (ruler, keys with focus on the controls, registry and burst), `tests/test_comandi.py`, `e2e_tolto_annulla` (file, panel, marks with ⌘Z and button; 6 s duration; reload = empty stack; Alt+Shift in a field), `e2e_reset` (copy freed after the 6 s).
