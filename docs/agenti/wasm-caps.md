# `.wasm` modules: caps, build rules and what costs the most

Every module's cap (`tetto`, bytes after `wasm-opt -O3`) and its last measurement (`misura`) live in ONE place: `tools/moduli/<name>.toml` (read only by `tools/moduli.py`; the build scripts, `tests_e2e/lib.wasm_cap`, `tools/ci_wasm_dimensioni.py` and the `wasm_opt.sh` lines of the workflows read it from there; `python3 tools/moduli.py --list <part>` lists them by part). No number about a module is written in a document or a workflow.

## Rules
- "Under the cap" = strictly under: a module equal to its cap fails, in `tools/wasm_opt.sh` (`-ge`) and in `tools/moduli.py` (`misura >= tetto`). The values of the CI "size" step are authoritative.
- A cap rises by at most 10 % per new function and never in a workflow or a script; `python3 tools/moduli.py --verify --base origin/main` lists every changed cap and fails beyond 1.10 times the base value; an added or removed module prints a `NEW`/`REMOVED` line (not a FAIL: the owner decides). A cap is the measurement x 1.10 rounded to 4 KiB; a merged module has ONE cap, never beyond the sum of the caps of the modules it replaces.
- Exception: a module's toml may carry `[exception]` (`cap`, `date`, `reason`); the 1.10 x check then passes for exactly that `cap` and no other (`tests/test_moduli_dati.py`). Only `chem` has one (owner decision 20 Nov 2026, sci-form 2.0.0).
- Only `python3 tools/moduli.py --measure <site/static>` writes `misura`, from a real build (engine and libraries with the "libraries" step of `sito-rust.yml`, the rest with `tools/build_feat_wasm.sh <dir> all`); two consecutive runs give the same result and `--verify` fails if a measurement exceeds the cap. A PR that makes a module grow says in its body: module, bytes before → after, growth in %, and whether the numbers are "raw" (`tools/cargo_wasm.sh build --release`, before `wasm-opt`) or optimized.
- **`wasm-opt`** is binaryen from the official GitHub tarball with SHA-256 pinned in `tools/wasm_opt.sh` (cached in `actions/cache`; the script reuses it only if the SHA-256 is the pinned one: `tests/test_wasm_opt_chiuso.py`). Without it the script falls back to the npm package, or errors with `MZLAB_WASM_OPT_STRICT=1` (production workflows) and warns in local builds and in `notte.yml`.
- **Steps**: `-O3` (ONE variable, `livello`, for every module; `MZLAB_WASM_OPT_LEVEL` is only for `tools/banco_wasm.py`); the field `wasm_opt` of the module file picks the list: `@estesi` (the readers and `hr`: `--converge` and `--const-hoisting` on top) or `@motore` (`--low-memory-unused --reorder-functions --strip-producers`: engine, libraries and the rest). `--low-memory-unused` is valid because rustc links with `--stack-first` (`tests/test_lettori_wasm.py`).
- **No relaxed SIMD**: never (CPU-dependent results, site = app byte for byte): `tests/test_guardie_regole.py`.
- **`-Zlocation-detail=none`** (no file:line in the panics of the site-only `.wasm` files, no effect on results) is added by `tools/cargo_wasm.sh`, which replaces `cargo build` in the wasm builds of the workflows and of `build_feat_wasm.sh` / `build_lettori_wasm.sh`: it sets `RUSTC_BOOTSTRAP=1` on the pinned stable toolchain and the flag with `--config`; `.cargo/config.toml` and native builds never see them.
- **Sorting**: `mzlab-mol` has its own `SortM` (`sortm_by`, `sortm_by_key`, `sortm`, `crates/mzlab-mol/src/sortm.rs`: one stable merge sort of indices, no std sort) and `SortedVec` instead of small `BTreeMap`/`BTreeSet`; every new `sort*` of `mzlab-lib`, `mzlab-feat` and `mzlab-feat-wasm` uses `SortC` (`sortc_by`, `sortc_by_key`, `sortc`; `tests/test_guardie_regole.py` rejects std `slice::sort*` outside `untargeted/` and `legacy/`); std `slice::sort*` costs 2-10 KB of `.wasm` per instance and stays only where `mzlab-core` marks it (hot path, or order with NaN that `sortc` does not preserve).
  `SortM`/`SortC` are stable and compare with the closure you give: an `f64` order with NaN needs `total_cmp` or an explicit decision (`asc_nan_last`), never a mechanical swap of `sort_by(partial_cmp)`.
  In per-point or per-peak sorts (`mzidx.rs`, `mzidx_order.rs`, `mapgrid.rs`, `maptiles.rs`, `specpeaks.rs`, `specdiff.rs`, `rawreader/extra.rs`) std stays with `#[allow(clippy::disallowed_methods)]`: at `opt-level = "z"` it is ~2× faster than `sortc`. Index sorting by an `f64` key: `specpeaks::order_by_f64` (`#[inline(never)]`, one copy).
- **One copy of the generic cold code**: the engine's `Mzml` has a single reader (`Src`), the header of the written mzML is `mzmlwrite::head_text`, `/api/spectra` of a profile is generic only in the scan reading (`profile_picks`, `Picks::json`), `in_spans` takes a `&mut dyn FnMut`, `Error::with` moves the common part into `with_text` (`#[inline(never)]`). Hot generic code (`mzml::parse_loop`, `Index::walk`, `decode_block`, `mzidx::Sec<f32|f64>`, `binned_cells`, `Accorpa`) stays one instance per type.
  Instances are counted from the names section (`tools/twiggy_wasm.sh`, workflow `twiggy-wasm.yml`, report only) before and after `wasm-opt`; the real gain is measured with the build, since `wasm-opt` already merges many copies.
- **Reader JSON texts**: `mzlab-lettori-wasm` writes them with `mzlab_core::json::Out` (`json_text.rs`), without `serde_json` (tests only); the chromatogram with points is written by `mzlab_lettori::json::chromatogram_json`, the same as the app.
- **Opt-level 3 crates** (`[profile.release.package.*]` in `Cargo.toml`: `mzlab-core`, `mzlab-picchi`, `quick-xml`, `base64`, `zlib-rs`, `miniz_oxide`, `sha1`, `sha2`, `opentfraw`; measured with `tools/banco_wasm.py`, `wasm-potential.md`): the read crates go together because alone they gain less than 5 % or lose in an engine; the caps move with the measurement. `--merge-similar-functions` was rejected (a few KB for +3-7 % time).
- **Experiments, never in production**: `wasm-nightly.yml` and `tools/wasm_nightly.sh` build engine, hr and attributio-all with a dated nightly (`-Zlocation-detail=none`, `build-std` with `-Cpanic=immediate-abort`) and print the bytes after `wasm-opt`.
- **Timing of `hr`**: `python3 tools/bench_hr.py [--extra "<steps or @set>"]` builds `hr`, applies `@motore` alone and with the extra steps, alternates the builds (ABBA, median and spread of `detect`, `preview`, `align`, `proofs` in Node on a synthetic series), compares the output SHA-256 and exits 1 if a median is slower than 3 % or an output changes.

## Modules (one `tools/moduli/<name>.toml` each; manifest key where it differs)
- **`motore`** (`mzlab-wasm`, the engine) and **`rotte`** (`mzlab-rotte-wasm`, `wasm-feat/rotte/`): the routes outside the engine (`stat_post`, `molecules`, `campioni`, `flow_command`, `flow_line`, `flow_sched`, `flow_job_recipe`, `flow_job_lineage`, `manual_table`, `manual_valleys`, `design_*`); loaded by `rust-worker.js` (`load("rotte")` of `rust-msngrafo.js`: manifest SHA-256, errors `error.featWasmHash` or `err.route.unavailable`) at the first request of Statistics, Molecules, Data or Flow; the desktop app answers natively.
  A route that is also in `motore` stays there (`lr_auto_integrate`, `manual_auto`), since the routes worker loads `motore` anyway.
- **`hr`** (`mzlab-feat-wasm`, features `detect,proofs,finder,finder_edge,finder_msn,flow_ms2`): ONE module with the manifest entries `feat`, `proofs`, `finder`, `finder_edge` and `finder_msn` pointing to the same file, instantiated once by `feat-worker.js` and `tpmine-worker.js`; it has no Flow planner (recipe and lineage of a job are written by `rotte`, `worker-flow-web.js`). `untargeted` and `interp` stay separate. The regulatory lists reader `normative_listings` (feature `finder`) is in it.
  **`attributio-all`** (`mzlab-attributio-wasm`, feature `all` = `rev` + `export` + `ms2`, bridge `mzfeat-attributio-all/1`): levels table, Bench 2b, exports and the MS2 engine; the manifest entries `attributio`, `attributio_rev`, `attributio_export`, `attributio_ms2` point to it, `classi-1.json` sits next to it; it also hosts `flow_rtpredict` (loaded by `flow-web-rtpredict.js`).
- **`msngrafo`** (`mzlab-msngrafo-wasm`), **`msnregole`** (`mzlab-msnregole-wasm`), **`msnfigura`**: separate, never merged. `msnregole` computes the structure rules (`expectations(smiles)` gives `[[rule, formula, ion 0|1], ...]`); `rust-msngrafo.js` loads it only if the request has `regole=1` and a SMILES and passes the result to `msngrafo` in the `attese` key (same bytes as the command, `tests/test_msnregole_wasm.py`); the engine worker loads `msngrafo` at the first `api/msn_grafo` or `api/msn_grafo/export`.
- **`llm`** (`mzlab-llm-wasm`): the assistant's facts and streams, loaded by `llm/llm-worker.js` for the facts without a model in the "research" profile and for the assistant after activation and consent (`llm.md`); the plan check belongs to the page.
- **Readers** (`lettori-<family>`, never merged: whoever opens a format downloads only its own; `aperti` and `sciex` count the damaged scan left out): `readers-module.md`. `lettori-wiffwrite` is the read-only `.wiff` inventory and verify, behind the `wiffwrite` feature of `mzlab-core` that only this module and the CLI turn on.
  In `lettori-sciex` the `.wiff` container is read by sector (`Ole::open_at`, `wiff/container.rs`) and the DAD stream on demand (`Wiff::load_dad`), the method document of an isolated `.wiff` and `list_samples` are served by it, and the memory refusal `Limits::fits` is out of line and `#[cold]`.
- **`table`** (`mzlab-feat-table-wasm`): the 15 checks and the value plausibility `plausible/1`; `tests/test_feat_table_wasm.py` compares its `check.json` with the native one byte for byte. **`flowtab`** (`mzlab-feat-wasm`, feature `flow_tab`, `run_tab.rs`): `table.drift` and the table steps of the Flow in the browser: `flow_drift`, `flow_group`, `flow_runorder`, `flow_methods`, `flow_plausible`, `flow_export_annot`, `flow_transitions`, `flow_uv_peaks` (executors `flow-web-drift.js`, `-group.js`, `-qcreport.js`, `-plausible.js`, `-export-annot.js`, `-transitions.js`,
  `-uv.js`). **`flowstat`** (feature `flow_stat`, `run_stat.rs`): `table.stats`, `table.pca`, `table.kmd` (`flow-web-stat.js`). **`formula`** (feature `flow_formula`): `formula.ms1` (`flow-web-ident.js`: `flow_ms1_rows`, `flow_ms1_join`). **`flowcal`** (`mzlab-flowcal-wasm`): `flow_quant` and `flow_curve` (`quant_text`, `curve_text`, bridge `flowcal/2`; `flow-web-quant.js`). ## What costs the most
- The check and substructure search of `mzlab-mol` weigh about 95 KB in the module that contains them: `molkit` is separate. Inside `molkit` each function grows less than 10 % (CIP, MOL/SDF export, colors), substructure search alone +44 % on a still small module, `descriptors/1` +12.2 % optimized (Crippen and TPSA +4.7 %; its `analysis/1` part brings the float formatter of `Out::num` and the full isotope table).
- **`flowcompare`** (feature `flow_compare`, `run_compare.rs`): `table.compare`, its own module: inside `flowstat` the reference engine and the shared name parser took it +20 % (331239 -> 398066 B, local `@motore`). `flowstat` keeps its base size; `flowcompare` is 225237 B.
- **`quanttargets`** (feature `quant_targets`, `run_quant.rs`): `quant.targets`, its own module (Coordinator decision): inside flowtab +19.3 % (over its cap), finder-lr +7.6 %, untargeted +18.8 %, interp +14.7 %; alone 210,630 B, cap 221,184 (measurement x 1.05 rounded to 4 KiB). `rt_ref` (the internal RT reference) took it to 220,916 B (+4.89 %, cap unchanged): the CLI-only hints are `cfg`'d out of the `.wasm` (223,370 B with them, +6.05 %).
- The three statistics steps inside `flowtab` took it up by 45 %: they live in `flowstat`. A `HashSet` in `ionnet::group` cost +12 KB: bisection is used.
- A module that receives the whole SCIEX reader carries the Unicode tables (+9.4 KB in `waters`): `FileSet::find` in the rejection stays only with the SCIEX reader.
- `libm` instead of std `exp`/`ln`/`pow`/trig changes the `.wasm` by less than 1 % (`mzlab-chem` keeps std for `fm.rs`). An error with the values of its text costs about 0.01-0.1 % per module. In the engine a read function costs 0.1-0.3 % on average; a growth above 1 % has its own line in the PR.

## Why a path lives where it does
- The routes outside the engine took the engine down by about a quarter in size (gzip and brotli) and in V8 compile time; `rotte` weighs about 800-900 KB.
- `mzlab-core` at opt-level 3 moved the caps of `hr`, `untargeted`, `table`, `finder-lr`, `msngrafo`, `msnregole` by about +10 % each.
- The damaged parts that a reader leaves out and counts (`bruker`, `agilent`, `waters`, `.wiff`) cost +0.3-0.6 % each on the reader and +0.1-0.3 % on the engine; the block index, the read estimate (`budget_mb`) and the gap fills move `hr`, `table`, `msngrafo`, `formula` and `flowtab` by +0.2-0.5 %.
