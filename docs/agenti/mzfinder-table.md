# mzFinder: product table (HR world)
Texts in English (mzFinder does not use `lang/`).
- **Data**: the Rust route `tp_table` (`TP_Mine/files/finder-tabella.js`, called by `tpmine-worker.js`; `tp::table` in `crates/mzlab-feat`, schema `tptab/1`, `docs/agenti/mzfeat-tp.md`) rereads the last `.mzfeat` and gives each row `in_t0`, `in_blank` and the ratios; roles are read in one place only (`role_of`); `samplesOf` writes the type and time chosen in the page into the `samples/1` model of `finder-ruoli.js` and takes the indices per role from `per_tp` (Rust) to build the route's `samples/1`.
  Without the `.mzfeat` file it answers `null`: the chips "Not at t0" and "Not in blank" stay off.
- **Row and traces**: the route `tp_row(id)` (`tpRow` of `finder-tabella.js`, fed by the Rust engine in `crates/mzlab-feat/src/tp/`, tested in `tp_view.rs` and `tp_table.rs`) gives `{row, traces}`: `row/2` of the table and `row_traces/2` from the `.mzidx` files in OPFS (pool of `feat-worker`); without traces (`traces: null`) the Overview XIC is not drawn and the panel writes the reason.
- **Page**: `TP_Mine/js/42_tp_table.js` (`window.TPTAB`; symbols, area squares, dots, legend, badges and area format in `41_tp_table_marks.js`, `window.TPTAB_MARKS`) is the single component of the Tabui tables: it draws `<table class="tpt" id=…>` with sortable button headers (`aria-sort`).
  The component takes its columns, chips and row model from the view that mounts it (Flow).
- **Options** (all optional beyond `id, cols, rowId, name, onOpen, esc`): column = `key, label, help` (key of the `TT` catalogue: `data-tt` if `TPHELP` exists, never `title`), `num`, `sort`, `cell`, `text(r)` (text for the export), `off`, `sticky` (the first columns fixed, `left` = sum of the widths), `width`; `height` (container with fixed header).
- **Menu and filters**: `columns: true` ("Columns" menu with checkboxes, choice in `localStorage` `mzlab.tabui.cols.<id>`, the fixed columns cannot be removed);
  `chips: [{key, label, help, test, on, off, group}]` (chips of the same `group` are ORed, different groups and chips without a group are ANDed; `setChip(key, on)` turns a chip on or off like a click, `chips` gives the active ones; `off` = key of the help that explains why the chip is off: `aria-disabled`, the click does nothing until `enableChip(key)` turns it on; `reset()` removes chips and search),
  `onCount(shown, total)` and `search: r => text` (the component filters, "N of M rows" in `role=status`, chips remembered in `mzlab.tabui.chips.<id>`, `onFilter(keys)`);
  `pin: r => bool` (row pinned on top with "★", outside filters and sorting).
- **Export and rows**: `exportName` ("Export view": CSV `;` with BOM and first line `# filters: …`, or Excel with `dlx`; filtered rows, in the visible order and with the visible columns, from the model and `text(r)`); `virtual` (threshold, default 300) and `rowHeight` (30 px, 40 on touch).
- **Symbols and badges**: `TPTAB.state(s)` (`detected|filled|targeted|absent|na` → ● ◐ ◌ ○ –, `role=img` with the word), `cells(states, max=12)` (row of symbols, "+N", label "5 detected, 1 filled"), `legend(keys)`, `level(lv, {decided})` (badge `.lvb lv-N` with "L"; decided = double outline, proposed in the label), `pred(n)` (badge "Prev. N" with outline only, style `pd-N`).
- **Row that opens**: only one at a time; the node `#tp-det` (`role=region`, `aria-labelledby` = Identity cell, or the first) is unique and is moved under the row (`tr.tpt-exp`). Opening another row closes the first and the clicked row stays still on the screen (it scrolls only if the panel falls off the bottom). Below 700 px the panel is as wide as the visible part.
- **Panel**: without `tabs` it is filled by `onOpen(id, panel)` (kinetics `#hr-kin` with `TDATI`, XIC, MS², structure and facts). With `tabs: [{key, label, help}]` it contains the header (`head(r, box)`), a `role=tablist` (← → Home End, tab remembered between rows) and a `role=tabpanel` filled by `onOpen(r, panel, tab)`; `.tpt-scroll` (max 420 px) is for inner tables.
- **Keyboard**: Tab on the `.tpt-tg` button (≥24 px, 40 px on touch), Enter/Space opens and closes, ↑ ↓ PgUp/PgDn Home End move between rows, Esc closes and puts the focus back.
- **Virtual rows**: above `virtual` rows the `<tbody>` keeps those in view ± 20 plus the pinned and the open one, with `aria-hidden` spacer rows, `aria-rowcount` and `aria-rowindex`; constant row height (`white-space: nowrap`, tabular digits in the `num` columns); scrolling redraws with `FRAME.next`.
- **Row panel (`TPRIGA`)**: `TP_Mine/js/44_tp_riga.js` (`window.TPRIGA`) draws the header and four tabs from a **row model** `TpRiga` (JSDoc in the file: `title, level, pred, decided, summary[{label,value,help}], actions[{key,label,help,run}], open{xic,ms2}, xic[{file,group,time,rt,y}], xicTip, apexRt, lo, hi, spectrum{kind:ms1|ms2|mirror,mz,y,ref?,iso?{obs,exp},tip}, spectrum2`;
  - Other model fields: `samples[…], identity{neutral,adduct,candidates[{name,formula,ion,dmz,ppm,proofs[{key,ok,text}]}]}, quality{…}, missing{}`) that each table builds from its engines; it computes no numbers and a missing field is not drawn (a sentence with the reason, from `missing`).
- **Use**: `TPRIGA.hook(r => model)` gives `tabs`, `head` and `onOpen` for `TPTAB.mount`; `head(box, m)` (badge and "Decided:" only with `decide` or with the field `decided` (tables that carry the chemist's decision), then the same summary line of the closed row in `.tpr-sum` chips with help, buttons), `slot("decided")`, `fill(panel, tab, m)`, `details(panel)` (closed "Details" box), `toData.xic(mz)` and `toData.ms2(prec, rt, file)`, `tabs`.
- **Tabs**: Overview = XIC of all samples (colour = time with 5 variables `--tpr-v0..4` viridis, blank/dark dashed grey, t0 black, edges `lo`-`hi` with `var(--sel)` inside the axis, line at the apex, legend per group) and spectrum (`ms1` with expected isotopes hollow and observed ones filled, `ms2`, `mirror` with the reference below) in a `minmax(300px,1fr)` grid.
  - Above each chart "XIC in Data" / "MS2 in Data" (from `open`): they switch to Data and open the XIC of the m/z (direct `openXic`) or the MS2 spectrum of the nearest precursor within 0.6 Da (`goMs2`), with no other steps; without suitable files a notice. RT window, extracted and observed m/z (`xicTip`) and the MS2 sentence (`tip`) are only in the chart help (`data-tt-text`), with the T key for the data table (`TDATI` without a visible button).
- **Samples tab and after**: Samples = mini-matrix per group (blank/dark, t0, times, others; 24×24 px cells, background = area on a log scale relative to the row maximum (white = none, darker green `--tpr-g0..4` = more area; `TPTAB.areaScale`/`paint`), `TPTAB.state` symbol on top, `aria-label`) and per-sample table (scrolls beyond 10 rows).
  - Identity = control of the decided level, compact table `.tpr-cand` with one row per candidate (name or modification, "Neutral formula", "[M+H]+ formula", Δppm in HR or Δm/z in Da in LR, proofs ✓/✗/– as `.tpr-pk` icons with help: Persistent, Not at t0, Grows, Not isotope, MS2; columns only if present; 5 rows and "+N").
  - Below, the closed "Details" box (neutral mass, adduct, sources, criteria in words and, in LR, the `.lr-idx` block).
  - Quality = `<dl>` (shape, S/N, FWHM, warnings in words, M+1/M, blank, t0, deviations). Every SVG has a title, a description and `TDATI`.
- **Help**: `ft.tab.*`, `ft.decided`, `ft.adduct`, `ft.q.flags` of the `TT` catalogue (`40_tabui_testi.js`); `ft.riga.*` and `ft.pf.*` are in the `TPRIGA.texts` block and `TPRIGA` adds them to `TABUI_T`. `TPHELP` reads `data-tt-text` before the catalogue, for the helps with the row's numbers.
- **Table search**: the lowercase text of each row (`search`) is computed once per typing burst and discarded at every other `refresh` (rows, chips, order, decisions); sorting decorates the rows once and compares the strings with an `Intl.Collator` (same order as `localeCompare`, empties at the bottom, stable ties); test `e2e_tabui_tptab.py` (component only, without the site).
- **Row drawing**: the per-column models (class and style of the cells) are made once per `draw`; opening or closing a row in a list without a virtual window replaces only those rows (`redrawRows`, fallback to `draw`), in virtual lists the window is redrawn. Test: `e2e_tabui_tptab.py` (same HTML as a full `draw`, focus, `aria-rowindex`).
- **Cached order**: the sorted list stays cached while only chips or search change (it filters without reordering); the sort values are rechecked at every reuse and an in-place edit redoes it; any other `refresh` discards it. Test: `e2e_tabui_tptab.py`.
- **`TPRIGA` test**: the same `e2e_tabui_tptab.py`, with fake models: header, four tabs, charts side by side at 1 280 px and stacked at 600, edges inside the axis, matrix, sentence for what is missing, height ≤ 420 px, data tables, axe in both themes.
- **Decided level (`TPDEC`)**: `TP_Mine/js/45_tabui_decisioni.js` (`window.TPDEC`) keeps the level chosen by the chemist and a note (≤ 200 characters) per row: `key(series)` = fingerprint of names and sizes of the sorted files plus the parent formula, `rowKey(mz, rt, hr)` = m/z at 0.01 (HR 0.0001) and RT at 0.05 min (not the id), `get`, `set`, `all`, `check(proposed, level)`, `text(level)`, `control(series, row, onChange)`.
- **`TPDEC` rule**: validation is `LIVELLI.decide` (a 3 without a letter counts as 3a); 2b is written only by the chemist and is refused with the message of `LIVELLI.decide` in a `role=status`. Entries: `(no decision)`, the levels of `LIVELLI.ORDER`, "Not a TP" (`nottp`).
- **Where the decisions live**: `localStorage` `mzlab.tabui.decisions` = object per series fingerprint `{t, rows: {rowKey: {l, n}}}`, at most 50 series (the oldest drops out), every access in try/catch; every `set` sends the `tpdec` event `{fp, rowKey}` and the views update by themselves. Style and helps `ft.decided`, `ft.decided.note`, `ft.chip.decided`, `ft.chip.hidenottp` (`40_tabui_testi.js`).
- **Hook-up**: with `TPDEC` present, `TPTAB.mount` accepts `decide: {series(), key(r), level(r)}` (`37_feature_table.js`): the Lvl column shows `TPTAB.level(lv, {decided})` ("not a TP" struck through with the word, class `tpt-nottp`), the chips "Decided" and "Hide not a TP" appear and "Export view" adds "Decided level" and "Note"; the row model has `decide: {series, key, level}` and `TPRIGA` puts the control in `slot("decided")` of the header and at the top of the Identity tab.

## Engine progress

- **Progress of the Rust engines**: the label and fraction of each step come from the engine, not the page: `legacy/progress.rs` (`finder_lr_progress(phase, done, total)` in the `finder-lr` `.wasm`, `finder_progress("hr", ...)` in `finder`) → `{phase, label, done, total, frac}`; the fraction never drops. "File k of N" comes from the pool (`onProgress` of `detectAll`); alignment reports slabs and fill files as a percentage (`progress` of `align_series_to`; in the app the route `feat/progress`, asked every 0.5 s, with the same steps); the hypotheses are a single job (0 of 1).
  Payload: `node` (MSn node just built), `features` (n per file), `candidate` (id, name, mz, rt, provisional score: LR after the analysis, HR before the IIMN families).

## Parent-TP pairs

- **Parent-TP pairs** (HR only; test `tests/golden/feat/tp_ms2.json`, synthetic, without the compound of the experience): (1) `pair_components` (`finder_base/similarity.rs`) matches the peaks once like the modified cosine and gives each pair a type: `equal` (same m/z: the fragment does not contain the modification), `shifted` (shifted by the Δm of the precursors: it contains it) and `loss` (shifted with a loss from the precursor ≤ min(65 Da, 35 % of the precursor): common neutral loss, Schollée et al., JASMS 2017);
  for each type the share of the cosine and of the hybrid entropy (the three add up to the totals, equal to `modified_cosine` and `entropy_similarity`), the peaks and the pairs for the mirror. The engine computes it for every candidate with MS2 (`ms2.sim`, in `rows[].sim` as `[equal, shifted, loss]` of `cos`, `ent`, `n`; in `detail` as `similarity` with `pairs`) next to `modcos`, which does not change.
  (2) `mass_network` (`legacy/network.rs`, `mass_network` in the summary): for every rule of the table (`transformation_table`: HR list, `chem` transformations, rows of the page's list passed by `api.run` as `settings.extra_transformations`) an edge a → b if the formula of b is that of a plus the rule (one step; a pair with a rule and its inverse gives one edge, in the direction of the kinetics: source = maximum earlier);
  fields `rule`, exact `dm`, `scope`, `kinetic` (consistent / against / n/a), `shifted` {n, share} from the MS2 of the two ends, `kind`: `both` (it is also the predecessor chosen in the product network), `mass` (Δm only), `network` (two-step predecessor or linked by the MS2).
  (3) the prediction (`legacy/dig/pred.rs`, `candidate.prediction`, `rows[].pred`): four-grade prediction scale after Löffler et al., ES&T 59:19095 (2025), label "Prev. N": it is a different scale from Schymanski's (label "L5/L4/L3", see `levels.md`), never confused:
  4 generic rule, 3 specific to the class (`CLASS_RULES`: the parent has the required group, from the formula and, if present, from the SMILES), 2 observed in the literature (row of the transformation list with comment `lit: <reference>`), 1 confirmed by the data (≥ 2 shifted peaks or common losses with ≥ 0.05 of the cosine and ion absent from the references); more steps = the weakest; a cleavage stays 4. The program never writes 2b.
  the user transformation list also applies in HR and its rows with `lit:` give level 2.
  Exports: columns "Level (Schymanski)", "Prediction (Löffler)" and cosine per type, sheet "Mass differences".
