# mzFinder (internal name TP Mine; hidden, only for the owner)
The core is in CLAUDE.md.

- The only tool that gives answers (TP search). Visible name "Tabui"; labels, placeholders and engine answers are in English and short (a few words: "Name", "Neutral formula", "RT (min)"; never "Formula" alone: neutral formula, or "Ion formula" with the adduct next to it), without `lang/` keys;
  explanations live in the helps, in English only whatever the page language, from the catalogue `40_tabui_testi.js`; internal names stay `tpmine`/TP Mine (folder, id `tpmine`, selectors `#tp-*`).
- **Access**: the "Tabui" tab (visible name of mzFinder; internal names are `mzfinder`, `tpmine`) is always in the header next to Draw, without keys, click or badge, in the site and in the app (no `#/…` address of its own). It is absent in the lab (`window.MZLAB_LAB`, `tools/lab/lab.js`: the package is not downloaded), on phones and in the plain web folder.
  The internal panels (position, reactivity, Pipeline engine) stay behind `uipAdminOn()` (`settings.js`, 5 clicks on the logo). Tool texts are in English even with the Italian language.
- **Substitution position** (panel in the tab of a TP with MS2 and parent SMILES, administrator only): `docs/agenti/positions.md`.
- **Loading**: `web/tpmine-loader.js` downloads `static/mzfinder/` once the page is usable (idle after `qqReady`) or on first use of a tab (`tpview`), with a single promise (`ensureLoaded`) (never on phones, never with `MZLAB_LAB`):
  `indice.json`, the files of `files/` (Web Worker source), then the scripts of `js/` inserted all together with `async=false` (same order), which register with `QTOOLS.register(...)`. `build_site.py` (`pack_mzfinder`) copies `TP_Mine/` in clear into `site/static/mzfinder/`. With the development server mzFinder is absent (it needs the built site).
- **Priority**: the package started at idle downloads index, files and scripts with `fetch(..., { priority: "low" })`, so a script requested by the page in the meantime (`carica`) goes first; started from a tab, at normal priority.
- **Lazy loading**: the files of `TP_Mine/lazy/` are not downloaded when Tabui opens; `pack_mzfinder` copies them into `static/mzfinder/lazy/` and lists them in `indice.json` under `"lazy"`, `window.TPLAZY.load(name)` (in `tpmine-loader.js`) adds one `<script>` per file, once only
  (the same promise on every request; an error can be requested again). A new file in `lazy/` goes in `tools/tipi/pagina.json` with `// @ts-check`. The first is `groups-samples.js` (file table with "Condition", `samples.md`); test: `tests_e2e/e2e_tabui_gruppi_file.py`.
- **Registry entries**: `QTOOLS.register({id, nav, ...})`; an entry with `nav` has its own tab, the others a toolbar button (`QTOOLS.list()`, `addTab`, `render` in `tpmine-loader.js`). Entries have no conditions: if the loader starts, they all exist.
- **Attributio**: no tab of its own, it is a Flow node; `50_attributio.js` exposes only the `ATTRIBUTIO_CORE` engine (English texts without `lang/` keys); details in `docs/agenti/attributio-pannello.md`.
- **New tools of the package** (Attributio, ...): they register with `QTOOLS.register`; the places of the page that mention Tabui arise from its results (`UNTUI`, `MAPFEAT`). Test: `tests_e2e/e2e_tpmine1.py` (tab at first start, no click); the e2e tests of the internal panels set `mzlab.admin` with a startup script.
- Sources in `TP_Mine/` (`js/`, `files/`, `lazy/`, `data/`, `tests/` with synthetic fixtures only); the engine is Rust (`crates/mzlab-feat`, `.wasm` `finder`, `finder-lr`, `feat`). The synthetic series of the e2e tests is `tests_e2e/finder_demo.py` (`make_demo(folder)`).
  Tests: `e2e_tpmine1` (tab visible from startup, state after reload, no encrypted file in the site), `e2e_tpmine2` (engine and tab), `e2e_tpmine_mem`.
- **Tab**: plain title "mzFinder" with a one-line description (no banner, no premium mark); sober style, colours only from CSS variables: the chosen row or card (table, network) has background `var(--sel)` and a 3 px left bar `var(--accent)`, no glows or infinite animations.
  "1 · Files": one-line summary ("13 LC-HRMS · 1 MSn · 2 blanks"), low-row table, with more than 6 files 6 are visible and "Show all N files" / "Show fewer" (`aria-expanded`; hidden rows stay in the DOM with `hidden`).
  ONE "Dig" button only (`#tp-go`, min 56 px, icon `QICON.get("mine")`; disabled with `aria-busy` while working) under the progress bar. "Unexpected ions" (LR only) is hidden in HR.
  On opening (and at every `tpshow` without files) it loads by itself the Full Scan / HRMS files already open in the Data tab (OPFS in LR, otherwise IndexedDB; `loadFromData`); in low resolution with the MS2 files of the series.
  In HR the XIC window is ±5 ppm and the RT tolerance 0.5 % of the gradient (editable); in low resolution ±0.35 Da and 0.25 min.
- **High resolution** (`crates/mzlab-feat/src/legacy/`, chosen automatically with an HR LC series): `finder_base/msn.rs`, `align.rs` and `gapfill.rs` (detection, alignment and filling: the 0 areas of the candidates in the treated files become the area of the same ion re-extracted at the `FLOOR` threshold at ±5 ppm
  and with the apex where the RT map of `rtmap.rs` expects it (reference RT of the group shifted by the file's offset, ±0.04 min + twice the file's residual; without a map, median of the apexes ± half width + 0.1 min);
  `gapfill.rs` rereads one file at a time after the filters, never the reference files; `rows[].filled` = indexes of the filled files, never a found peak: empty circle in the kinetics chart and in the legend, "○N" in the table, column "Filled areas (t, min)" in the exports; kinetics and scores use the filled areas),
  `rtmap.rs` (minimal RT alignment: anchors = strong features, isolated (no other group within 5 ppm and 0.5 min) and seen in all files, or at least in 75 % if they are fewer than 8;
  RT offset of each file against the median of the files, piecewise interpolated between the medians of anchor groups; no signal is moved; `RtMap`, summary: `rt_align` {files, rt, shift, anchors, rms}, shown as an offset-versus-RT chart in the "Retention time alignment" tab of `21_tpmine_hr.js`, Okabe-Ito colours from `--rta-N` and dashing per file),
  `filters.rs`, `family.rs` (ion families of the candidates; `relations` and `dimer_of` have the adduct table of [M+H]+ and of [M-H]-, and are also used by `finder_base/identity.rs`), `finder_base/ms2.rs`,
  `finder_base/similarity.rs` (modified cosine and spectral entropy of Li & Fiehn, also hybrid; the entropy is shown in `rows[].entropy` but does NOT enter the score: on the truth it does not improve the ranking;
  `pair_components`: the three parent-TP components, see "Parent-TP pairs"), `finder_base/msn/localize.rs`, `legacy/kinetics.rs`,
  `legacy/network.rs` (score and proposed levels 5/4/3, `mass_network`, with `diagnostic` if there is diagnostic evidence: 2b is written only by the chemist in "level decided", never 2a/1; badges and colours of `livelli.js`), `legacy/dig/pred.rs` (prediction level), `legacy/dig.rs` (the Dig, one file at a time); interface `js/21_tpmine_hr.js`.
- **Data and tests**: specification and truth in the private repository (`scratchpad/`, `coordinamento/`); public tests use synthetic files (`tests_e2e/finder_demo.py`, fixtures of `TP_Mine/tests/`). The test compound and its TPs do not appear in the public repository.
- **Traps**: the alignment has NO cap in RT (`max_rt_span=None`), only in ppm; flat ones are dropped (max/min < 3) except transients of 3-4 samples and fragment masses of the parent; ranking by score. WebAssembly memory does not shrink (0.85 GB with 13 files).
  In the mzML files the `<precursor>` elements are in no guaranteed order and incomplete (the path is in the filter string); in MSn the precursors are nominal; **composite traces dropped**: building channels in ppm on the peaks of ALL files
  (intensities summed on a common RT grid, a single peak search, then quantification of each file in the feature window) gives worse results on the truth (do not do it):
  RT drift (0.05-0.1 min) and continuous background make different peaks and nearby ions merge in the composite (without a cap on the m/z distance an intense ion is swallowed by the neighbouring background and disappears; with the cap, up to 4 TPs out of 23 are not found again),
  and cutting the composite at the valleys the groups go from 32 000 to 320 000, the candidates from 444 to 1092 and the time from 114 to 465 s; file-by-file detection with alignment stays; ESI saturation of the parent is not a kinetic;
  Fourier-transform artefacts must be removed before alignment; co-eluting isomers are separated with MS2 factorisation; HCD cosine against HCD; for the rate matrices no `eig`: Padé matrix exponential.
- **Parent** ("2 · Parent" tab): the "Name" field (`#tp-name`, ARIA combobox) searches after 2 characters in the curated list `TP_Mine/data/composti.csv` (columns `names;neutral_formula;mass;smiles;inchikey`, < 300 KB, known environmental and pharmaceutical compounds;
  NEVER the experiment compound nor its TPs nor method contaminants; test `tests/test_composti.py`).
  The list is generated by hand from PubChemLite for Exposomics (Zenodo, CC-BY-4.0, version and SHA-256 fixed, note in `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.md`) with `python3 tools/generate_compounds.py` starting from `tools/composti_nomi.txt` (one line per compound, `name;synonym`, `|FORMULA` if PubChemLite writes only the IUPAC name):
  never at time of use, no external API. `pack_mzfinder` copies it into `static/mzfinder/` and puts it in `indice.json` (`files`), so it is downloaded together with mzFinder.
  Arrows and Enter choose; the final entry "Search PubChem for "<word>"" opens `https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/#query=<word>` in a new tab (`noopener`).
  Separate fields "Neutral formula" (`#tp-mol`) and "SMILES" (`#tp-smi`): the engine receives `parent.neutral` (formula, or the SMILES if the formula is missing) and `parent.smiles`; a missing formula is computed from the SMILES with OpenChemLib;
  the formula/SMILES comparison is between compositions (`MOLUI.compare`, element order irrelevant; parentheses, hydrates and trailing charges allowed) and formulas are always shown in Hill order (`MOLUI.hillOf`);
  if they really do not match the warning (`#tp-warn`) reports both in Hill and the elements that differ ("F: formula 4, SMILES 3"). The compound name is shown with only the first letter capitalised (`MOLUI.cap`: field, list, headers, tables; presentation only, `parent.name` and the exported files stay as in the data).
  Structures are drawn with `MOLUI.svg` (bonds and C in the text colour, heteroatoms with `--mol-*` variables readable ≥ 3:1 in both themes, theme change without reload; with "light"/"dark" it gives a standalone SVG with background for exports); the Ketcher editor stays on a white canvas in every theme.
  Shared helper: `mzlab/web/molui.js` (test `tests/test_molui.py`, `e2e_molui`). A SMILES typed in the formula field counts as a SMILES.
  "Draw" (`#tp-draw`, hidden on phones) leads to the Draw tab with a "Use this structure" bar (`#tp-drawbar`, SMILES from `TPDraw.smiles()`) that returns to mzFinder; the editor starts on request: the bar says "Loading the editor..." and "Use" stays off until `TPDraw.when()` resolves,
  a start or read error is written in the bar, and a SMILES already typed in Tabui is shown in the editor (`TPDraw.load`; Ketcher has undo for the previous drawing). Test: `tests_e2e/e2e_tabui_disegno.py`; the structure (OpenChemLib) appears in the tab (`#tp-struct`).
  A drawing with Δ or an uncertain attachment arrives with the formula with the Δ and the note "position uncertain, level 3" (`drawing.md`, test `e2e_tabui_incerto`).
- **Low resolution** (`crates/mzlab-feat/src/legacy/lr/`, `.wasm` `finder-lr`, details in `docs/agenti/mzfinder-lr.md`; test `crates/mzlab-finder-lr-wasm/tests/lr_dig.rs` with the series of `TP_Mine/tests/lr_synth.py`): the summary exposes `timing` per phase.
  **Growth** (`legacy/lr/labels.rs`): Spearman correlation on the part up to the maximum (`SPEARMAN_MIN` 0.8) and rise ≥ ×2 (`RISE_MIN`); with growth passed, the kinetic generation (1st/2nd generation/undecidable, with the parent's rate) appears as the informative criterion "kinetic generation" (state n/a, not in the score) and as `generation` in the row.
- **Parent fragmentation tree** (`finder_base/fragtree.rs` for HR, `legacy/lr/tree.rs` for LR; entries `frag_tree` / `frag_tree_compare`, drawing `js/23_tpmine_albero.js` = `window.TPALBERO.mount`; test `e2e_tpmine_albero`):
  a single JSON format for LR and HR, nodes `{id, parent, level, kind ion|frag, mz, formula, rel, loss, ppm, ghost, smiles[≤3 with weight], alternatives}`.
  HR: the MSn tree (`finder_base/msn.rs`) with the strong peaks (≥ 5 %) of every spectrum as leaves, neutral loss from the parent, substructures (`mask_smiles`, `finder_base/fragtree/mol.rs`); without MSn files but with the parent's DDA MS2, two levels with exact formulas.
  LR: "supposed" two-level tree from the parent's MS2 alone (`supposed: unit resolution`): every fragment above 5 % keeps ALL the subformulas of the ion within ±0.5 Da and the common losses as alternatives (the best is shown).
  SVG with tree layout, border thickness = intensity, dashed border = "ghost" node, colour by MSn level (Okabe-Ito from CSS variables `--alb-*`), zoom/drag, arrows (propagation stops: the page uses the same arrows between candidates), Enter/click = details with structures (OpenChemLib) and alternatives, SVG/PNG export;
  it sits in the HR results (in place of the `<details>` list, which stays as the "MSn tree" sheet in Excel) and in the LR detail of the parent and of the products with MS2.
  "Compare with parent tree" colours the nodes found again in the product (✓ same, Δ shifted; the rest dimmed): HR from the U/S evidence of `finder_base/msn/localize.rs`, LR from the fragments of the product's MS2 (± 0.5 Da, or + mass Δ).
- **Live Dig** (`js/24_tpmine_diretta.js` = `window.TPLIVE`, progress, `#tp-live-cancel`, `DIAG.LONG`): `docs/agenti/mzfinder-table.md`.
- **Untargeted tables** (feature model, mzMine/FBMN/MS-DIAL/GNPS2 readers, library match, exports, open data, feature identities and their view: `js/25_untarget.js`…`js/30_untarget_fair.js`, `finder_base/identity.rs`, `finder_base/edge.rs`): `docs/agenti/mzfinder-untargeted.md`.
- **Parent-TP pairs** (HR, `mass_network`, `legacy/dig/pred.rs`, `CLASS_RULES`, `js/21_tpmine_hr.js`, "More" section, exports "Level (Schymanski)"): `docs/agenti/mzfinder-table.md`.
- **Features on the map** (`js/31_map_features.js` = `window.MAPFEAT`, pure; test `tests/test_map_features.py`): rereads the last HR result (rows, files, settings, inclusion list, captured by wrapping `TPHR.render`) and offers it to the page's 2D map (`mappa-estrai.js`, `docs/agenti/data.md`):
  one feature per row with area > 0 in the file, window ± 5 ppm (alignment tolerance), integration edges only for the inclusion list, filters with the thresholds used (`min_height`, `min_treated`, `fold`, `rt_min`). Read-only: no recomputation.
- **HR products table** (`TPTAB` + `TPRIGA`: columns, chips, tabbed row, "More", Rust routes `tp_table` and `tp_row`): `docs/agenti/mzfinder-table.md`.
- **LR Dig page** (`TP_Mine/js/`): `19_tpmine_base.js` (`TPBASE`: helps, `CSS`, worker, charts), `20_tpmine.js` (`open`: files, parent, Dig), `19_tpmine_results.js` (`TPRESULTS(env)`: card, table, row, exports).
- **Tabui texts and help**: `TP_Mine/js/40_tabui_testi.js` (`window.TABUI_T`, `window.TT(key, params)`: English only, no `lang/` keys, `{nome}` replaced) is the `en` catalogue of every help, ≤ 240 characters;
  `tests/test_tabui_testi.py` checks it and `tests/test_tabui_inglese.py` fails if Italian or a bare "formula" appears in Tabui. Every new help is a key there, never hand-written text.
- **Tabui errors and warnings** (`error.*`, `tpmine.feat.*`): `window.TE(key, params)` from the English table `TABUI_E` in the same file, never `I18N` nor `lang/` keys (an unknown key shows the engine message); `tests/test_tabui_testi.py` fails if Italian appears or a key used in the code is missing.
- **`TPHELP`** (`41_tabui_aiuto.js`): no "?" button in Tabui; the help is `data-tt="key"` on the field AND on its label (helper `dt(key)` in `20_tpmine.js`; name, formula and SMILES have no label).
  `attach(root)` wires a single `role="tooltip"` popover (`#tph-pop`): it opens with the mouse (500 ms) and with focus, the id enters the `aria-describedby` tokens; Esc, typing, outside click or focus loss close it; it sits to the right of the form, never over the next field. No `title`. Test: `e2e_tabui_aiuto.py`.
- **Field errors** (`TPFIELD`, `41_tabui_campi.js`): `set(fields, message)` marks every field of the group `aria-invalid="true"` (double red border and left bar, style in `19_tpmine_base.js`, plus the message linked with `aria-describedby`: never colour alone);
  the group returns to normal with a click or a key in one of its fields (`watch`). Parent formula and SMILES (`#tp-mol`, `#tp-smi`) are one group; same rule in the fields of the transformation list (`43_trasformazioni.js`).
- **LR results tab** (`render()`, `mountTable()`, `show()` in `19_tpmine_results.js`; details in `docs/agenti/mzfinder-lr.md`): the summary card (below), tabs Table, Reaction map, Kinetics and below `.tp-mores` with MRM and "MS2 matches" as closed `<details name="tp-more">`.
  Animated counters and confetti (`.confetti`) only without `prefers-reduced-motion`. A click on a map node calls `show(id)`: it returns to "Table" and opens the row.
  - **Step "1 · Files"** (removing files, duplicates, name help): `docs/agenti/mzfinder-ms2.md`.
  - **Hideable loading panel** (`#tp-sb` above `.tp`, `aria-pressed`, label "Hide/Show the loading panel"): the class `tp-nol` makes the grid one column and hides `#tp-left`, so `#tp-right` (table) takes the full width; the state is `mzlab.tabui.side` remembered in localStorage (`"0"` = hidden). Test: `e2e_tpmine_pannello.py`.
  - **Summary card**: `.hero` in three blocks `.hero-b`: Parent (name typed by the user, "Neutral formula", "[M+H]+ m/z", RT, t½, "m/z offset" if applied),
    Output (strong, possible, unexpected in `#tp-cnt`, "N weak evidence hidden" always present with "show"/"hide" that toggles the Weak chip of the table, "N unexpected not listed", ions analysed, computation time) and Download (Excel/CSV/JSON/Copy summary);
    every `.hc` item has `data-k` and its help `ft.sum.*` or `ft.chip.*`, without an outcome title.
  - **Fukui in the summary**: in the Parent block `#tp-fuk` (`role=status`) shows, with the parent's reactivity computed (administrator only, `tp-reatt` event of `TPREATT`), the three heavy atoms with the highest "Prev. f(−)" (`C4 0.210 · …`, 1-based index): no level, no verdict, no computation in JS.
- **MS2 matched to rows in low resolution** ("MS2 matches" tab, corrections, detail, exports): `docs/agenti/mzfinder-ms2.md`.

## patRoon: ideas yes, code no
- patRoon (an R package with a TP module) is GPL-3 and mzLab is MIT: its ideas are taken, never its code. Specifications come from papers, the manual and observed behaviour; never paste or translate patRoon code; the proof of correctness is the same result on the same data.
