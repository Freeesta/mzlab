# mzfeat: the transformation-product view (`tp/1`)

- **What it is** (`crates/mzlab-feat/src/tp/`, schema `docs/schemi/tp-1.json`): for each row of the table a state, notes, the way it is explained and the kinship with the parent. It does not decide the identity (that stays the cascade of `livelli/1`) and does not delete rows.
  - The block lives in the `.mzfeat` as meta `tp` (thresholds used, id of the parent, counts) plus four u8 arrays `tp_status`, `tp_notes`, `tp_via`, `tp_kinship` (`Aligned::tp`, `store.rs::write_table_tp`); a table without the block is read normally (empty view).
  - It is written by the proofs step (`run.rs::evidence_to`, or `evidence_roles_to` with the `TpRoles` roles read from `samples/1` with `TpRoles::from_json`; `TpRoles::kinetic_files` gives the kinetics files) when the parent is in the table and the series has controls; otherwise the block is empty. The `samples/1` is an optional argument of the `.wasm`; without it, the gate reads only the minutes.
  - With the `.mzidx` files (and the `.mzfeatf`) the step (`run.rs::evidence_sources_to`, optional arguments of `build_evidence_to`) measures the facts of the rows with `row_facts` (row spectrum from `ms2spec::spectra_with`: at maximum TIC, or consensus and without the blank background if the recipe asks for it, `Recipe.ms2`) and passes to `TpInputs.fragments` the fragments of the MS2 of the parent; without, the facts are empty and the output is identical. Memory goes through `MemBudget` (phase `proofs`).
- **Measured facts** (`evid/rowfacts.rs::row_facts(aligned, registry, parent, spectra, indexes, unit)`): per row the isotopic score against the formula of the hypothesis (5 ppm; not computed at unit resolution), the purity of the MS2 and the share of MS2 explained by the parent (`network_share`, tolerance 0.01 Da), plus the peaks of the MS2 of the parent. The caller chooses the MS2 of each row: the example `checks` the best by purity, the `.wasm` the one at maximum TIC. At low resolution the MS2 never raises the level. Proofs: `tests/tp_rowfacts.rs`.
- **Gates** (`tp/gates.rs`, in order):
  - 1 formed, with the minutes only (`build`): the maximum of the treated (time > 0) exceeds by `rapporto` (3) the maximum among the files without time (NaN, controls) and the t0; a row already in the t0 that passes stays with the note `NOTE_T0_PRESENT`. Without treated or without controls it is `error.tpNoControls`.
  - 1 formed, with the roles (`build_by_role`, `TpRoles::of(&[Campione])`): same `rapporto` against each reference in order (blank, t0, dark); the first that wins rejects the row (`ST_CONTROL`) with its note (`NOTE_BLANK`, `NOTE_T0`, `NOTE_DARK`: it also forms in the dark). The infusion is never a reference nor a treated; standard, QC and files without time do not count. Without t0 the gate runs with blank and dark and every row carries `NOTE_NO_T0`.
  - The native step `tp.link` records `warn.flow.link.noT0` in `warnings` of the run; the sample editor of the Flow (`flow-samples.js`) shows the roles and warns beforehand.
  - Meta of the gate by role: it adds `scartate_bianco`, `scartate_t0`, `scartate_buio` in `conteggi` and `ruoli` (`senza_t0`, `buio_su_t0` = parent in the dark / at t0, shown and never a threshold, `bianchi` = types of the blanks). In the kinetics the controls are only blanks and dark.
  - 2 is not an in-source fragment: an ion lighter than the parent, in the RT window `isf_rt`, with a constant area ratio to the parent (CV ≤ `isf_cv`, at least `isf_punti` times with both measured) and, if the spectrum of the parent is known, present among the fragments of its MS2 (`fragments`) or among the MS1 ions of the `infusion` file (`TpInputs.infusion`, from `run_evid::infusion_ions`); same test between an ion and an accepted product at the distance of a common neutral loss (`LOSSES`). Non-constant ratio with the ion in the MS2 or in the infusion: it stays, with `NOTE_ISF_COELUTES`.
  - 3 explained: a rule, cleavage or imported hypothesis that covers the ion (`match_hypotheses`) or a "network" MS2 proof in favor (`VIA_MS2`); the formed ones not explained are the second list (`ST_UNEXPLAINED`). Note `NOTE_CHIMERIC_MS2` if the MS2 purity is below `purezza_min`.
- **Candidates from formula** (`tp/candidates.rs`, `TpBlock::add_fit_candidates` after `tp_block`; high resolution only):
  - For each `ST_UNEXPLAINED` row the neutral formulas within 5 ppm with the elements of the parent (`HypoRun.parent`; O up to +6, H up to +8, no new element), at most 50; score `element_fit` = max(Σmin(p,c)/Σc, Σmin(p,c)/Σp), `ms2_explained` = share of MS2 intensity explained by subformulas within 0.01 Da (`None` without MS2), `isotope_fit` = isotopic score isoscore (`None` without peaks), `total = element_fit + ms2_explained + isotope_fit`; at most 3 per row in the meta `tp.candidati_formula` and in `tptab/1`.
  - A lead, never a verdict: state, notes, via, kinship and level do not change. In the open row (`44_tp_riga.js`, `m.fitCandidates`) the table "Element fit candidates". Proofs: `tests/tp_candidates.rs`.
- **Kinship** (`tp/kinship.rs`): doubtful if a kinship proof is against (kinetics, RT, MS2 relation, controls except the row already in the t0), certain with MS2 relation in favor, probable with rule/cleavage/imported plus kinetic shape in favor (not if `saturo`), otherwise possible. The proofs come from `evid::rowproofs::row_signs` (one byte per type and row); `run_evid.rs` derives them from the same identities of the graph (`evid::identities`, one per row) when they are in memory.
- **Table of the products** (`tp/table.rs`, schema `tptab/1`, route `tp_table_json` (`crates/mzlab-ops/src/run/table/core.rs`; `tp_table(read, size, campioni_json, righe_json)` of the `table` `.wasm`)): for the rows product, formed not explained or parent (or for the index list `righe_json`, with state `null` without the block) it gives `row` (from 0), id, m/z, RT, state, kinship, via, notes, `n_det`, `max_area`, `max_height`, `tmax`, `series` (areas of the treated over time, `times` at the head), `in_t0`/`in_blank` with `t0_ratio`/`blank_ratio`.
  Role of the samples: only `role_of`. Without the block and without rows: `error.tpNoBlock`.
- **Conditions in the table** (`tp/groups.rs`, `table_json_cond`, `mzlab_ops::run::table::core::tp_table_cond_json(read, size, samples_json, rows_json, series_json, infos_json)`, in the desktop `finder/tp_table` with `samples_doc` and `infos`; the `table` `.wasm` does not expose it): `infos` = `[{row, neutral_formula, ion_formula, ppm}]` from the best hypothesis of each row (the table carries no formulas).
- **With ≥ 2 conditions** (`series` of `samples/1`) `tptab/1` adds `groups` (`groups/1` on the requested rows, with neutral formula, ion formula and ppm) and `network` (treated times of each condition and `used`: the network reads its correlations only with ≥ `points` times);
- **Conditions in the table, fields**: `times` stays the axis of all the treated files, and `series`, `in_t0`, `in_blank` and `max_*` of a row are those of the condition where it is strongest (the other files are 0 in `series`, tie to the first). With one condition or none the text is identical to `table_json`. Proofs: `tests/tp_groups.rs`.
- Proofs: `tests/tp_view.rs` (states, kinship, parent fragments, absent controls, block in the file); `tests/tp_roles.rs` (reason of each rejection, infusion outside the references, series without t0, meta and round trip, minutes only).
- **Measurement** (`tools/valida/cancello_tp.py`): with the `tp/1` block in the table the view is the set of rows with state 0, ordered by kinship, points and area; without it, the raw view of the candidates stays. On D-TIM (13 files): the view drops from 132 to 98 rows and the median rank from 10 to 8.5 (rank_90 from 35 to 28), but the true TPs in view stay 12 out of 22.
  - Of the missing ten: six are formed and not explained (the two-step rules do not reach them: they need generators and observed network), 261 and 188 fall at the controls gate (ISF of the parent in the t0) and two are in a family present in the controls.
- `chem/graph.rs`: `Mol::parse(smiles)` (graph of the heavy atoms from SMILES: organic subset, square brackets, lowercase aromatics, `%nn` rings; salts and mixtures rejected `error.grafo.mixture`, invalid SMILES `error.grafo.smiles`, up to 200 atoms) `from_molfile` (V2000 with atom order kept, `M  CHG`, aromatic type 4) and `formula()`; `chem/fragments.rs`: `fragments_json` (formula, mass, m/z [F+H]+ of the two sides). No SMARTS or perceived aromaticity. Used only by `mzlab-feat-grafo-wasm`, not by the proofs engine.
- `Mol::groups()`: sorted names of the functional groups, the preconditions of `regole_tp/3`: `carboxylic_acid`, `ester`, `amide`, `aldehyde`, `ketone`, `amine_primary|secondary|tertiary|aromatic`, `n_alkyl`, `n_methyl`, `n_isopropyl`, `n_tbutyl`, `ether`, `o_alkyl`, `alcohol`, `phenol`, `sulfide`, `sulfoxide`, `sulfonyl`, `sulfonamide`, `nitro`, `halogen` and `fluoro|chloro|bromo|iodo`, `alpha_ch_hetero` (sp3 C-H next to N or O), `ring_hetero_saturated`, `aromatic_ring`, `heteroaromatic`.
- `Mol::cleavages()`: every single bond outside the rings; each side takes an H, and at an ether, ester, amide or sulfonamide bond one side takes OH and the other H, in both directions. `fragment_formulas()` gives the distinct formulas that `hypo::from_fragments` accepts; the rings are not opened here (the group `ring_hetero_saturated` is the precondition of the rules).
- **Rules `regole_tp/3`** (`TP_Mine/data/regole_tp_3.json`, schema `docs/schemi/regole_tp-3.json`, `hypo/rules.rs`): `{format:"regole_tp", version:3, default_profile, profiles:{name:{inherits?, frozen?, rules:[...]}}}`; a rule is `{id, name?, class?, formula_delta, max_steps, prediction_level, source, rt_direction (more_polar|less_polar|free), requires:{all?, any?, groups?, groups_any?}, fallback?}`, and `source` is `transformation_table`, `lit:<ref>` or `class:<name>`. A profile inherits the rules of another; `frozen` forbids using it (`error.hypo.profileFrozen`, unknown profile `error.hypo.profile`). Older files (`regole_tp/1` flat list, `/2` with Italian keys) are refused with `error.hypo.rulesVersion`, never converted: write the rules again in the new format. Profiles: `photocatalysis`, `ozone_chlorine` (frozen), `metabolism_I_II`.
- **Groups and structure**: with the structure known (`Inputs.groups = Some`) the rules with groups present and the simple ones apply, not the `fallback` ones; without (`None`) the simple ones and the `fallback` ones apply, not those with groups; with `Some(&[])` only the simple ones. The structure arrives at `run_hypotheses` as JSON (`hypo/structure.rs`: `profile`, `formula`, `groups`, `fragments` from the `grafo` wasm; empty = no structure; formula different from the parent = `error.hypo.structureMismatch`, malformed JSON `error.hypo.structure`). Proofs: `tests/hypo_rules2.rs`.
- **Observed network** (`tp/network.rs`, `SRC_NETWORK` = 32, name `rete` in `ipotesi/1`): only on the rows formed and not in-source fragments. `arcs`: between rows whose areas co-vary along the series (Pearson correlation ≥ `corr` 0.8 on at least `points` 4 treated times; from the parent the opposite correlation, because it decays) the mass Δ of the rules without preconditions of groups or elements (the rows have no formula) are searched within `ppm` 5; the arc goes from the row the rule starts from to the one it gives.
- `explain_by_arcs` (called by `build` when `TpInputs.rules` is given): a formed row that no rule explained, reached by an arc from the parent or from an explained row, passes from not explained to product with `VIA_NETWORK`; at most `max_steps` 3 steps in all from the parent; fixed order, so same result. It returns the arcs used with the generation.
- **Network per condition** (`arcs_in`, `explain_by_arcs_in`, `TpRoles::conditions`): with ≥ 2 conditions the correlation along the series is computed inside each condition on its treated times (at least `points`: with fewer no arc) and an arc holds if it holds in at least one; with one condition `arcs` and `explain_by_arcs` read as before. The bits of `notes` are all used: the reason for an absent network is in `network` of `tptab/1`.
- MEASUREMENT rete_serie (`crates/mzlab-feat/tests/tp_series_kinetics.rs`, 50 Bateman A→B→C chains, k1 0.05/min, k2 0.15-0.40/min, times 0, 5, 10, 20, 30, 45, 60 min, 5 % noise, fixed seed): with the default parameters `arcs` keeps 0 A→B arcs out of 50 and 0 B→C arcs out of 50. Bench control: a product that only grows keeps its arc.
- MEASUREMENT rete_serie, cause: over the 6 treated times the intermediate B rises and then falls, so it co-varies with A (Pearson A-B from +0.85 to +0.99, while from the parent the arc wants ≤ -0.8) and in counter-phase with C (B-C from -0.99 to -0.90, while between products it wants ≥ +0.8).
- MEASUREMENT cinetica_serie (same series, `kinetic/evidence.rs`, `POINTS_MAX = 0`): on the intermediate B 0 "against" out of 50 (49 "for"), on C 0 "against" (5 "for"); the points stay 0 by construction.
- MEASUREMENT tp_isf_formula (`crates/mzlab-feat/tests/tp_isf_fragment.rs`, hand-built table: 1 blank, t0, 7 treated times; a true product with the formula of an MS2 fragment of the parent, 2 % of the parent at t0 and 40 % at the last time; default gates, ratio 3): it stays "formed but not explained" (state 3) if the parent decays down to 100, 50, 20 and 10 % of the area at t0 and passes to the controls (state 1) at 5 %, because the gate wants the best area to exceed 3 times the one at t0 (the maximum is mid-series). The fragment list only adds the coelution note.
- MEASUREMENT tp_isf_formula, control: a constant ratio of 2 % (a true in-source fragment) goes to the controls (state 1) with and without the list, never to the ISF state.
- `next_generation`: the accepted products with kinship ≥ `min_kinship` (probable) and a hypothesis with formula become parents of a one-step round of rules, label `<hypothesis>><rule>`, `flow steps` +1, source `SRC_NETWORK`; `Registry::append` adds them without moving ids and outcomes. The next round (searching for them in the files) is not linked to the targeted pass.
- `HypoRun` carries the bytes of the rules and the profile (state `MZHYPO02`, with `flow steps` of the hypotheses) to read them back in the proofs step. Proofs: `tests/tp_network.rs`.
- `similarity::pair_components_tol(a, prec_a, b, prec_b, tol)` is `pair_components` with the tolerance in Da as a parameter; `pair_components` calls it with `TOL_HR` (0.005), so the HR numbers do not change. Low resolution uses it with 0.5 Da (`mzfinder-lr.md`, "MS2 similarity with the parent").
- `polpair::cross_polarity(pos, neg, params)` (`table` `.wasm`, export `cross_polarity`; `polpair/1`): for the two `.mzfeat` of the same files (same order, otherwise `error.polpair.files`; `unit` table: no pairs) it pairs the rows with the same neutral mass within 5 ppm according to the forms of `polarity_pairs` ([M+H]+ [M+Na]+ [M+NH4]+ against [M-H]- [M+FA-H]- [M+Cl]-), RT within 0.15 min and `shape_r` ≥ 0.8 on ≥ 4 common files. Fields: `row_*`, `id_*`, `ion_*`, `ppm`, `d_rt`, `r`, `n_common`; a row can have more pairs.
- `polpair/1` is an extra proof, never a verdict: it changes neither state, kinship nor level. Proofs: `tests/polpair.rs`.
- Pipeline, box "Both polarities" (`#pipe-both`, `34_pipeline_run.js`): after the run the opposite polarity runs (same recipe, without proofs), `pool.crossPolarity` (in the app the route `feat/polpair`: `stash` sets aside the finished run, `pair` pairs with the held one and puts it back) and `window.PIPEPOL.rows(id)` gives the pairs; the open row (`44_tp_riga.js`, `m.polpair`) shows "Same neutral mass in ESI± (Δ ppm, ΔRT, r)".
- **Identities per row** (`evid::identities`): the rows are independent; in native builds they are split over up to 8 threads (`std::thread::scope`, blocks joined in row order, same bytes) from 256 rows up; in the `.wasm` a single thread.
- LR Dig (`legacy/lr`, `TP_Mine/files/finder-lr.js`, `flow_digl.rs`, `finder-desktop.js`): the sign of the adduct of the parent (`adduct` of the parent JSON, `ParentLr.adduct`) is the polarity of the products: the page, the desktop and the Flow pass `{polarity:-1}` to `hypotheses` for `[M-H]-`; `rows::hypotheses`, the candidates, the MS2 fragments and the adduct of the header use that adduct (m/z = neutral mass − 1.00728 in negative). Test: `crates/mzlab-finder-lr-wasm/tests/lr_negative.rs`.
