# Agilent `.D` LC/MS: QqQ (MRM, dMRM), Q-TOF (profile, centroids, MS/MS), LC/MS method

Code in `crates/mzlab-core/src/agilent_ext/` (`mod.rs`, `run.rs`, `reader.rs`, `doc.rs`, `datams.rs` copies with the LC/MS branch of those in `agilent/`; `lcms.rs`, `lcmethod.rs`, `lcdoc.rs` new), which `lib.rs` chooses in place of `agilent/` with the `estesi` feature (only the `agilent` module turns it on, `readers-module.md`); `info`, `meta`, `method`, `peak`, `quant` and `scan` are the same files as in `agilent/` (`#[path]`; `scan.rs` carries the LC/MS layouts at the bottom behind `cfg`).

Every change to `agilent/` changes `mzlab_wasm.wasm` (even the panic line numbers enter the binary), so it is touched only for a fix that also applies to the engine (the collision energy from the record); a fix to the five copied files must be made in both folders. GC/MS and `data.ms` are in `agilent.md`. Formats derived from fourteen real runs (private data repository, `dati/formati/agilent/D/`: QqQ 6460, 6470, 6490, 6495; Q-TOF 6520, 6530, 6550) and from the msconvert mzML files of four pairs; no data in the public repository.

The page (`agilent-d.js`, `NEED` and the `AcqData/*` files not listed, key `x:<path>`) also passes to the engine and the module `MSProfile.bin`, `MSMassCal.bin`, `DefaultMassCal.xml`, `AcqMethod.xml`, `sample_info.xml`, `sequence.xml` and the other files under `AcqData/` (the DAD, the module traces); `MSPeak.bin` is not required if `MSProfile.bin` is present.

Proofs: `crates/mzlab-lettori/tests/agilent_lcms.rs` (skips without data), `crates/mzlab-core/tests/agilent_msconvert.rs`, `robustness_agilent.rs`, `tests_e2e/e2e_lettori_modulo.py` (folders of `tools/agilent_sintetico.py::write_lcms`).

## `MSScan.bin` of an LC/MS run
- Head: offset of the `i32` records at byte 88. The record follows `MSScan.xsd` of the folder, with the nested types (`DataDependentScanParamType`: `DDScanID`, `DDScanParamMz`; `SpectrumParamsType`) expanded in place in schema order (`Layout::from_xsd_ext`, at most 4 levels, an unknown type is `err.file.agilent`). Sizes seen: QqQ 186 and 196 bytes, Q-TOF 216 and 220 bytes. Some Q-TOF xsd files (6520/6530/6540) declare `ChromScaleFactor` as `double` but the record has a 4-byte `float`: `Layout::fit` chooses the layout that divides the record length.
- `ScanType`: 1 full scan, 2 SIM, 256 MRM (QqQ), 512 product ion (MS/MS). `IonPolarity`: 0 positive, 1 negative (seen in real runs). `MzOfInterest` = Q1 of an MRM visit and precursor of an MS/MS; `CollisionEnergy`, `Fragmentor` are in the record: every spectrum with a precursor carries the energy of its record in the `<activation>` (MS:1000045, `ce_of`, also in the engine without `estesi`; a layout without the field gives no energy, never 0), a spectrum without a precursor carries it as userParam `collision energy` if > 0; `fragmentor voltage` is always a userParam.
- An MRM visit whose points do not decode (`err.file.*`) is dropped from the SRM chromatograms and those left out are counted in `method/1.warnings` (`meta.damaged`, "N MRM visits whose points do not decode"): `srm()` reads the visits before the document (`method`, `mzml_header`). A SIM scan that does not decode adds nothing to its SIC; it is counted where the spectrum is read (`readers::Skips`), a SIC requested on its own does not count it.
  Limit (AG-01): a Q-TOF "All Ions" run records the fragmented scans with `ScanType` 1 and `CollisionEnergy` > 0; the reader writes them as MS1 with userParam `collision energy` (50 eV on 1554 spectra of a public run [executed]; the manufacturer's library, in the readers review, calls them MS2 and the run without MS1): they enter the MS1 TIC and the feature search.
- The record of a `scan=N` spectrum is found with `scan::record_of`. Proof `crates/mzlab-core/tests/agilent_qtof_cal.rs`.
- `SpectrumFormatID` 3 (QqQ): `n` f64 with the Q3 values then `n` f32 with the intensities (`ByteCount = 12n`); the msconvert mzML writes the stored Q3 minus 0.004 and our id `SRM SIC Q1=<q1> Q3=<q3>` uses the msconvert thousandths (proven against their chromatograms). Format 2 (Q-TOF centroids): `n` f64 flight times and `n` f32 intensities, m/z from the calibration.
- Format 1 with `UncompressedByteCount = 16 + 4n` (Q-TOF profile, in `MSProfile.bin`): `x0`, `dx` (f64) and `n` `i32` intensities, as an LZF stream (liblzf) or a block with head `00 ff 02 90` and run-length (negative value `v`: skips `!v >> 2` points and changes the width to `1 << (!v & 3)` bytes).
- A profile whose decoded `n` (28 B per point: stream intensity, flight time, m/z, spectrum intensity) exceeds the target memory (`budget::TARGET_BYTES`: 192 MB the site, 1 GB the app) is `error.memoryBudget` before reading the block: a 24-byte run-length stream can declare up to 16.7 million points (`lcms_tests.rs`).
- Q-TOF calibration (`Calibration`): m/z = (A·(t − T0))² − Σ c_k · clamp(t, tmin, tmax)^p_k; the coefficients of each scan are in its record of `MSMassCal.bin` (head 72 bytes, record 84 bytes: `u32` 10 and ten `f64`); `DefaultMassCal.xml` gives only the polynomial powers ("Polynomial" step, `ValueUseFlags` mask).
- The scan record is `MassCalOffset` of its `MSScan.bin` record: with `MassCalOffset` > 0 and no valid record (file missing or empty, record cut or emptied) it is `err.file.agilentCalibration` with `scan`, never the fallback coefficients of `DefaultMassCal.xml`; a `CalibrationID` without a step in `DefaultMassCal.xml` is the same error.
- Without `DefaultMassCal.xml` a scan of flight times is `err.file.agilent`.
- The msconvert profile is thinned and interpolated, not the same points: the proof checks the nearest sample within 12 ppm and height ≥ 90 %. TIC and BPC of a run of MRM visits only have one point per visit. The centroid Q-TOF at 284 bytes per record (profile and centroids together) is NOT verified.
- A missing or empty `MSPeak.bin` gives `err.file.agilentNoPeaks`; it is in `errori` of the `agilent` row of `lettori-famiglie.js`, so the site passes the run to the module, which reads `MSProfile.bin` when present, like the CLI (with neither of the two it stays `err.file.agilentNoPeaks`). `probe` of the module gives the reason `variante` for every LC run (`agilent::reader::variante`, `mzlab_lettori::reasons_together`): xsd with nested types or records that are not the 16-byte-per-point GC/MS.

## `method/1` of an LC/MS run (`lcmethod.rs`, `lcdoc.rs`)
- `AcqData/AcqMethod.xml` is a report with two XML documents as text: `RCDevicesXml` (`Section` rows: module, type, section, table, row, parameter, value, unit; sampler, pump, oven, detector) and `SCICDevicesXml` (`SectionInfo` rows: repeaters, sections, tables, columns, parameters in document order; the spectrometer). Parameters before the first "Time Segment" have segment 0 and go to the first. An old method in plain XML (seen in a public run) does not decode into sections: it stays flattened in `fields` with the warning `meta.notDecoded`.
- QqQ: tables "Time Segments", "Scan Segments" (Cpd Group/Name, ISTD?, Prec Ion, MS1 Res, Prod Ion, MS2 Res, Dwell, Frag (V), CE (V), Cell Acc (V), Ret Time, Ret Window, Polarity: they are the `transitions` with `group`, `fragmentor_V`, `cell_acc_V`, `rt_min`, `window_rt_min` of the dMRM, `polarity`, `istd`, `resolution_q1/q3`), "Source Parameters" (+ and − value), "Scan Parameters".
- Q-TOF: "Reference Masses" (reference mass lists), "Scan Segments" (collision energy, polarity), "Instrument/Source/Scan Source Parameters", "Auto MS/MS Preferred/Exclude Table" (`precursor_lists` of the experiment), "Acquisition Mode X" sections.
- LC: gradient (two pump table formats: Time/A/B/Flow/Pressure and Time/Function/Parameter with "Solvent composition A: x % B:y" and "Flow: z mL/min"; Binary, Quat., Iso. pumps), oven, sampler, `instrument.moduli` from `Devices.xml` (all modules), `sample` from `sample_info.xml`/`sequence.xml`. New optional keys of `method-1.json` (the schema is open): above, `channels.reference_nm`, `reference_bandwidth_nm`, `nome`, `sample.tipo`.
- The cells of the transitions table are in the `transitions` and are not repeated in `fields` (`TRANSITION_COLUMNS`): `Mzml::run_head` reads at most 1 MB after `<run `. A non-numeric value goes to `fields`, `parameters.valore` is numeric only.
- DAD/UV: the ChemStation `.ch`/`.uv` files do NOT exist in real runs. The real DAD is in `AcqData/DAD1.cd/.cg/.sd/.sp` (and pump/sampler files): NOT decoded, listed with size and SHA-1 (if small) in `fields`/`raw` and with the warning `meta.notDecoded` (`raw-not-extracted.md`). `detectors` and `channels` (wavelength, band, reference, spectrum range and step, peak width, lamp) come from `AcqMethod.xml`, not from the data.

## Robustness
- Every number read from the file goes through `Limiti::ok_entries`/`profondita_ok` before allocating; `AcqMethod.xml` and the other small members are read within the byte budget (beyond `UNDECODED_MAX` = 4 MiB only the size remains); side XML files nested beyond 256 levels are not read (`meta.rs::MAX_DEPTH`).
- `robustness_agilent.rs::lc_ms_folders_that_are_not_what_they_say`: truncated LZF, run-length index outside the block, absurd counts, offset beyond the file, unknown xsd type, profile without calibration, method of 16 EB / with 50 000 open sections / truncated, detector file of 16 EB.
- Proof classes (`[executed]`, `[read]`, `[untested]`) and readers review abbreviations: as in `waters.md`.
