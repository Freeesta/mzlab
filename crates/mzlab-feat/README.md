# mzlab-feat

Feature engine of mzLab in pure Rust: ion traces of the MS1 peaks of a `.mzidx`, alignment, families, hypotheses, proofs, Attributio and untargeted pipelines.
Used by mzFinder through the bridges `mzlab-feat-*-wasm`, by the desktop app and by `mzlab-cli`.
Area file: `docs/agenti/mzfeat.md`.
