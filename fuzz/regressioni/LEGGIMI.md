A crash found by `cargo fuzz` goes here as `fuzz/regressioni/<target>/<name>` (the artifact file, unchanged), together with the fix. `crates/mzlab-lib/tests/fuzz_regressioni.rs` runs every file through the entry point of its target in `cargo test`; the `lettori_*` targets are replayed by `crates/mzlab-lettori/tests/fuzz_lettori.rs` (feature `tutte`).
