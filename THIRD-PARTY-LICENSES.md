## Example files

The files in `mzlab/web/esempi/` (offered by «Try the example files») are anonymous synthetic data, created for teaching: they do not come from real samples or instruments. They can be used, copied and modified freely (**CC0 1.0**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

## In silico candidate package (PubChemLite)

The package `static/insilico/` (downloaded by the user with one click, used by `spectra.insilico`) contains the structures of **PubChemLite for Exposomics** (Schymanski, Bolton et al., University of Luxembourg, LCSB; Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.22953038), version 3.3.0 of 25/09/2026, SHA-256 `6a258459e1ccf29a5d08e82fcf5240a2fc8afbabe5ba9c678e39aa0801dd89cd`, licence **CC-BY-4.0** (the data come from PubChem, public domain). For each structure: identifier, name, SMILES, formula and monoisotopic mass, unmodified. Built by `mzlab-cli db insilico pack` in the Pages CI, never in the repository; the attribution is also in the package catalogue. https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22953038
